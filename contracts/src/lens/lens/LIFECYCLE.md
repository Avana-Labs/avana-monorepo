Function links point to the interface that declares each function and, after it, to the module code that implements it. Every call enters through [`Lens`](Lens.sol), which forwards it unchanged by STATICCALL to the module of its interface piece ([`LensAccountModule`](LensAccountModule.sol), [`LensMarketModule`](LensMarketModule.sol), [`LensPriceModule`](LensPriceModule.sol), [`LensLiquidationModule`](LensLiquidationModule.sol), [`LensRiskModule`](LensRiskModule.sol), [`LensPreviewModule`](LensPreviewModule.sol), [`LensMarketsModule`](LensMarketsModule.sol), [`LensPositionsModule`](LensPositionsModule.sol), [`LensSpeedModule`](LensSpeedModule.sol); [`MODULES`](ILens.sol#L77)); the views added for the app pages are served by the modules the 2.17 Storage table lists (fees by the markets module, after-action previews and the removal sizing by the positions module, the deleverage sizing by the price module, the close-account preview by the account module, the Hub-asset view by the risk module, the speed views by the speed module).

# Lens lifecycle

The Lens is the read-only view contract for the app, keepers and monitoring: it assembles account, market, price, risk and liquidation state from every Avana contract and returns previews sized to stay valid until a transaction's deadline; no contract relies on it.

## Walkthrough

Alice's account in market 4 (USDC/USDT, 6-decimal vault token at $2.00, collateral factor 80 %, on the Stable LP Hub's CDP Spoke with a GHO reserve at a fixed $1.00 and a USDC reserve priced by its DebtOracle at $0.9999), the numbers of `test/integration/Lens.Journey.integration.t.sol` and the unit suites.

0. **Market screen: [`getMarkets`](ILensMarkets.sol#L18) ([impl](LensMarkets.sol#L37))`(0, 200)` and [`getMarket`](ILensMarkets.sol#L27) ([impl](LensMarkets.sol#L54))`(4)`** list market 4 with its fee tier (100 pips), range `[-50, 50]`, accepted $2.00, 10,000 vault tokens supplied ($20,000), CF 80 %; [`getDebtReserves`](ILensMarkets.sol#L45) ([impl](LensMarkets.sol#L73)) and [`getDebtReserve`](ILensMarkets.sol#L36) ([impl](LensMarkets.sol#L63)) give each reserve's utilization, drawn and supply rates and rate curve (9,000 / 0 / 400 / 6,000 bps). [`previewPledge`](ILensPositions.sol#L29) ([impl](LensPositions.sol#L58)) returns the RiskModule's pledge preview with the block.
1. **Before the pledge: [`predictProxy`](ILensAccount.sol#L20) ([impl](LensAccount.sol#L40))`(Alice, 4)`** returns the ProxyManager's CREATE2 address and `deployed = false`; [`getPriceState`](ILensLiquidation.sol#L22) ([impl](LensPrice.sol#L28))`(vaultToken)` shows Live, served and accepted $2.00, each input 600 s old against its 88,200 s limits; [`getCapHeadroom`](ILensMarket.sol#L54) ([impl](LensMarket.sol#L114)) shows `minPledgeUsd` $10 and the room under each cap; [`getApplicationState`](ILensLiquidation.sol#L44) ([impl](LensPrice.sol#L65)) and [`getPauseReason`](ILensLiquidation.sol#L54) ([impl](LensPrice.sol#L74)) show None.
2. **After pledging 20,000 vault tokens ($40,000): [`getAccount`](ILensAccount.sol#L32) ([impl](LensAccount.sol#L50))`(Alice, 4)`** returns her proxy, `deployed = true`, Aave's account data, one `DebtPosition` per enabled reserve (GHO and USDC, zero debt), and the market position with 20,000e6 credited and supplied; [`getAccounts`](ILensAccount.sol#L44) ([impl](LensAccount.sol#L60)) returns the same view per listed market; [`getRecords`](ILensAccount.sol#L60) ([impl](LensAccount.sol#L73)) lists record 1; [`checkBacking`](ILensMarket.sol#L20) ([impl](LensMarket.sol#L40)) shows supply = credited.
3. **Borrow screen: [`previewBorrowRecommended`](ILensPreview.sol#L42) ([impl](LensPreview.sol#L66))`(Alice, 4, GHO, now)`** returns the recommended `32,000 / 1.10` GHO and the maximum `32,000 / 1.005` GHO of [`previewBorrowMax`](ILensPreview.sol#L21) ([impl](LensPreview.sol#L54)) at a zero rate; borrowing the recommended amount lands at health factor 1.10. [`getDebtPriceState`](ILensLiquidation.sol#L34) ([impl](LensPrice.sol#L54)) shows GHO fixed and fresh, and USDC fresh until its accepted round is older than 4,200 s.
4. **Repay screen: [`quoteRepayAll`](ILensPreview.sol#L57) ([impl](LensPreview.sol#L78))`(Alice, 4, GHO, now + 600)`** is `⌈debt × (1 + rate × 600) × 1.001⌉`, at least the debt at the deadline.
5. **Top-up screen: a third party adds 1,000 vault tokens of liquidity; [`previewRecredit`](ILensPreview.sol#L73) ([impl](LensPreview.sol#L100))`(4, key)`** returns `added = 1,000,000,000`, `usdCharge = $2,000` and `rescueOnly = false` (true for a frozen depositor, under `Paused(General)`, a market breaker, the pool-share gate or a charge below `minPledgeUsd`).
6. **Deleverage screen: [`previewRepayWithCollateral`](ILensPreview.sol#L91) ([impl](LensPreview.sol#L119))`(Alice, 4, key, 1,000,000,000, GHO)`** with 8,000 GHO owed on 10,000 vault tokens returns `x = ⌊(1,979.999998 − 1) × 10⁴ / 10,010⌋` GHO, `V − x − φ` to the wallet and the higher health factor; `(0, 0, hf)` when the call would revert.
7. **Price drop to $1.30: [`previewLiquidation`](ILensLiquidation.sol#L85) ([impl](LensLiquidation.sol#L79))`(proxy, GHO, now + 120)`** returns every gate, Aave's own sizing (`LensAaveSizing`), ready-to-send `LiquidationParams` and the expected seizure, floor and payouts; the liquidation executed with `params.debtToCover` repays and seizes exactly `expectedDebtRepaid` and `expectedUnitsSeized`. [`getRecords`](ILensAccount.sol#L60) then shows `creditedAfterReconcile = 20,000e6 − seized`, equal to `Collateralization.previewRelease`. Errors: [`UnknownProxy`](ILensErrors.sol#L28), [`UnknownDebtReserve`](ILensErrors.sol#L23).
8. **Repay all and withdraw: [`getPendingSettlement`](ILensMarket.sol#L44) ([impl](LensMarket.sol#L95))`(vaultToken)`** shows the seized units pending and settleable, valued in the settlement reserve's asset; [`checkBacking`](ILensMarket.sol#L20) shows supply = credited + pending.
8a. **Account risk and withdrawal: [`getAccountRisk`](ILensPositions.sol#L18) ([impl](LensPositions.sol#L31))`(Alice, 4)`** with 15,000 GHO owed on 10,000 vault tokens at CF 80 % returns the liquidation price 187,500,000 ($1.875) and each reserve's borrow rate with her risk premium; [`previewWithdraw`](ILensPositions.sol#L43) ([impl](LensPositions.sol#L70)) with 8,000 GHO owed shows HF 1.00 and `allowed` for 5,000 tokens, 0.9998 and refused for 5,001.
9. **Payout escrow: [`getEscrowClaimable`](ILensAccount.sol#L76) ([impl](LensAccount.sol#L96))`(keeper, [GHO, USDT, USDC])`** returns `[3e18, 0, 320e6]` after one 3 GHO and forty 8 USDC escrowed payouts.
10. **Monitoring: [`getBreakerStatus`](ILensRisk.sol#L21) ([impl](LensRisk.sol#L51)), [`getDebtBreakerStatus`](ILensRisk.sol#L31) ([impl](LensRisk.sol#L60)), [`isFrozenAddress`](ILensRisk.sol#L40) ([impl](LensRisk.sol#L70)), [`checkRecordBacking`](ILensMarket.sol#L34) ([impl](LensMarket.sol#L73)), [`getTailCoverage`](ILensRisk.sol#L53) ([impl](LensRisk.sol#L79))`(Hub, GHO)`** (USDC worst at $256,000 against $100,000 funded, 3,906 bps), [`uncoveredDeficit`](ILensRisk.sol#L64) ([impl](LensRisk.sol#L104)), [`getHubAssetExposure`](ILensRisk.sol#L74) ([impl](LensRisk.sol#L114)): with deficits of 1,500,001 and 2,800,000 GHO base units against 4,000,000 in the Avana Umbrella, 300,001 is uncovered until the DAO deficit offset behind it (the Spoke whose `FIRST_LOSS_LAYER()` names the Umbrella) holds 300,001, and its slice shows kind `DeficitOffset`. Every view reverts [`Locked`](ILensErrors.sol#L12) while the Lock is held and the paged views [`LimitTooHigh`](ILensErrors.sol#L17) above [`LENS_MAX_PAGE`](ILens.sol#L61) ([impl](LensBase.sol#L77)); [`UnknownDebtAsset`](ILensErrors.sol#L33) for a pair no reserve draws; [`InvalidModules`](ILensErrors.sol#L38) at construction.
11. **Position fees: [`getPositionFees`](ILensFees.sol#L19) ([impl](LensFees.sol#L59))`(4, key)`** returns what `collectFees` would pay for one position from the pool's fee growth (owed tokens plus `liquidity × (feeGrowthInside − last) / 2^128` per token, as the position manager computes it); [`getAccountFees`](ILensFees.sol#L29) ([impl](LensFees.sol#L69))`(Alice, 4)` returns it for each of her records. With the pool's tick inside the range, a growth of 2 and 3 units per unit of liquidity and `L = 2e12`, the view shows 4e12 and 6e12, and `collectFees` pays exactly that (`test/unit/Lens.Fees.unit.t.sol`).
12. **Before signing: [`previewBorrowAfter`](ILensActions.sol#L19) ([impl](LensActions.sol#L42))`(Alice, 4, GHO, 8,000e18)`** with 10,000 vault tokens and no debt shows HF 2.00 and liquidation price $1.00 after the borrow; [`previewRepayAfter`](ILensActions.sol#L34) ([impl](LensActions.sol#L61)), [`previewPledgeAfter`](ILensActions.sol#L49) ([impl](LensActions.sol#L78)) and [`previewWithdrawAfter`](ILensActions.sol#L62) ([impl](LensActions.sol#L94)) do the same for a repay, a pledge and a withdrawal; each equals Aave's account data once the action has run.
13. **Withdraw screen: [`previewMaxWithdraw`](ILensSizing.sol#L21) ([impl](LensRemoval.sol#L27))`(Alice, 4, 1.25e18)`** with records of 10,000 and 6,000 vault tokens and 10,000 GHO owed returns `maxUnits = 8,187.5` vault tokens, the 6,000 record as the one that fits, and HF 1.60 after withdrawing it.
14. **Deleverage to a target: [`previewDeleverageTo`](ILensSizing.sol#L36) ([impl](LensSizing.sol#L21))`(Alice, 4, key, GHO, 1.6e18)`** with 12,000 GHO owed returns the fewest units of the record whose repay with collateral reaches HF 1.60, with the debt repaid, the payout and the health factor `previewRepayWithCollateral` gives for them.
15. **Close the account: [`previewCloseAccount`](ILensSizing.sol#L51) ([impl](LensClose.sol#L28))`(Alice, 4, deadline)`** lists each debt with its repay-all spend cap and each record with its withdrawal units and token amounts at the oracle prices; running those calls leaves the account empty.
16. **Lend page: [`getHubAsset`](ILensHubAssets.sol#L18) ([impl](LensHubAssets.sol#L28))`(Hub, GHO)`** with 1,000,000 GHO supplied through the Lend Spoke and 8,000 GHO borrowed returns `added = 1,000,000`, `owed = 8,000`, available `liquidity = 992,000`, utilization 80 bps, the Lend Spoke's $1.00 price and one slice per Spoke (fee receiver, Lend, CDP) whose shares add up to the Hub's; [`getMarket`](ILensMarkets.sol#L27) ([impl](LensMarkets.sol#L54)) adds the market's collateral risk, liquidation configuration, caps and the borrow rate `drawnRate × (1 + collateralRisk)` of every account of the market. [`UnknownHubAsset`](ILensErrors.sol#L44) for an asset the Hub does not list.
17. **Speed limits: [`getBorrowSpeed`](ILensSpeed.sol#L22) ([impl](LensSpeedModule.sol#L32))`(4, GHO)`** returns the market's and the (Hub, GHO) borrow windows from the RiskModule and `availableNow`, the smaller room in GHO base units (market room $250,000 at $1.00 gives 250,000 GHO when the asset window has more), with `moreAt` the earlier time more frees up; [`getPledgeSpeed`](ILensSpeed.sol#L31) ([impl](LensSpeedModule.sol#L50))`(4)` returns the market's pledge window in 8-decimal USD.

## What the UI reads and calls

- Lens: every function above; it is the UI's main read surface.
- Events to index: none (view-only contract).
- User calls: none; every result is advisory and the target contract re-checks it.
