Function links point to the interface that declares each function and, after it, to the module code that implements it. Every call enters through [`Lens`](Lens.sol), which forwards it unchanged by STATICCALL to the module of its interface piece ([`LensAccountModule`](LensAccountModule.sol), [`LensMarketModule`](LensMarketModule.sol), [`LensPriceModule`](LensPriceModule.sol), [`LensLiquidationModule`](LensLiquidationModule.sol), [`LensRiskModule`](LensRiskModule.sol), [`LensPreviewModule`](LensPreviewModule.sol), [`LensMarketsModule`](LensMarketsModule.sol), [`LensPositionsModule`](LensPositionsModule.sol); [`MODULES`](ILens.sol#L65)).

# Lens lifecycle

The Lens is the read-only view contract for the app, keepers and monitoring: it assembles account, market, price, risk and liquidation state from every Avana contract and returns previews sized to stay valid until a transaction's deadline; no contract relies on it.

## Walkthrough

Alice's account in market 4 (USDC/USDT, 6-decimal vault token at $2.00, collateral factor 80 %, on the Stable LP Hub's CDP Spoke with a GHO reserve at a fixed $1.00 and a USDC reserve priced by its DebtOracle at $0.9999), the numbers of `test/integration/Lens.Journey.integration.t.sol` and the unit suites.

0. **Market screen: [`getMarkets`](ILensMarkets.sol#L18) ([impl](LensMarkets.sol#L37))`(0, 200)` and [`getMarket`](ILensMarkets.sol#L27) ([impl](LensMarkets.sol#L54))`(4)`** list market 4 with its fee tier (100 pips), range `[-50, 50]`, accepted $2.00, 10,000 vault tokens supplied ($20,000), CF 80 %; [`getDebtReserves`](ILensMarkets.sol#L45) ([impl](LensMarkets.sol#L73)) and [`getDebtReserve`](ILensMarkets.sol#L36) ([impl](LensMarkets.sol#L63)) give each reserve's utilization, drawn and supply rates and rate curve (9,000 / 0 / 400 / 6,000 bps). [`previewPledge`](ILensPositions.sol#L29) ([impl](LensPositions.sol#L64)) returns the RiskModule's pledge preview with the block.
1. **Before the pledge: [`predictProxy`](ILensAccount.sol#L20) ([impl](LensAccount.sol#L36))`(Alice, 4)`** returns the ProxyManager's CREATE2 address and `deployed = false`; [`getPriceState`](ILensLiquidation.sol#L22) ([impl](LensPrice.sol#L28))`(vaultToken)` shows Live, served and accepted $2.00, each input 600 s old against its 88,200 s limits; [`getCapHeadroom`](ILensMarket.sol#L54) ([impl](LensMarket.sol#L111)) shows `minPledgeUsd` $10 and the room under each cap; [`getApplicationState`](ILensLiquidation.sol#L44) ([impl](LensPrice.sol#L64)) and [`getPauseReason`](ILensLiquidation.sol#L54) ([impl](LensPrice.sol#L73)) show None.
2. **After pledging 20,000 vault tokens ($40,000): [`getAccount`](ILensAccount.sol#L32) ([impl](LensAccount.sol#L46))`(Alice, 4)`** returns her proxy, `deployed = true`, Aave's account data, one `DebtPosition` per enabled reserve (GHO and USDC, zero debt), and the market position with 20,000e6 credited and supplied; [`getAccounts`](ILensAccount.sol#L44) ([impl](LensAccount.sol#L56)) returns the same view per listed market; [`getRecords`](ILensAccount.sol#L60) ([impl](LensAccount.sol#L69)) lists record 1; [`checkBacking`](ILensMarket.sol#L20) ([impl](LensMarket.sol#L37)) shows supply = credited.
3. **Borrow screen: [`previewBorrowRecommended`](ILensPreview.sol#L42) ([impl](LensPreview.sol#L85))`(Alice, 4, GHO, now)`** returns the recommended `32,000 / 1.10` GHO and the maximum `32,000 / 1.005` GHO of [`previewBorrowMax`](ILensPreview.sol#L21) ([impl](LensPreview.sol#L73)) at a zero rate; borrowing the recommended amount lands at health factor 1.10. [`getDebtPriceState`](ILensLiquidation.sol#L34) ([impl](LensPrice.sol#L53)) shows GHO fixed and fresh, and USDC fresh until its accepted round is older than 4,200 s.
4. **Repay screen: [`quoteRepayAll`](ILensPreview.sol#L57) ([impl](LensPreview.sol#L97))`(Alice, 4, GHO, now + 600)`** is `⌈debt × (1 + rate × 600) × 1.001⌉`, at least the debt at the deadline.
5. **Top-up screen: a third party adds 1,000 vault tokens of liquidity; [`previewRecredit`](ILensPreview.sol#L73) ([impl](LensPreview.sol#L119))`(4, key)`** returns `added = 1,000,000,000`, `usdCharge = $2,000` and `rescueOnly = false` (true for a frozen depositor, under `Paused(General)`, a market breaker, the pool-share gate or a charge below `minPledgeUsd`).
6. **Deleverage screen: [`previewRepayWithCollateral`](ILensPreview.sol#L91) ([impl](LensPreview.sol#L137))`(Alice, 4, key, 1,000,000,000, GHO)`** with 8,000 GHO owed on 10,000 vault tokens returns `x = ⌊(1,979.999998 − 1) × 10⁴ / 10,010⌋` GHO, `V − x − φ` to the wallet and the higher health factor; `(0, 0, hf)` when the call would revert.
7. **Price drop to $1.30: [`previewLiquidation`](ILensLiquidation.sol#L85) ([impl](LensLiquidation.sol#L76))`(proxy, GHO, now + 120)`** returns every gate, Aave's own sizing (`LensAaveSizing`), ready-to-send `LiquidationParams` and the expected seizure, floor and payouts; the liquidation executed with `params.debtToCover` repays and seizes exactly `expectedDebtRepaid` and `expectedUnitsSeized`. [`getRecords`](ILensAccount.sol#L60) then shows `creditedAfterReconcile = 20,000e6 − seized`, equal to `Collateralization.previewRelease`. Errors: [`UnknownProxy`](ILensErrors.sol#L28), [`UnknownDebtReserve`](ILensErrors.sol#L23).
8. **Repay all and withdraw: [`getPendingSettlement`](ILensMarket.sol#L44) ([impl](LensMarket.sol#L92))`(vaultToken)`** shows the seized units pending and settleable, valued in the settlement reserve's asset; [`checkBacking`](ILensMarket.sol#L20) shows supply = credited + pending.
8a. **Account risk and withdrawal: [`getAccountRisk`](ILensPositions.sol#L18) ([impl](LensPositions.sol#L39))`(Alice, 4)`** with 15,000 GHO owed on 10,000 vault tokens at CF 80 % returns the liquidation price 187,500,000 ($1.875) and each reserve's borrow rate with her risk premium; [`previewWithdraw`](ILensPositions.sol#L43) ([impl](LensPositions.sol#L76)) with 8,000 GHO owed shows HF 1.00 and `allowed` for 5,000 tokens, 0.9998 and refused for 5,001.
9. **Payout escrow: [`getEscrowClaimable`](ILensAccount.sol#L76) ([impl](LensAccount.sol#L91))`(keeper, [GHO, USDT, USDC])`** returns `[3e18, 0, 320e6]` after one 3 GHO and forty 8 USDC escrowed payouts.
10. **Monitoring: [`getBreakerStatus`](ILensRisk.sol#L21) ([impl](LensRisk.sol#L44)), [`getDebtBreakerStatus`](ILensRisk.sol#L31) ([impl](LensRisk.sol#L53)), [`isFrozenAddress`](ILensRisk.sol#L40) ([impl](LensRisk.sol#L63)), [`checkRecordBacking`](ILensMarket.sol#L34) ([impl](LensMarket.sol#L70)), [`getTailCoverage`](ILensRisk.sol#L53) ([impl](LensRisk.sol#L72))`(Hub, GHO)`** (USDC worst at $256,000 against $100,000 funded, 3,906 bps), [`uncoveredDeficit`](ILensRisk.sol#L63) ([impl](LensRisk.sol#L96)), [`getHubAssetExposure`](ILensRisk.sol#L73) ([impl](LensRisk.sol#L106)). Every view reverts [`Locked`](ILensErrors.sol#L12) while the Lock is held and the paged views [`LimitTooHigh`](ILensErrors.sol#L17) above [`LENS_MAX_PAGE`](ILens.sol#L40) ([impl](LensBase.sol#L74)); [`UnknownDebtAsset`](ILensErrors.sol#L33) for a pair no reserve draws; [`InvalidModules`](ILensErrors.sol#L37) at construction.

## What the UI reads and calls

- Lens: every function above; it is the UI's main read surface.
- Events to index: none (view-only contract).
- User calls: none; every result is advisory and the target contract re-checks it.
