# Avana Umbrella (Coverage Spoke) lifecycle

The Coverage Spoke holds the First-Loss Reserve of one `(Hub, debt asset)` as Hub added shares. Avana Labs funds it, the Aave DAO controls it through its AccessManager, and its shares leave only to eliminate a recorded deficit of an allowlisted CDP Spoke on its own asset, or through a DAO withdrawal after 14 days.

## Walkthrough

Ethereum GHO instance on the Stable LP Hub (illustrative 400,000 GHO minimum; the funding ARFC sets the real one).

1. **Avana deploys [`CoverageSpoke`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L91)`(hub, GHO, ghoAssetId, aaveAccessManager, 1,209,600)`** after the Hub lists GHO. The constructor fixes [`HUB`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L268), [`ASSET`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L262), [`ASSET_ID`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L274) and [`RESERVE_WITHDRAWAL_DELAY`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L280). Errors: [`ZeroAddress`](ICoverageSpokeErrors.sol#L13), [`InvalidHubAsset`](ICoverageSpokeErrors.sol#L18) (the Hub does not list GHO with its 18 decimals under that ID), [`InvalidWithdrawalDelay`](ICoverageSpokeErrors.sol#L23).
2. **The Aave DAO's payload** registers it on GHO with `drawCap = 0` and `addCap = MAX_ALLOWED_SPOKE_CAP` (so yield can never fill a cap and stop `fund`), grants it `HUB_DEFICIT_ELIMINATOR_ROLE`, maps `coverDeficit` to `COVERAGE_ROLE` (granted to Avana's Emergency Guardian and the Aave Protocol Guardian, either able to cover within 72 hours) and the DAO functions to `RESERVE_ADMIN_ROLE`, then calls [`setAllowedBorrowSpoke`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L159)`(uniswapV3Stable, true)` and [`setFirstLossReserveMinimum`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L174)`(400,000e18)`. Events: [`BorrowSpokeAllowlistUpdated`](ICoverageSpokeAdmin.sol#L16), [`FirstLossReserveMinimumUpdated`](ICoverageSpokeAdmin.sol#L22). Error: [`BorrowSpokeNotListed`](ICoverageSpokeErrors.sol#L48) for a Spoke the Hub does not list on GHO.
3. **Avana Labs → [`fund`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L116)`(400,000e18)`** after approving it. The 400,000 GHO move straight to the Hub, `Hub.add` credits the shares to the Coverage Spoke, and the Hub's balance rise must equal the amount. Event: [`ReserveFunded`](ICoverageSpoke.sol#L31). Errors: [`ZeroAmount`](ICoverageSpokeErrors.sol#L27), [`TransferAmountMismatch`](ICoverageSpokeErrors.sol#L33); the Hub's `SpokeHalted`, `SpokeNotActive` and, for an amount below one share's value, `InvalidShares`. `enableDebtReserve` can now name the instance and the GHO `drawCap` can rise.
4. **A liquidation leaves an account with no collateral**; the CDP Spoke reports a 5,200 GHO deficit to the Hub.
5. **`COVERAGE_ROLE` → [`coverDeficit`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L136)`(uniswapV3Stable, 5,200e18)`.** Amount `= min(5,200, ceil(deficitRay / RAY), fundedBalance()) = 5,200 GHO`; `Hub.eliminateDeficit` burns the matching shares, rounded up, and clears the deficit. Event: [`DeficitCovered`](ICoverageSpoke.sol#L42), then [`ReserveBelowMinimum`](ICoverageSpoke.sol#L56)`(394,800e18, 400,000e18)`. Errors: [`SpokeNotAllowlisted`](ICoverageSpokeErrors.sol#L38), [`NothingToCover`](ICoverageSpokeErrors.sol#L43), the AccessManager's `AccessManagedUnauthorized`. It works while either Spoke is halted.
6. **Monitoring pages on the alert** ([`isBelowMinimum`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L242) is true); GHO borrows continue unless a guardian trips the debt breaker. Avana Labs calls [`fund`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L116)`(5,200e18)` and the alert clears with no reset call.
7. **Pending settlement.** LPVaultSwap's [`settlePending`](../../liquidation/lp-vault-swap/ILPVaultSwapLiquidation.sol#L76) recovers 83.8 GHO and calls [`fund`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L116)`(83.8e18)` like any funder; `ReserveFunded` names LPVaultSwap. An amount below one share's value would revert `InvalidShares`, so `settlePending` carries such an amount to the next settlement instead.
8. **Credit line closed.** The DAO calls [`requestWithdrawal`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L185)`(400,000e18, avanaLabs)` at `1,780,000,000`; it is executable from `1,781,209,600`. Event: [`WithdrawalRequested`](ICoverageSpoke.sol#L64). Errors: [`ZeroAmount`](ICoverageSpokeErrors.sol#L27), [`InvalidRecipient`](ICoverageSpokeErrors.sol#L53). [`cancelWithdrawal`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L201) stops a Pending request ([`WithdrawalCancelled`](ICoverageSpoke.sol#L69)).
9. **After 14 days, the DAO → [`executeWithdrawal`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L213)`(1)`.** It withdraws `min(400,000e18, fundedBalance())` from the Hub straight to Avana Labs. Event: [`WithdrawalExecuted`](ICoverageSpoke.sol#L78) and, below the minimum, [`ReserveBelowMinimum`](ICoverageSpoke.sol#L56). Errors: [`WithdrawalNotPending`](ICoverageSpokeErrors.sol#L58), [`WithdrawalNotReady`](ICoverageSpokeErrors.sol#L64), [`DeficitOutstanding`](ICoverageSpokeErrors.sol#L70) while an allowlisted Spoke still has a recorded GHO deficit (coverage comes first); the Hub's `InsufficientLiquidity`, `SpokeHalted` and `InvalidAmount`. Every revert leaves the request Pending. The totals are signed: with a year of yield the reserve can cover more than was ever funded.

## What the UI reads and calls

- Lens: [`getTailCoverage`](../../lens/lens/ILensRisk.sol#L50)`(asset)`, [`uncoveredDeficit`](../../lens/lens/ILensRisk.sol#L59)`(hub, asset)`.
- Views: [`fundedBalance`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L236), [`isBelowMinimum`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L242), [`firstLossReserveMinimum`](ICoverageSpoke.sol#L142), [`fundedShares`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L248), [`getWithdrawal`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L254), [`allowedBorrowSpoke`](ICoverageSpokeViews.sol#L23).
- Events to index: `ReserveFunded`, `DeficitCovered`, `ReserveBelowMinimum`, `WithdrawalRequested`, `WithdrawalCancelled`, `WithdrawalExecuted`, `BorrowSpokeAllowlistUpdated`, `FirstLossReserveMinimumUpdated`.
- User calls: none; [`fund`](CoverageSpoke.sol#L116) is open to anyone but has no user flow.
