# 2.23 Uniswap v4 Integration

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-15
>
> **Requirement prefix:** `REQ-AMM-V4-NNN` · **Math prefix:** `SPEC-AMM-V4-NNN`

## Purpose

The Uniswap v4 integration is Avana's code for Uniswap v4 concentrated-liquidity positions on Ethereum (chain ID 1). It is one immutable Uniswap v4 LPVault that implements the AMM integration interface ([2.19](19-amm-integration-interface.md)) with `AMM_PROTOCOL() = keccak256("UNISWAP_V4")` and the `INTEGRATION_ID` registered for it in the chain's VaultTokenFactory (`IUniswapV4Integration`, `contracts/src/amm/uniswap-v4/`), plus the v4 pricing module of the LPOracle ([2.10](10-lp-oracle.md), `LPOracleV4`). This page holds every rule that names the v4 PoolManager, the PositionManager, a `PoolKey` or a pool hook. The concentrated-liquidity custody and pricing code is shared with the Uniswap v3 LPVault ([2.20](20-uniswap-v3-integration.md)). The AMM-neutral custody rules are on [2.2](02-lp-vault.md), the ledger rules on [2.3](03-collateralization.md), and the comparison with the other AMM protocols on [2.22](22-amm-integration-readiness.md).

A Uniswap v4 market is one (`PoolId`, `tickLower`, `tickUpper`) of a hookless pool: an exact range, which can be the full usable range or a bounded one (`SPEC-BACK-001`). The market's `pool` is `address(0)` and its `poolId` is `keccak256(abi.encode(PoolKey))`. Units are `⌊L / 10^k⌋` of the position's v4 liquidity `L` (`uint128`, read from the PoolManager), with the market's unit exponent `k` of `SPEC-ORC-005`; one unit per vault-token base unit. A position is a PositionManager NFT; its `positionKey` is `bytes32(tokenId)`. A native ETH leg is stored as the wrapped native token, so the market's tokens are always two ERC-20s and every payment of that leg is made in WETH. v4 pools keep no time-weighted price or liquidity, so each pool has a *reference pool*: the canonical Uniswap v3 pool of the same pair (`REQ-AMM-V4-007`). It supplies the consistency read's mean tick (`REQ-AMM-V4-005`) and the depth (`REQ-AMM-V4-008`).

## Requirements

### Hooks and subscribers

- `REQ-AMM-V4-001` — A v4 market MUST NOT be listed unless its `PoolKey.hooks` is `address(0)` or a hook on Avana's hook allowlist whose address permission bits clear every remove-liquidity flag and every delta-returning swap or remove flag: `uint160(hooks) & HOOK_MASK == 0` with `HOOK_MASK = BEFORE_REMOVE_LIQUIDITY (1 << 9) | AFTER_REMOVE_LIQUIDITY (1 << 8) | BEFORE_SWAP_RETURNS_DELTA (1 << 3) | AFTER_SWAP_RETURNS_DELTA (1 << 2) | AFTER_REMOVE_LIQUIDITY_RETURNS_DELTA (1 << 0)`, checked on-chain at `bindMarket` and again at every `receivePosition`. An allowlisted hook MUST be immutable, with its code hash pinned in the market and its removal gas measured. The first v4 LPVault has an empty allowlist: `isAllowedPoolHook` is true for `address(0)` only, so only hookless pools are listed. Why: a hook runs inside every removal, so it can revert (every position of the pool becomes unliquidatable), burn gas past the per-transaction cap, or keep part of the removed tokens through its returned delta; a 1% delta leaves 9,876 realizable against an oracle value of 9,975, and a 100% delta leaves 0 (`AMMN-RT-06`, `AMMN-GAS-03`). A swap hook that returns a delta runs its own pricing curve, so the pool's v3-style liquidity no longer describes what a position redeems and `SPEC-AMM-V4-001` would misprice it. Example: a hook at an address ending `…0300` sets bits 8 and 9 and is refused; one ending `…0008` sets bit 3 and is refused; one ending `…0080` passes the mask. The first v4 LPVault, whose allowlist is empty, enforces this as `hooks == address(0)` at both points; the mask and the pinned code hash apply once an allowlist exists, and `HOOK_MASK()` reports the mask (review S3). (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V4-002` — `receivePosition` MUST refuse a position whose `PositionInfo` has `hasSubscriber` set (`SubscribedPosition`), and the LPVault MUST never call `subscribe`. Why: a subscriber's `notifyModifyLiquidity` runs on every liquidity change without a try/catch and can revert the removal. (Source: Q-302)

### Reads

- `REQ-AMM-V4-003` — Every v4 quote and custody read MUST require the PoolManager to be locked (not inside an `unlock` callback), read through `exttload` of the PoolManager's `IS_UNLOCKED` transient slot, and fail closed otherwise (`PoolLocked`; reason bit 17 `POOL_REENTRANCY_LOCKED` in the LPOracle). Liquidity is read from the PoolManager (`getPositionLiquidity(poolId, positionManager, tickLower, tickUpper, salt = bytes32(tokenId))`, through `extsload`), not from the PositionManager; the PoolKey and range come from `PositionManager.getPoolAndPositionInfo(tokenId)`, and the position belongs to the market's pool only when `keccak256(abi.encode(poolKey))` equals the market's `PoolId`. The guarded reads are `receivePosition`, `validatePosition`, `unwind`, `releaseToDepositor` and the LPOracle's pool check, each failing with `PoolLocked`; `consistencyDeviation` reports itself unavailable while the PoolManager is unlocked; `riskMetadata` reports the lock as `readSafe`; `collectFees`, `returnPosition` and `returnStray` fail inside an `unlock` through the PositionManager's own lock (review S7). (Source: Q-302)

### Removal and fees

- `REQ-AMM-V4-004` — The v4 unwind, release and fee collection MUST each be one `PositionManager.modifyLiquidities` call (which runs inside the PoolManager's `unlock`) built only from these actions, in this order: A removal to someone other than the depositor whose batch reverts falls back to `REQ-AMM-V4-013`. On a native-ETH market `WRAP(CONTRACT_BALANCE)` and `SWEEP` also deliver any ETH or WETH already held by the PositionManager, so the recipient of that step can receive more than the returned amounts; anyone can sweep those balances, and every Avana consumer measures by balance delta (review V4-05). The depositor's balance deltas around a partial `releaseToDepositor` therefore include the fees of the same batch (`REQ-VAULT-088`, review V4-06).
  1. Fees: `DECREASE_LIQUIDITY(tokenId, 0, 0, 0)`, then `TAKE_PAIR(currency0, currency1, depositor)`. Fee collection skips the call when the position's liquidity is 0, since a v4 position with no liquidity accrues no fee.
  2. Principal (unwind and release only): `DECREASE_LIQUIDITY(tokenId, liquidity, amount0Min, amount1Min)`, then `TAKE_PAIR(currency0, currency1, recipient)`.
  3. Native ETH: when `currency0` is native ETH, each `TAKE_PAIR(…, to)` above is replaced by `TAKE(ETH, PositionManager, OPEN_DELTA)`, `WRAP(CONTRACT_BALANCE)`, `TAKE(currency1, to, OPEN_DELTA)`, `SWEEP(WETH, to)`, so the ETH leg reaches `to` as WETH.

  The removal amounts the LPVault returns and checks against the minimums (`BelowMinimum`) are computed before the call from the pool's `slot0` with the PoolManager's own rounding (`SPEC-AMM-V4-003`); nothing else runs in the batch, so they equal the principal the PoolManager credits. No Avana contract receives native ETH, implements `unlockCallback`, or calls `SWEEP` for any currency other than WETH to the recipient of the same call. v4 pools used as swap routes MUST be hook-free. Why: a v4 decrease pays out every accrued fee in the same delta as the principal, and there is no separate `collect`; taking the fees first keeps the rule that fees belong to the depositor and the principal to the recipient (`SPEC-BACK-008`). (Source: Q-297, Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V4-010` — Every fee read of a v4 position (the Lens's uncollected-fee view, `ILensV4FeeReads`) MUST compute `feeGrowthInside` and `(feeGrowthInside − feeGrowthInsideLast) · liquidity / 2^128` with unchecked subtraction modulo 2^256, as the PoolManager's own `Position.update` does. Uncollected fees never enter a quote, a removal amount or a removal minimum. Why: fee-growth accumulators wrap by design, and a checked subtraction panics on a wrapped accumulator and blocks liquidation (a high-severity finding in an audit of another Uniswap v4 lending protocol). (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V4-013` — When the recipient of a removal is not the depositor of record (a seizure to LPVaultSwap, a deleverage release, a reconciliation to Collateralization) and the batch of `REQ-AMM-V4-004` reverts, for example because a pool token refuses the depositor's address, the LPVault MUST run one more `modifyLiquidities` batch with the principal step alone, `DECREASE_LIQUIDITY(tokenId, liquidity, amount0Min, amount1Min)` and the take to the recipient, which then receives the principal and every accrued fee, and MUST return the principal amounts plus the fees owed computed before the call (`REQ-AMM-V4-010`). A removal to the depositor runs the one batch and reverts with it. Why: a v4 decrease pays every accrued fee in the same delta as the principal, so a token that blocklists the borrower's wallet would otherwise make the borrower's account impossible to liquidate; the PayoutEscrow ([2.18](18-payout-escrow.md)) accepts deposits only from LPVaultSwap. In the fallback the fees join the liquidation proceeds, which LPVaultSwap measures by balance delta, so they reduce the debt left or return to the borrower as surplus through the PayoutEscrow. A caller cannot force the fallback by limiting gas: the first call gets 63/64 of the gas left, so if it runs out the rest cannot pay for the second batch. Example: a borrower whose USDC address is blocklisted holds a USDC/USDT record with 3.10 USDC and 2.95 USDT of accrued fees; the seizure's first batch reverts at the USDC fee transfer, the second takes 5,000 USDC + 5,000 USDT of principal and the fees to LPVaultSwap, and `unwind` returns 5,003.10 USDC and 5,002.95 USDT. (Source: review V4-01, decision of 2026-11-15)

### Consistency and depth

- `REQ-AMM-V4-005` — v4 pools have no built-in TWAP. A v4 market's consistency check MUST combine two reads: a spot gate on the pool's `slot0` tick, read through `extsload`, and the arithmetic-mean tick over the market's `twapWindow` of the same pair's Uniswap v3 pool (the market's *reference pool*, `REQ-AMM-V4-007`), read with the mean-tick reader of the `PoolTwap` second source (`SPEC-ORC-010`, `SPEC-ORC-020`). `SPEC-AMM-V4-002` gives the deviation. A deviation above `consistencyToleranceBps` sets reason bit 4 (`CONSISTENCY_DEVIATION`) and the market is Drifting, so borrowing stops while liquidation continues at the fair price; a reference pool whose buffer does not cover the window sets reason bit 5 (`CONSISTENCY_UNAVAILABLE`, Drifting). The per-block quote-change cap applies (`REQ-AMM-CORE-014`). `capabilities().hasTwap` is true for v4, because the integration's `consistencyDeviation` and `riskStatus` read the reference pool. Why: without a time-weighted source the market would always report `CONSISTENCY_UNAVAILABLE` and stay Drifting, so it could never be borrowed against (`REQ-ORACLE-014`, `REQ-ORACLE-015`). The spot read is manipulable inside one block, but only toward failing closed, since composition is at the oracle price (`SPEC-AMM-V4-001`); it catches a pool parked off-price, whose redeemable amounts are worse than the fair composition. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V4-007` — A v4 market's reference pool MUST be the canonical Uniswap v3 pool of the market's two tokens (`factory.getPool(token0, token1, fee) == pool`, factory pinned in the LPVault), with native ETH read as the wrapped native token, in either token order. The LPVault and the LPOracle store it at listing, and the LPOracle's listing checks require the two to name the same pool and its observation cardinality to cover `twapWindow` at the chain's block time (`SPEC-ORC-022`). A token per-token `PoolTwap` second source MAY name the same pool. The LPOracle's listing checks MUST also require that the market is the oracle's own: the MarketRegistry's record names no pool address, the oracle's `PoolId` and its range (the core's `CHECK_MARKET`, `REQ-ORACLE-046`), and the integration's `POOL_MANAGER()` is the oracle's PoolManager (bit 10 of the module, `REQ-ORACLE-105`). Why: an oracle initialized on another fee tier or another range of the same pair would read the spot gate and the lock of a pool the market does not use, and compose a range the vault tokens do not hold (review V4-03). (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V4-008` — The pool-share cap's depth for a v4 market (`riskStatus`, `SPEC-CAP-005`) MUST be the reference pool's harmonic-mean in-range liquidity over the window, in the market's units (`⌊L / 10^k⌋`), unavailable exactly when the reference pool's `observe` fails or the window is 0. Why, against spot liquidity accepted only when stable over N blocks: Several markets can draw on one reference pool: v4 markets of the same pair at different fee tiers, and a Uniswap v3 market on the reference pool itself, each with its own pool-share counter. Listing governance MUST set the `poolShareMaxBps` of every market drawing on one reference pool so that their sum stays within the cap intended for that pool's depth (review V4-04).
  - v4 keeps no seconds-per-liquidity accumulator, so its own depth is spot `getLiquidity`, which a just-in-time add raises inside one block;
  - a stability test over N blocks needs a stored sample history and a keeper to write it, which the LPVault's storage rule forbids (no record, counter, timer or queue, `REQ-VAULT-085`);
  - a seized v4 position is sold through the pair's v3 and Curve routes, not through the v4 pool, so the v3 pool's time-weighted liquidity is the depth a liquidation actually uses.

  Liquidity units are the same for both pools of one pair (`√(x·y)` under the same tick math), so the cap formula is unchanged. (Source: Q-302)

### Adding collateral

- `REQ-AMM-V4-014` — A pledged v4 position MUST be treated as unable to take more liquidity: `topUpPosition` reverts `TopUpNotSupported`, and a re-credit of a v4 record finds no units above `credited`. The app and the Lens MUST offer a new position, not a top-up, as the way to add collateral to a v4 market, and show that an account at the record cap (`REQ-AMM-V4-012`) can only add collateral by repaying debt or withdrawing a record first. Why: the v4 PositionManager adds liquidity only for the NFT's owner or an approved address (`onlyIfApproved`), unlike the Uniswap v3 NonfungiblePositionManager; the LPVault owns every pledged NFT and approves no one, and the PoolManager keys positions by their owner, so no one else can add to it. Letting the LPVault add liquidity itself would make it pull and hold the depositor's tokens, which no LPVault does. (Source: review V4-02, decision of 2026-11-15)

### Listing bounds and record cap

- `REQ-AMM-V4-009` — A v4 market MUST NOT be listed unless both of its tokens report `totalSupply() < 2^127` at `bindMarket` (the wrapped native token for an ETH leg); listing governance delists a market whose token later exceeds the bound. The bound is checked at listing only, never inside a quote, unwind or fee collection. Why: the PoolManager and PositionManager narrow every delta, fee and take to `int128`, so a token amount of `2^127` or more makes a removal revert; a bound checked inside valuation or removal turns into a liquidation lockout once a price moves (an overflow found in an audit of another Uniswap v4 lending protocol). (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V4-012` — The v4 LPVault's `maxRecordsPerAccountMarket()` MUST return 12 (`REQ-AMM-CORE-020`): an account holds at most 12 open records in a v4 market, and a 13th pledge reverts `TooManyRecords(12)`. Why: a v4 removal (one PositionManager batch, fees first, native ETH wrapped and swept) costs about 253,000 gas per record in a whole-account seizure against about 167,000 for Uniswap v3; 16 native ETH/USDC records with one debt measured 7,574,354 gas, above the 7,500,000 liquidation budget, while 12 records with the CDP Spoke's 4 borrowed reserves stay within it. Checked by: TEST-331. The measurement is `TEST-331`'s gas test: records pledged without accrued fees, cold storage, a full removal of each record, and the native ETH leg wrapped and swept. (Source: Q-412)

## Math

Units: liquidity units (`uint128`); `prices` in USD per whole token, 8 decimals; Q64.96 square-root prices; ticks. Rounding follows the PoolManager's `SqrtPriceMath` and `FullMath`.

- `SPEC-AMM-V4-001` — **v4 composition.** Identical to `SPEC-AMM-V3-001` over the position's `(tickLower, tickUpper)` and the `PoolKey`'s currencies, with the market's exact range checked per `SPEC-BACK-001`. Why: v4 pools use the v3 tick and liquidity math; only the custody and hook surface differ. Units, rounding, bounds and example as `SPEC-AMM-V3-001`. Checked by: AMM-A1a, AMM-A3f, AMM-A4a, AMM-A5a, HYP-AMM-01, HYP-AMM-05. (Source: Q-302)
- `SPEC-AMM-V4-002` — **v4 consistency deviation.** With `tickRef = tickAtSqrtRatio(SPEC-ORC-009(q0, q1))` of the uncapped quotes in the v4 pool's token order, `tickSpot` the pool's `slot0` tick, and `tickTwap` the reference pool's mean tick of `SPEC-ORC-010`, negated when the reference pool orders the two tokens the other way: `deviation = max(|tickSpot − tickRef|, |tickTwap − tickRef|)` ticks. The check passes when `deviation ≤ consistencyToleranceBps`. Why: both reads must agree with the feeds, and taking the maximum lets either one stop borrowing. Units: ticks (one tick is about 1 bp). Rounding: the mean tick rounds toward negative infinity before the negation, so a reversed reference pool can read one tick lower than a same-order one. Example: an ETH/USDC v4 pool at tick −196,250 and a WETH/USDC v3 reference pool (tokens in the other order) at mean tick 196,262, with feeds giving `tickRef = −196,255`: the deviations are 5 and 7 ticks, `deviation = 7`, which passes a 50-tick tolerance; a swap that parks the v4 pool at −196,400 gives 145 and fails it. (Source: Q-302)
- `SPEC-AMM-V4-003` — **v4 removal amounts.** With the pool's `slot0` `(sqrtPriceX96, tick)`, the range's `sa = sqrtRatioAtTick(tickLower)` and `sb = sqrtRatioAtTick(tickUpper)`, and liquidity `L` removed: `tick < tickLower` gives `(amount0(sa, sb, L), 0)`; `tick ≥ tickUpper` gives `(0, amount1(sa, sb, L))`; otherwise `(amount0(sqrtPriceX96, sb, L), amount1(sa, sqrtPriceX96, L))`, each rounded down (`SqrtPriceMath.getAmount0Delta` and `getAmount1Delta` with `roundUp = false`). Why: these are the PoolManager's own `Pool.modifyLiquidity` branches for a negative liquidity delta, so the returned amounts equal the principal credited. Units: raw token units. Bounds: `L < 2^128`. The code branches on `sqrtPriceX96` against `sa` and `sb` (`ClPriceMath.amountsForLiquidity`), as the PoolManager's `SqrtPriceMath` does; the two forms give the same amounts in every case, including a price exactly on a bound after a downward crossing, where `tick = tickLower − 1` and either form gives a zero `token1` leg (review S6). (Source: Q-302)

## Permissions

As [2.19](19-amm-integration-interface.md) and [2.2](02-lp-vault.md); in addition, `onERC721Received` accepts calls only from the PositionManager. The LPVault never calls `subscribe`, `approve`, `setApprovalForAll`, `mint`, `increaseLiquidity` or `burn` on the PositionManager, implements no `unlockCallback` and never holds native ETH.

## Failure Conditions

| ID | Condition | Behavior (revert / pause / fallback) |
|---|---|---|
| `REQ-AMM-V4-006` | A hook with a bit of `HOOK_MASK`, a non-allowlisted hook, a hook whose code hash changed, or a position with a subscriber | `bindMarket` reverts `HookNotAllowed`; `receivePosition` reverts `HookNotAllowed` or `SubscribedPosition` (Source: Q-302) |
