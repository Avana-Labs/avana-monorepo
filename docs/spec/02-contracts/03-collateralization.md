# 2.3 Collateralization

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-10
>
> **Requirement prefix:** `REQ-COLL-NNN` (rules written for this contract) and `REQ-VAULT-NNN` (backing rules that keep their IDs) · **Math prefix:** `SPEC-BACK-NNN`

## Purpose

Collateralization is the immutable, AMM-neutral backing ledger of every pledged position on one chain (`Q-224`, `Q-240`, `Q-291`). One Collateralization serves every Hub, every CDP Spoke, every market and every AMM integration of the chain; it reaches each AMM protocol only through that protocol's LPVault and the interface of [2.19](19-amm-integration-interface.md), and it is reachable only through the AaveAdapter (pledge, release, partial removal, re-credit, repay with collateral, zero-credit release, fee collection) and LPVaultSwap (seizure, pending settlement). It is responsible for:

- validating a pledge: the market's vault token, its integration and LPVault, the proxy's market binding, the record limit, and the exact-shape match of the custodied position (`AMMN-RT-01`);
- crediting each position's units exactly once and telling the AaveAdapter how many vault-token units to mint (`credited`);
- the backing ledger: one record per pledged position keyed by (`integrationId`, `positionKey`), at most 16 per account and market, the seizure list per account and market (largest-first at insertion, `SPEC-BACK-006`), the per-market and per-account credited totals, and the USD exposure counters, with the token, depositor and pool counters keyed by Hub (`AMMN-RT-05`);
- the seizure snapshot and the seizure checks (the proxy's share drop and the token's supply drop each equal the debited units), the allocation of seized units over records, and asking the LPVault to remove exactly those units;
- reconciling unsettled seizures (`Q-201`) and holding the pending-settlement balance (the `n` market tokens of reconciled units) until LPVaultSwap settles it;
- closing records and asking the LPVault to return the position to its depositor of record;
- the record side of partial removal and of re-crediting units added to a pledged position (`Q-254`), of the release behind a repay with collateral (`Q-255`), and of fee collection for the depositor of record;
- booking each record's rescue units and reporting their total per account and market (`Q-252`, `Q-253`).

It is not responsible for: position custody, the AMM protocol's pool and shape checks, unit removal and fee payout (LPVault, [2.2](02-lp-vault.md), and its integration page); minting, burning and registering proxies in the vault token (AaveAdapter, [2.6](06-aave-adapter.md), and the vault token, [2.4](04-vault-tokens.md)); proxy creation and the Spoke supply (ProxyManager and PositionProxy, [2.5](05-position-proxy-and-proxy-manager.md)); pricing (LPOracle, [2.10](10-lp-oracle.md)); eligibility, `minPledgeUsd`, caps and the cap charge (RiskModule, [2.7](07-risk-module.md), with values in the ParameterStore, [2.9](09-parameter-store.md)); the application pause state (MarketRegistry, [2.8](08-market-registry.md), read only by the Adapter); health-factor and debt checks on withdrawal (Adapter and RiskModule); liquidation sizing, funding, conversion and payouts (Aave Spoke and LPVaultSwap, [2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)).

The account's budget part is not stored: since the idle part was removed every account charge sits in `Regular` with its slice part in `Slice` (`Q-396`), and no reader needs an account's own booked charge or its latest pledge time, because the RiskModule receives the market, token, depositor, pool and record counters as `checkPledge` arguments. The vault-token reserve and the Hub asset are derived at call time (`REQ-COLL-002`).

`BackingRecord` fields, packed in 5 slots (`SPEC-BACK-016`, `AMMN-GAS-15`, `Q-303`; `contracts/src/custody/collateralization/types/BackingRecord.sol`): slot 1 `positionKey` (`bytes32`); slot 2 `depositor` (`address`, the wallet the Adapter passed and the LPVault took the position from), `integrationId` (`uint32`), `marketId` (`uint32`, the vault token's `marketId()`), `inList` (`bool`, true while the record has credited units and sits in the seizure list); slot 3 `proxy` (`address`, the depositor's PositionProxy for the market), `pledgedAt` (`uint40`), `lastDebitedAt` (`uint40`); slot 4 `credited` and `originalUnits` (`uint128` each, bounded by `MAX_CREDITED_UNITS = 2^120 − 1`; the charge basis of `SPEC-BACK-005`, rebased only by a re-credit per `SPEC-BACK-013`); slot 5 `rescueUnits` (`uint128`, the record's rescue units per `SPEC-BACK-014`, never above `credited`), `originalUsdCharge` and `currentUsdCharge` (`uint64` each, USD 8 decimals; `2^64` is about $184 billion, above every cap). The seizure list is the separate `seizureList` array, so the record carries no list links. A record exists while `depositor != address(0)`; its market is the vault token whose `marketId()` equals `record.marketId`.

`Pending` fields: `units` (`uint256`) and `amounts` (`uint256[]`, one per pool token of the market in the order `marketOf(marketId)` returns). `Bucket` is `enum {Regular, Slice}`, used to key `marketUsd`. `CounterKind` names the counter in `CounterSaturated`. `PledgeCounters` (argument of `checkPledge`; `contracts/src/types/PledgeCounters.sol`): `hub = vaultToken.hub()`, `regularUsed = marketUsd[vaultToken][Regular]`, `sliceUsed = marketUsd[vaultToken][Slice]`, `depositorUsed = depositorUsd[hub][depositor]`, `accountRecords = openRecordCount[proxy][vaultToken]` and `tokenUsed[i] = tokenUsd[hub][tokens[i]]` for each pool token (USD 8 decimals). The pool-share cap uses the vault token's total supply and the integration's `riskStatus` depth ([2.7](07-risk-module.md) `SPEC-CAP-005`), not a ledger counter. `PledgeCharge` (return of `checkPledge`, layout owned by [2.7](07-risk-module.md)): `usdCharge`, `sliceUsd` (USD 8 decimals), `rescue` (`bool`, true when the pledge needed a rescue exemption of `Q-252`) and `rescueUnits` (`uint128`, the units the RiskModule counts as rescue units).

## Requirements

### General

- `REQ-COLL-001` — Collateralization MUST be deployed as a non-upgradeable contract: no proxy, no `delegatecall`, no `selfdestruct`, no owner, no admin, no pause of its own, no setter, no sweep or rescue function, no generic call, multicall or ERC-1271. Why: the backing ledger decides how many units each position backs and to whom each position returns; keeping it immutable means no upgrade of the contracts around it (the Adapter, LPVaultSwap, the RiskModule) can rewrite a record, a total or the depositor of a position. (Source: Q-226, Q-240)
- `REQ-COLL-002` — Collateralization MUST take its Avana addresses only from constructor immutables (`ADAPTER`, `LP_VAULT_SWAP`, `RISK_MODULE`, `LOCK`, `VAULT_TOKEN_FACTORY`), each market's integration, LPVault (`integration()`), `marketKey`, Hub, CDP Spoke, `marketId` and LPOracle only from the immutables of a validated vault token, and each market's tokens only from that LPVault's write-once market table (`marketOf(marketId)`); it MUST derive the vault-token reserve as `reserveId = Spoke.getReserveId(hub, Hub.getAssetId(vaultToken))`. It MUST NOT read the MarketRegistry, the ParameterStore or any other upgradeable contract for an address, an ID or a value it acts on, and it holds no address of any AMM protocol contract. `marketId` and `lpOracle` reach only the LPOracle poke and the RiskModule check, never custody, credited amounts, debits or recipients. Deployment uses the CREATE3-predicted addresses of the Adapter proxy, LPVaultSwap, the RiskModule, the Lock and the factory; the constructor reverts unless the five are non-zero and pairwise distinct, and the deployment check verifies each immutable against the deployed address. Why one ledger per chain: every Hub, Spoke, market and AMM integration of the chain shares it, so Hub-wide counters live in one place and a later Spoke or AMM protocol needs no new ledger and no Lock redeploy (`AMMN-ARCH-01`, `AMMN-RT-05`). (Source: Q-119, Q-226, Q-240, Q-291, Q-295, Q-296)
- `REQ-COLL-003` — A vault token is valid for Collateralization only if `VAULT_TOKEN_FACTORY.isVaultToken(vaultToken)` and `VAULT_TOKEN_FACTORY.tokenOf(vaultToken.spoke(), vaultToken.marketKey()) == vaultToken`, `VAULT_TOKEN_FACTORY.lpVaultOf(vaultToken.integrationId()) == vaultToken.integration()`, `vaultToken.collateralization() == address(this)`, `vaultToken.adapter() == ADAPTER` and `vaultToken.lpVaultSwap() == LP_VAULT_SWAP`; every function that takes a `vaultToken` MUST revert with `InvalidVaultToken` otherwise. The factory's registries are write-once, so a second token for a market key or a second LPVault for an integration can never be accepted, and the token's `marketId()` and `lpOracle()` are the values its market was registered with ([2.4](04-vault-tokens.md) `REQ-VTOKEN-038`). (Source: Q-068, Q-224, Q-226, Q-291, Q-299)
- `REQ-COLL-004` — Collateralization MUST NOT hold a position or a vault token and MUST NOT call any AMM protocol contract: it moves positions and units only through the market's LPVault (`receivePosition`, `topUpPosition`, `returnPosition`, `unwind`, `releaseToDepositor`, `collectFees`, [2.2](02-lp-vault.md)) and reads only that LPVault's `unitsOf`, `marketOf`, `marketOfPosition` and `checkReadSafe`; the only assets it holds are the market tokens of the pending-settlement balance. Why: one contract per AMM protocol holds the positions and makes every AMM call, so the rule that a position returns only to its depositor of record is enforced where the position sits, whatever this ledger asks, and the ledger needs no code of any AMM protocol. (Source: Q-224, Q-240, Q-291)
- `REQ-COLL-005` — Collateralization MUST trust the Adapter for the `depositor`, `proxy`, `units` and `requireRescue` arguments of `pledge`, `release`, `releasePartial`, `recredit`, `releaseForDeleverage`, `releaseZeroCredit` and `collectFees` (the Adapter's code and its timelock are trusted for custody of proxy positions, `Q-223`), and MUST still enforce, whatever the Adapter passes: `pledge` reverts with `InvalidDepositor(depositor)` if `depositor` is the zero address, the market's LPVault or `address(this)`; the position is taken only from its current owner, who becomes the depositor of record (the LPVault's `receivePosition` checks that `depositor` owns the position and refuses itself as owner); the position's shape is exactly the market's; `credited` equals the units read after custody, and a re-credit adds exactly the live units above `credited`; one open record per position; at most 16 records per account-market; the proxy is registered in the market's vault token and bound to that market only; a record is released, reduced or re-credited only for the proxy named in it, and re-credited, released at zero credit or fee-collected only for its depositor; the position, the principal of a partial removal and the fees go only to the recorded depositor; seized units are debited only when the proxy's share drop and the token's supply drop both equal them. Blast radius of a faulty or hostile Adapter at this contract: it can pledge any position whose owner approved the LPVault into a proxy of its choice, release, reduce or deleverage any record of a proxy whose units it withdrew without the burn being checked here, and have a deleverage release send that record's units to LPVaultSwap; it cannot pledge a stray position the LPVault holds, send a position or a partial removal's principal to anyone but its depositor of record, debit another proxy's records in a seizure, or touch the pending-settlement balance. Example: a hostile Adapter calls `pledge(alice, eveProxy, vt, key42, false)` for the Uniswap v3 position whose `positionKey` is `key42` (NFT 42), which alice approved to the LPVault; the record names alice as depositor and `eveProxy` as proxy, so eve could borrow against it, but every exit of NFT 42 still sends it to alice; a call naming the Uniswap v3 LPVault as depositor for a stray NFT 99 reverts `InvalidDepositor`. (Source: Q-223, Q-224, Q-254, Q-255, Q-258, Q-293, Q-294, Q-296, Q-304)
- `REQ-COLL-006` — At the end of every transaction, a position has an open record in Collateralization under (`integrationId`, `positionKey`) if and only if that integration's LPVault holds it in custody with `depositorOf(positionKey) == record.depositor`: `pledge` calls `receivePosition` in the same call that writes the record, and closing a record (`release`, `releaseZeroCredit`, or a `releaseForDeleverage` that debits every credited unit) calls `returnPosition` in the same call. ### Top-ups and record limits

- `REQ-VAULT-024` — Every pledge MUST create a new record for a new position; adding collateral to an account that already exists in the market (a top-up) means pledging another position or re-crediting units the depositor added to a position already pledged (an NFT's `increaseLiquidity`, or `topUpPosition` for fungible LP shares). Credited units of an existing record MUST increase only through `recredit`, by exactly the position's live units above its credited units, with every check a top-up of that size runs (`REQ-COLL-015`). (Source: Q-067, Q-132, Q-254)
- `REQ-VAULT-025` — An account MUST hold at most `MAX_RECORDS_PER_ACCOUNT_MARKET = 16` open records in one market, records at 0 credited units included: `pledge` reverts with `TooManyRecords(openRecords)` when `openRecordCount[proxy][vaultToken] == 16`, rescue top-ups included. A borrower at the limit adds collateral by re-crediting units added to an existing position (`REQ-COLL-015`). Why: Aave's dust rule seizes the last chunk of an account whole, so every record of an account may have to unwind in one liquidation; without a bound an account built from many small records needs more gas than a transaction may use and becomes liquidatable but unliquidatable (about 125 records of $8 need 31.3M gas against the 16,777,216 per-transaction cap, `AMMN-GAS-01`). The bound also caps the pledge-side list walk at 16 steps (`SPEC-BACK-016`). (Source: Q-194, Q-230, Q-294)
- `REQ-VAULT-026` — Each PositionProxy belongs to exactly one (CDP Spoke, market), so its Aave account holds exactly one collateral reserve and its health factor covers only that market; nothing is cross-collateralized, and a wallet that uses two markets has two proxies (`REQ-COLL-031`). The CDP Spoke's per-user reserve limit (`spokeUserReservesLimit`, Aave DAO) therefore counts that one collateral reserve plus the proxy's debt reserves; the RiskModule refuses a borrow that would exceed it (`UserReservesLimit`) and the Spoke reverts with `MaximumUserReservesExceeded` as the second layer. (Source: Q-193, Q-234, Q-304)
- `REQ-VAULT-027` — A position MUST back at most one open record: `pledge` reverts with `AlreadyPledged` if `recordIdOf[integrationId][positionKey] != 0`. Keying by (`integrationId`, `positionKey`) keeps positions of two AMM protocols, or of two position managers of one AMM protocol, from colliding (`AMMN-FLOW-24`). (Source: Q-067, Q-224, Q-292)

### Top-up argument and budget parts

- `REQ-COLL-037` — **Top-up for fungible shares.** `recredit` MUST take `bytes topUp`. When `topUp` is non-empty it MUST call `topUpPosition(positionKey, depositor, topUp)` on the market's LPVault before it reads `unitsOf`, so the LP amount the depositor adds to a fungible-share position (Uniswap v2, Curve, Balancer) joins the same record; an NFT integration reverts that call with `TopUpNotSupported`, so its re-credit passes an empty `topUp` and credits units added through the AMM protocol's own `increaseLiquidity`. In both cases the credit is exactly `unitsOf(positionKey) − credited`, measured after the top-up, and the record does not count again against the 16-record limit. Why: a fungible LP share has no public way to add units to a position the LPVault holds, so without the argument a borrower at 16 records could not add collateral (`AMMN-GAS-01`). Example: a Curve record credited at 1,000e18 LP units; the depositor re-credits with `topUp` encoding 250e18 LP; the LPVault's LP balance rises by 250e18, `unitsOf` returns 1,250e18 and `added = 250e18`. (Source: Q-254, Q-294, Q-297)
- `REQ-COLL-038` — **No idle part (`AMMN-FLOW-16`, accepted risk).** Collateralization MUST have no idle budget part and no function that moves a charge out of `Regular` while the position stays pledged: an account's charge stays in `marketUsd[vt][Regular]` (and its slice in `Slice`) until a withdrawal, partial removal, seizure or reconciliation debits it (`SPEC-BACK-005`). A depositor who pledges and never borrows therefore keeps occupying the market budget. Why: moving dormant charges to a separate budget needed a third cap, a utilisation test and permissionless moves that a borrower could time around; the occupancy is bounded by `marketUsdCap`, `depositorUsdCap` and the pool-share cap set at listing, and monitoring watches the share of each market's budget held by accounts with no debt, so a squatter can delay new pledges but never create unbacked debt or block an exit. Example: `marketUsdCap = $1,000,000`; an LP pledges $900,000 and never borrows; new pledges into that market fit only within the remaining $100,000 until the LP withdraws, and the risk team can raise the cap (at most ×1.5 per normal delay) if the market's other users need room. (Source: Q-310, Q-354, Q-396)
- `REQ-COLL-039` — `markIndebted(proxy)` MUST be callable only by the Adapter holding the Lock, inside a borrow after `preBorrowCheck`; it reverts with `UnknownProxy(proxy)` for a proxy that has no market yet (`proxyMarket[proxy] == address(0)`, so the count could not be kept per market), sets `indebted[proxy] = true` and increments `indebtedCount[vt]` with `vt = proxyMarket[proxy]` when the flag was false, and otherwise changes nothing. It moves no charge: there is no idle part to move from (`Q-396`). (Source: Q-310, Q-354, Q-355, Q-396)
- `REQ-COLL-040` — `markDebtFree(proxy)` MUST be callable only by the Adapter or LPVaultSwap holding the Lock (`NotAdapter` for any other caller, `LockNotHeld` when the caller does not hold it); for a marked account it reads `Spoke.getUserAccountData(proxy).borrowCount` on `vaultToken.spoke()` of `vt = proxyMarket[proxy]` and, when it is 0, clears the flag and decrements `indebtedCount[vt]`; otherwise, and when that read returns short data or fails with at least `DEBT_MARK_GAS_FLOOR = 50,000` gas left, it changes nothing and does not revert; when the read fails with less than `DEBT_MARK_GAS_FLOOR` gas left it reverts with `InsufficientGas(gasLeft, DEBT_MARK_GAS_FLOOR)`. Why the floor: the read forwards all but 1/64 of the gas left, so a read that ran out of gas leaves at most 1/64 of what it was given; 50,000 left proves the read had at least 63 × 50,000 = 3,150,000 gas, which the floor takes to be above the cost of `getUserAccountData` for any CDP account (one collateral reserve and its debt reserves, each priced once). That bound is provisional until the fork gas calibration (`TEST-269`) measures `getUserAccountData` on the costliest supported CDP account at the pinned Spoke and served prices; if the measured cost reaches 3,150,000 gas, `DEBT_MARK_GAS_FLOOR` rises so that `63 × floor` stays above it. With the bound holding, a caller cannot starve the read to keep a mark set, while a read that genuinely fails never blocks the repay or liquidation that called it. `getMarketDebtFree(vaultToken)` returns `indebtedCount[vaultToken] == 0` and is what the MarketRegistry's `closeMarket` reads ([2.8](08-market-registry.md) `REQ-CONFIG-080`). A mark can stay set on a debt-free account when its last debt left in a call whose read failed (a price source of the account was down during the repay or liquidation), or without any Avana call (an outside liquidator's dust `liquidationCall` that repays the last debt and seizes 0 units, `SPEC-BACK-011`); the AaveAdapter's `refreshDebtMark(proxy)` lets anyone clear it: it takes the Lock, calls `markDebtFree(proxy)` and releases, moves no asset and reads no MarketRegistry state, pause, freeze, breaker or price, so it works in every market state, Offboarding included ([2.6](06-aave-adapter.md)). Every Avana flow that can remove an account's last debt calls it before releasing the Lock: `repay` and `repayAllAndWithdraw`, `repayWithCollateral` once Aave reports no borrow left ([2.6](06-aave-adapter.md) `REQ-ADAPTER-080`), and every `liquidate` ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md) `REQ-SWAP-026`, step 9), including a liquidation whose rest Aave reports as deficit. A stale `true` flag therefore only delays `closeMarket` until someone calls `refreshDebtMark` for that account, and it never blocks an exit, because no exit reads it. Example: in an Offboarding market bob's account is the last one marked; the liquidation that repaid bob's last 5 GHO ran `markDebtFree` while the Spoke's read of his account reverted, so `indebtedCount` stayed 1 and no later Avana call touches his account; once the read works again anyone calls `refreshDebtMark(bobProxy)`, the Spoke reports `borrowCount = 0`, the count drops to 0 and `closeMarket` can proceed. A `markDebtFree` sent with so little gas that the Spoke read runs out of it leaves under 50,000 gas and reverts `InsufficientGas`, so the caller resends with more gas. Checked by: TEST-272, MATH-UO-70. (Source: Q-355)

### Backing ledger

- `REQ-VAULT-028` — Collateralization MUST be the only writer of backing records, per-market and per-account totals, the pending-settlement balance and the exposure counters; every other contract that computes over them is a stateless reader. (Source: Q-224, Q-158, Q-201)
- `REQ-VAULT-029` — Collateralization MUST keep `totalCredited[vaultToken]`, `accountCredited` and `pending[vaultToken]` per `SPEC-BACK-009`, so that at the end of every transaction `totalCredited[vaultToken]` equals the sum of `credited` over the market's open records and `vaultToken.totalSupply() ≤ totalCredited[vaultToken] + pending[vaultToken].units` for any LPVaultSwap implementation, with equality while every `burnSeized` is followed in the same call by the `settleSeizure` or `settlePending` that debits it; this is the backing invariant in [4.1 Global Invariants](../04-security/01-global-invariants.md). The Adapter checks after every pledge, withdrawal, partial removal, re-credit and repay with collateral that the call kept the backing gap unchanged; Collateralization's own supply checks enforce it on seizure (`SPEC-BACK-007`) and pending settlement (`SPEC-BACK-012`). Why one-sided: an absolute equality would let one `burnSeized` that a faulty LPVaultSwap never settled make every later pledge and withdrawal of the market revert, debt-free exits included, until an Adapter upgrade; the one-sided form still proves every unit in circulation is backed, and the units behind such a burn stay in custody, so the market is more than fully backed. (Source: Q-066, Q-201, Q-224, Q-257)
- `REQ-VAULT-030` — A record's `positionKey`, `depositor`, `proxy`, `vaultToken` and `pledgedAt` MUST NOT change while it is open; no function moves a record to another depositor, proxy or market. `originalUnits` and `originalUsdCharge` MUST change only in `recredit`, which rebases them per `SPEC-BACK-013` so later exits release exactly the charge each remaining unit carries. A record is deleted when it closes, and a re-pledge of the same position writes every field fresh. (Source: Q-030, Q-067, Q-119, Q-254)
- `REQ-VAULT-031` — A record's `credited` MUST decrease only through debits in `settleSeizure`, reconciliation (`REQ-VAULT-134`), `releasePartial` and `releaseForDeleverage`, MUST increase only through `recredit`, and is cleared only by closing the record in `release`, `releaseZeroCredit` or a `releaseForDeleverage` of every credited unit. (Source: Q-134, Q-201, Q-254, Q-255)
- `REQ-VAULT-032` — Collateralization MUST keep each record's live units at or above its credited units (`SPEC-BACK-010`): it asks the LPVault to remove units only in `settleSeizure`, reconciliation, `releasePartial` and `releaseForDeleverage`, and only the units debited from that record in the same call; `recredit` credits at most the live units above `credited`. (Source: Q-066, Q-067, Q-201, Q-254, Q-255)
- `REQ-VAULT-033` — Closing a record MUST remove it from the seizure list if `inList`, else from the zero-credit list (at most 16 entries in all), remove it from the market's record index, drop its rescue units from `accountRescueUnits`, delete it from `records` and `recordIdOf`, and only then call `returnPosition(positionKey)` on the market's LPVault; a later pledge of the same position creates a fresh record with a new `recordId` and every field read anew. (Source: Q-119, Q-134, Q-224)

### Exposure counters

- `REQ-VAULT-034` — Collateralization MUST hold the exposure counters `marketUsd[vaultToken][bucket]`, `tokenUsd[hub][token]` and `depositorUsd[hub][depositor]`, all in USD with 8 decimals; the ParameterStore holds the cap values, the RiskModule computes the charge and receives the counter values as arguments of `checkPledge`. The token and depositor counters are keyed by the vault token's Hub, never by Spoke or market, so a Hub-wide cap is checked against every Spoke and market of that Hub on the chain together and adding Spokes never multiplies it (`REQ-COLL-026`). (Source: Q-055, Q-158, Q-240, Q-295)
- `REQ-VAULT-035` — The cap charge of a pledge is computed by the RiskModule at a price of at least `stressFloorUsd` per whole vault token ([2.7](07-risk-module.md) `SPEC-CAP-001`); Collateralization MUST book exactly the returned `usdCharge` as the record's original and current charge (`SPEC-BACK-003`). Health, liquidation and `minPledgeUsd` use the served LPOracle price. (Source: Q-175, Q-209, Q-230)
- `REQ-VAULT-036` — Every exit (credited units leaving a record through `settleSeizure`, reconciliation, `release`, `releasePartial` or `releaseForDeleverage`) MUST release exposure per `SPEC-BACK-005`, using the units actually exited, updating the record's `currentUsdCharge` and every aggregate counter it touched in the same transaction. (Source: Q-174, Q-201, Q-254, Q-255)
- `REQ-VAULT-037` — Exits MUST NOT call the RiskModule, the Adapter, the MarketRegistry or the ParameterStore, and the counter subtraction MUST saturate at 0 and never revert, so a policy upgrade or a counter error can never block a withdrawal or liquidation; if saturation ever clamps a value, Collateralization emits `CounterSaturated`, which monitoring treats as a bug alarm. Checked by: MATH-UO-25. (Source: Q-055, Q-158, Q-240)
- `REQ-VAULT-038` — Exposure released by an exit MUST leave the market counters slice first (`SPEC-BACK-005`): the released amount reduces `marketUsd[vaultToken][Slice]` up to its current value and the rest reduces `marketUsd[vaultToken][Regular]`. (Source: Q-157, Q-175, Q-209)

(Source: Q-160, Q-201, Q-254, Q-255)

### Liquidation seizure

- `REQ-VAULT-046` — `beginSeizure(proxy, vaultToken)` MUST revert with `NotLPVaultSwap` unless `msg.sender == LP_VAULT_SWAP`, and with `LockNotHeld` unless `LOCK.holder() == LP_VAULT_SWAP`. (Source: Q-148, Q-160)
- `REQ-VAULT-047` — `beginSeizure` MUST revert with `SharesNotEqualAssets` unless `Hub.getAddedAssets(assetId) == Hub.getAddedShares(assetId)` for the vault token's Hub asset. (Source: Q-103, Q-189)
- `REQ-VAULT-048` — `beginSeizure` MUST validate the vault token (`REQ-COLL-003`), revert with `SeizureActive` if a snapshot is already active and with `UnknownProxy(proxy)` unless `vaultToken.isValidProxy(proxy)`, and store in transient storage `proxy`, `vaultToken`, `reserveId`, `sharesBefore = Spoke.getUserSuppliedShares(reserveId, proxy)` and `supplyBefore = vaultToken.totalSupply()`. LPVaultSwap calls it after its pre-checks and before `Spoke.liquidationCall`. (Source: Q-160, Q-224)
- `REQ-VAULT-049` — The seizure snapshot MUST be taken by Collateralization itself while the lock is held; no snapshot value supplied by the caller is accepted. (Source: Q-160)
- `REQ-VAULT-050` — `settleSeizure(proxy, vaultToken, units, minAmounts)` MUST revert with `NotLPVaultSwap` unless `msg.sender == LP_VAULT_SWAP`, with `LockNotHeld` unless `LOCK.holder() == LP_VAULT_SWAP`, and with `SeizureMismatch` unless an active snapshot exists for exactly this `proxy` and `vaultToken`. (Source: Q-148, Q-160)
- `REQ-VAULT-051` — `settleSeizure` MUST read `sharesAfter = Spoke.getUserSuppliedShares(reserveId, proxy)` and `supplyAfter = vaultToken.totalSupply()`, revert with `DebitMismatch` unless `sharesBefore − sharesAfter ≥ units` (and `sharesAfter == sharesBefore` when `units == 0`: Aave pays its fee only out of a seizure, and LPVaultSwap reverts `NothingSeized` before it settles zero units, so a share drop with no seized units is never a liquidation), and revert with `SupplyDropMismatch(units, supplyBefore − supplyAfter)` unless `supplyBefore − supplyAfter == units` (`SPEC-BACK-007`); the excess `fee = sharesBefore − sharesAfter − units` is the shares Aave's liquidation fee moved to its fee receiver inside the Hub, which `settleSeizure` reconciles into the pending balance (`REQ-VAULT-176`). Between the two calls LPVaultSwap has had the Adapter burn the seized units from its own balance (`Adapter.burnSeized`, [2.6](06-aave-adapter.md)); the supply check proves that burn happened for exactly the units being debited, so no units are removed for vault tokens that still circulate. (Source: Q-160, Q-189, Q-224)
- `REQ-VAULT-176` — Liquidation fee on a vault-token key. When Aave's `liquidationFee` of the account's dynamic key is above 0, a liquidation moves `f` supplied shares from the proxy to Aave's fee receiver inside the Hub in addition to the `u` units the Hub sends to LPVaultSwap; no vault token leaves the Hub for them. `settleSeizure` MUST then reconcile the account-market against `sharesBefore − f`, which debits the `f` units (with any earlier shortfall) from the records largest-first, raises `pending.units` by them and books their principal, removed to Collateralization, in the pending balance (`REQ-VAULT-133`), before it debits and unwinds the `u` seized units to LPVaultSwap. Liquidation therefore never stops on the fee: the drift check still refuses new borrowing and new pledges while it is set (`REQ-ADAPTER-030`), the account's credit equals its supplied shares after the call, and the backing gap is unchanged (`SPEC-BACK-007`). The `f` vault tokens stay in the Hub credited to Aave's fee receiver and cannot leave it (the vault token admits no such recipient); their principal stays in the pending balance, which `settlePending` releases only for burned units (`SPEC-BACK-012`). Example: the fee at 10 % on Bob's key, a liquidation that sends 1,000 units to LPVaultSwap and moves 100 fee shares: the records lose 1,100 units, `pending.units` rises by 100, the supply falls by 1,000 and the gap does not move. Checked by: TEST-055, PROP-004, MATH-UO-78. (Source: Q-080, Q-160)
- `REQ-VAULT-052` — If `units == 0`, `settleSeizure` MUST only verify the zero share drop and the zero supply drop, clear the snapshot and return one zero per market token, with no debit, reconciliation or LPVault call. LPVaultSwap itself reverts a liquidation that seizes 0 units ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)), so this path is reached only by a non-conforming LPVaultSwap and changes nothing. (Source: Q-144, Q-160)
- `REQ-VAULT-053` — If `units > 0`, `settleSeizure` MUST, in this order: reconcile the account-market with `sharesBefore` as the supplied amount (`REQ-VAULT-133`), allocate and debit the seized `units` over the records per `SPEC-BACK-006`, release exposure per `SPEC-BACK-005` for each debited record, subtract `units` from `totalCredited` and `accountCredited`, clear the snapshot, then for each record debited for the seizure call `unwind(positionKey, take, zeros, LP_VAULT_SWAP)` on the market's LPVault, which checks the pool lock first (`REQ-VAULT-010`; the Uniswap v3 calls are [2.20](20-uniswap-v3-integration.md) `REQ-VAULT-057`), and finally require the summed `amounts[i] ≥ minAmounts[i]` for every pool token (`BelowMinimum(i, received, minimum)`), the per-token oracle minimums LPVaultSwap computed ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)). All of it happens in one call; any failure reverts the whole liquidation, including Aave's seizure and the Adapter's burn. (Source: Q-201, Q-224, Q-305)
- `REQ-VAULT-054` — The debit MUST walk the account-market's seizure list from the head and stop as soon as the seized units are covered (`SPEC-BACK-006`); it reads and writes only the records it debits, and a record debited to 0 credited units is unlinked from the list. An account-market holds at most 16 records (`REQ-VAULT-025`), so even a seizure that debits every record fits within the gas bound of `SPEC-BACK-016`, whatever `debtToCover` the dust rule forces. (Source: Q-194, Q-294)
- `REQ-VAULT-055` — If the seized units exceed the account-market's credited units left after reconciliation, `settleSeizure` MUST revert with `InsufficientCredit`; it never leaves a remainder undebited. Checked by: MATH-ST-45. (Source: Q-194, Q-160, Q-201)
- `REQ-VAULT-056` — A record debited to 0 credited units MUST stay open (`REQ-VAULT-074`); nothing is pushed to the depositor during a liquidation, and the position stays in LPVault custody for the depositor to withdraw with its fees. (Source: Q-061, Q-134)
- `REQ-VAULT-059` — Liquidation and reconciliation MUST NOT reduce the fees owed to the depositor; the depositor's fee claim survives even when the last unit of the position is removed. (Source: Q-009, Q-061, Q-134, Q-201)
- `REQ-VAULT-060` — `settleSeizure` MUST return the summed `amounts` that `unwind` paid to LPVaultSwap, one per market token, as information only; LPVaultSwap sizes its swaps and checks from its own before/after balances. Principal reconciled in the same call goes to the pending-settlement balance and is not included. (Source: Q-061, Q-124, Q-201, Q-297)
- `REQ-VAULT-061` — Market tokens MUST NOT rest in Avana's custody contracts except the pending-settlement balance of `Q-201`, which Collateralization holds only until LPVaultSwap settles it: seized principal and the principal of a repay with collateral go from the AMM protocol to LPVaultSwap, the principal of a partial removal and fees go from the AMM protocol to the depositor, without resting in the LPVault or Collateralization (an LPVault that receives native ETH wraps and forwards it in the same call, [2.2](02-lp-vault.md) `REQ-VAULT-175`). Fee-on-transfer and rebasing tokens are excluded at listing, but an issuer can switch a transfer fee on later (USDT), so every amount an Avana contract receives is measured by its balance delta and every later step uses the measured amount, which is at most the principal removed (`SPEC-BACK-015`); the issuer breaker then stops new exposure to the token ([2.7](07-risk-module.md)). (Source: Q-061, Q-124, Q-201, Q-224, Q-241, Q-271, Q-297)
- `REQ-VAULT-062` — `settleSeizure` MUST emit one `RecordDebited` per record debited for the seizure and one `SeizureSettled` per call. (Source: Q-076)
- `REQ-VAULT-063` — Collateralization MUST NOT compute or check liquidation size, bonus, health factor, price state, pause state or keeper identity; those belong to the Aave Spoke and LPVaultSwap. (Source: Q-060, Q-116, Q-128, Q-227)

### Records at zero credited units

- `REQ-VAULT-074` — A record whose credited units reach 0 through seizure or reconciliation MUST move from the seizure list to the account-market's zero-credit list and stay open with its depositor, proxy and market, so the depositor can collect fees and withdraw the position; it cannot be debited again and is never re-credited (`recredit` reverts `NothingToRecredit`): the depositor recovers the position with `releaseZeroCredit` and pledges it again as a new record. A partial removal never leaves 0 credited units, and a `releaseForDeleverage` that does closes the record in the same call (`REQ-COLL-017`). (Source: Q-134, Q-201, Q-254, Q-255)
- `REQ-VAULT-075` — `releaseZeroCredit(depositor, vaultToken, positionKey)` MUST revert with `NotAdapter` unless `msg.sender == ADAPTER` and with `LockNotHeld` unless `LOCK.holder() == ADAPTER`, with `RecordNotFound` without an open record, with `InvalidVaultToken` unless `record.vaultToken == vaultToken`, and with `NotDepositor` unless `depositor == record.depositor`; then reconcile the account-market (`REQ-VAULT-132`), revert with `RecordHasCredit` unless `record.credited == 0`, close the record and call `returnPosition(positionKey)` on the market's LPVault. The Adapter reaches it from its own `releaseZeroCredit`, which passes `msg.sender` as `depositor` and reads no pause, freeze, price or policy ([2.6](06-aave-adapter.md)). Apart from the Spoke and Hub views and the LPVault calls of reconciliation it makes no Spoke or Hub call, no debt check and no burn, so it works in every pause state. Why through the Adapter: Collateralization is not a Lock member, and the Lock's two members are the only entry points to custody (`AMMN-ARCH-01`); the position still goes only to the depositor of record. (Source: Q-134, Q-201, Q-223, Q-227, Q-296)
- `REQ-VAULT-076` — No address other than the depositor of record (not the proxy, the Adapter, LPVaultSwap, governance or the stray-position path) can collect fees from, release or move a position whose record is open at 0 credited units. (Source: Q-104, Q-134)
- `REQ-COLL-032` — `collectFees(depositor, vaultToken, positionKey)` MUST revert with `NotAdapter` unless `msg.sender == ADAPTER` and with `LockNotHeld` unless `LOCK.holder() == ADAPTER`, with `RecordNotFound` without an open record, with `InvalidVaultToken` unless `record.vaultToken == vaultToken`, and with `NotDepositor` unless `depositor == record.depositor`; it then calls `collectFees(positionKey, depositor)` on the market's LPVault, which pays every fee to the depositor of record only, and returns the amounts. It reads no pause, breaker, freeze, price or policy and changes no record, total or counter, so fees stay collectable in every state, at any health factor and for a record at 0 credited units. The Adapter passes `msg.sender` as `depositor`. Why through the Adapter: the LPVault is not a Lock member and its value-moving functions run only inside a Lock member's operation (`AMMN-ARCH-01`); a hostile Adapter can at most block a collection, never redirect it, because the LPVault pays only the depositor of record. (Source: Q-011, Q-030, Q-296)

(Source: Q-119)

### Reconcile on exit

An unsettled seizure arises when the Aave Spoke has moved a proxy's vault-token collateral to LPVaultSwap but `settleSeizure` never ran, which only a non-conforming LPVaultSwap implementation can cause. The units then sit in LPVaultSwap, unburned, while the records still credit them, so the proxy's supplied shares are below its credited units and, without reconciliation, the last records could never be withdrawn. Reconciliation treats exactly the missing units as seized.

- `REQ-VAULT-129` — Collateralization MUST keep `accountCredited[proxy][vaultToken]` equal to the sum of `credited` over the account-market's open records: `+L` at `pledge` and `+added` at `recredit`; `−units` in `settleSeizure`, `release`, `releasePartial` and `releaseForDeleverage`; `−shortfall` in reconciliation. (Source: Q-201, Q-254, Q-255)
- `REQ-VAULT-130` — Reconciliation MUST compute `shortfall` per `SPEC-BACK-011` from `accountCredited[proxy][vaultToken]` and the proxy's supplied shares before the current call's own movement. It MUST run only while `Hub.getAddedAssets(assetId) == Hub.getAddedShares(assetId)` for the vault token's Hub asset, because only then are supplied shares equal to vault-token units; while they differ, `release`, `releasePartial`, `recredit`, `releaseForDeleverage` and `releaseZeroCredit` skip reconciliation, and an unequal Hub asset also stops liquidation (`REQ-VAULT-047`), so no new seizure can arise in the meantime. (Source: Q-189, Q-201)
- `REQ-VAULT-131` — `release`, `releasePartial` and `releaseForDeleverage` MUST reconcile before their units check, with supplied shares `Spoke.getUserSuppliedShares(reserveId, proxy) + units`: the proxy has just withdrawn `units` in the same Adapter call, so adding them back gives the supply before the withdrawal; `recredit` MUST reconcile before it reads the uncredited units, with supplied shares `Spoke.getUserSuppliedShares(reserveId, proxy)`. (Source: Q-201, Q-254, Q-255)

### Partial removal, re-credit, deleverage release and rescue units

These three Adapter-driven calls change an open record without opening or closing custody of a new position. Each one preserves, whatever its inputs:

- **Backing:** the record and `totalCredited` change by exactly the units the Adapter burns from or mints to the proxy in the same call, so the market's backing gap `totalCredited + pending.units − totalSupply` is the same after the call as before it (`SPEC-BACK-009`).
- **Live units cover credit:** the position's live units fall by exactly the units debited, or credit rises by at most the live units above `credited` (`SPEC-BACK-010`).
- **Custody:** principal leaves only to the depositor of record (partial removal) or to LPVaultSwap (deleverage), and the position returns only to its depositor.
- **Exposure:** exits release exactly the charge the exited units carry (`SPEC-BACK-005`); a re-credit books the full charge of the added units and rebases the record (`SPEC-BACK-013`).
- **Rescue units:** never above the record's credited units (`SPEC-BACK-014`).

Health, debt, pause, breaker and freeze checks are the Adapter's and the RiskModule's ([2.6](06-aave-adapter.md), [2.7](07-risk-module.md)); Collateralization reads none of them on these paths, so a debt-free partial removal works under any policy implementation.

- `REQ-COLL-014` — `releasePartial(proxy, vaultToken, positionKey, units, minAmounts)` MUST, in this order: revert with `NotAdapter` unless `msg.sender == ADAPTER` and with `LockNotHeld` unless `LOCK.holder() == ADAPTER`; revert with `RecordNotFound`, `NotRecordProxy` or `InvalidVaultToken` unless `positionKey` has an open record with `record.proxy == proxy` and `record.vaultToken == vaultToken`; reconcile the account-market (`REQ-VAULT-131`); revert with `UnitsMismatch` if `units == 0` and with `PartialRemovalTooLarge(units, credited)` unless `units < record.credited`; debit `units` from the record, which keeps its place in the seizure list; release exposure per `SPEC-BACK-005` with exited units `units`; cap the record's rescue units per `SPEC-BACK-014`; subtract `units` from `totalCredited[vaultToken]` and `accountCredited[proxy][vaultToken]`; emit `RecordReduced(recordId, units, capReleased)`; then read the depositor's balance of each market token, call `releaseToDepositor(positionKey, units, minAmounts)` on the market's LPVault, which removes exactly `units` and collects only the principal that removal returned to the depositor of record ([2.2](02-lp-vault.md) `REQ-VAULT-161`), and return `remaining = record.credited` with the depositor's measured balance deltas as `amounts`. The Adapter has had the proxy withdraw `units` and burned them before the call, and with debt it checks `RM.checkRemainingRecord(marketId, remaining)` after it. Why `units < credited`: removing every credited unit is a withdrawal of the position, which returns it whole (`REQ-VAULT-069`); one exit path per outcome keeps the record states simple and never leaves a zero-credit record by choice. Why reconcile first: without it a partial removal could take units that back units Aave already seized and never settled. The depositor's `minAmounts` bound what a moved pool price can cost the depositor; the remaining collateral is valued at oracle prices, which a pool trade cannot move. Example: alice's NFT 42 credits 5,000,000,000 units with a $10,000 charge; she removes 1,000,000,000 units with no debt; the record keeps 4,000,000,000 units and a charge of `⌈10^12 × 4,000,000,000 / 5,000,000,000⌉ = 800,000,000,000` ($8,000), the counters fall by $2,000, about 1,000 USDC and 1,000 USDT go to alice, the vault-token supply and `totalCredited` both fall by 1,000,000,000, and a request for 4,000,000,000 units reverts `PartialRemovalTooLarge(4,000,000,000, 4,000,000,000)`. Checked by: TEST-237, PROP-065. (Source: Q-061, Q-201, Q-254, Q-257)
- `REQ-COLL-015` — `recredit(depositor, proxy, vaultToken, positionKey, topUp, requireRescue)` MUST, in this order: revert with `NotAdapter` unless `msg.sender == ADAPTER` and with `LockNotHeld` unless `LOCK.holder() == ADAPTER`; validate the vault token (`REQ-COLL-003`); revert with `RecordNotFound`, `InvalidVaultToken`, `NotRecordProxy` or `NotDepositor` unless `positionKey` has an open record with `record.vaultToken == vaultToken`, `record.proxy == proxy` and `record.depositor == depositor`; reconcile the account-market (`REQ-VAULT-131`); when `topUp` is non-empty, call `topUpPosition(positionKey, depositor, topUp)` on the market's LPVault (fungible LP shares; NFT integrations revert `TopUpNotSupported`, [2.19](19-amm-integration-interface.md) `REQ-AMM-CORE-011`); read `live = unitsOf(positionKey)` on the market's LPVault and set `added = live − record.credited`, reverting with `NothingToRecredit(positionKey)` if `record.credited == 0` or `added == 0`, and with `LiquidityAboveBound` if `record.credited + added > MAX_CREDITED_UNITS`; call `poke()` on `vaultToken.lpOracle()`; call `RISK_MODULE.checkPledge(depositor, proxy, vaultToken.marketId(), positionKey, added, counters, requireRescue)` with the current counters, exactly as a pledge of `added` units into the same account (its revert data propagates unchanged); validate the charge as `REQ-VAULT-015` does with `L = added`; rebase the record per `SPEC-BACK-013`; add `added` to `record.credited`, `totalCredited[vaultToken]` and `accountCredited[proxy][vaultToken]`; book the counters per `SPEC-BACK-004` and the rescue units per `SPEC-BACK-014`; emit `RecordRecredited(recordId, added, usdCharge, rescueUnits)`; and return `(added, charge.rescueUnits)`. The record keeps its place in the seizure list. The Adapter mints exactly `added` to the proxy and has it supply them in the same call ([2.6](06-aave-adapter.md)). Why only the depositor: a third party could otherwise book cap space and borrowing power against a borrower's position; units anyone adds to a custodied position stay uncredited until its depositor re-credits it ([2.2](02-lp-vault.md) `REQ-VAULT-077`). Why every top-up check: the added units are new collateral and new exposure, so the caps, the price state, the breakers and the rescue rules must see them as a top-up of that size. Example: after the partial removal above alice calls `increaseLiquidity` on NFT 42 for 1,500,000,000 units; `previewRecredit(UNISWAP_V3, key42)` returns 1,500,000,000; at `price8 = 200,000,000` the RiskModule charges $3,000; the record's credited units become 5,500,000,000, its charge basis $11,000 over 5,500,000,000 units, and 1,500,000,000 units are minted to her proxy. Checked by: TEST-238, PROP-065. (Source: Q-157, Q-201, Q-224, Q-252, Q-253, Q-254, Q-257)
- `REQ-COLL-016` — Collateralization MUST book on each record its rescue units per `SPEC-BACK-014`: `rescueUnits += charge.rescueUnits` at `pledge` and `recredit`, and `rescueUnits = min(rescueUnits, credited)` after every debit (seizure, reconciliation, partial removal, deleverage release); it MUST keep `accountRescueUnits[proxy][vaultToken]` equal to the sum of `rescueUnits` over the account-market's open records, and drop a record's rescue units from the sum when it closes. Collateralization only books and reports them: Aave's health factor, liquidation and the seizure order count rescue units in full, and only the RiskModule's health check after a borrow, a withdrawal with debt or a partial removal with debt values them at zero ([2.7](07-risk-module.md) `SPEC-CF-005`). Why: units admitted through the slice or a rescue exemption exist to keep an indebted account out of liquidation; if they could support new debt, a borrower could top up from the slice on every dip and borrow back to the opening floor, turning the rescue budget into growth past the caps. Example: a top-up of 1,500,000,000 units charged $3,000 with $2,000 from the slice books `⌈1,500,000,000 × 2,000 / 3,000⌉ = 1,000,000,000` rescue units; after a seizure leaves the record 300,000,000 credited units it keeps 300,000,000 rescue units. Checked by: TEST-235, TEST-236. (Source: Q-157, Q-252, Q-253)
- `REQ-COLL-017` — `releaseForDeleverage(proxy, vaultToken, positionKey, units, minAmounts)` MUST, in this order: revert with `NotAdapter` unless `msg.sender == ADAPTER` and with `LockNotHeld` unless `LOCK.holder() == ADAPTER`; revert with `RecordNotFound`, `NotRecordProxy` or `InvalidVaultToken` unless `positionKey` has an open record with `record.proxy == proxy` and `record.vaultToken == vaultToken`; reconcile the account-market (`REQ-VAULT-131`); revert with `UnitsMismatch` unless `0 < units ≤ record.credited`; debit `units`, release exposure per `SPEC-BACK-005`, cap rescue units per `SPEC-BACK-014` and subtract `units` from `totalCredited` and `accountCredited`; if no credited units remain, remove it from the seizure list and close the record (`REQ-VAULT-033`) and set `closed = true`; emit `RecordReduced(recordId, units, capReleased)` and, when closed, `CollateralReleased` with `unitsBurned = units`; then call `unwind(positionKey, units, minAmounts, LP_VAULT_SWAP)` on the market's LPVault (`minAmounts` are the per-token oracle minimums LPVaultSwap computed for the deleverage; `LengthMismatch` for a wrong length) and, when closed, `returnPosition(positionKey)` on the market's LPVault, and return the removal's `amounts` (information only; LPVaultSwap measures what it receives) and `closed`. The Adapter reaches it only from its repay with collateral, after the proxy repaid the debt in the same call, and has had the proxy withdraw `units` and burned them before the call ([2.6](06-aave-adapter.md)). Why to LPVaultSwap: it converts the market tokens into the repaid debt asset with an oracle-derived minimum on every leg and repays the flash loan; the Adapter must hold no market token at the end of a call. Why close at zero: Aave no longer counts the record, so the position returns to its depositor with any uncredited units and every fee instead of waiting for a `releaseZeroCredit`. Example: alice repays with collateral using 800,000,000 units of NFT 7 (4,000,000,000 credited, $8,000 charge); the record keeps 3,200,000,000 units and a $6,400 charge, the 800,000,000 units' principal goes to LPVaultSwap and `closed = false`; a call naming all 4,000,000,000 units closes the record and returns NFT 7 to alice. Checked by: TEST-239, PROP-065, PROP-072. (Source: Q-201, Q-224, Q-255, Q-257)
- `REQ-COLL-018` — `previewRecredit(integrationId, positionKey)` MUST return the `added` units `recredit` would credit now: `unitsOf(positionKey) on the market's LPVault − credited` after reconciling the account-market's current shortfall with the proxy's current supplied shares, and 0 for a record at 0 credited units; it reverts with `RecordNotFound` without an open record and with `Locked` per `REQ-VAULT-091`. `getAccountRescueUnits(proxy, vaultToken)` MUST return `accountRescueUnits[proxy][vaultToken]` and `poolUnits(poolKey)` MUST return `poolUnits[poolKey]`; both MUST NOT revert while the lock is free or held by the AaveAdapter, and revert with `Locked` while any other member holds the lock. Why the exception: the RiskModule reads `getAccountRescueUnits` inside the Adapter's borrow, withdrawal-with-debt and partial-removal-with-debt calls for the health check of `Q-253`, and `poolUnits` inside the Adapter's borrow and withdrawal-with-debt calls for the market breaker's concentration gate ([2.7](07-risk-module.md) `SPEC-BRK-002`, step 12 of the borrow-side checks); every borrow and every withdrawal with debt runs under the Adapter's lock, so a holder-only rule would make every one of them revert. Both are read while no Collateralization call is running (a borrow makes none; a withdrawal with debt and a partial removal with debt run the check after `release` or `releasePartial` has returned (`REQ-ADAPTER-078`, `REQ-PROXY-038`, `REQ-RISK-068`), so a withdrawn record's rescue units are already removed and are not subtracted twice), and Collateralization updates every record and total before it calls out (`REQ-VAULT-089`), so the value read is always a settled one; a contract reached from inside a Collateralization call under the Adapter's lock (a token or pool callback during a withdrawal) reads the values as they stand after that call's own updates, which is no less settled. Both are aggregates that move no asset, so reading them mid-call gives no lever over custody. Example: alice's account holds 1,000,000,000 rescue units in the USDC/USDT market and Avana holds 2,000,000,000,000 units of its pool, whose cap is 10 % of a 20,000,000,000,000-unit depth; during her borrow the RiskModule reads `getAccountRescueUnits(aliceProxy, vt) = 1,000,000,000` and `poolUnits(poolKey) = 2,000,000,000,000` (at the cap, not above it: the gate stays open) under the Adapter's lock, while the same calls made by a pool callback during a seizure, with LPVaultSwap holding the lock, revert `Locked`. Checked by: TEST-028, TEST-060, TEST-236. (Source: Q-148, Q-201, Q-253, Q-254)

## Math

Units: credited units are the market's units ([2.19](19-amm-integration-interface.md); liquidity for concentrated liquidity, LP base units for fungible shares) and equal vault-token base units (multiplier 1, [2.4](04-vault-tokens.md) `SPEC-UNIT-002`). USD amounts have 8 decimals. `d` is the vault token's `decimals()`. Rounding uses OpenZeppelin `Math.mulDiv` (v5.1.0). The examples use a full-range USDC/USDT 0.01% market of the Uniswap v3 Stable Spoke on Ethereum (6 decimals each, tick spacing 1, `d = 6`), `stressFloorUsd = 1.00000000` per whole vault token, `minPledgeUsd = $10` (`10e8`), and alice's account: NFT 42 with 5,000,000,000 units and NFT 7 with 4,000,000,000 units, pledged when one whole vault token was worth $2.00.

- `SPEC-BACK-002` — Credited units and mint. `c` = the `units` that the LPVault's `receivePosition` returns, read after it took custody (for concentrated liquidity the position's liquidity, [2.20](20-uniswap-v3-integration.md) `REQ-VAULT-147`); `credited = c`, returned to the Adapter, which mints `minted = credited` to the proxy. Why: reading after custody fixes the amount the LPVault actually holds, and a 1:1 unit means no conversion can round in anyone's favour; returning the amount instead of minting keeps the ledger and the mint in one Adapter call. Units: market units = vault-token base units. Rounding: none (exact integer). Bounds: `0 < c ≤ MAX_CREDITED_UNITS = 2^120 − 1`, stored in `uint120`; each integration states why its markets stay below it ([2.4](04-vault-tokens.md) `SPEC-UNIT-006`). Example: NFT 42 has liquidity 5,000,000,000, so `pledge` returns `credited = 5,000,000,000` and the Adapter mints 5,000,000,000 base units = 5,000 whole tokens to alice's proxy, worth about $10,000 at $2.00 per whole token (well above `minPledgeUsd = $10`). Checked by: MATH-UO-04, MATH-UO-30, MATH-UO-33, HYP-01, HYP-40, HYP-41, HYP-44, QNT-03. (Source: Q-066, Q-224, Q-291)
- `SPEC-BACK-003` — Pledge cap charge (computed by the RiskModule, booked by Collateralization). The RiskModule returns `usdCharge = mulDiv(c, max(price8, stressFloorUsd[m]), 10^d, Ceil)` with `price8` the LPOracle price of one whole vault token (8 decimals) and `sliceUsd ≤ usdCharge` ([2.7](07-risk-module.md) `SPEC-CAP-001`, `SPEC-CAP-002`). Collateralization requires `0 < usdCharge < 2^64` and `sliceUsd ≤ usdCharge` and stores `originalUsdCharge = currentUsdCharge = usdCharge`. Why: booking at no less than the stress floor keeps the market's credited units inside the Hub `addCap` sized at that floor (`Q-175`), and rounding up means a pledge of any size charges at least 1. Units: USD, 8 decimals. Rounding: up. Bounds: `c < 2^120`, `price8 < 2^64` under listing bounds, so the product stays below `2^184`; a value of `2^64` or more reverts `InvalidCharge`, because the record stores charges as `uint64` (`SPEC-BACK-016`). Example: NFT 42 at `price8 = 200,000,000` charges `⌈5,000,000,000 × 200,000,000 / 10^6⌉ = 1,000,000,000,000` ($10,000.00000000); the same position at `price8 = 90,000,000` is booked at the floor: `5,000,000,000 × 100,000,000 / 10^6 = 500,000,000,000` ($5,000). Checked by: MATH-UO-27, MATH-UO-28, MATH-UO-29, HYP-10, HYP-55. (Source: Q-055, Q-119, Q-175, Q-209)
- `SPEC-BACK-004` — Counter increments at pledge and re-credit. The slice part `sliceUsd` is non-zero only for a top-up of an indebted account whose health factor is below the rescue top-up target `rescueHealthFactor` (1.10, the Lens recommended level, which replaces `minOpeningHealthFactor`, `Q-398`), and it is at most the amount that restores that target ([2.7](07-risk-module.md) `SPEC-CAP-003`). With `hub = vaultToken.hub()`: `marketUsd[vt][Regular] += usdCharge − sliceUsd` (there is no idle part, `Q-396`); `marketUsd[vt][Slice] += sliceUsd`; `tokenUsd[hub][tokens[i]] += usdCharge` for every market token `i`; `depositorUsd[hub][depositor] += usdCharge`, with `tokens` read from the LPVault's market table. Why: the full charge goes to each token's counter because a fall in any token lowers the whole position's value; the market budget is split so the rescue slice stays reserved for top-ups; the token and depositor counters are Hub-wide so every Spoke and market of the Hub draws on one cap (`AMMN-RT-05`). Units: USD, 8 decimals. Rounding: none. Bounds: checked `uint256` addition; with charges below `2^64` and caps below `2^128` no counter can overflow. Example: alice's new-account pledge of NFT 42 (`usdCharge = 1,000,000,000,000`, `sliceUsd = 0`) adds $10,000 to the regular bucket, to the Stable LP Hub's USDC and USDT counters and to her depositor counter on that Hub. A later top-up charged `300,000,000,000` with `sliceUsd = 200,000,000,000` (an indebted account below `rescueHealthFactor` with $1,000 of regular room left) adds $1,000 to the regular bucket, $2,000 to the slice bucket and $3,000 to each token and depositor counter. Checked by: MATH-UO-29, HYP-10, HYP-44, HYP-48, HYP-55. (Source: Q-055, Q-119, Q-157, Q-158, Q-175, Q-209, Q-295)
- `SPEC-BACK-005` — Exposure release on exit (fixed basis, slice first). For a record with `U0 = originalUnits`, `V0 = originalUsdCharge` (the basis set at pledge, or the rebased basis of the last re-credit, `SPEC-BACK-013`), `chargeBefore = currentUsdCharge`, `unitsBefore = credited` and actual exited units `x ≤ unitsBefore`: `remainingUnits = unitsBefore − x`; `remainingCharge = mulDiv(V0, remainingUnits, U0, Ceil)`; `capReleased = chargeBefore − remainingCharge`; set `currentUsdCharge = remainingCharge`. Market counters: `fromSlice = min(capReleased, marketUsd[vt][Slice])`; `marketUsd[vt][Slice] −= fromSlice`; `marketUsd[vt][Regular] = max(marketUsd[vt][Regular] − (capReleased − fromSlice), 0)`. Each of `tokenUsd[hub][tokens[i]]` for every market token and `depositorUsd[hub][depositor]` becomes `max(counter − capReleased, 0)`. Why: the charge depends only on the remaining units, so any split of exits gives the same total release and `remainingUnits = 0` releases exactly what is left, so counters never drift with prices (`Q-174`); releasing the slice first restores the scarce rescue budget before the regular budget and shrinks the part of the token and depositor counters that sits above their caps. `capReleased ≥ 0` because the charge is monotone in remaining units. Units: USD, 8 decimals. Rounding: up on the remaining charge (conservative). Bounds: `V0 < 2^64`, `remainingUnits < 2^120`, product below `2^184`; subtraction saturates and never reverts. Example: reconciling 1,575,000,000 units from NFT 42 (`U0 = 5,000,000,000`, `V0 = chargeBefore = 1,000,000,000,000`) leaves `remainingCharge = ⌈10^12 × 3,425,000,000 / 5,000,000,000⌉ = 685,000,000,000`, so `capReleased = 315,000,000,000` ($3,150); with the slice counter at 0 it all leaves the regular bucket. For the top-up record above (`V0 = 300,000,000,000`) leaving in full while the slice counter holds `200,000,000,000`, `fromSlice = 200,000,000,000` and the regular bucket drops by $1,000. A partial removal and a deleverage release use the same formula with `x = units`. Checked by: MATH-UO-25, MATH-UO-26, MATH-UO-29, HYP-10, HYP-44, HYP-48. - `SPEC-BACK-006` — Seizure list and allocation. Each account-market keeps the record IDs with `credited > 0` in the array `seizureList[proxy][vaultToken]`, at most 16 entries. Insertion of a new record with credited `c` (its `recordId` is the highest so far): walk from the head (index 0) and insert before the first record whose `credited < c`, or at the tail if there is none, so the list is largest-first at insertion with ties by lowest `recordId`. Debit of `u` units (a seizure or a reconciliation shortfall): `rem = u`; from the head, for each record `r` while `rem > 0`: `take_r = min(rem, credited_r)`, `credited_r −= take_r`, `rem −= take_r`, and remove `r` from the array if `credited_r = 0`, shifting the later entries down by one and appending `r` to the account-market's zero-credit list `zeroCreditList[proxy][vaultToken]`; then require `rem == 0`. Only the last record touched can be partially debited; it keeps its place at the head. A partial removal, a deleverage release and a re-credit change a record's credited units in place, without moving it, so after any partial change the list is in insertion order, not credited-descending: a debit walks the stored order, whatever the records' current credited units. Why: the pledger pays the ordering gas at insertion and no later call sorts; within one debit of a list no partial change has touched the result equals the `Q-194` order (credited descending, ties lowest ID). For Aave and the liquidator units are interchangeable, because every record of a market has the market's exact shape, so the order changes neither the seized units nor the principal unwound per unit. It does decide which records carry the debit, and two per-record quantities depend on that: the charge released (`SPEC-BACK-005` releases from each debited record by its own basis `V0 / U0`, so debiting a record pledged at a higher price releases more cap room per unit) and the rescue units kept (`SPEC-BACK-014` caps each debited record's rescue units at its credited units, so debiting a record that holds rescue units can lower `accountRescueUnits`, and debiting one that holds none leaves them unchanged). Neither moves an asset or changes what any depositor receives; both are booked from the records actually debited. Units: market units. Rounding: none (exact integers). Bounds: a debit touches exactly the records it debits; `u ≤ Σ credited_r` or the call reverts; an account-market holds at most 16 records, so insertion walks at most 16 steps (`SPEC-BACK-016`). Example: list [NFT 42 (5,000,000,000), NFT 7 (4,000,000,000)]; a third position with 4,000,000,000 is inserted after NFT 7 (tie, higher ID). A debit of 6,000,000,000 takes all of NFT 42 (moved to the zero-credit list, stays open at 0) and 1,000,000,000 of NFT 7, which stays at the head with 3,000,000,000. If instead alice re-credits NFT 7 to 6,000,000,000 units, the list stays [NFT 42 (5,000,000,000), NFT 7 (6,000,000,000)] and a debit of 1,000,000,000 takes NFT 42 first, releasing `⌈10¹² × 4,000,000,000 / 5,000,000,000⌉ − ⌈10¹² × 3,000,000,000 / 5,000,000,000⌉ = 200,000,000,000` ($2,000) of NFT 42's basis, where the same units taken from NFT 7, pledged at $1.00 per whole token, would release $1,000. - `SPEC-BACK-007` — Debit equality (supply drop = debit; share drop = debit + Aave's fee). At `beginSeizure`: `sharesBefore = Spoke.getUserSuppliedShares(reserveId, proxy)`, `supplyBefore = vt.totalSupply()`. At `settleSeizure(proxy, vt, u)`: `sharesAfter = Spoke.getUserSuppliedShares(reserveId, proxy)`, `supplyAfter = vt.totalSupply()`; require `supplyBefore − supplyAfter == u` (else `SupplyDropMismatch(u, supplyBefore − supplyAfter)`) and `sharesBefore − sharesAfter ≥ u`, with no drop at all when `u = 0` (else `DebitMismatch`); `f = sharesBefore − sharesAfter − u` is the shares Aave's `liquidationFee` moved to its fee receiver inside the Hub, for which no vault token left the Hub; the account-market is reconciled against `sharesBefore − f`, so `f` (with any earlier shortfall) is debited into `pending.units` and its principal into the pending balance (`REQ-VAULT-176`), and then `Σ take_r = u = Σ units removed to LPVaultSwap`. The backing gap is unchanged: `(C − u − f) + (P + f) − (S − u) = C + P − S` (`MATH-UO-78a`). Why: Collateralization measures Aave's seizure and the Adapter's burn itself instead of trusting the upgradeable LPVaultSwap's count, so a non-conforming LPVaultSwap can neither debit the wrong account or amount nor release units for vault tokens it kept; with `Hub.getAddedAssets == Hub.getAddedShares` for the asset (checked at `beginSeizure`), Aave's shares to liquidate equal the units the Hub transferred. Units: Hub shares = vault-token base units = market units. Rounding: none. Bounds: shares are `uint120` in the Spoke; the subtractions are checked and revert on underflow (a supply or share rise between the calls fails the check). Example: alice's supply is 9,000,000,000 shares and the market's total supply 12,000,000,000 at `beginSeizure`; Aave seizes 1,575,000,000 to LPVaultSwap and the Adapter burns them; `sharesAfter = 7,425,000,000` and `supplyAfter = 10,425,000,000`, so both drops equal 1,575,000,000 and the check passes; a report of 1,600,000,000 units reverts `DebitMismatch`, and a liquidation that skipped the burn reverts `SupplyDropMismatch(1,575,000,000, 0)`. - `SPEC-BACK-009` — Per-market and per-account totals and the backing gap. `totalCredited[vt] += c` and `accountCredited[P][vt] += c` at pledge and `+= added` at re-credit; both `−= u` at `settleSeizure`, `−= shortfall` at reconciliation, `−= units` at a partial removal and a deleverage release, and `−= credited_r` at `release`; `pending[vt].units += shortfall` at reconciliation and `−= units` at `settlePending`. The Adapter mints `c` at pledge and `added` at re-credit, and burns the withdrawn units at release, partial removal and deleverage release; LPVaultSwap has the Adapter burn `u` before `settleSeizure` and `units` before `settlePending`. Define the backing gap `gap[vt] = totalCredited[vt] + pending[vt].units − vt.totalSupply()`. At the end of every transaction `totalCredited[vt] = Σ credited_r` over open records of `vt`, `accountCredited[P][vt] = Σ credited_r` over `P`'s open records of `vt`, and `gap[vt] ≥ 0` for any LPVaultSwap implementation, with `gap[vt] = 0` while every `burnSeized` is followed in the same call by the `settleSeizure` or `settlePending` that debits it. After every pledge, withdrawal, partial removal, re-credit and repay with collateral the Adapter requires `vt.totalSupply() ≤ totalCredited[vt] + pending[vt].units` and `gapAfter = gapBefore`, with `gapBefore` read before its first mint, burn or call into Collateralization (for a pledge, before `pledge`, since the record is written before the mint) and `gapAfter` after its last call into Collateralization (`BackingMismatch(totalSupply, totalCredited, pendingUnits)`, [2.6](06-aave-adapter.md)). Why: every unit in circulation is backed either by credited units in a custodied position or by market tokens held in the pending-settlement balance, and nothing else creates or destroys units; comparing the gap before and after proves the call's own mint or burn matched its record change, while a gap left by a non-conforming LPVaultSwap's unsettled `burnSeized` cannot block the market's pledges and exits (`Q-257`). A positive gap means more backing than units, never less. Units: market units. Rounding: none. Bounds: `Σ credited ≤` the market's supply bound `< 2^120` ([2.4](04-vault-tokens.md) `SPEC-UNIT-006`); the gap is computed as `totalCredited + pending.units − totalSupply` only after checking `totalSupply ≤ totalCredited + pending.units`, so it never underflows. Example: after alice's reconciled withdrawal of NFT 42 in the worked example below, `totalCredited = 4,000,000,000`, `pending.units = 1,575,000,000` and `totalSupply = 5,575,000,000`, a gap of 0. If instead a non-conforming LPVaultSwap had the Adapter burn the 1,575,000,000 seized units and never settled them, the gap is 1,575,000,000 from that moment; alice's withdrawal of NFT 7 then reads `gapBefore = 9,000,000,000 + 0 − 7,425,000,000 = 1,575,000,000` and `gapAfter = 3,425,000,000 + 1,575,000,000 − 3,425,000,000 = 1,575,000,000`, so it passes, as does every later pledge and withdrawal in the market. Checked by: MATH-UO-30, MATH-UO-33, QNT-03, QNT-37, HYP-01, TEST-241, PROP-065. (Source: Q-066, Q-224, Q-201, Q-254, Q-255, Q-257)
- `SPEC-BACK-010` — Live units cover credit. For every open record, `live_r = unitsOf(positionKey) ≥ credited_r`: pledge sets `credited_r = live_r`, an addition by anyone (an NFT's `increaseLiquidity`) raises `live_r` only, a re-credit raises `credited_r` to `live_r`, a seizure, reconciliation, partial removal or deleverage release lowers both by the units debited, and nothing else changes either. Why: every credited unit must be removable at seizure time. Units: market units. Rounding: none. Bounds: `uint120` for credit; live units are the AMM protocol's own type. Example: NFT 42 pledged at 5,000,000,000 receives a donation of 100,000,000 (live 5,100,000,000); reconciling 1,575,000,000 leaves credited 3,425,000,000 and live 3,525,000,000; a re-credit then makes both 3,525,000,000. Checked by: MATH-UO-30, QNT-05, HYP-02. (Source: Q-066, Q-067, Q-201, Q-254)
- `SPEC-BACK-011` — Reconciliation shortfall. `shortfall = accountCredited[P][vt] > S ? accountCredited[P][vt] − S : 0`, where `S` is the proxy's supplied shares before the current call's own movement: `Spoke.getUserSuppliedShares(reserveId, P) + units` in `release`, `releasePartial` and `releaseForDeleverage`, `Spoke.getUserSuppliedShares(reserveId, P)` in `releaseZeroCredit` and `recredit`, and `sharesBefore` in `settleSeizure`. The shortfall is debited per `SPEC-BACK-006` and its units removed per `SPEC-BACK-015` into `pending[vt]`. Why: proxies supply every minted unit and only withdraw them paired with `release`, so supplied shares fall below credited units only when Aave seized units that were never settled; the difference is exactly those units, and debiting them keeps the outstanding tokens backed while freeing the rest of the borrower's positions. A proxy's supply can never exceed its credited units, because vault tokens reach a proxy's Spoke position only by the Adapter's mint and supply on a pledge; the `max(…, 0)` form makes any other case a no-op. A liquidation by anyone other than LPVaultSwap seizes no units: if it would seize any, the vault token refuses the Hub's transfer and the call reverts, and a dust liquidation that seizes 0 units (a `debtToCover` whose collateral rounds to 0) succeeds on the Spoke but moves no supplied share, so it creates no shortfall and is never mistaken for an LPVaultSwap seizure. Units: market units (= shares while Hub shares equal assets). Rounding: none. Bounds: `shortfall ≤ accountCredited < 2^120`. Example: after an unsettled seizure of 1,575,000,000 units alice has `accountCredited = 9,000,000,000` and supply 7,425,000,000. Withdrawing NFT 42 with `units = 3,425,000,000` leaves supply 4,000,000,000, so `S = 7,425,000,000` and `shortfall = 1,575,000,000`. If an honest liquidation instead seizes 500,000,000 units first, `sharesBefore = 7,425,000,000` gives the same shortfall, reconciled before the 500,000,000 are debited. Checked by: QNT-03, QNT-07, QNT-15, QNT-35, QNT-T09, QNT-T26. (Source: Q-189, Q-201, Q-254, Q-255)
- `SPEC-BACK-012` — Pending settlement: burn check and payout. For `settlePending(vt, x)` with `0 < x ≤ p.units` (`p = pending[vt]`): require `vt.totalSupply() + x ≤ totalCredited[vt] + p.units` (else `SupplyDropMismatch(totalCredited[vt] + p.units − x, vt.totalSupply())`, the largest supply the check admits and the supply found); for each market token `i`, if `x == p.units`, `out_i = p.amounts[i]`, otherwise `out_i = mulDiv(p.amounts[i], x, p.units, Floor)`. Then `p.units −= x` and `p.amounts[i] −= out_i`. Why: after the call the supply must still be at most `totalCredited + p.units`, so market tokens never leave for units still in circulation: with a conforming LPVaultSwap the supply equalled `totalCredited + p.units` before the burn and the check proves the Adapter burned at least `x` units in this call; after an unsettled `burnSeized` the supply is already lower by the backing gap, and a settlement of `x` up to that gap retires pending units whose vault tokens no longer exist, which leaves every circulating unit backed (`Q-257`). Each settled unit takes its pro-rata share of every token removed for it, rounding down keeps the remaining units backed by at least their share, and the final settlement pays the exact remainder so no token is stranded. Units: `x` in vault-token base units; `out_i` in raw units of token `i`. Rounding: down, except the final settlement. Bounds: `p.amounts[i] < 2^128` per position summed over at most `2^120` units; `mulDiv` handles the 512-bit product. Example: with `totalCredited = 0`, `p = {units 1,575,000,000; amounts [1,259,999,999, 1,968,749,999]}` and the supply at 575,000,000 after the Adapter burned `x = 1,000,000,000`, the check `575,000,000 + 1,000,000,000 ≤ 0 + 1,575,000,000` passes; the call pays `out_0 = ⌊1,259,999,999 × 10^9 / 1,575,000,000⌋ = 799,999,999` and `out_1 = 1,249,999,999`, leaving `{575,000,000; [460,000,000, 718,750,000]}`; settling the remaining 575,000,000 pays exactly 460,000,000 and 718,750,000; a call for 575,000,000 made while the supply is still 575,000,000 without a burn fails `575,000,000 + 575,000,000 ≤ 575,000,000` and reverts `SupplyDropMismatch(0, 575,000,000)`. Checked by: QNT-03, QNT-37, QNT-T09. (Source: Q-124, Q-201, Q-224, Q-257, Q-297)
- `SPEC-BACK-013` — Charge basis after a re-credit. For a record with `u_b = credited` after reconciliation and `c_b = currentUsdCharge` (equal to `mulDiv(V0, u_b, U0, Ceil)` by `SPEC-BACK-005`), a re-credit of `added` units that the RiskModule charges `usdCharge` sets `U0' = u_b + added`, `C0' = c_b + usdCharge`, `originalUnits = U0'`, `originalUsdCharge = currentUsdCharge = C0'` and `credited = U0'`; later exits release per `SPEC-BACK-005` on the new basis, `remainingCharge = mulDiv(C0', remainingUnits, U0', Ceil)`. Why: the record's booked charge after the re-credit is what its remaining units carried plus what the added units cost, and spreading it over all credited units keeps the exit rule's two properties: any split of later exits releases exactly `C0'` in total, and the record's last exit leaves 0 (`remainingCharge = 0` at `remainingUnits = 0`). Keeping the old basis would charge the new units at the old pledge's price and release more than was booked when `U0' > U0`, driving counters to saturation. Units: market units; USD 8 decimals. Rounding: none in the rebase (exact sums); exits round up. Bounds: `U0' ≤ MAX_CREDITED_UNITS < 2^120` (else `LiquidityAboveBound`); `C0' < 2^64` (else `InvalidCharge`). Example: NFT 42 (`U0 = 5,000,000,000`, `V0 = 1,000,000,000,000`) after a partial removal of 1,000,000,000 has `u_b = 4,000,000,000` and `c_b = 800,000,000,000`; a re-credit of 1,500,000,000 at `price8 = 200,000,000` charges `⌈1,500,000,000 × 200,000,000 / 10^6⌉ = 300,000,000,000`, so `U0' = 5,500,000,000` and `C0' = 1,100,000,000,000` ($11,000). A later partial removal of 2,750,000,000 leaves `⌈1.1 × 10^12 × 2,750,000,000 / 5,500,000,000⌉ = 550,000,000,000` and releases $5,500, and withdrawing the rest releases the last $5,500: over the record's life $2,000 + $5,500 + $5,500 = $13,000 left the counters, exactly the $10,000 booked at pledge plus the $3,000 of the re-credit. Checked by: MATH-UO-39a, MATH-UO-39b, MATH-UO-39d, TEST-238. (Source: Q-174, Q-254)
- `SPEC-BACK-014` — Rescue units. For a pledge or re-credit of `L` units whose charge is `(usdCharge, sliceUsd, rescue, rescueUnits)`, the RiskModule computes `rescueUnits = L` when the pledge needed a rescue exemption (`rescue = true`, `Q-252`), else `rescueUnits = mulDiv(L, sliceUsd, usdCharge, Ceil)` when `sliceUsd > 0`, else 0 ([2.7](07-risk-module.md)); Collateralization requires `rescueUnits ≤ L` (and `= L` when `rescue`) and sets `record.rescueUnits += rescueUnits`. After every debit of the record, `record.rescueUnits = min(record.rescueUnits, record.credited)`. `accountRescueUnits[P][vt] = Σ record.rescueUnits` over `P`'s open records of `vt`, updated by the same deltas. Why: the slice part of a charge and the whole of an exempted pledge exist only to rescue an indebted account, so their units must not support new debt (`Q-253`); rounding up excludes at least the slice's share, and keeping rescue units when other units leave (capping only at `credited`) is the conservative choice for the borrow-side check. Units: market units. Rounding: up. Bounds: `rescueUnits ≤ credited < 2^120`; `mulDiv` handles the product. - `SPEC-BACK-015` — Unwind accounting for `n` tokens. For a removal of `take` units from a record, the LPVault returns `a[i]` (the AMM protocol's principal amounts, rounded down by the AMM protocol, fees excluded) and pays them to the recipient, which receives `r[i] ≤ a[i]` (less only when token `i` charges a transfer fee). Every later step uses the received amount: LPVaultSwap measures its own balance deltas, Collateralization books `min(r[i], a[i])` into `pending[vt].amounts[i]` (`REQ-VAULT-135`) and returns the depositor's measured deltas from `releasePartial`. Why: measuring receipts keeps settlement working at the reduced amounts if an issuer switches on a transfer fee, and capping at `a[i]` keeps a token that over-delivers from inflating the pending balance; the oracle-based floors of LPVaultSwap still compare against full value. Units: raw units of each market token. Rounding: the AMM protocol's own, always down on removal (less than one raw unit per token per removal). Bounds: `n ≤ 8`; each `a[i] < 2^128`. - `SPEC-BACK-016` — Record bound and packing. `openRecordCount[P][vt] ≤ R = 16`; worst-case seizure gas `G = G_base + R · g_record`, with `G_base ≈ 1,310,000` (liquidation call, flash loan, burn, settlement legs) and `g_record ≈ 240,000` per debited record for the launch integrations (one record: 1,550,000; the gas model `G(R)` of [1.5](../01-architecture/05-parameters.md) `SPEC-CHAIN-001`, which sets no minimum debt, `Q-381`), so `G ≈ 5,150,000 ≤ 8,000,000`, under half of the 16,777,216 per-transaction gas cap; the pledge-side insertion walk is at most `R` steps of two cold slots (about 4,200 gas each). A `BackingRecord` occupies 5 storage slots and carries no list links; the seizure list is a separate array of at most 16 record IDs. Why: Aave's dust rule can force a seizure of every record in one transaction, so the bound must hold whatever `debtToCover` a keeper chooses (`AMMN-GAS-01`); packing the record from 10 slots (8 fields plus 2 link mappings) to 5 saves about `5 × 22,100 = 110,500` gas per pledge, less the one array slot the list entry writes (`AMMN-GAS-15`). Units: gas; slots. Rounding: none. Bounds: record IDs below `2^48` (`2.8 × 10^14` pledges), `uint40` timestamps, `uint64` charges (`REQ-VAULT-015`), `uint128` unit fields bounded by `2^120 − 1`, `uint32` `integrationId` and `marketId`. Example: an account of 16 records of $10 each, all seized in its last chunk, costs about 5,150,000 gas on Ethereum or Base; a seventeenth pledge reverts `TooManyRecords`. Checked by: a fork gas snapshot of a 16-record seizure on Ethereum and on Base, at most 8,000,000 gas. (Source: Q-294, Q-303)

### Worked example: reconcile on exit

Alice pledges NFT 42 (record 1, 5,000,000,000 units, charge $10,000) and NFT 7 (record 2, 4,000,000,000 units, charge $8,000) in the USDC/USDT market through the Adapter and borrows GHO. USDT depegs to $0.64, so one whole vault token is worth $1.60 and her account becomes liquidatable. A non-conforming LPVaultSwap implementation calls `Spoke.liquidationCall` for 2,400 GHO of debt, receives 1,575,000,000 units ($2,520 at a 5% bonus), never has them burned and never calls Collateralization.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Unsettled seizure | 7,425,000,000 | 9,000,000,000 | 5,000,000,000 / 4,000,000,000 | 0; 0; 0 | 9,000,000,000 | 1,575,000,000 |

| Alice withdraws NFT 7 (shortfall 0) | 0 | 0 | closed / closed | 1,575,000,000; 1,259,999,999; 1,968,749,999 | 1,575,000,000 | 1,575,000,000 |
| A conforming LPVaultSwap is installed; a keeper calls `LPVaultSwap.settlePending(marketId)`, which has the Adapter burn 1,575,000,000 and calls `settlePending(vt, 1,575,000,000)` | 0 | 0 | closed / closed | 0; 0; 0 | 0 | 0 |

In the third step the Adapter has the proxy withdraw 3,425,000,000 units and burns them, then calls `release`, which computes `S = 4,000,000,000 + 3,425,000,000 = 7,425,000,000` and `shortfall = 1,575,000,000` (`SPEC-BACK-011`), debits it from record 1 at the head of the list (5,000,000,000 → 3,425,000,000), releases $3,150 of exposure (`SPEC-BACK-005`), has the LPVault remove 1,575,000,000 units and pay 1,259,999,999 USDC and 1,968,749,999 USDT raw units to Collateralization's pending balance (`SPEC-BACK-015`), emits `RecordDebited` and `SeizureReconciled`, then debits record 1's remaining 3,425,000,000 units, closes it and has the LPVault return NFT 42 with its remaining 3,425,000,000 liquidity and all fees. The Adapter's backing check then reads a gap of `4,000,000,000 + 1,575,000,000 − 5,575,000,000 = 0`, the same as before the withdrawal. Had she withdrawn NFT 7 first, `previewRelease(7)` would return 4,000,000,000 and the same shortfall would still come from record 1. Every row satisfies `totalCredited + pending.units = totalSupply` (a gap of 0, since this LPVaultSwap never had the units burned) and, after each exit, supply `= accountCredited`. In the last step LPVaultSwap reads `getPending(vt)` before taking the lock (`REQ-VAULT-145`); after the burn the supply is 0, so `0 + 1,575,000,000 ≤ 0 + 1,575,000,000` passes, and Collateralization sends the 1,259,999,999 USDC and 1,968,749,999 USDT raw units to LPVaultSwap (`SPEC-BACK-012`), which converts them to about 2,520 GHO, the debt asset repaid in this example, for the (Stable LP Hub, GHO) First-Loss Reserve (`Q-211`).

## Permissions

| Function | Allowed caller | How the permission is set |
|---|---|---|
| `pledge`, `release`, `releasePartial`, `recredit`, `releaseForDeleverage`, `releaseZeroCredit`, `collectFees` | AaveAdapter (`ADAPTER`), while it holds the Lock; `recredit`, `releaseZeroCredit` and `collectFees` only for the record's depositor | Constructor immutable; depositor fixed in the record at pledge |
| `beginSeizure`, `settleSeizure`, `settlePending` | LPVaultSwap (`LP_VAULT_SWAP`), while it holds the Lock | Constructor immutable |
| Views | Anyone while the Lock is free; only the current holder while it is held; `getAccountRescueUnits` and `poolUnits` anyone while the Lock is free or held by the AaveAdapter, nobody while LPVaultSwap holds it | `LOCK.holder()` |

No role can change a record, a total, a counter or an immutable, move the pending-settlement balance outside `settlePending`, or upgrade Collateralization. The Adapter and LPVaultSwap are upgradeable behind the chain's timelock at the normal delay ([2.15](15-governance-and-timelock.md)); the custody limits of `REQ-COLL-005` hold under any implementation of either.

Indexers key collateral by `chainId + Collateralization + recordId` and positions by `chainId + integrationId + positionKey` (`Q-076`, `Q-292`).

## Restrictions

What Collateralization must never allow is stated in the rules above: what it may credit and debit (`REQ-VAULT-042`, `REQ-VAULT-043`, `REQ-VAULT-024`, `REQ-VAULT-031`), what it may hold (`REQ-COLL-004`, `REQ-COLL-009`), the limits that hold under any Adapter (`REQ-COLL-005`), and that exits read no pause, breaker, freeze or policy (`REQ-COLL-008`, `REQ-VAULT-037`).
