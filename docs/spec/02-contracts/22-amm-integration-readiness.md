# 2.22 AMM Integration Readiness

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-10
>
> **Requirement prefix:** `REQ-AMM-V2-NNN`, `REQ-AMM-CURVE-NNN`, `REQ-AMM-BAL-NNN`, `REQ-AMM-V4-NNN`, `REQ-READY-NNN` (token listing rules that apply to every AMM protocol) · **Math prefix:** `SPEC-AMM-V2-NNN`, `SPEC-AMM-CURVE-NNN`, `SPEC-AMM-BAL-NNN`, `SPEC-AMM-V4-NNN`

## Purpose

The AMM-neutral core ([2.19](19-amm-integration-interface.md)) supports four AMM protocols beyond the launch integrations: Uniswap v2, Curve StableSwap-NG, Balancer weighted and stable pools, and Uniswap v4. For each one this page fixes the pricing model, the custody shape and the guards its integration must pass before its first market is listed. No market of these AMM protocols is listed until its integration page, its fork tests and its gas measurement exist (`REQ-AMM-CORE-015`, `REQ-AMM-V2-008`).

## Summary

| | Uniswap v2 | Curve StableSwap-NG | Balancer weighted / stable | Uniswap v4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pricing model | `2·√(r0·r1·p0·p1)` per effective supply | `min_i(p_i) · D` per supply | Weighted invariant per actual supply; stable pools as Curve | Concentrated liquidity, as Uniswap v3 |
| `exact` | Yes | No (lower bound) | Weighted yes; stable no | Yes |
| `pool` / `poolId` | Pair address | Pool address | `poolId` | `PoolId` |
| `positionKey`, units | `keccak256(marketId, depositor, nonce)`, LP base units | Same, LP base units | Same, BPT base units | PositionManager `tokenId` as `bytes32`, liquidity |
| Fees separable | No (compound) | No (compound) | No (compound) | Yes |
| Consistency price | Cumulative prices | `price_oracle()` EMA | None (feed-only check) | None without an oracle hook |
| Reentrancy guard before a read | Private lock; read `getReserves` outside a swap | A state-changing, reentrancy-locked pool call | `ensureNotInVaultContext` | PoolManager locked (`exttload`) |
| Extra guards | `getReserves`, pending `feeTo` supply, spot within 1%, minimum supply | No rate token without a capped feed; reverting `D` is a price failure | `getActualSupply`-style supply | Hook allowlist with no remove-liquidity hooks or deltas; no subscriber |

Every one of them also needs the per-block quote-change cap (`REQ-AMM-CORE-014`).

## Common custody shape for fungible LP shares

Uniswap v2, Curve and Balancer positions are ERC-20 LP shares with no per-position identity. Their LPVault holds the LP tokens itself and books one custody entry per deposit: `positionKey = keccak256(abi.encode(marketId, depositor, nonce))`, units = LP base units actually received (measured by balance delta), shape = whole pool (`shape = 0`). `receivePosition` pulls the amount in `position` from the owner; `topUpPosition` adds to the same entry (`REQ-AMM-CORE-011`); `returnPosition` sends the entry's LP tokens back; `unwind` and `releaseToDepositor` redeem exactly `units` LP pro rata. Fees compound into the pool, so `capabilities().feeMode == RolledIn`, `collectFees` reverts `FeesRolledIn`, and each unit's value grows with the fees; the depositor receives them when the position returns. `unitsOf` is the entry's LP balance, which never changes except by Avana's own removals and top-ups.

Every readiness section below names the four parts [2.10](10-lp-oracle.md) `REQ-ORACLE-103` requires of the AMM protocol's pricing module: its `composition(units, prices)`, which is the same function as the integration's `composition` ([2.19](19-amm-integration-interface.md) `REQ-AMM-CORE-012`); its consistency read (`consistencyDeviation`, or a feed-only check where the protocol has no time-weighted price); its pool-lock read (`checkReadSafe`, the `poolLocked()` probe: for Curve and Balancer a held reentrancy lock fails the market with reason bit 17 `POOL_REENTRANCY_LOCKED`); and its per-block quote-change cap (`maxQuoteChangeBps`, `REQ-AMM-CORE-014`).

## Uniswap v2

- `REQ-AMM-V2-001` — The v2 integration MUST read reserves only through `getReserves()`, never `balanceOf(pair)`. Why: a donation raises `balanceOf` at once and `skim` takes it back at no cost, so a `balanceOf` quote doubles for free, while `getReserves` moves only on `sync`, `mint`, `burn` or `swap`. Checked by: MATH-AMM-V2-PRICE-06, AMM-B7, HYP-AMM-09. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V2-002` — The supply in every quote MUST be the effective supply `totalSupply + pendingFeeLiquidity`, where `pendingFeeLiquidity` is what `_mintFee` would mint now when `feeTo != address(0)`: `totalSupply · (√k − √kLast) / (5·√k + √kLast)`. Why: the protocol-fee mint pending since `kLast` is missing from `totalSupply`, which overstates each LP unit until the next mint or burn (0.814% after 20 round trips of 5M in a test pool, bound `(√(k/kLast) − 1)/6`). Checked by: MATH-AMM-V2-PRICE-05, MATH-AMM-V2-PRICE-06, AMM-B5a, AMM-B5b, AMM-B10, HYP-AMM-08. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V2-003` — A v2 market MUST be refused as `Cached` for borrowing whenever the pair's spot price differs from the oracle ratio by more than 1%. Why: when the pool sits off the oracle price, a synced donation raises the fair value by more than its cost (1.657× at a 4:1 imbalance); within 1% the gain is at most 1.005× the donation. Checked by: MATH-AMM-V2-PRICE-03, AMM-B3a, AMM-B3b, AMM-B3c. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V2-004` — A v2 market MUST NOT be listed unless `totalSupply ≥ 10^6 × MINIMUM_LIQUIDITY` (10^9 LP base units) and the pool's value at oracle prices is at least the market's listed minimum pool value. Why: at low supply one LP unit carries a large rounding share, and a depositor of 1.9 LP units' worth received 1 LP unit and lost 47.3% in a test pool. Checked by: MATH-AMM-V2-PRICE-06, AMM-B9. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V2-005` — The per-block quote-change cap (`REQ-AMM-CORE-014`) MUST be at most 1% for v2 markets. Why: one swap raises `k`, and so the fair per-LP quote, by less than `1/√0.997 − 1 = 0.1503%`; a cap of a few swaps' headroom admits normal fee growth and serves larger moves as `Cached`. Checked by: MATH-AMM-V2-PRICE-02, MATH-AMM-V2-PRICE-06, AMM-B2a, AMM-B2b, AMM-B11, HYP-AMM-07. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V2-006` — The v2 consistency check MUST use the pair's cumulative prices over the market's TWAP window; the pair's private lock means reserves cannot be read mid-swap, and `getReserves` read outside a swap is consistent. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V2-007` — The v2 unwind MUST transfer exactly `units` LP to the pair and call `burn(recipient)`, which pays both tokens pro rata; a pair that holds native ETH is never listed (v2 pairs hold WETH). (Source: Q-297)
- `REQ-AMM-V2-008` — No market of Uniswap v2, Curve, Balancer or Uniswap v4 MUST be listed until its integration page states every rule of its interface functions, fork tests of every guard on this page pass on the market's chain, and its seizure gas meets `REQ-AMM-CORE-015`. (Source: Q-291, Q-302)
- `SPEC-AMM-V2-001` — **Constant-product composition.** With reserves `(r0, r1)` from `getReserves()` in raw units, prices `(p0, p1)` per raw unit, effective supply `S = totalSupply + pendingFeeLiquidity` and `u` units: the fair reserves at the oracle ratio are `r0* = √(k · p1 / p0)`, `r1* = √(k · p0 / p1)` with `k = r0 · r1`, and `composition` returns `a_i = ⌊r_i* · u / S⌋ − 1`. The value is `2·√(k·p0·p1) · u / S`. Why: `k` cannot fall on a swap and the fair reserves are the minimum of `r0·p0 + r1·p1` over every reserve pair with the same `k`, so moving the pool only makes the redeemable value exceed the quote. Units: raw token units. Rounding: down, then −1. Bounds: `k < 2^224` for `uint112` reserves; the integer square root is floored. Example: a 1,000,000 USDC / 500 WETH pool with WETH at $2,000 quotes `2·√(10^6 · 500 · 1 · 2,000) = $2,000,000`; a flash-funded 9,000,000 USDC swap makes a spot-reserve quote ×5.05 ($10.1M), while this quote moves +0.135%. Checked by: MATH-AMM-V2-PRICE-01, MATH-AMM-V2-PRICE-02, MATH-AMM-V2-PRICE-03, MATH-AMM-V2-PRICE-04, MATH-AMM-V2-PRICE-06, AMM-B1a, AMM-B1b, AMM-B2c, AMM-B2d, AMM-B3d, AMM-B4a, AMM-B4b, AMM-B6, HYP-AMM-06. (Source: Q-302)

## Curve StableSwap-NG

- `REQ-AMM-CURVE-001` — The Curve quote MUST be `min_i(p_i) · D / supply`, returned with `exact = false`, and never a mean or maximum price. Why: StableSwap has no closed-form composition at an oracle price; since `Σ x_i ≥ D` for every amplification and imbalance, valuing `D` at the cheapest coin's price is a lower bound of the redeemable value, while a mean or maximum price can exceed it. Checked by: MATH-AMM-CURVE-PRICE-01, AMM-C2a, AMM-C2b, AMM-C2c, AMM-C2d, HYP-AMM-10. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-CURVE-002` — Before every read of `D`, `get_virtual_price()` or `totalSupply`, the integration MUST call a state-changing, reentrancy-locked function of the pool chosen and fork-tested at listing for that pool version (`withdraw_admin_fees()` or `remove_liquidity(0, [0, …])`), and treat a revert as `PoolLocked`, failing closed. Why: a pool that sends native ETH or calls a token hook inside `remove_liquidity` before its balances settle lets the receiver read a stale `D` and supply and borrow against a high quote (`AMMN-RT-07`); Avana's Lock cannot see the pool's own lock. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-CURVE-003` — A reverting `get_D` or `get_virtual_price` read MUST count as a price failure for the market. Why: at extreme imbalance (about 10^5:1 with `A = 10`) the integer `get_D` iteration does not converge and the pool reverts. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-CURVE-004` — A Curve market MUST NOT list an ERC-4626 or rate-oracle coin unless its rate comes from an approved, capped rate feed of the LPOracle (`REQ-AMM-CORE-004`). Why: a donation to the underlying vault raises the stored rate, and `D` and the LP value jump inside one transaction. (Source: Q-297, Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-CURVE-005` — A Curve pool that pays native ETH MUST have its integration wrap the ETH into WETH inside the unwind and accept ETH only while the Lock is held and only from that pool; a pool of more than 8 coins is never listed, and a pool whose 16-record seizure exceeds the gas limit of `REQ-AMM-CORE-015` is listed only with a single-token exit (`remove_liquidity_one_coin`) whose one output carries LPVaultSwap's oracle-derived minimum (`AMMN-GAS-13`). (Source: Q-297)
- `REQ-AMM-CURVE-006` — The per-block quote-change cap MUST apply. Why: one imbalancing swap of 5,000,000 into a 1,000,000/1,000,000 pool (`A = 200`, 4 bps, coin off peg ×2) raises the virtual price up to ×1.27; the quote gain ($531,000) is far below the swapper's loss ($4.0M), but the cap keeps even that one-block move away from borrowing power. A 10% one-coin donation raises the virtual price ×1.05. Checked by: MATH-AMM-CURVE-PRICE-02, AMM-C3, AMM-C4, AMM-C5. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-CURVE-007` — The Curve consistency check MUST use the pool's `price_oracle()` EMA. (Source: Q-302)
- `SPEC-AMM-CURVE-001` — **StableSwap lower bound.** `value = mulDiv(min_i(p_i), D · u, supply · 10^18, Floor)` with `D` and `supply` in 18-decimal pool units and `p_i` per whole coin (8 decimals); `composition` returns the whole amount `⌊D · u / supply⌋ − 1`, rescaled to the cheapest coin's decimals, in that coin and 0 in the others, so `Σ a_i · p_i` equals the bound. Why: representing the bound as an all-cheapest-coin composition keeps the core's single value formula. Units: raw coin units. Rounding: down, then −1. Bounds: `D · u < 2^256` for `D < 2^128`. Example: a 1,000,000 USDC / 1,000,000 USDT pool (`D ≈ 2,000,000`) with USDT at $0.99 quotes `0.99 × 2,000,000 = $1,980,000` against a redeemable $1,990,000. Checked by: MATH-AMM-CURVE-PRICE-01, MATH-AMM-CURVE-PRICE-02, AMM-C2c, AMM-C4, HYP-AMM-10. (Source: Q-302)

## Balancer weighted and stable pools

- `REQ-AMM-BAL-001` — A Balancer market MUST be keyed by `poolId` (`bytes32`, with `pool = address(0)`, `capabilities().poolKeying == Id`), with its tokens and balances read from the Balancer vault; there is no pool address per market. (Source: Q-292)
- `REQ-AMM-BAL-002` — Before every read, the integration MUST call the vault reentrancy check (`VaultReentrancyLib.ensureNotInVaultContext`) and fail closed if the vault is mid-join or mid-exit (`AMMN-RT-07`). (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-BAL-003` — The supply in every quote MUST be the actual supply including pending protocol-fee BPT and excluding pre-minted BPT (`getActualSupply`-style); a raw `totalSupply` is never used. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-BAL-004` — Weighted pools have no consistency price; their LPOracle implementation MUST use a feed-only consistency check (the second oracle source of [2.10](10-lp-oracle.md)). Stable pools follow `REQ-AMM-CURVE-001` and `REQ-AMM-CURVE-003` with the pool's invariant as `D`. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-BAL-005` — The per-block quote-change cap MUST apply; one swap into token `i` grows the weighted invariant by at most `(1 − fee)^(−w_i)` (+0.2407% for the 80% token at a 0.3% fee). Rate tokens, native ETH, token count and single-token exits follow `REQ-AMM-CURVE-004` and `REQ-AMM-CURVE-005`. Checked by: MATH-AMM-BAL-PRICE-01, MATH-AMM-BAL-PRICE-02, AMM-C1c, AMM-C6, HYP-AMM-11. (Source: Q-297, Q-302)
- `SPEC-AMM-BAL-001` — **Weighted composition.** With balances `b_i`, normalized weights `w_i` (`Σ w_i = 1`), prices `p_i` and actual supply `S`: `k = ∏ b_i^{w_i}`, fair value `V = k · ∏ (p_i / w_i)^{w_i}`, fair balances `b_i* = w_i · V / p_i`, and `composition` returns `a_i = ⌊b_i* · u / S⌋ − 1`. Why: at the oracle prices an arbitraged weighted pool holds value share `w_i` in token `i`, and the invariant cannot fall on a swap, so the fair value is at most the redeemable value at every pool state. Units: raw token units. Rounding: down, then −1; the fractional powers use Balancer's `LogExpMath` with the error bound rounded against the quote. Bounds: 18-decimal fixed point. Example: an 80/20 pool of 800,000 USDC and 100 WETH with WETH at $2,000 has `V = $1,000,000`; if the WETH price moves to $2,200 before arbitrage, `V = $1,019,244.88` while the pool's balances are worth $1,020,000. Checked by: MATH-AMM-BAL-PRICE-01, AMM-C1a, AMM-C1b, AMM-C1d, HYP-AMM-11. (Source: Q-302)

## Uniswap v4

- `REQ-AMM-V4-001` — A v4 market MUST NOT be listed unless its `PoolKey.hooks` is `address(0)` or a hook on Avana's hook allowlist whose address permission bits clear every remove-liquidity flag: `uint160(hooks) & REMOVE_MASK == 0` with `REMOVE_MASK = BEFORE_REMOVE_LIQUIDITY (1 << 9) | AFTER_REMOVE_LIQUIDITY (1 << 8) | AFTER_REMOVE_LIQUIDITY_RETURNS_DELTA (1 << 0)`, checked on-chain at `bindMarket` and again at every `receivePosition`. An allowlisted hook MUST be immutable, with its code hash pinned in the market and its removal gas measured. Why: a hook runs inside every removal, so it can revert (every position of the pool becomes unliquidatable), burn gas past the per-transaction cap, or keep part of the removed tokens through its returned delta; a 1% delta leaves 9,876 realizable against an oracle value of 9,975, and a 100% delta leaves 0 (`AMMN-RT-06`, `AMMN-GAS-03`). Example: a hook at an address ending `…0300` sets bits 8 and 9 and is refused; one ending `…0080` passes the mask. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V4-002` — `receivePosition` MUST refuse a position whose `PositionInfo` has `hasSubscriber` set (`SubscribedPosition`), and the LPVault MUST never call `subscribe`. Why: a subscriber's `notifyModifyLiquidity` runs on every liquidity change without a try/catch and can revert the removal. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V4-003` — Every v4 quote and custody read MUST require the PoolManager to be locked (not inside an `unlock` callback), read through `exttload`, and fail closed otherwise. Liquidity is read from the PoolManager, not the PositionManager. (Source: Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V4-004` — The v4 unwind MUST run `modifyLiquidities` inside the PoolManager's `unlock`, take only the principal of the removal, and wrap a native-ETH currency into WETH (`REQ-AMM-CORE-004`); any fee the liquidity change pays out is sent to the depositor of record in the same call. v4 pools used as swap routes MUST be hook-free. (Source: Q-297, Q-302)
- `REQ-AMM-V4-005` — v4 pools have no built-in TWAP; the LPOracle implementation MUST use a feed-only consistency check unless the pool's allowlisted hook is an oracle hook, and the per-block quote-change cap applies. (Source: Q-302)
- `SPEC-AMM-V4-001` — **v4 composition.** Identical to `SPEC-AMM-V3-001` over the position's `(tickLower, tickUpper)` and the `PoolKey`'s currencies, with the market's exact range checked per `SPEC-BACK-001`. Why: v4 pools use the v3 tick and liquidity math; only the custody and hook surface differ. Units, rounding, bounds and example as `SPEC-AMM-V3-001`. Checked by: AMM-A1a, AMM-A3f, AMM-A4a, AMM-A5a, HYP-AMM-01, HYP-AMM-05. (Source: Q-302)

## Tokenized stocks

A tokenized stock is a pool token whose price follows a share traded on an exchange with market hours. Its feed stops updating while that exchange is closed, while the pool keeps trading around the clock; a split or dividend can change the token's balances or its feed on a different block from the pool; and the issuer can restrict which addresses may hold the token. None is listed at launch, which has one Stable LP Hub on Ethereum and one on Base; the rule below is what a Volatile LP Hub listing of such a token must meet.

- `REQ-READY-001` — A pool token whose reference price comes from a market-hours feed MUST NOT be listed, in any market of any AMM protocol, unless all three of these hold:
  1. **Closed-market staleness.** The token's feed configuration marks it as market-hours, and while the reference market is closed (the feed's own market-status flag, or an age past its heartbeat inside the published closed window) new borrows, withdraw-with-debt and pledges that open a new account stop for every market holding the token, exactly as for a stale feed ([2.10](10-lp-oracle.md)); liquidation keeps running and values the token at its stress price, so a weekend gap can neither open new debt on a stale price nor block a liquidation.
  2. **Corporate actions.** A split, reverse split, dividend or other corporate action announced by the issuer pauses new activity in every market holding the token (a market breaker trip, [2.8](08-market-registry.md) `tripMarketBreaker`) from before the action's effective time until the feed and the pool have both been re-checked against each other at the normal delay; exits, top-ups and liquidation continue throughout.
  3. **Holder allowlist.** When the issuer restricts holders, its allowlist MUST include, before listing, the AMM protocol's LPVault, LPVaultSwap and every contract on the market's swap routes that holds the token during a swap; the listing batch checks each with the issuer's own allowlist view and reverts if any is missing, so the issuer cannot refuse the unwind or the swap of a seized position.

  Why: a feed that is valid but frozen over a weekend lets a borrower draw against a price the pool has already left; a split that the pool sees before the feed (or the reverse) moves the quote by the split ratio for a block; and a token that LPVaultSwap cannot hold turns every liquidation of the market into a revert. Example: a token priced at $200 by a feed that stops at Friday 16:00 while the pool trades at $180 on Saturday: without rule 1 a $10,000 position borrows against $200; with it no new borrow runs until the feed resumes, and a liquidation on Saturday values the token at its stress price. (Source: Q-363, Q-365)

## Failure Conditions

| ID | Condition | Behavior (revert / pause / fallback) |
|---|---|---|
| `REQ-AMM-V2-009` | A v2 quote read while the spot price is more than 1% from the oracle ratio, or after a per-block move above the cap | The market is served as `Cached`; borrowing against it stops until a later block confirms (Source: Q-302) |
| `REQ-AMM-CURVE-008` | The pool's reentrancy probe reverts, or `get_D` does not converge | `PoolLocked` or a price failure; no quote is served (Source: Q-302) |
| `REQ-AMM-BAL-006` | `ensureNotInVaultContext` reverts | `PoolLocked`; no quote is served (Source: Q-302) |
| `REQ-AMM-V4-006` | A hook with a remove-liquidity bit, a non-allowlisted hook, a hook whose code hash changed, or a position with a subscriber | `bindMarket` reverts `HookNotAllowed`; `receivePosition` reverts `HookNotAllowed` or `SubscribedPosition` (Source: Q-302) |
