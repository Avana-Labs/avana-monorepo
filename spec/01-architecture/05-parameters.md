# 1.5 Protocol Parameters

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12

Every tunable value and protocol constant in Avana, per chain, with its unit, the bounds enforced on-chain, its value at launch, where it is stored, and who can change it with what delay. Other pages refer to parameters by these names.

Every economic or risk value below carries its number marked "proposed default; final value set in the listing ARFC" (or the funding ARFC for credit lines and first-loss coverage): Avana proposes the number, and the Aave governance proposal that lists the market or funds the line, prepared with the Aave V4 risk service providers, sets the final value. Engineering constants (gas limits, buffer sizes, `MAX_*` and other implementation bounds) are fixed values (`Q-394`).

## Change classes

Every privileged change to an Avana contract is an operation of its chain's AvanaTimelock ([2.15](../02-contracts/15-governance-and-timelock.md)): a proposer (the governance multisig or the Aave governance executor) schedules it, it waits its delay, and anyone executes it once ready and before `GRACE_PERIOD` ends. The setters themselves require `APPLICATION_ADMIN` (upgradeable contracts) or `msg.sender == TIMELOCK` (immutable contracts), both held only by the timelock.

- `SPEC-CHAIN-001` — Liquidation gas model per chain. Formula: `G(R) = G_base + R × g_record` gas for one liquidation that seizes `R` records, funded through the flash liquidation helper (a caller funding from its own balance uses less), where `G_base` and `g_record` are measured on a fork of the chain for each AMM integration (table above). Two uses. (1) Record cap: `G(MAX_RECORDS_PER_ACCOUNT_MARKET) ≤ gasLimitTx / 2`, with `gasLimitTx` the per-transaction gas limit, so one liquidation of the largest account always fits in one transaction with half the limit to spare. (2) Avana's keeper gas budget for a period: `gasBudget = N × G(R̄) × gasPrice + N × l1DataFee`, with `N` the expected number of liquidations, `R̄` the expected records per liquidation (at most 16), `gasPrice` the gas price Avana budgets for and `l1DataFee` the L1 data fee per liquidation on an L2 (0 on Ethereum). Units: gas; wei per gas; wei. Rounding: measured gas rounds up to the next 10,000; the budget rounds up. Why: Avana has no minimum debt; its keeper, the committed operator on each chain, liquidates every account below health factor 1 whatever its size and pays the gas as Avana's operating cost, and `liquidate` is open to every other address beside it, so liquidation never depends on a keeper making a profit (`Q-381`, `Q-404`). The one gas limit that can make an account unliquidatable is the per-transaction limit, because Aave's dust rule can force a seizure of every record of an account in one call; the record cap bounds it. Worked example, Ethereum and Base: `G(1) = 1,310,000 + 240,000 = 1,550,000`; `G(16) = 1,310,000 + 16 × 240,000 = 5,150,000 ≤ 8,388,608 = 16,777,216 / 2`. The largest cap that fits is `⌊(8,388,608 − 1,310,000) / 240,000⌋ = 29` records, so 16 leaves room for `g_record` to be re-measured up to `⌊(8,388,608 − 1,310,000) / 16⌋ = 442,413` gas per record. A month of 200 liquidations at the worst case of 16 records needs `200 × 5,150,000 = 1,030,000,000` gas, which at a budgeted gas price of 5 gwei is 5.15 ETH. Checked by: MATH-CH-01. (Source: Q-287, Q-381)

## Circuit breakers

Avana's breakers stop new activity only (`Q-399`). Repayment, debt-free exits, rescue top-ups, repay with collateral and eligible liquidations stay available through every breaker. Liquidations still need a valid price, Aave eligibility and executable settlement, and an Aave reserve pause can independently block repay or liquidation.

| Trigger | Setting | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Oracle or stablecoin band breach | The existing oracle and peg checks (price states, debt-price gate, `usdBandLow` / `usdBandHigh`, issuer probe) | Block new borrowing and collateral withdrawals that leave debt |
| Price drop | 3 % in 2 hours | Alert only (no on-chain trip) |
| Avana's combined share of a pool | Above 25 % across all borrowers and Spokes on the chain | Alert only; a per-pool cap applies only where its listing sets one: above `poolShareMaxBpsOf(poolKey)` (default 10,000 bps, off), block new borrowing and pledges that increase pool exposure (`Q-403`) |
| Collateral seized | Above 20 % of a market in 1 hour | Alert only |
| Swap-route failures | 3 in 1 hour | Alert. A guardian or the timelock freezes new borrowing (`tripMarketBreaker`, `tripDebtBreaker`) only when the approved swap routes are confirmed unable to settle valid liquidations at the required size. There is no on-chain failure counter, because a reverted call cannot persist a count and a counter can be gamed |
| Outstanding deficit | `breakerDeficitMax`: 10,000 GHO for the affected CDP Spoke's GHO reserve; a per-reserve materiality set at listing for other assets | Freeze new borrowing on that debt reserve automatically. Alert on every new deficit |
| Reopening | `gateRecoverySeconds`: 30 minutes of healthy observations, for temporary price or depth breaches | Clear the temporary gate. A deficit or security freeze needs an authorized reopening (`resetMarketBreaker`, `resetDebtBreaker`: guardian at once or timelock at the normal delay, [2.15](../02-contracts/15-governance-and-timelock.md)) |

The 30-minute recovery window is a provisional launch setting.
