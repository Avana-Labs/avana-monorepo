# 1.4 Permissions and Roles

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-11

Avana's authorities on each chain: that chain's AvanaTimelock and the roles on each Avana contract there, who holds them, what each can call and with what delay, the caller checks that are not roles, the Aave-side roles Avana depends on, the pause state of each Hub, keeper management, and what each key's compromise can do. Permission rules are `REQ-ROLE-…`. The role and delay tables of [2.15](../02-contracts/15-governance-and-timelock.md) are authoritative for role identifiers, holders and timelock mechanics, and the roles below map to them exactly; the pause switch is specified in [2.8](../02-contracts/08-market-registry.md); parameter values and their change class are in [1.5](05-parameters.md); emergency behaviour per state is in [3.6](../03-operations/06-emergency-operations.md).

Every chain has its own AvanaTimelock, governance multisig and Emergency Guardian with the same roles and delays; no role on one chain can act on another (`REQ-CHAIN-002`).

## Delay classes

| Class | Delay | Who can cancel | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public | None | None (nothing is queued) | Borrower, payer, depositor and liquidation entry points (`liquidate` is open to every address that is not frozen) |
| Contract | None | None (nothing is queued) | Calls only a fixed Avana contract address may make (checked against an immutable or the implementation constant) |
| Immediate (guardian) | None | None (nothing is queued) | `freeze`, `pause(reason)`, `unfreeze`, `unpause`, `freezeAddress`, `tripMarketBreaker`, `tripDebtBreaker`, `resetMarketBreaker`, `resetDebtBreaker` and `useStandbyRoute` by a guardian role on the MarketRegistry; `useStandbySecondSource` by `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN` on the ParameterStore; `cancelStandbySwitch` by the Aave governance executor; `cancel` on the timelock |
| Immediate (risk steward) | None | None (nothing is queued) | Every ParameterStore `tighten*` setter and `setAvanaFeeShareBps` within 0 – 5,000 bps, by `APPLICATION_RISK_STEWARD` (the Joint Configuration Safe), with the same checks as the timelock path (`REQ-ROLE-117`) |
| Short | `SHORT_DELAY` = 43,200 s (12 hours) | `CANCELLER_ROLE`; for a restoring operation `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` only | Operations in which every call is on the short-delay list (`REQ-ROLE-105`), a subset of the immutable `SHORT_ELIGIBLE`: tighten setters, route disabling and restoring a previously approved route, keeper removal down to `keeperFloor`, market offboarding, market activation and registration cancel, and the new-activity stops `freeze`, `pause`, `freezeAddress`, `tripMarketBreaker`, `tripDebtBreaker` and `useStandbyRoute` |
| Normal | `normalDelay()` = 432,000 s (5 days) at launch, always within `[MIN_NORMAL_DELAY, MAX_DELAY]` = [345,600 s, 432,000 s] | `CANCELLER_ROLE`; for a role-change or restoring operation `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` only | Every other privileged call: upgrades, the three deployment operations, the flash source, debt reserves, new routes and price sources, loosen and structural setters, fee changes, keeper additions, stray-position return, `unfreeze`, `unpause`, breaker resets, `unfreezeAddress` and `restorePriceRoute` by `APPLICATION_ADMIN`, role grants and revokes, the timelock's own settings |
| Immutable | Cannot change | None | Values fixed in bytecode |
| Aave governance | Aave DAO process | Aave governance | Hub and Spoke configuration, credit lines, each Avana Umbrella (Coverage Spoke), Avana's protocol safety module named after Aave's Umbrella |

Every scheduled operation becomes executable at its ready time and expires `GRACE_PERIOD` = 1,209,600 s (14 days) later; an expired operation can only be cleaned up. While public execution is on (launch value `true`), anyone can execute a ready operation.

## Roles

### Timelock and contract roles

The AvanaTimelock is an OpenZeppelin `TimelockController` extension that is not upgradeable and holds its own `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE`. On every upgradeable Avana contract (AaveAdapter, RiskModule, MarketRegistry, ParameterStore, each LPOracle, each DebtOracle, LPVaultSwap) the timelock holds `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` and `APPLICATION_ADMIN`, and a grant of either to any other account reverts `AdminRoleOnlyTimelock` (`REQ-ACCESS-153`); immutable contracts with a privileged function check `msg.sender == TIMELOCK`. The role constants are in `contracts/src/governance/roles/AvanaRoles.sol`; the timelock interface is under `contracts/src/governance/timelock/`.

| Role | Contract | Holder | Role admin | Can do | Delay | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` (timelock) | AvanaTimelock | The timelock itself only; a grant to any other account reverts `AdminRoleOnlyTimelock` | Itself | Grant and revoke `PROPOSER_ROLE`, `CANCELLER_ROLE`, `EXECUTOR_ROLE` and `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` on the timelock | Normal (self-call, role-change operation) | (Source: Q-228, Q-259) |
| `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` (contract) | Each upgradeable Avana contract | Timelock only; a grant to any other account reverts `AdminRoleOnlyTimelock` | Itself | Grant and revoke `APPLICATION_ADMIN`, only ever to the timelock | Normal (role-change operation) | (Source: Q-228, Q-259) |
| `APPLICATION_ADMIN` | Each upgradeable Avana contract | Timelock only; a grant to any other account reverts `AdminRoleOnlyTimelock` | `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` | Upgrades (`_authorizeUpgrade`), `setFlashSource`, fee changes, every setter, the deployment operations, debt reserves, price sources and routes, keepers, offboarding, `unfreeze(hub)`, `unpause(hub)`, `freeze(hub)`, `freezeAll`, `pause(hub, reason)`, `pauseAll`, `freezeAddress`, `unfreezeAddress`, breaker trips and resets, `useStandbyRoute`, `restorePriceRoute`; grant and revoke both guardian roles | Short or normal per `REQ-ROLE-105` | (Source: Q-226, Q-228, Q-241, Q-242, Q-248, Q-252, Q-259, Q-401) |

| `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN` | MarketRegistry | Emergency Guardian, Aave Protocol Guardian, Joint Configuration Safe | `APPLICATION_ADMIN` | `pause(reason)`, `unpause`, `unfreeze`, `resetMarketBreaker`, `resetDebtBreaker`, `useStandbyRoute` | Immediate | (Source: Q-227, Q-228, Q-241, Q-248, Q-252) |
| `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN` | ParameterStore | Emergency Guardian, Aave Protocol Guardian | `APPLICATION_ADMIN` | `useStandbySecondSource` only: puts the standby second source recorded at the normal delay in force; no parameter value changes (`REQ-PSTORE-029`) | Immediate | (Source: Q-385) |
| `APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN` | MarketRegistry | Emergency Guardian, Aave Protocol Guardian, Joint Configuration Safe | `APPLICATION_ADMIN` | `freeze`, `freezeAddress`, `tripMarketBreaker`, `tripDebtBreaker`; none of them can be undone with this role alone | Immediate | (Source: Q-227, Q-228, Q-241, Q-242) |
| `APPLICATION_RISK_STEWARD` | ParameterStore | Joint Configuration Safe (2-of-3: one Avana Labs, one Aave Labs, one LlamaRisk signer) | `APPLICATION_ADMIN` | The 20 `tighten*` setters (each only strictly safer, within its hard and liveness bounds) and `setAvanaFeeShareBps` in either direction within 0 – 5,000 bps; nothing else (`REQ-ROLE-117`) | Immediate | (Source: Q-228, Q-241) |

Holders at a glance: the timelock holds every admin role and is its own admin, and no grant can give an admin role to anyone else; the governance multisig (4-of-7) proposes and cancels and has no immediate power over any Avana contract; the Emergency Guardian (3-of-5, with one Aave Labs and one LlamaRisk seat) and the Aave Protocol Guardian each pause (with a reason), freeze, unfreeze, unpause, freeze addresses, trip and reset breakers, switch a token to its standby route or standby second source, cover recorded deficits (`COVERAGE_ROLE`, granted by the Aave DAO) and cancel, and have no upgrade or parameter power; the Joint Configuration Safe (2-of-3: Avana Labs, Aave Labs, LlamaRisk) holds the two MarketRegistry guardian roles and `APPLICATION_RISK_STEWARD`, so it tightens risk parameters and sets Avana's fee share within its bound at once, and cannot loosen, upgrade, change routes, sources, keepers or roles, cover a deficit or cancel; the Aave governance executor is the second proposer, cancels every operation including role changes and restoring operations, and undoes a standby switch at once; keepers hold no role.

A role-change operation is one in which every call is `grantRole`, `revokeRole` or `renounceRole` on an Avana contract or on the timelock. A restoring operation is one in which every call is `restoreRoute`, `useStandbyRoute`, `addKeeper` or `unfreezeAddress` (`REQ-ACCESS-156`). Only `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` can cancel either, so neither a guardian nor the governance multisig can block its own removal or a replacement's appointment, or hold a market unliquidatable or an address frozen by cancelling every repair.

Avana holds no role on any Aave contract. The Aave Protocol Guardian and the Aave governance executor hold Avana roles, listed above.

- `REQ-ROLE-001` — Avana MUST use one AvanaTimelock, an OpenZeppelin `TimelockController` extension that is not upgradeable, as the only authority over every privileged Avana function: every upgradeable Avana contract is a UUPS proxy (ERC-1967) using OpenZeppelin `AccessControl` whose `_authorizeUpgrade` requires `APPLICATION_ADMIN`, with `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` and `APPLICATION_ADMIN` held only by the timelock; every immutable contract with a privileged function checks `msg.sender == TIMELOCK`; no Avana contract has an `Ownable` owner or a ProxyAdmin, and no upgrade path bypasses the timelock. (Source: Q-043, Q-092, Q-119, Q-226, Q-228)
- `REQ-ROLE-002` — The timelock MUST hold its own `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` with no other admin, so its proposers, cancellers, executors and settings change only through operations that wait the timelock's own delays; the deployer MUST hold no role on the timelock or on any Avana contract once deployment ends (the timelock's constructor `admin` is `address(0)` and each upgradeable contract's `initialize` grants its admin roles only to the timelock), and the deployment check MUST confirm it before the deployment is marked verified. (Source: Q-119, Q-228)
- `REQ-ROLE-003` — Every privileged selector MUST be assigned explicitly to a role or to the `TIMELOCK` immutable in the [2.15](../02-contracts/15-governance-and-timelock.md) role table and the permission matrix below; no admin, setter, pause or upgrade selector is callable by any other address. A fork test MUST fail on any deviation. (Source: Q-119, Q-228)
- `REQ-ROLE-004` — The timelock MUST reject any operation scheduled with a delay below `requiredDelay(targets, payloads)`: `SHORT_DELAY` (43,200 s) when every call is on the short-delay list and `normalDelay()` (432,000 s at launch) otherwise. `updateDelay` MUST revert; `normalDelay()` changes only by `setNormalDelay`, a self-call at the normal delay, within `[MIN_NORMAL_DELAY, MAX_DELAY]` = [345,600 s, 432,000 s]. An operation MUST NOT execute before its ready time or after its ready time plus `GRACE_PERIOD` (1,209,600 s). The proposers (the governance multisig and the Aave governance executor) only propose; the cancellers only cancel. Example: an upgrade scheduled at t = 1,800,000,000 is ready at 1,800,432,000 and expires at 1,801,641,600. (Source: Q-228, Q-245, Q-262)
- `REQ-ROLE-005` — `CANCELLER_ROLE` MUST be held by exactly the governance multisig, the Emergency Guardian, the Aave Protocol Guardian and the Aave governance executor, and `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` by exactly the Aave governance executor; `cancel` of a role-change operation MUST revert `RoleChangeCancelRestricted`, and of a restoring operation `RestoringCancelRestricted`, for a caller without `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE`. A fork test MUST show every queued operation type can be cancelled by exactly these parties. (Source: Q-136, Q-228, Q-263)
- `REQ-ROLE-006` — Granting or revoking any role on an Avana contract or the timelock, including both guardian roles, MUST be a timelock operation at the normal delay, cancellable only by the Aave governance executor; the role admins are fixed: `APPLICATION_ADMIN` ← `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE`, `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN`, `APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN` and `APPLICATION_RISK_STEWARD` ← `APPLICATION_ADMIN`, the timelock's own roles ← the timelock's `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE`; no role held outside the timelock administers another role, and `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` and `APPLICATION_ADMIN` can be granted only to the timelock (`REQ-ACCESS-153`). (Source: Q-136, Q-228, Q-248, Q-259)
- `REQ-ROLE-007` — The governance multisig MUST be a 4-of-7 Safe with mixed signers (Avana Labs plus external), the Emergency Guardian a 3-of-5 Safe whose five seats are three Avana Labs signers, one Aave Labs signer and one LlamaRisk signer (no seat acts alone, and no organisation reaches the threshold alone), and the Joint Configuration Safe a 2-of-3 Safe of one Avana Labs, one Aave Labs and one LlamaRisk signer; no signer key MUST be shared between the Emergency Guardian, the governance multisig, the Joint Configuration Safe and the Aave Protocol Guardian, so no single key both proposes a change and controls the guardian response to it. An organisation may hold seats on several Safes, each through a different person and key. Every signer follows the key-security standard of `REQ-ACCESS-104` (governance/README). (Source: Q-114, Q-228, Q-245)
- `REQ-ROLE-008` — `renounceRole` is accepted; monitoring MUST alert when `PROPOSER_ROLE` has fewer than two members, when `CANCELLER_ROLE`, `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE`, `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN` or `APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN` has no member, when any address other than the timelock holds `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` or `APPLICATION_ADMIN`, when `APPLICATION_RISK_STEWARD` is held by any address other than the chain's Joint Configuration Safe, when an operation is scheduled, cancelled or executed, or when active keepers fall below `keeperFloor` (1), at which point new borrowing on the chain halts; the release manifest (a JSON-Schema document approved by governance multisig signers) carries a role snapshot that MUST match chain state after every governance event, and the live-config check also asserts the required Aave reserve flags. (Source: Q-043, Q-053, Q-068, Q-119, Q-228, Q-242, Q-245, Q-400)
- `REQ-ROLE-009` — For the handover of administration to the Aave DAO, a role-change operation at the normal delay MUST, under the pre-agreed plan, grant `EXECUTOR_ROLE` to the Aave governance executor (already a proposer) and `PROPOSER_ROLE` and `EXECUTOR_ROLE` to a second DAO-named holder, and revoke both roles from the governance multisig in the same operation, revoking last, so `PROPOSER_ROLE` never has fewer than two holders (`REQ-ACCESS-105`, `REQ-ACCESS-160`); the guardian roles, `CANCELLER_ROLE` and `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` are unchanged by the handover. (Source: Q-114, Q-228, Q-245)

## Permission matrix

"Cancel" names who can cancel a queued call; "None" means the call is immediate or never scheduled. Every Normal or Short row is executed by the timelock (`APPLICATION_ADMIN` or `TIMELOCK`) after an operation a proposer scheduled; "DAO only" marks a restoring call (`REQ-ACCESS-156`).

## Pausing and emergency actions

### Application pause state

Each Hub has its own pause state, with a reason while Paused, held by the chain's MarketRegistry (`pauseAll` and `freezeAll` set every Hub of the chain at once); a pledge reads its Spoke's home Hub and a borrow also the debt reserve's funding Hub. The state is and read by the AaveAdapter (pledge, re-credit, borrow, withdraw-with-debt, removal with debt, repay with collateral) and the Lens. Beside it the MarketRegistry holds the chain's address freeze and the circuit-breaker state (temporary-gate breach times and latched security freezes), which the AaveAdapter, ProxyManager, LPVaultSwap (callers only) and RiskModule read on new-activity paths (`REQ-ACCESS-082`). No Avana contract keeps a per-function, per-keeper or per-route restriction, an incident clock or a tolerance override. Per-market containment uses the market breaker, the Aave reserve freeze or pause and the MarketRegistry `Offboarding` status; the Aave reserve flags are a separate source the RiskModule reads.

| Action | None | Frozen | Paused(General) | Paused(MintPathSuspect) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| New pledge (account with no debt) | Yes | No (`ApplicationFrozen`) | No (`NotRescueTopUp`: the Adapter sets `requireRescue`) | No (`ApplicationPaused`) |
| Top-up or re-credit of an indebted account | Yes | Yes | Rescue top-up only | No (`ApplicationPaused`) |
| Borrow | Yes | No (`ApplicationFrozen`) | No (`ApplicationPaused`) | No (`ApplicationPaused`) |
| Withdraw or remove liquidity with debt; repay with collateral | Yes | Yes, with every RiskModule check | No (`ApplicationPaused`) | No (`ApplicationPaused`) |
| Repay (any payer), `repayAllAndWithdraw` | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Fee collection | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Debt-free withdrawal and removal, zero-credit release | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Liquidation (`liquidate`, any caller) | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Pending settlement | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Transitions, per Hub: `freeze(hub)` None → Frozen; `pause(hub, reason)` None or Frozen → Paused(reason), or a change of reason while Paused; `unfreeze(hub)` Frozen → None; `unpause(hub)` Paused → None; `freezeAll` and `pauseAll` apply the same transition to every Hub of the chain. ## Exit safety under the timelock

- `REQ-ROLE-104` — The normal delay MUST be at least the time a borrower needs to leave plus the cancellers' reaction time, and every change that could harm a borrower's position (an upgrade of the AaveAdapter, RiskModule or LPVaultSwap, a replacement of the flash source, a looser parameter, a new swap route, a role change) MUST take the normal delay. Reasoning: repay and debt-free withdrawal never read a pause state or any gate (`REQ-ROLE-068`), so leaving needs one `repay` per debt reserve (or one `repayAllAndWithdraw`) and one `withdraw` per remaining position, each a single transaction; the 5-day delay, never below the 4-day `MIN_NORMAL_DELAY` (`SPEC-GOV-015`, with the chain's block time), leaves the borrower days for those few transactions and gives the cancellers the same window to cancel. Example: an Adapter upgrade on Ethereum is scheduled at t = 1,800,000,000 and is ready at 1,800,432,000; a borrower with GHO and USDC debt on one market's proxy and three positions there sends `repayAllAndWithdraw` (both reserves, first position) and two `withdraw` calls within the first hour, well before 1,800,432,000. The short delay (12 hours) applies only to operations that reduce risk or restore previously reviewed configuration, so it never shortens the window before a change that loosens anything. (Source: Q-223, Q-227, Q-228, Q-262, Q-285)
- `REQ-ROLE-105` — The short-delay list MUST be exactly the immutable `SHORT_ELIGIBLE` set of `REQ-ACCESS-155`: every `tighten*` setter of the ParameterStore (including `tightenFloorRoundingAllowance` and `tightenMaxFlashFeeBps`), `disableRoute`, `restoreRoute`, `removeKeeper`, `setMarketOffboarding`, `activateMarket`, `cancelRegistration`, and the new-activity stops `freeze`, `pause`, `freezeAddress`, `tripMarketBreaker`, `tripDebtBreaker` and `useStandbyRoute`; each chain's timelock's registered short-delay selectors (`isShortDelaySelector`) MUST equal this list at launch and can only lose entries or regain entries of `SHORT_ELIGIBLE`, and an operation containing any other call MUST take the normal delay. `usdBandLow`, `usdBandHigh`, `graceDuration` and `graceHealthFactor` have no tighten setter and change only at the normal delay. The guardians' immediate powers and `cancel` are outside the timelock.
