# 1.3 Trust Boundaries

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12

Every place where data, assets or authority cross from one trust domain into another in Avana, what is checked at each crossing, what Avana does when a dependency fails, and every entry point where an attacker can supply input. Full assumptions about each external dependency are in [4.3](../04-security/03-external-dependencies.md); attacks are analysed in [4.2](../04-security/02-attack-scenarios.md); the invariants these checks preserve are in [4.1](../04-security/01-global-invariants.md). Every row with a `TB-…` ID is a requirement.

## Trusted components

Avana sorts components into five trust levels. A component may rely on a component at a higher level, and only for what that level is trusted for.

| Level | Components | Trusted for | Not trusted for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Immutable Avana code | Every LPVault (one per AMM protocol per chain), Collateralization, vault tokens, VaultTokenFactory, Lock, swap-route versions, `IFlashSource` implementations, PayoutEscrow (the payout escrow), every PositionProxy (logic-free), AvanaTimelock | Custody of positions and the rule that a position returns only to its depositor of record, the backing ledger and exposure counters keyed by Hub, the seizure share-drop and supply-drop checks, reconciliation of unsettled seizures, the vault-token recipient rule that makes LPVaultSwap the only liquidator, the `deleverage` flash-loan checks, escrowed payouts, the timelock's delays and roles | Nothing beyond their own code; they trust upgradeable contracts only for yes-or-no answers, prices and values within bounds |

## Custody trust and blast radius

On each chain, the AaveAdapter's code and the timelock that upgrades it are trusted for custody of every proxy position; PositionProxies carry no logic. The bound on each upgradeable component, if its implementation is faulty or hostile:

- AaveAdapter implementation faulty or hostile: borrow every debt reserve against every proxy of its chain up to each `drawCap`; withdraw supplied vault tokens; mint vault tokens to a Hub, itself or any registered proxy (bounded by the Hub `addCap`); burn any holder's vault tokens (including a Hub's); register any address as a proxy; spend allowances granted to the Adapter; pledge any position whose owner approved an LPVault. It cannot send a position to anyone but its depositor of record, receive seized vault tokens, raise any Aave cap, spend a Coverage Spoke or act on another chain. The ProxyManager is immutable and cannot change an existing proxy. Bound: the normal delay (5 days at launch, never below 4), cancellers, Hub/Spoke caps; exits (repay, debt-free withdraw) are never paused, so borrowers can leave inside the delay.
- RiskModule faulty: can approve a borrow below 1.10/1.05 (bounded by Aave HF ≥ 1 and drawCaps) or block borrowing and pledges; cannot touch exits.
- LPVaultSwap faulty: can seize only accounts Aave deems liquidatable, through Aave sizing; Collateralization's share-drop and supply-drop checks stop it releasing liquidity it did not burn; it holds no budget it could draw; reconcile-on-exit (`Q-201`) handles seizures it never settles.
- MarketRegistry/ParameterStore faulty: can misprice gates within bounds, list bad routes, pause, freeze addresses, trip breakers, misreport breach times or keep security freezes latched; cannot move funds, and cannot block repay, debt-free withdrawal or fee collection, which read neither contract (`Q-260`).
- DebtOracle faulty: it is the Aave price source of its debt reserve on every CDP Spoke of its chain that lends the asset (`TB-080`), so it can overprice the debt (making accounts liquidatable early, bounded by Aave's sizing and bonus) or underprice it (letting accounts borrow more, bounded by each `drawCap` and the RiskModule's own debt-price gates); the drift check and the keepers' code check watch its source and implementation.
