# 5.2 Formal Properties

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-09

Two parts. Part A indexes the properties already checked at the spec stage by the models in this folder, with what each result does and does not establish. Part B lists the implementation-stage properties (`PROP-…`) to prove on the Solidity with Certora CVL (`contracts/certora/`, `Q-041`), Halmos (symbolic Foundry tests in `contracts/test/`) and the Solidity SMTChecker. Each `PROP-…` carries a `verifies:` tag naming its requirement IDs in its spec file, so [`trace.py`](trace.py) links it (`Q-173`). Proof reports, tool versions and reproduction commands go in `audit/formal/`.

Result vocabulary in part A:

- **Proved**: Z3 found the negation `unsat`, or SymPy reduced the residual to 0 or proved a non-negative certificate. Holds for all inputs inside the stated bounds, including unbounded integers where stated.
- **Computed**: exact-integer evaluation or exhaustive enumeration over a finite set, not a solver query.
- **Holds within bounds**: no violation in bounded random exploration (Quint simulator, Hypothesis, simulations). This is evidence, not proof.
- **Counterexample**: a concrete violating state was found; each one is a `FINDING-…` in [5.3](03-audit-findings.md).

### Quint (`quint/`)

The Quint model (`quint/avana.qnt`) is written against decisions `Q-001`…`Q-221`. Its README records every property `QNT-01`…`QNT-43` as holding under the random simulator (`quint run`; full mode 10,000 traces × 20 steps per property, quick mode 500) and all 35 deterministic scenarios of `avana_tests.qnt` (`QNT-T01`…`QNT-T30`) as passing. Apalache bounded and inductive checking (`quint verify`) is pending until Java is installed (`Q-152`); the model uses only finite sets and integers, so it is ready for it. Model sizes: 3 wallets, 7 NFTs, 3 markets on 2 Spokes, 3 keepers, at most 2 records per account per market and 2 markets per proxy. Not modelled: the destination of pending-settlement proceeds (`Q-211`), conversion routes, USD caps, the USD band and timelock delays. The model's counterexample IDs C-QNT-1…C-QNT-4 have final statuses: C-QNT-1 (hostile LPVaultSwap strands NFTs) closed by reconcile on exit (`Q-201`); C-QNT-2 (Aave Guardian `pauseReserve` without expiry) accepted as an Aave-governed risk with the listing AIP commitment (`Q-202`); C-QNT-3 (a hostile check module approves borrowing) closed for Aave market freezes by the frozen-flag check (`Q-203`, now a RiskModule check), with Drifting a check of the reviewed RiskModule bounded by `drawCap`, Aave's HF 1 check and the 5-day timelock (`Q-228`, `Q-398`); C-QNT-4 (zero-seizure budget draws) closed by the `NothingSeized` revert in LPVaultSwap, which still refuses every 0-unit seizure now that the budget is removed (`Q-397`).

Scope relative to `Q-223`…`Q-240`: the model's proxies are keyed by wallet alone and created by a Factory, its vault tokens are minted and burned by the LPVault, its borrow-side checks sit in a separately upgradeable policy (`stepHostilePolicy`), its restrictions are per-function pauses with an incident clock, quarantine and Recovery, and every account borrows GHO only. The properties of custody, backing, the per-proxy ledger, seizure and settlement (`QNT-01`…`QNT-13`, `QNT-22`…`QNT-31`, `QNT-35`…`QNT-37`) constrain the same state that Collateralization, the AaveAdapter and LPVaultSwap now hold and are re-proved on code by the `PROP-…` rows that cite them; the properties of exits (`QNT-14`…`QNT-17`, `QNT-39`) hold a fortiori because the pause state never blocks repay, debt-free withdrawal or liquidation (`Q-227`). `QNT-32`…`QNT-34` check the incident clock, quarantine and Recovery, which `Q-227` and `Q-228` removed; they back no current rule. The Adapter call path, the PositionProxies, the pause state, the timelock and several debt reserves are stated as `PROP-054`…`PROP-064` and the tests `TEST-186`…`TEST-214` of [5.1](01-test-requirements.md). The rules of `Q-241`…`Q-278` (circuit breakers, the address freeze, stress valuation, standby routes and the DebtOracle, the per-leg swap minimum, the shortfall top-up and the automatic in-kind trigger (removed by `Q-401`), rescue top-ups and rescue units, partial removal and re-credit, repay with collateral, the one-sided backing check and the timelock allowlists) are outside every model of this part; they are stated as `PROP-065`…`PROP-074` and the tests `TEST-215`…`TEST-263`.

## Verification limitations

- **Summaries are assumptions.** Certora rules on Avana contracts summarise the Aave V4 Spoke and Hub, the Uniswap position manager and Chainlink. Each summary is listed in the row; a wrong summary voids the proof. Aave-dependent behaviour (seizure path, dust rule, fee shares, premium threshold, the debt-reserve set) is therefore checked on real bytecode by fork tests: the seizure proof `TEST-109`, `TEST-110`, the premium-threshold test `TEST-111`, the drift tests `TEST-114` and the debt-set check `TEST-204`.
- **Reviewed and upgradeable code.** The AaveAdapter, which drives every PositionProxy, is trusted for custody (`Q-223`, `Q-225`, `Q-359`); the PositionProxy holds no logic of its own and its code never changes: `PROP-001`, `PROP-006`, `PROP-007`, `PROP-009` (origin part), `PROP-010`, `PROP-031`…`PROP-033`, `PROP-046`, `PROP-054` and `PROP-056` hold for the reviewed implementations. A hostile Adapter implementation is not bounded by a proof; it is bounded by the 5-day timelock delay, the cancellers, the Hub and Spoke caps and the exits that no pause blocks, and the tests `TEST-130`, `TEST-195` and `TEST-210` check that a borrower can repay and withdraw everything inside the delay. Properties of the RiskModule, MarketRegistry, ParameterStore, LPOracle and LPVaultSwap hold for the verified version only (including `PROP-051`); the properties that must survive a hostile upgrade of those contracts (`PROP-002`…`PROP-005`, `PROP-008` (recipient part), `PROP-011`…`PROP-014`, `PROP-020`, `PROP-045`, `PROP-055`, `PROP-063`, `PROP-064`) are stated over the immutable contracts and the reviewed Adapter with the others havocked. A hostile LPVaultSwap that seizes and never settles leaves the proxy's supply below its credited units only until the next withdrawal or settlement, which reconciles it (`PROP-045`).
- **Bounded tools.** Halmos unrolls loops (records lists to 8 entries); Certora needs loop summaries for the records list; Quint, Hypothesis and simulations explore random traces within their sizes. Apalache bounded and inductive checking of the Quint model is pending a Java install.
- **Superseded model checks.** The Quint and Hypothesis models predate `Q-223`…`Q-240` (part A): their hostile-policy runs, Factory registry, per-function restrictions, incident clock, quarantine and Recovery checks back no current rule, and their GHO-only debt does not exercise non-GHO settlement; the `PROP-054`…`PROP-064` rows and the tests `TEST-186`…`TEST-214` carry those rules. No model of part A covers `Q-241`…`Q-278`; `PROP-065`…`PROP-074`, the rewritten `PROP-001`, `PROP-015`, `PROP-020`, `PROP-022`, `PROP-032`, `PROP-041`, `PROP-049`, `PROP-054`, `PROP-057`, `PROP-058` and the tests `TEST-215`…`TEST-263` carry them. The Quint model reconciles an unsettled seizure in a separate pass after the settlement's own debit, while 2.3 reconciles before it; both orders debit the same units in total, and the 2.3 order is proved on code by `PROP-045` and `TEST-151`. `MATH-AR-16`…`MATH-AR-19` modelled the repay floor of `Q-146`, superseded by `Q-153`. `MATH-UO-25`…`MATH-UO-27` modelled `Q-109`; `PROP-028` proves the adopted `Q-174` form. Quint and the simulations do not model the USD band of `Q-176` and `Q-195`; Hypothesis `HYP-63` and `TEST-043` cover it. The Quint and Hypothesis models of the custody core use one Uniswap v3 full-range market and one Hub; the AMM pricing checks and the topology model of part A carry the AMM-neutral, multi-Hub rules at the spec stage (`Q-359`…`Q-362`), and `PROP-075`…`PROP-084` and `TEST-264`…`TEST-283` carry them on code (`Q-366`). `MATH-ST-68`…`70` modelled the GHO buy leg that `Q-362` removes; caller funding in the debt asset replaces it, and `PROP-015` and `PROP-017` state the arithmetic without it.
- **Gas and liveness.** No formal tool here proves that a liquidation fits in a block or that keepers act; `TEST-123`, `TEST-124` and the keeper tests cover them.
- **Economics.** A proof of an arithmetic rule says nothing about whether parameter values are safe. Pool risk factors, caps, `minPledgeUsd`, `buySlippageAllowanceBps`, the bonus curve, grace and the 48-hour incident window used for cap sizing rest on the simulations, the halt-deterioration test `TEST-127` and the risk service providers (`Q-197`).
- **Off-chain code.** Ponder, Convex, the keeper and the browser calldata builder are covered by tests only (sections H and I of [5.1](01-test-requirements.md)).
