# 4.1 Global Invariants

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-10

Properties that hold at the end of every transaction, in every price state, application pause state (None, Frozen, Paused with reason General or MintPathSuspect), breaker state, address-freeze state, sequencer state and governance state, on every chain and for every Hub, CDP Spoke, market and AMM integration, unless a row states a narrower scope. Each invariant has one `INV-NNN` ID; the contract and operation rules that preserve it link to that ID.

## Conventions

- **Market** `m`: one (chain, AMM protocol, pool, exact position shape) approved in the chain's MarketRegistry; for concentrated liquidity (Uniswap v3, Aerodrome Slipstream) the shape is the exact `[tickLower, tickUpper]` range. One market = one vault token `vaultToken[m]` = one collateral reserve `reserveId_m` on its CDP Spoke = one LPOracle instance. A **CDP Spoke** is a standard Aave V4 Spoke, one per (chain, Hub, AMM protocol, risk tier), and lists several markets ([1.1](../01-architecture/01-protocol-overview.md)). A **Hub** is one Avana Hub tier (Stable LP, Correlated LP or Volatile LP) on one chain; at launch a Stable LP Hub on Ethereum and one on Base.
- **Debt reserve** `r`: a reserve on a CDP Spoke approved for that Spoke in the MarketRegistry by `enableDebtReserve`, recorded with its asset, decimals, funding Hub, Hub asset ID, price source and the Coverage Spoke of its (funding Hub, asset) ([2.8](../02-contracts/08-market-registry.md)). Each CDP Spoke has a home Hub; a debt reserve whose funding Hub is another Hub is a cross-Hub line (`INV-094`), closed at launch. At launch the only debt reserve on each chain is GHO on that chain's Stable LP Hub.
- **Record**: a Collateralization backing record with `integrationId`, `positionKey` (bytes32; for Uniswap v3 `bytes32(tokenId)`, for Slipstream `bytes32((uint256(generation) << 248) | tokenId)` with the market's position manager and factory generation in the high byte, [2.19](../02-contracts/19-amm-integration-interface.md) `SPEC-AMM-CORE-002`), `depositor`, `proxy`, `market`, `credited`, `originalUnits`, `originalUsdCharge`, status. "Open" includes records at 0 credited units ([2.3](../02-contracts/03-collateralization.md)). The position itself is held by the LPVault of its AMM protocol on that chain ([2.2](../02-contracts/02-lp-vault.md)). Token amounts are n-token arrays, one entry per pool token.
- **Proxy** `p`: the PositionProxy of one wallet in one market of one CDP Spoke, a logic-free immutable contract created by the chain's ProxyManager with CREATE2; `proxyOf(w, m)` is recorded by the AaveAdapter and equals `ProxyManager.predictProxy(w, s, marketId)`, the CREATE2 address with salt `keccak256(abi.encode(w, s, marketId))` ([2.5](../02-contracts/05-position-proxy-and-proxy-manager.md)). It executes only calls from the chain's AaveAdapter, and its code is fixed at creation and never upgraded. An account is one proxy: one collateral reserve (its market) and its debt reserves under one Aave health factor, so no two markets share a health factor (`Q-359`).
- **Immutable contracts**: vault tokens, VaultTokenFactory, every LPVault (one per AMM protocol per chain), Collateralization (one AMM-neutral ledger per chain), Lock, PayoutEscrow (the payout escrow), every PositionProxy, the ProxyManager (a CREATE2 deployer with no roles and no upgrade), every swap route version and the AvanaTimelock. Their privileged functions check `msg.sender == TIMELOCK`. **Upgradeable contracts** (UUPS, `_authorizeUpgrade` requires `APPLICATION_ADMIN`, held only by the chain's timelock): AaveAdapter, MarketRegistry, ParameterStore, RiskModule, DebtOracle, each market's LPOracle and LPVaultSwap (`Q-226`). The `IFlashSource` implementation, which serves only repay with collateral (`deleverage`), is replaced through the timelock (`MarketRegistry.setFlashSource`). The Lens and the flash liquidation helpers are periphery outside the audited core; each helper is immutable, with no owner, setter, upgrade or sweep (Q-404).
- **Fixed Avana set**: the two Lock members AaveAdapter and LPVaultSwap (`INV-091`); the inner-call contracts Collateralization and each LPVault, reachable only through a Lock member; plus the vault tokens, the Lock itself, the `IFlashSource` implementation and the swap route contracts.
- **Scope by chain and Hub.** Every contract in 1.1's per-chain list exists once per chain, with its own Timelock, Safe and Guardian set. Every cap, pause, breaker, route permission and emergency control is keyed by Hub, and by CDP Spoke and market where it applies. An invariant that names "the Hub" holds for each Hub on each chain; one that names a market holds for each market of each AMM protocol.
- **AMM integration.** Avana's code for one AMM protocol. Positions are identified by `positionKey` plus `integrationId`; `unitsOf(positionKey)` is the position's liquidity units, `unwind(positionKey, x, minAmounts, recipient)` removes exactly `x` units and returns n token amounts. Where an invariant gives a Uniswap v3 or Slipstream call, the same rule holds through each integration's equivalent call.
- **Settlement asset.** The settlement asset `T` of a liquidation is the debt asset of the reserve repaid: the caller's funding, the swaps, the floor, the keeper reward, Avana's fee and the borrower's surplus are all in that asset, and GHO is used only when the debt is GHO (`INV-070`). Settlement terms follow [2.11](../02-contracts/11-lpvaultswap.md), all in debt-asset base units: `V` the oracle value of the seizure, `D` the debt Aave pulled, `Kf` the caller funding LPVaultSwap received from `msg.sender` (`0 < Kf ≤ debtToCover`), `R` the debt asset recovered by the swaps, `minRec` the recovery floor, `ε = floorRoundingAllowance(spoke, r)`, `shortfall` and `maxShortfall` the caller's top-up and its bound, `R' = R + shortfall`, `S` the realized surplus, `G` the oracle gain, `A` Avana's fee, `kr` the keeper reward, `B` the borrower's surplus and `keeperOut` the caller's payout. LPVaultSwap takes no flash loan in a liquidation, so no flash principal or premium appears in a liquidation term; a caller without the debt asset borrows it through a flash liquidation helper, which carries its own loan `F` and premium `φ` (Q-404). `F` and `φ` of the core `IFlashSource` appear only in repay with collateral (`INV-089`). No Avana contract funds any part of a settlement: there is no settlement budget (Q-397).
- **Scope labels.** "Any implementation" means the invariant holds for every implementation of RiskModule, MarketRegistry, ParameterStore, LPOracle and LPVaultSwap, because immutable contracts or the reviewed custody contracts enforce it. "Reviewed implementation" means it holds for the implementation that passed review; the AaveAdapter is always in this scope, because it drives every logic-free proxy and is trusted for custody of every proxy position (`Q-223`, `Q-359`). A hostile upgrade can break a reviewed-scope invariant only through a timelock operation that waits the normal delay (5 days at launch, never below 4 days), during which cancellers can stop it and borrowers can exit (`INV-018`, `INV-068`); [4.2 Attack Scenarios](02-attack-scenarios.md) records the residual risk, its owner and its bound.
- **Verified by** lists spec-stage checks from [5 Verification](../05-verification/README.md): `MATH-*` (Z3), `SYM-*` (SymPy), `QNT-*` (Quint), `HYP-*` (Hypothesis), `SIM-*` (simulation). A check marked "(CE)" produced the counterexample named in that row; the row states how Avana closes or bounds it. `None` means no spec-stage model checks the row. **Implementation checks** names the Foundry tests (`TEST-…`, [5.1](../05-verification/01-test-requirements.md)) and formal properties (`PROP-…`, [5.2](../05-verification/02-formal-properties.md)) that check each invariant on code.
- Parameter names follow [1.5 Parameters](../01-architecture/05-parameters.md).

| `INV-058` | Reconcile on exit. In `release` (withdrawal), `releasePartial` (partial removal), `releaseForDeleverage` (deleverage release), `recredit` (re-credit), `releaseZeroCredit` (zero-credit release) and `settleSeizure` (liquidation settlement) for proxy `p` and market `m`, Collateralization computes `shortfall = max(0, C − S)`, where `C` is `p`'s credited units in `m` (`getAccountCredited`) and `S` is `p`'s Spoke supply before the call's own movement: the current supply plus the units withdrawn earlier in the same Adapter call in `release`, `releasePartial` and `releaseForDeleverage`, the current supply in `recredit` and `releaseZeroCredit`, and the `beginSeizure` snapshot in `settleSeizure` (2.3 `SPEC-BACK-011`). A positive shortfall means a seizure that was never settled: Collateralization debits it from `p`'s records largest-first (`INV-015`), moves exactly that liquidity (principal only) into `pending[vaultToken[m]]` (units and the matching pool-token amounts), reduces the exposure counters by the debited charges, emits one `RecordDebited` per debited record and one `SeizureReconciled`, and at the end of the call `S == C` (`INV-008`). `release` reconciles before its units check and then releases the rest of the position; `releaseZeroCredit` reconciles before its credited check; `settleSeizure` reconciles after the current seizure's units are burned and before debiting them, so the current seizure is debited only from credit Aave still held. Reconcile runs only while the vault-token Hub asset's added shares equal its added assets; while they differ it is skipped and liquidation is stopped as well (`INV-004`), so no new seizure arises in the meantime. Pending units are retired only through `LPVaultSwap.settlePending(marketId)`, which anyone may call in every pause state: LPVaultSwap takes `units = min(its vault-token balance for m, pending.units)` (`NothingPending` at 0), has the AaveAdapter burn them from LPVaultSwap with `burnSeized`, and calls `Collateralization.settlePending(vaultToken, units)`, which requires `totalSupply + units ≤ totalCredited + pending.units` (`INV-080`) before debiting the pending balance, transfers the pro-rata pending pool tokens (rounded down except that the final settlement takes the exact remainder, 2.3 `SPEC-BACK-012`) from Collateralization to LPVaultSwap (2.3 `REQ-VAULT-139`), and emits `PendingSettled`. In the same call LPVaultSwap swaps those pool tokens into the market's pending-settlement asset over the market's swap routes, reverts with `RecoveryBelowFloor` unless the amount recovered is at least `minPending` (the pending tokens' oracle value less `slippageAllowanceBps`, `U` and `ε`, 2.11 `SPEC-LIQ-014`), adds all of it to the Coverage Spoke of the market's (Hub, debt asset) through `fund` and emits `PendingSettlementForwarded`; no keeper, caller or borrower receives any of it, and only route residue, counted as zero recovery, goes to `feeRecipient`. The shortfall check reads the Spoke directly, so it holds for any LPVaultSwap implementation. Why: a hostile or faulty LPVaultSwap version could seize and never settle; without reconcile the borrower could never withdraw the rest of the position, and the outstanding vault tokens must stay backed until an honest version settles them. The proceeds go to the Coverage Spoke of the market's (Hub, debt asset) because the First-Loss Reserve absorbs the losses such an event causes, and a fixed destination means nobody profits from triggering one. Example: `p` has records of 600 and 400 units; an LPVaultSwap version seizes 250 units and never settles, so `S = 750`, `C = 1,000`. The wallet repays and withdraws the 400-unit record: the Spoke withdrawal leaves 350, so `S = 350 + 400 = 750` and `shortfall = 250`; Collateralization debits 250 from the 600-unit record (now 350) and moves 250 units of liquidity into pending, the Adapter burns the 400 withdrawn units and the LPVault returns that position; afterwards `p`'s supply is 350 = credited 350, and total supply 600 = 350 + 250 pending. After an honest LPVaultSwap returns, a `settlePending` call burns the 250 stranded vault tokens and receives all the pending pool tokens, and LPVaultSwap converts them to the debt asset and, once the floor is met, funds the Coverage Spoke of the market's (Hub, debt asset) with all of it. Every CDP Spoke is a standard, unmodified Aave V4 Spoke, and for every Avana collateral reserve on it: `receiveSharesEnabled = false`; `liquidationFee = 0`, or the fee is paid to Avana's fee receiver; no reinvestment controller on the vault-token Hub asset; the reserve is not borrowable; its price source is the market's LPOracle. The listing AIP sets them, `listCollateralMarket` and `deployCdpSpoke` check them before the market goes Live, and the drift check (`INV-029`) re-reads them on every new-risk call. A drift trips the breaker of that (Hub, CDP Spoke), which stops new pledges, borrows and withdrawals that leave debt; it never stops repay, exits, fee collection, liquidation or pending settlement (`INV-081`). Why: on a standard Spoke these settings are Aave-side configuration, and with `receiveSharesEnabled` or a fee a third party could take vault-token shares with no ledger debit, leaving debt that can never be liquidated because every seizure needs a token transfer. Residual: the exposure lasts from the Aave change until the drift check trips `CONFIG_DRIFT`; the check runs on every Avana entry point and on a per-block keeper poke (`pokeConfig`), so the window ends at the first Avana call or keeper poke after the change, normally the next block. A seizure outside LPVaultSwap in that window (a third party's `liquidationCall` with `receiveShares = true`, or its borrow against seized shares) is an accepted residual risk, bounded by the Spoke draw caps of the debt reserves it can borrow; the Aave DAO owns the configuration (`ATTACK-155`). | `INV-094` | Cross-Hub credit lines. A debt reserve on a CDP Spoke whose funding Hub `F` is not the Spoke's home Hub draws within a cap owned by `F`: one registry per chain sums, per (funding Hub, asset), the `drawCap`s of every CDP Spoke of any other Hub that draws `(F, a)`, and every configuration call that sets or raises such a `drawCap` reverts unless `Σ drawCap ≤ crossHubCap[F][a] ≤ firstLossCoverage[F][a]` (the funded balance of `F`'s Coverage Spoke for `a`). A pause of `F` stops every new draw of `F`'s liquidity, including draws by other Hubs' Spokes; repay, liquidation and exits read neither Hub's pause (`INV-081`). Only `F`'s governance path sets `crossHubCap[F][a]`. At launch every `crossHubCap` is 0, so no cross-Hub line is open. Why: a line checked only by the borrowing Hub lets a funding Hub's suppliers carry risk its own registry never approved, and a deficit on a foreign Spoke must stay within coverage that exists. Example: `firstLossCoverage[Stable][USDC]` = $1,200,000 and `crossHubCap[Stable][USDC]` = $1,000,000; a Volatile-Hub Spoke with `drawCap` $600,000 is registered; a second at $500,000 reverts (sum $1.1M above $1.0M).
