# 4.3 External Dependencies

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-10

Every contract or service outside Avana's code that Avana relies on, on every chain where Avana is deployed: what Avana relies on, what it assumes, what happens if the assumption breaks, and the rule that limits the damage. Addresses come from the Aave address book (Ethereum and Base files), from each AMM protocol's canonical deployments, or from Chainlink's feed directory. Per-market feed and swap-route addresses, per-debt-reserve price sources and per-chain flash sources are recorded in each listing payload, in the chain's MarketRegistry and in the verified deployment manifest (`deployments/verified/`). Interfaces are vendored and checked against deployed bytecode (Source: Q-043, Q-074, Q-233, Q-365).

AMM protocols with a full integration at launch: Uniswap v3 on Ethereum (`EXT-006`, `EXT-007`) and Aerodrome Slipstream on Base (`EXT-028`). Uniswap v2, Curve StableSwap-NG, Balancer weighted and stable pools and Uniswap v4 are supported by the core and are listed only after their readiness guards pass (`EXT-032`). CoW Swap is out of scope (Source: Q-001, Q-062, Q-365).

## Aave V4

### EXT-001: Aave V4 Hubs

|---|---|

Avana's Hubs come in three tiers: Stable LP, Correlated LP and Volatile LP; each chain can host any of them. At launch there is one Avana Stable LP Hub on Ethereum and one on Base. Each Avana Hub holds the vault-token assets of its CDP Spokes' markets and the debt assets supplied through the chain's Lend Spoke. A debt reserve records its funding Hub; when that Hub is another Hub than the CDP Spoke's home Hub, the reserve is a cross-Hub credit line (`EXT-022`). Proxy and implementation addresses per chain are in the verified manifest. A Hub has no `extSload`, so its implementation is checked off-chain by the scheduled fork check on each chain |
| What Avana relies on | `add`, `remove`, `draw`, `restore`, `reportDeficit` and `eliminateDeficit` accounting per asset; `getAssetId(token)` to derive each vault-token asset; `getAddedAssets` and `getAddedShares` for the shares-equal-assets check; per-Spoke `addCap` and `drawCap` per asset, stored per `(asset, Spoke)` in each Hub; each debt asset carrying Avana's debt in that asset on its funding Hub, and each Coverage Spoke's shares of that asset on that Hub |

The scheduled off-chain check compares each Hub implementation on each chain with the manifest and alarms on a change (Source: Q-002, Q-068, Q-140, Q-175, Q-181, Q-189, Q-206, Q-233, Q-236, Q-365) |

## Aave V3 flash liquidity

### EXT-030: Aave V3 Pool `flashLoan` (the IFlashSource implementation and the flash liquidation helper)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Version / address | `aave-dao/aave-v3-origin` Pool, `flashLoan(receiver, assets, amounts, interestRateModes, onBehalfOf, params, referralCode)` with one asset and `interestRateModes = [0]`. Ethereum Core Pool `0x87870Bca3F3fD6335C3F4ce8392D69350B4fA4E2`; Base Pool `0xA238Dd80C259a72e81d7e4664a9801593F98d1c5`. Each chain's `IFlashSource` implementation is an Avana contract that calls this Pool for `deleverage` only; it is replaced only through the chain's timelock. The flash liquidation helper (periphery, `contracts/src/periphery/flash-liquidator/`), one public immutable instance per chain plus any operator's own instance of the operator variant, calls the same Pool directly. Aave V4 has no flash loan: neither `IHub` nor `ISpoke` exposes one |
| What Avana relies on | Two borrowers use the Pool, each borrowing the debt asset itself (GHO, USDC, USDT or WETH as listed) and repaying `amount + premium`, which the Pool pulls from the receiver with `safeTransferFrom`: LPVaultSwap's `deleverage` (a borrower's repay with collateral), through the chain's `IFlashSource` implementation; and the flash liquidation helper, which borrows for a caller that holds no debt asset, calls `LPVaultSwap.liquidate` and repays from the caller payout; the helper borrows every debt asset, GHO included, from this Pool and from no other lender (2.11 `REQ-HELPER-015`). `liquidate` itself takes no flash loan and depends on the Pool only through a caller that chooses the helper (Q-404). The Pool's checks: the reserve is active, not paused and `flashLoanEnabled`, and the aToken's supply covers the amount; a frozen reserve still lends. The Pool calls `executeOperation(assets, amounts, premiums, initiator, params)` on the receiver and has no reentrancy lock around the callback; the CDP Spoke's `nonReentrant` guard is per Spoke, so calling `liquidationCall` (through `liquidate`) or the deleverage repayment from inside the callback is allowed |
| Assumptions | Premium: the Pool charges `amount × FLASHLOAN_PREMIUM_TOTAL / 10⁴` in debt-asset base units rounded half-up (V3.4 `percentMul`), set to 5 bps at deployment and changeable by Aave governance; the source and the helper quote the ceiling `⌈amount × FLASHLOAN_PREMIUM_TOTAL / 10⁴⌉`, which is never below the charge; `flashLoan` (not `flashLoanSimple`) charges 0 when the receiver holds `FLASH_BORROWER` (`EXT-012`). The live premium and flags are read at each call, not assumed. The ceiling is the Pool's idle balance of the asset (deposits minus loans); on 2026-11-10 it was about 33M GHO, 200M USDC, 120M USDT and $800M of WETH on Ethereum Core, and about 0.13M GHO, 18M USDC and $44M of WETH on Base, at 76–95% utilisation, so it moves with every borrow and withdrawal |
| Failure mode | A reserve pause, deactivation or `flashLoanEnabled = false` (the Aave Guardian can pause a reserve in one transaction), a premium increase, or idle liquidity below the amount stops `deleverage` and helper-funded liquidation of that debt asset on that chain; a caller that holds the debt asset still liquidates by calling `liquidate` directly, so liquidation itself never depends on the Pool; long-run V3 liquidity may shrink as deposits move to V4 |
| Mitigating rule | `INV-095`: before a `deleverage` loan the source checks `amount ≤ idle` and `premium × 10⁴ ≤ maxFlashFeeBps × amount` (exact, no rounding), and reverts otherwise; in the callback it requires `msg.sender == POOL`, `initiator == address(this)`, one asset, and the active deleverage context, and approves the Pool for exactly `amount + premium`; the source reports the pool or Hub it borrows from (`LENDER()`, the V3 Pool here), and the source is refused by `MarketRegistry.setFlashSource` (`InvalidRoute`) and by LPVaultSwap on every `deleverage` call (`FlashSourceIsHub`) when `isRecordedHub(LENDER())`, that is when that address equals any Hub recorded on the chain (`ATTACK-195`), so a later flash Spoke that draws on a registered Hub is refused even though its own address is not a Hub; LPVaultSwap measures the debt asset it receives from the source by balance delta, and a debt asset with a non-zero transfer fee trips that reserve's debt breaker (`ISSUER`, read by the issuer probe `basisPointsRate()`) and is refused at listing (`ATTACK-197`). The helper applies the same rules to its own loans (2.11 `REQ-HELPER-002` to `REQ-HELPER-009`): it refuses a Pool recorded as a Hub, reverts with `InsufficientFlashLiquidity` before the Pool is called when idle liquidity is short, binds the Pool, initiator, asset, amount, maximum premium and a single-use context in its callback, and rejects a premium above its caller's `maxPremium` or above `maxFlashFeeBps`. When the source refuses, the borrower repays from its own funds; when the helper refuses, the caller funds `debtToCover` from its own debt asset through `liquidate` (`INV-046`), and each committed operator holds debt-asset inventory of at least the largest single liquidation the chain's caps allow (2.11 `REQ-SWAP-014`); the timelock can replace the deleverage source (`INV-051`). Worked example: a 1,000,000 USDC loan at 5 bps costs `⌈1,000,000·10⁶ × 5 / 10⁴⌉ = 500·10⁶` base units (500 USDC) without the role and 0 with it; with `maxFlashFeeBps = 0` a helper call without the role reverts before LPVaultSwap is called, and the caller either funds the debt itself or waits for the grant.
