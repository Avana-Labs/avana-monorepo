# 3.6 Emergency Operations (FLOW-006)

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-09
>
> **Step prefix:** `EMG-NNN` · failures `EMG-F-NNN` · postconditions `EMG-POST-NNN`

The emergency matrix below lists, for every state Avana can be in, which user actions are open and which contract enforces each block; the procedures after it move the protocol between those states, and the playbooks give the ordered response to each kind of incident. The matrix is `Q-118` as amended by `Q-121`, `Q-128`, `Q-132`, `Q-140`, `Q-162`, `Q-182`, `Q-183`, `Q-189`, `Q-192`, `Q-195`, `Q-199`, `Q-202`, `Q-203`, `Q-206`, `Q-208`, `Q-209`, `Q-227`, `Q-229`, `Q-233`, `Q-234`, `Q-235`, `Q-236`, `Q-238`, `Q-241`, `Q-242`, `Q-247`, `Q-248` and `Q-252`. Every control is per chain: Ethereum and Base each have their own AvanaTimelock, governance Safe, Emergency Guardian Safe, MarketRegistry, keepers and alert routing, and no call on one chain changes state on the other. Within a chain every control is keyed by Hub, and by CDP Spoke and market where it applies. Avana restricts new activity at three levels, all held by the chain's MarketRegistry: one application pause state per Hub (`None`, `Frozen` or `Paused` with a reason), which covers every CDP Spoke and market homed on that Hub; circuit breakers per market and per (CDP Spoke, debt reserve), which the RiskModule evaluates; and a set of frozen addresses per chain, which the AaveAdapter, the ProxyManager and LPVaultSwap check. Two chain-level gates sit beside them: the sequencer gate on an L2 (`Up`, `Down`, `Grace`, Q-357) and the Aave-side flags of each CDP Spoke and reserve (Q-351). None of the three Avana levels ever reaches repay, debt-free withdrawal or partial removal, fee collection, zero-credit release, pending settlement or liquidation. Contract-level rules for each gate are in [2.6 Aave Adapter](../02-contracts/06-aave-adapter.md), [2.7 Risk Module](../02-contracts/07-risk-module.md), [2.8 Market Registry](../02-contracts/08-market-registry.md), [2.9 Parameter Store](../02-contracts/09-parameter-store.md), [2.10 LP Oracle](../02-contracts/10-lp-oracle.md), [2.11 LPVaultSwap](../02-contracts/11-lpvaultswap.md) and [2.15 Governance and Timelock](../02-contracts/15-governance-and-timelock.md). Parameters are named as in [1.5 Protocol Parameters](../01-architecture/05-parameters.md). Alerts `M-01` to `M-27` are defined in the monitoring section of [services](../../services/README.md#monitoring-and-alerting).

## Objective

Keep exits and liquidation open and losses bounded when prices, Aave configuration, external dependencies, keepers, Avana code or governance keys fail: repay, fee collection, debt-free withdrawal and partial removal, pending settlement and liquidation never stop for an Avana pause, breaker or address freeze, and stop only when Aave itself blocks them or, for liquidation, when the seized market or the repaid debt reserve has no fresh, valid and agreed price; a failing price elsewhere in the account only makes the account easier to liquidate. New risk stops first; withdraw-with-debt stops whenever liquidation of any reserve the account holds is halted; rescue top-ups stay open except when the minting code itself is suspect; and every loosening lands through the timelock, where borrowers can see it and leave before it executes.

## Preconditions

Every emergency selector is restricted to exactly the roles listed under Initiator, as mapped in [2.15](../02-contracts/15-governance-and-timelock.md) and [1.4](../01-architecture/04-permissions-and-roles.md); none is public except execution of a ready timelock operation, `poke()` and the exit and liquidation paths. The application pause state, the address freeze and the breakers are read at the time of each call; price states, breaker conditions and Aave-side flags are evaluated per read.

## Emergency matrix

`Y` = allowed; `N` = blocked; `Y*` = allowed with the stated condition. The enforcing contract is named in brackets: (Aave) upstream check; (Adapter) AaveAdapter pause-state, address-freeze and call-bound check; (RiskModule) pledge and borrow-side checks, breakers included, which the Adapter and Collateralization call and whose errors they return; (LPVaultSwap); (Collateralization). Column meanings: **New pledge** opens an account or adds collateral to an account with no debt on any debt reserve; **Top-up** adds collateral to an account with debt on at least one debt reserve, and includes a re-credit of added liquidity; **Withdraw (no debt)** is withdrawal or partial removal by an account with zero debt on every debt reserve and includes `repayAllAndWithdraw` and the release of a zero-credit record, which makes no Spoke call; **Withdraw (with debt)** includes partial removal with debt and repay with collateral; **Liquidation** covers `liquidate`, the one entry point (direct or through a flash liquidation helper, `Q-404`), and pending settlement and follows the per-reserve price gate of [FLOW-005 `LIQ-006`](05-liquidation.md): only the seized collateral market and the repaid debt reserve need fresh, agreeing prices (`Q-247`). **Y\* rescue only** admits a rescue top-up: an account with debt whose health factor is below the rescue target HF 1.10 (the Lens recommendation; no opening floor exists, `Q-398`), a booked charge at most the restore amount, a pledge of at least `min(minPledgeUsd, $10)`, and every other pledge check (exact range or shape, price state Live or Drifting, market not offboarding, cap tests, Hub `addCap`); any other top-up reverts `NotRescueTopUp` (RiskModule, `REQ-RISK-066`, `Q-252`). Units admitted this way are rescue units and never support new debt (`REQ-RISK-068`). Repay, fee collection, debt-free withdrawal and liquidation never read the application pause state, a breaker or the address freeze, so none of them blocks those paths; repay with collateral reads only `Paused(MintPathSuspect)` (Q-356). The Aave-side rows (`EMG-014` to `EMG-016`) and the sequencer rows (`EMG-093`, `EMG-094`) are the exceptions to "exits never stop": they come from Aave and from the L2 itself, not from an Avana control. Each row applies per Hub (pause state) or per market and debt reserve (the other rows) on the chain where it holds. Withdraw-with-debt is blocked whenever liquidation of any reserve the account holds is halted, because a withdrawal that cannot be followed by a liquidation would let an account take collateral out while its debt is unprotected (`Q-206`).

## Transitions and procedures

### Oracle states and recovery

- `EMG-030` Each market's LPOracle computes one state per read as a predicate with precedence Expired > Cached > Drifting > Live; one stale leg makes the market Cached, the cache is stored per feed, and the TWAP is compared with the price actually served (`Q-130`). States have no hysteresis, because they are computed per read. The only stored oracle state is the cached price, the Cached start time, the disagreement start time (`EMG-036`), the grace latch (`EMG-034`) and the hourly price checkpoint of the price-drop breaker (`REQ-ORACLE-088`). Live: every token leg has a fresh, valid primary or alternate Chainlink answer and no Drifting condition holds. Drifting: the TWAP is outside `consistencyToleranceBps` (read in ticks for concentrated liquidity) of the Chainlink-implied tick, `observe()` fails, an answer breaches its wide bound, the USD level leaves the band (`EMG-038`), or both routes of a token are fresh and disagree (`EMG-036`). A USD-band breach alone does not block new-account pledges (`EMG-004`). The band's upper bound is tested on capped stable prices and its lower bound on uncapped prices, and valuation applies `stableCeilingUsd`, so GHO markets stay Live above peg. Each non-GHO debt reserve's DebtOracle runs the same pipeline on its asset's routes: freshness, failover, agreement, Cached and Expired, without a stable ceiling. The exact predicates are in [2.10 LP Oracle](../02-contracts/10-lp-oracle.md). (Source: Q-112, Q-119, Q-128, Q-130, Q-176, Q-177, Q-195, Q-200, Q-241, Q-248, Q-275)
- `EMG-031` Failover from a token's primary to its alternate Chainlink route is automatic inside the feed; the market stays Live while the alternate passes freshness, and the agreement check runs only while both report. While the alternate serves, liquidation of the market also requires every alternate leg to be at most its `borrowPriceMaxAge` old, else the token counts as Cached for liquidation (`REQ-ORACLE-090`). No governance action is involved. (Source: Q-036, Q-091, Q-130, Q-274)
- `EMG-032` Cached starts at the first read (by `poke()` or an Avana call) at which both routes of a token fail freshness or validity, and lasts `cachedWindow`. The feed keeps serving the last accepted positive price with its original timestamp; reading or caching the same round never refreshes that timestamp. Borrow and withdraw-with-debt stop in the RiskModule, which pokes every collateral market of the account and checks every collateral and debt price's source timestamp against `borrowPriceMaxAge` (`Q-229`); liquidation stops in LPVaultSwap for seizures of that market only, and the account's other reserves stay liquidatable with the market at its last accepted price. (Source: Q-079, Q-128, Q-182, Q-183, Q-229, Q-247)
- `EMG-033` Expired begins when Cached has lasted `cachedWindow`. It is flagged only from the source timestamps of both routes, never from a caught error. From then on the LPOracle serves `latestAnswer() = 1` (10⁻⁸ USD per whole vault token) until a fresh price is accepted: it never reverts and never returns 0, so Aave keeps valuing positions and repay and debt-free withdrawal keep working, and the market counts as worthless in the health factor of every account holding it, which can only make those accounts easier to liquidate on their fresh reserves. A DebtOracle that is Expired serves its last accepted price raised by `debtStressRiseBps`, which can only raise the debt's value. Avana's own valuations (caps, minimum pledge value, restore amount, previews) keep using the last accepted price (`REQ-ORACLE-087`). (Source: Q-056, Q-128, Q-154, Q-183, Q-247, Q-248)
- `EMG-034` Grace after recovery: the first fresh, valid update after an Expired flag stores `recoveredAt` (that update's source timestamp) and emits `GraceStarted(recoveredAt, graceEndsAt, fromLatch)`; until `recoveredAt + graceDuration` LPVaultSwap liquidates only accounts below `graceHealthFactor`; one grace window per outage. When the recovering round is the first of a new Chainlink aggregator phase, the recovery evidence is read from the last round of the previous phase through the proxy's `phaseAggregators(phaseId − 1)` (`REQ-ORACLE-089`); without usable recovery evidence the window starts at the newest input of the fresh price, `max(t0, t1)`, never after `block.timestamp`. `poke()` requires `pokeMinGas` and a gas-starved `poke()` reverts without changing any latch, price or timestamp. Checked by: QNT-24, HYP-37, HYP-50, SIM-02E. (Source: Q-088, Q-129, Q-154, Q-264, Q-272)
- `EMG-035` Outage procedure: keepers call `poke()` on every LPOracle and DebtOracle every heartbeat, since `poke()` is the permissionless cache write that any Avana transaction also performs (`Q-056`); on Cached or Expired, operators are paged (`M-01`, `M-03`, `M-04`), confirm that the alternate route took over (failover is immediate), and follow playbook P-02 (`EMG-076`): when a listed standby route is fresh and agrees, a guardian switches to it at once (`EMG-091`); a new price source or a change to price computation is a timelock operation at the normal delay, because a price source the listing did not review decides who can be liquidated. (Source: Q-091, Q-119, Q-182, Q-199, Q-228, Q-243, Q-248)

### Feed disagreement and USD band

- `EMG-036` When `poke()` or a successful Avana call first observes a token's primary and alternate routes both fresh and differing by more than `feedAgreementToleranceBps` (`|p − b| × 10,000 > T × min(p, b)`), the LPOracle stores that time and emits `DisagreementRecorded`. The market is Drifting while the disagreement lasts. Seizure of the market waits from the stored time until `disagreementPauseMax` after it; if the routes still disagree at that bound, the market is treated as Expired for its own seizure until the routes agree again, a standby switch makes it Live (`EMG-091`) or a fix executes through the timelock (`Q-208`). The account's other reserves stay liquidatable, with the disagreeing market valued at the LP price built from the lower of each token's two route answers (`REQ-ORACLE-086`), so the disagreement can only make an account easier to liquidate on its fresh reserves. Repay and debt-free withdrawal stay open, and borrowing and withdraw-with-debt stay blocked while the disagreement lasts. A later return to agreement or a standby switch clears the stored time. The market's own seizure waits rather than resuming at the served price, because one of two fresh routes contradicts that price and seizing at it would let a faulty route drive seizures. Example: with primary and alternate deviation thresholds of 0.25 % and 1 % and the 50 bps margin, `T = 175` bps; USDT at `p = 0.99800000` and `b = 0.97900000` gives `|p − b| × 10,000 = 0.019 × 10,000 = 190 > 175 × 0.979 = 171.3`, so the routes disagree. Checked by: QNT-22, QNT-T18, HYP-56, SIM-01E. (Source: Q-112, Q-162, Q-183, Q-184, Q-196, Q-199, Q-208, Q-247, Q-248)
- `EMG-037` Disagreement procedure (playbook P-02, `EMG-076`): operators determine which route is wrong from `getPriceState`; if the token's listed standby route is fresh and agrees with the correct route, a guardian replaces the faulty slot with it at once (`EMG-091`); otherwise replacing the faulty route is a new price source scheduled at the normal delay. Meanwhile the Aave Protocol Guardian MAY freeze the market's vault-token reserve on Aave (`EMG-050`) to stop new pledges into it, a guardian MAY trip the market breaker, and the Emergency Guardian MAY freeze the application if the fault spans markets. (Source: Q-091, Q-162, Q-208, Q-227, Q-241, Q-248)
- `EMG-038` When the LP's USD level per unit of liquidity leaves `[usdBandLow, usdBandHigh]`, new borrowing and withdraw-with-debt against that market stop; pledges, repay, fee collection, debt-free withdrawal and liquidation continue, liquidation only while both Chainlink routes of each token are fresh and agree. Operators treat a breach as a possible common-mode feed error (`M-05`, playbook P-03) and decide whether to ask for an Aave reserve freeze of the market, trip the market breaker or freeze the application. (Source: Q-176, Q-195, Q-206, Q-227, Q-241, Q-243)

### Application pause state

- `EMG-039` The chain's MarketRegistry holds one `PauseState` per Hub, `None`, `Frozen` or `Paused`, and with `Paused` a `PauseReason`, `General` or `MintPathSuspect` (`REQ-MREG-024`); a guardian acts on one Hub with `freeze(hub)` or `pause(hub, reason)`, or on every Hub of the chain in one call with `freezeAll()` or `pauseAll(reason)` on the MarketRegistry, which leave a Hub already in the target state unchanged, emit `PauseStateChanged` once per Hub changed and revert `AlreadyFrozen` or `AlreadyPaused` when no Hub changes; a Hub the MarketRegistry does not list reverts. Ethereum and Base are paused separately by their own guardians. The AaveAdapter reads it on pledge, re-credit, borrow, withdraw-with-debt, partial removal with debt and repay with collateral, and the Lens reports it; LPVaultSwap, the LPVault, Collateralization's zero-credit release, the RiskModule, the ProxyManager and the ParameterStore never read it, and no contract carries a switch of its own. Frozen blocks pledges by accounts with no debt and borrowing; it allows top-ups of indebted accounts, which add collateral to accounts that may need rescue, and withdraw-with-debt with every check of `Q-229`. `Paused(General)` also blocks withdraw-with-debt and admits only rescue top-ups (`EMG-018`) and repay with collateral that only reduces debt (Q-356), so a borrower facing liquidation can still add collateral, capped at the restore amount and never usable for new debt. `Paused(MintPathSuspect)` blocks every pledge, top-up and re-credit, rescue included (`EMG-071`), because every top-up mints vault tokens and is unsafe when the Adapter's or Collateralization's minting code itself is suspect. Neither state stops repay, fee collection, debt-free withdrawal or partial removal, zero-credit release, liquidation or pending settlement: stopping liquidation lets unhealthy accounts lose value with nobody able to close them, so a pause would turn a code incident into bad debt; a fault in LPVaultSwap is bounded instead by Aave's liquidation sizing, Collateralization's share-drop and supply-drop checks and the timelock; LPVaultSwap holds no budget (`Q-397`). Checked by: QNT-14, QNT-15, QNT-16. (Source: Q-118, Q-164, Q-227, Q-252)
- `EMG-040` Transitions, per Hub `h`: `freeze(h)` moves `None` → `Frozen`, callable by `APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN` or `APPLICATION_ADMIN`; `pause(h, reason)` moves `None` or `Frozen` → `Paused(reason)` and `Paused(r)` → `Paused(r′)` for `r′ ≠ r`, callable by `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN` or `APPLICATION_ADMIN` (`REQ-ACCESS-162`); `reason = None`, or the current reason while `Paused`, reverts `InvalidPauseReason`; `unfreeze(h)` moves `Frozen` → `None` and `unpause(h)` moves `Paused` → `None` and clears the reason, each callable only by `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN` or `APPLICATION_ADMIN`. A chain-wide stop is the same transition applied per Hub from `getHubs()`, with the same roles. Every successful transition emits `PauseStateChanged(hub, previous, current, caller)` and every reason change `PauseReasonChanged(hub, previous, current, caller)`. Both guardians hold both guardian roles, so either raises `General` to `MintPathSuspect` at once. A move from `Paused` to `Frozen` is two calls by the pause guardian, `unpause(h)` then `freeze(h)`. The guardian holders are the Emergency Guardian and the Aave Protocol Guardian; `APPLICATION_ADMIN` is held only by the timelock (`REQ-ACCESS-153`). (Source: Q-227, Q-228, Q-252, Q-259)
- `EMG-041` A pause state, a breaker and an address freeze have no on-chain expiry and need none (a temporary price or depth gate clears after 30 minutes of healthy observations; every other latch needs authorized reopening, `Q-399`), because none of them stops repay, fee collection, debt-free withdrawal or liquidation. Caps are sized by `Q-192` for the 48-hour incident window, which covers the liquidation stops that remain, each with its real maximum: an Aave-side reserve pause (`EMG-051`) lasts until the Aave DAO unpauses, which the listing asks it to do within 48 hours; a market's own price failure (`EMG-005` to `EMG-007`) or a debt reserve's failing price (`EMG-020`) halts only that market's seizure and that reserve's repayment, and a guardian ends it within the incident window by switching to the listed standby route (`EMG-091`); where the price is fresh but conversion fails, by restoring or replacing a swap route (`EMG-057`, P-04); only when the standby also fails does the stop last until a new price source executes at the normal delay, during which stress valuation keeps the account's other reserves liquidatable (`EMG-073`). The 48-hour cap sizing assumes the standby switch. (Source: Q-192, Q-202, Q-227, Q-241, Q-242, Q-247, Q-248)
- `EMG-042` Only the pause guardian or the application admin lifts a state: the Emergency Guardian or the Aave Protocol Guardian immediately, or the timelock through an `unfreeze(hub)` or `unpause(hub)` call in a normal-delay operation. Both guardians hold both `APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN` and `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN` at launch; the roles are separate so that a key holding only `APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN`, issued for fast containment, can freeze the application, freeze addresses and trip breakers without being able to unfreeze, unpause or reset, and a guardian that will not lift a state is overridden by the governance multisig's normal-delay operation. (Source: Q-227, Q-228, Q-241, Q-242)
- `EMG-043` Restoring reviewed configuration: a route disabled during an incident is restored with `MarketRegistry.restoreRoute`, which accept only a previously approved route, in a short-delay operation (12 hours) that only `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` can cancel (`EMG-048`); a token's listed price routes are restored after a standby switch with `restorePriceRoute` at the normal delay (`EMG-091`); every other value is the value the timelock set, with no approved/live split and no instant restore. (Source: Q-151, Q-228, Q-248, Q-263)
- `EMG-044` A guardian may freeze or pause again at any time after a lift; there is no cooldown. A guardian holding a state open indefinitely costs only new pledges, borrowing, non-rescue top-ups and withdraw-with-debt, never exits, rescue top-ups (outside `MintPathSuspect`) or liquidation; the governance multisig lifts it with a normal-delay `unpause(hub)` or `unfreeze(hub)` and removes the guardian with a role-change operation that the guardian cannot cancel (`EMG-048`). (Source: Q-227, Q-228, Q-252)
- `EMG-045` Address freeze: the MarketRegistry keeps a set of frozen addresses (`REQ-MREG-019` to `REQ-MREG-023`); a frozen address, as the calling wallet, cannot open an account, make a non-rescue top-up or re-credit, borrow or be a borrow receiver, withdraw or remove liquidity with debt, repay with collateral, have a proxy created, call `liquidate` directly, or be added as a committed operator (`EMG-070`). The freeze binds `msg.sender` only: a frozen address that liquidates through the public flash liquidation helper reaches LPVaultSwap as the helper, so the freeze stops its direct liquidation calls and removes a frozen committed operator from `keeperFloor`, and cannot stop it from liquidating; every check that protects Avana and the borrower runs whoever calls (`REQ-SWAP-105`, `Q-404`). It keeps repay as payer or borrower, debt-free withdrawal and partial removal, `repayAllAndWithdraw`, fee collection, zero-credit release, rescue top-ups, PayoutEscrow claims and `settlePending`, its positions are liquidated as any account's are, and contract-to-contract calls are never checked. The vault token is unchanged: immutable, with no blocklist, because a token-level block would also stop liquidation transfers. Avana has no per-function restriction and no Avana market freeze, pool-broken flag, keeper or route suspension, tolerance override, quarantine, incident clock, new-incident cooldown or separate recovery signer set; per-market and per-debt-reserve restriction is the circuit breakers (`EMG-068`, `EMG-069`), together with the Aave freeze or pause of the market's vault-token reserve, which the RiskModule reads, and the MarketRegistry status `Offboarding`. A freeze limits an address, not a person: a frozen actor can exit debt-free and start again from another wallet, so the freeze is an incident tool for stopping an attacker's contract while the incident is handled. (Source: Q-227, Q-241, Q-242, Q-404)
- `EMG-046` Compromised or failing actors are handled without quarantine: a misbehaving committed operator is removed from the keeper registry with `LPVaultSwap.removeKeeper` in a short-delay operation while at least `keeperFloor` active committed operators remain, or its address is frozen at once with no floor check; neither removes any power over liquidation, because the registry gates no call (`Q-404`); a queued hostile operation is cancelled by any canceller before it is ready; a compromised Emergency Guardian can only freeze, pause, freeze addresses outside the protected set (at most 20 in any 24 one-hour buckets), trip and reset breakers, switch a token to its listed standby route (which the Aave governance executor undoes at once) and cancel operations that are neither role changes nor restoring operations; it never touches exits or liquidation, cannot hold a market unliquidatable by cancelling its repairs, and loses its roles through a normal-delay role-change operation that only the Aave governance executor can cancel; a compromised governance multisig can only propose, every proposal waits its full delay in public with independent cancellers, and the Aave governance executor, as second proposer, schedules the repair if the multisig's quorum is lost (`REQ-ACCESS-160`). Checked by: QNT-34, HYP-39. (Source: Q-139, Q-155, Q-228, Q-241, Q-242, Q-245, Q-248, Q-263, Q-404)
- `EMG-047` Whenever liquidation of a reserve an account holds is halted (a collateral market Cached, Expired, in a disagreement pause or treated as Expired after a persistent disagreement; a failing non-fixed debt price; Hub shares ≠ assets; an Aave pause of a vault-token or debt reserve of the account; both swap-route records of a pool token disabled; or zero active committed operators on the chain, which halts new borrowing on every Hub of the chain while `liquidate` stays open to every caller), the RiskModule blocks borrowing and withdraw-with-debt for that account; repay stays open where Aave permits. The halt applies to seizing that market or repaying that debt; the account's other reserves stay liquidatable (`EMG-073`). The application pause state, the breakers and the address freeze are not liquidation halts; the Adapter checks the pause state and the freeze separately and the RiskModule the breakers. Checked by: QNT-38. (Source: Q-189, Q-206, Q-208, Q-213, Q-227, Q-229, Q-234, Q-238, Q-242, Q-247, Q-404)
- `EMG-048` Cancellation: any `CANCELLER_ROLE` holder (governance multisig, Emergency Guardian, Aave Protocol Guardian, Aave governance executor) cancels any queued operation that is neither a role-change nor a restoring operation, immediately. An operation whose every call is `grantRole`, `revokeRole` or `renounceRole` on an Avana contract or on the timelock is cancellable only by `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` (the Aave governance executor), so no guardian can block its own removal or a replacement's appointment. An operation whose every call is in the DAO-only cancel set (`restoreRoute`, `useStandbyRoute`, `addKeeper`, `unfreezeAddress`; `REQ-ACCESS-156`) is likewise cancellable only by `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` and reverts `RestoringCancelRestricted(id)` for any other canceller, so a compromised canceller cannot keep a market unliquidatable or an address frozen by cancelling every repair; an operation that mixes such calls with any other call keeps the ordinary cancel rule, so a proposer cannot hide another call behind a repair. (Source: Q-136, Q-228, Q-263)
- `EMG-049` Tightening and new-activity stops run through the timelock at the short delay (12 hours) only when every call in the operation is in the immutable `SHORT_ELIGIBLE` set fixed in the AvanaTimelock's constructor (`REQ-ACCESS-155`): every `tighten*` setter of the ParameterStore (the breaker thresholds, `tightenFloorRoundingAllowance`, `tightenMaxFlashFeeBps` and `tightenMaxShortfallBps` included; the SettlementBudget and its setters no longer exist, `Q-397`), `disableRoute` and `restoreRoute` (previously approved routes only), `removeKeeper` down to `keeperFloor` (1), `setMarketOffboarding`, `activateMarket`, `cancelRegistration`, and the stops `freeze`, `pause`, `freezeAddress`, `tripMarketBreaker`, `tripDebtBreaker` and `useStandbyRoute`. `setShortDelaySelector` can only disable an entry or enable one inside `SHORT_ELIGIBLE`. Anything else waits the normal delay (5 days), which `setNormalDelay` can never set below `MIN_NORMAL_DELAY` (4 days), the bound that keeps every borrower's exit window (`SPEC-GOV-015`); each loosening is also limited in size and spacing by the ParameterStore's step limits (`REQ-ACCESS-161`, `SPEC-GOV-011`). A tighten setter reverts unless the new value is strictly safer and inside its hard bounds and the liquidation-liveness bounds. The stops are on the list so the governance multisig can halt new activity within 12 hours when both guardians are unavailable; fast containment inside those 12 hours is the guardians' immediate stops and the Aave reserve freeze. (Source: Q-114, Q-119, Q-139, Q-228, Q-246, Q-262)

### Per-market containment

- `EMG-050` A market is contained by the Aave freeze of its vault-token reserve (`SpokeConfigurator.freezeReserve`, one-way for the Aave Protocol Guardian), which makes Aave reject new supply. The RiskModule rejects every pledge into a market whose vault-token reserve is frozen (`AaveReserveUnavailable`) and every borrow by an account holding any frozen vault-token reserve (`ReserveFrozen(reserveId)`); withdrawals, repay, fee collection and liquidation continue. The freeze is lifted only by the Aave DAO (`updateFrozen`), because the DAO grants the Aave Protocol Guardian only the one-way `freezeReserve` and `pauseReserve` selectors, never `updateFrozen`, `updatePaused` or the domain-admin role. Avana's own immediate per-market stop is the manual market breaker (`EMG-089`). A lasting problem moves the market to `Offboarding` (`EMG-060`). Checked by: QNT-19, QNT-T04. (Source: Q-118, Q-119, Q-203, Q-227, Q-241)

### Aave-side freeze, pause and halt

- `EMG-051` Aave-side reserve freezes and pauses of vault-token and debt reserves are set by the Aave Protocol Guardian (one-way) or the DAO and lifted only by the DAO; Avana cannot lift them, so a pause of a vault-token or debt reserve can block liquidation until the DAO acts. This is an Aave-governed risk: the listing AIP asks the Aave DAO to pause Avana reserves, every debt reserve included, only for exploits and to unpause within the 48-hour incident window, and caps are sized for such a pause with the pause-loss bound (`Q-192`, [FLOW-008 `MKT-013`](08-market-lifecycle.md)). Avana's gates read the reserve flags, and the matrix rows `EMG-013` to `EMG-015` apply. (Source: Q-118, Q-119, Q-192, Q-202, Q-227)
- `EMG-052` A Hub halt of a CDP Spoke (`HubConfigurator.haltSpoke`, `deactivateSpoke`, or `updateSpokeHalted` for one asset) is set and lifted by Aave governance; while it lasts the Hub refuses `restore`, `remove` and the seizure transfer, so repay, debt-free withdrawal and liquidation of every market on that Spoke stop, and only fee collection and zero-credit release stay open (`EMG-016`). This is Aave-side containment, not an Avana pause, and it is the one exception to "pause states never block exits", an accepted risk handled by playbook P-16 (AMMN-FLOW-12). Avana's listing agreement with the Aave DAO therefore states the containment order for a CDP Spoke: (1) freeze the vault-token reserve, (2) set `drawCap = 0` on every debt asset for the Spoke, (3) Avana's own pause and breakers; `haltSpoke`, `deactivateSpoke` and `pauseReserve` are used only when the Spoke itself is faulty and no debt can be served safely. The RiskModule reports a halt as drift reason `SpokeHalted`, monitoring pages within one block (`M-20`), and playbook P-16 (`EMG-099`) applies. `Hub.eliminateDeficit` checks only that the caller Spoke is active, so deficit coverage by each Coverage Spoke continues. (Source: Q-140, Q-142, Q-351)

### Config drift and shares ≠ assets alarms

- `EMG-053` The RiskModule checks on every new-account pledge, every borrow and every withdraw-with-debt, and monitoring (`M-20`) and the deployment check verify continuously, for each CDP Spoke, which is a standard, unmodified Aave V4 Spoke: that its implementation (read through `extSload` at the EIP-1967 slot) is the one approved in the MarketRegistry; that the Spoke's approved debt reserves equal the MarketRegistry's set and each records its funding Hub; that each approved debt reserve's Aave oracle source equals the source the MarketRegistry recorded for it (the reserve's DebtOracle for a non-GHO asset, the fixed source for GHO); that the Spoke is active and not halted on the Hub for every asset it uses (reason `SpokeHalted`, Q-351); that for each market: the vault-token reserve has `receiveSharesEnabled = false`, `borrowable = false` and `liquidationFee = 0`; its price source is the market's LPOracle; its `collateralRisk ≤ riskPremiumThreshold − 1` bps; the vault-token Hub asset is listed only on its CDP Spoke and its fee receiver, every Spoke has `drawCap = 0` on it and its drawn shares are 0; `reinvestmentController = address(0)` on the vault-token and debt assets; and no position manager is approved on any registered proxy; and that Aave's risk configuration matches what Avana's caps assume: no dynamic key's collateral factor above `expectedCollateralFactorBps(m)`, `⌈maxLB × CF / 10⁴⌉ < 10⁴`, the minimum bonus at least `minLiquidationBonus`, and the target health factor equal to `aaveTargetHealthFactor` (`REQ-RISK-069`, drift reasons `CollateralFactorAboveExpected`, `BonusBoundViolated`, `LiquidationConfigChanged`). A drift in any standard-Spoke setting trips the market's CONFIG_DRIFT breaker (row `EMG-095`, playbook P-14), which stops new activity and never liquidation: liquidation exclusivity does not depend on the settings alone, because the vault token admits a transfer from the home Hub only to a registered proxy of the market (withdrawal) or to LPVaultSwap (seizure), so any other liquidator's `liquidationCall` that seizes at least one share reverts on the seizure transfer (Q-352). Avana's keeper simulates `RiskModule.pokeConfig(spoke)` on every CDP Spoke and `pokeBreakers(marketId)` on every market every block, and sends the transaction only when the simulated call would change state: `pokeConfig` finds a drift, so `ConfigDriftDetected` is emitted on-chain and `M-20` pages, or `pokeBreakers` finds a reason to record (`recordBreach` refreshing a breach time or latching a security freeze). Detection therefore still happens in the block after a change, while blocks with nothing to record cost no gas (Q-382). A DAO upgrade of the CDP Spoke closes new-account pledges, borrows and withdraw-with-debt like any other drift until the chain's timelock re-approves the reviewed implementation (`approveSpokeCodeHash` for its hash, then `deployCdpSpoke(def)` with it, normal delay); repay, debt-free withdrawals, fee collection and liquidation continue. (Source: Q-080, Q-172, Q-178, Q-181, Q-222, Q-237, Q-238, Q-248, Q-270, Q-352)
- `EMG-054` On drift the RiskModule blocks new accounts, pledges by accounts without debt, borrows and withdraw-with-debt, while top-ups of accounts with debt, repay, debt-free withdrawals and liquidation continue; monitoring alerts; the Emergency Guardian decides on an application freeze and the Aave DAO restores the setting by payload. For a collateral-factor cut Aave plans, Avana first tightens `poolRiskFactor` at the short delay so the drift check stops new borrowing while Aave's factor is above the new expected one (`REQ-ACCESS-161`). (Source: Q-080, Q-144, Q-190, Q-227, Q-246)
- `EMG-055` When `Hub.getAddedShares(assetId) ≠ Hub.getAddedAssets(assetId)` for a vault-token asset, the RiskModule stops new pledges, borrows and withdraw-with-debt for accounts holding that market, top-ups of accounts with debt continue, and liquidation of that market stops (LPVaultSwap and Collateralization revert with `SharesNotEqualAssets`) until equality is restored by Aave governance; monitoring raises the alarm and the Aave Protocol Guardian freezes the vault-token reserve. While the drift checks of `EMG-053` hold, Hub `add` and `remove` keep shares equal to assets. Checked by: MATH-ST-41, MATH-AR-26, MATH-AR-27. (Source: Q-103, Q-144, Q-181, Q-189, Q-206)

### Route failure, flash source and keepers

- `EMG-056` Route failure: in `PrimaryThenBackup` mode LPVaultSwap falls back, per pool token, to the backup swap route into the debt asset inside the call; a backup DEX route never trades through the market's own pool, because each seizure removes depth from that pool. Every failed primary leg emits `RouteFailed`; monitoring alerts at 3 route failures in 1 hour, and a guardian freezes new borrowing only once the approved routes are confirmed unable to settle valid liquidations at the required size (`EMG-092`, `Q-399`). No on-chain failure counter exists, because reverted calls cannot persist counts and a counter could be gamed. The governance multisig disables a failing route with `MarketRegistry.disableRoute` and restores a previously approved one with `restoreRoute`, each in a short-delay operation; adding or changing a route (`setRoute`) waits the normal delay. A route that fails at run time needs no action for liquidation to continue, because the fallback runs inside each call. (Source: Q-062, Q-084, Q-151, Q-228, Q-235, Q-241, Q-273, Q-358, Q-399)
- `EMG-057` When both swap routes of a market are disabled or failing, there is no in-kind window to open (`Q-401`). Committed operators keep trying the primary and backup routes with the shortfall top-up from their own debt-asset funds; a guardian freezes new borrowing on the market (`tripMarketBreaker`, MANUAL) once the approved routes are confirmed unable to settle valid liquidations at the required size; the governance multisig restores a route at the short delay (`restoreRoute`, DAO-only cancel) or adds a new route at the normal delay. Liquidation, repay and debt-free exits stay open throughout. Why: in-kind settlement needed its own valuation, governance trigger and expiry, and the route set, caller top-ups and route replacement settle every liquidation without it. Example: both USDT→USDC routes of market 1 revert from 14:00; at 14:20 the third failure in the hour pages on-call, at 14:35 a fork simulation confirms neither route fills 9,800 USDC within `slippageAllowanceBps`, the Emergency Guardian calls `tripMarketBreaker(1)`, and a `setRoute` operation for a new route is scheduled; accounts below HF 1 are liquidated once it executes. Checked by: SIM-06B. (Source: Q-133, Q-163, Q-228, Q-235, Q-251, Q-263, Q-399, Q-401, Q-404)
- `EMG-058` A liquidation takes no flash loan (`Q-404`): the caller funds `debtToCover` from its own debt asset or through a flash liquidation helper, which flash-borrows from the chain's Aave V3 Pool, calls `liquidate` and repays from the payout. When the Pool cannot lend the debt asset (idle liquidity below the loan, reserve paused, inactive or with flash loans disabled, premium `φ` with `φ × 10⁴ > maxFlashFeeBps × F` or above the caller's `maxPremium`), the helper reverts before LPVaultSwap is called with `InsufficientFlashLiquidity(available)`, `FlashFeeTooHigh` or `PremiumTooHigh(premium, maxPremium)`, and the caller liquidates from its own debt asset, or borrows up to the idle balance and funds the rest from its own; committed operators hold debt-asset inventory of at least the largest single `debtToCover` the chain's caps allow (`REQ-SWAP-014`), and no protocol budget funds principal or gaps (`Q-397`). The helper refuses a Pool that is a Hub recorded on the chain (`FlashSourceIsHub`). The core flash source serves only repay with collateral: when it cannot lend, `deleverage` reverts with LPVaultSwap's `FlashLiquidityShort(available, amount)` or the source's `InsufficientFlashLiquidity(available)` or `FlashFeeTooHigh(premium, maxPremium)` and the borrower repays from its own funds; LPVaultSwap reads that source from `MarketRegistry.flashSource()`, and the chain's timelock can replace it at the normal delay (`MarketRegistry.setFlashSource`). `maxFlashFeeBps` bounds the premium of repay with collateral and of the helper; it is 10 at launch and is tightened to 0 with `tightenMaxFlashFeeBps` once the flash source and the public helper hold `FLASH_BORROWER`; playbook P-15 (`EMG-098`). (Source: Q-063, Q-135, Q-144, Q-235, Q-358, Q-404)
- `EMG-059` Keeper outage: the keeper registry lists committed operators, Avana's own keeper at launch and others added at the normal delay, and Avana pays its own keeper's gas (`Q-381`). Avana's standby keeper runs on separate infrastructure and takes over when monitoring shows the primaries have stalled (`M-09`); there is no on-chain primary/standby distinction. The registry gates no call: if every committed operator stalls, any address that is not frozen can still liquidate through `liquidate` with either route mode, funded with its own debt asset or through the public flash liquidation helper, settling exactly as a committed operator does (`Q-404`); such a caller acts only where the keeper reward covers its own gas, so accounts whose bonus is below the gas depend on the committed operators (`LIQ-087`). With zero active committed operators left on the chain, the RiskModule treats liquidation as halted for new borrowing and withdraw-with-debt (`REQ-RISK-071`) while `liquidate` stays open to everyone, and a guardian trips the breaker of any market whose accounts stay unliquidated past 3,600 s (300 Ethereum blocks, 1,800 Base blocks) (playbook P-08). Adding a committed operator (`LPVaultSwap.addKeeper`) is a normal-delay operation that only `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` can cancel, and removing one (`removeKeeper`) a short-delay operation that reverts below `keeperFloor`; monitoring alerts on stalls and on committed-operator counts at the floor. Checked by: SIM-04A, SIM-04B, QNT-34, HYP-39. (Source: Q-082, Q-139, Q-147, Q-155, Q-213, Q-215, Q-228, Q-241, Q-242, Q-263, Q-404)

### Offboarding, wind-down and DAO handover

- `EMG-060` Offboarding follows [FLOW-008](08-market-lifecycle.md) and puts the market in row `EMG-010`: `MarketRegistry.setMarketOffboarding(marketId)` in a short-delay operation together with the Aave freeze of the vault-token reserve; repay, withdrawal (with and without debt) and liquidation stay open; after the notice the liquidation threshold ramps down to `windDownFinalLtBps` over `windDownRampSeconds`, remaining borrowers are liquidated through the normal flow once below HF 1, and when the market's total debt is 0 anyone calls `closeMarket(marketId)`, which moves it to `Closed` (row `EMG-096`) and releases its feed, swap routes and keeper duty (Q-355). (Source: Q-052, Q-110, Q-119, Q-227, Q-228, Q-355)
- `EMG-061` Wind-down of a Hub's credit lines follows the pre-agreed plan, per chain and per (Hub, asset): the Aave DAO lowers every CDP Spoke drawCap on every debt asset of that Hub to 0; Avana offboards every market homed on the Hub; repay, withdrawals and liquidation continue and each debt asset returns to its funding Hub as debt is repaid; there is no forced repayment; the liquidation-threshold ramp of `EMG-060` makes every remaining borrower repay or be liquidated, so the wind-down ends: each market reaches `Closed` when its debt is 0, and keepers, feeds and routes are released market by market; only idle Lend Spoke supply is withdrawn; after debt and deficits are resolved, each (Hub, asset)'s First-Loss Reserve is withdrawn through its Avana Umbrella's `requestWithdrawal` and `executeWithdrawal` after `RESERVE_WITHDRAWAL_DELAY`; there is no SettlementBudget to unwind (`Q-397`). Debt-free positions stay withdrawable after `Closed` without any price. A bug in an immutable LPVault or in Collateralization follows playbook P-19 (`EMG-102`): pause new activity, let users exit, relist on a new CDP Spoke. (Source: Q-095, Q-101, Q-114, Q-119, Q-167, Q-228, Q-236, Q-355)
- `EMG-062` DAO handover: as agreed in the wind-down plan, the governance multisig or the Aave governance executor, which already holds `PROPOSER_ROLE` as backup proposer, schedules, on each chain's AvanaTimelock, a role-change operation at the normal delay, that grants `EXECUTOR_ROLE` to the Aave governance executor and `PROPOSER_ROLE` and `EXECUTOR_ROLE` to a second DAO-named holder, and revokes `PROPOSER_ROLE` and `EXECUTOR_ROLE` from the governance multisig last in the batch, so `PROPOSER_ROLE` keeps at least two holders throughout (`REQ-ACCESS-105`, `REQ-ACCESS-160`); the guardian, canceller and DAO canceller roles are unchanged; the timelock holds its own `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` and refuses to grant it to anyone else (`REQ-ACCESS-153`), so this is the only way to change who proposes, and only the Aave governance executor can cancel it. The immutable LPVaults, Collateralization, vault tokens and PositionProxies keep working without any admin; the Lens is periphery outside the audited core. (Source: Q-114, Q-119, Q-228, Q-245, Q-259)

### Pool failure and Aave reserve checks

- `EMG-063` Pool failure: when a market's own AMM pool or a pool token malfunctions, the Aave Protocol Guardian freezes the market's vault-token reserve on Aave (`EMG-050`), a guardian trips the market breaker if the POOL_SHARE, POOL_DEPTH or ISSUER condition has not already tripped it, and the Emergency Guardian pauses every Hub whose markets hold the token when the failure can reach other markets (for example a shared pool token, or a token paused by its issuer that is also a swap-route token of other markets, AMMN-GAS-08). The governance multisig disables a failed swap route at the short delay; a lasting failure moves the market to `Offboarding`. Avana keeps no pool-broken flag (`Q-227`): the Aave freeze and the breaker stop new collateral and borrowing against the market, liquidation runs over the remaining route while the pool still executes the integration's unwind, a pool spot off the oracle reference neither reverts a liquidation nor switches it to in kind (Q-350), and withdraw-with-debt stops only once both route records are disabled (`EMG-025`) or the breaker is tripped. (Source: Q-118, Q-171, Q-227, Q-241, Q-350)
- `EMG-064` Aave reserve pause checks: the RiskModule's borrow and withdraw-with-debt checks revert `AaveReservePaused(reserveId)` when any vault-token reserve the account holds is paused on Aave, and `DebtReservePaused(debtReserveId)` when any debt reserve the account holds, or the reserve being borrowed, is paused. A paused reserve halts liquidation of the accounts holding it, so withdraw-with-debt stops with it (`EMG-047`). (Source: Q-202, Q-206, Q-209, Q-229, Q-233)
- `EMG-065` Freeze check: the RiskModule's `preBorrowCheck` refuses to borrow, with `ReserveFrozen(reserveId)`, while any of the account's vault-token reserves is frozen on Aave; withdraw-with-debt does not read the freeze and follows the matrix. Drifting stays a RiskModule check. A faulty Adapter implementation could skip the check; that case is the Adapter blast radius bounded by the timelock and the Hub and Spoke caps (`Q-223`). Checked by: QNT-19, QNT-T04. (Source: Q-203, Q-223, Q-229)

### Code fault response

- `EMG-067` Response to a fault in Avana code (playbook P-05, `EMG-079`): the Emergency Guardian or the Aave Protocol Guardian pauses the application at once, with reason `MintPathSuspect` when minting, the Adapter or Collateralization is suspect (`EMG-071`) and `General` otherwise (`EMG-018`), which stops every new pledge, borrow and withdraw-with-debt while repay, fee collection, debt-free withdrawal, pending settlement and liquidation continue; a guardian freezes the attacker's addresses (`EMG-090`); every canceller reviews the timelock queue and cancels any operation that touches the faulty contract; the governance multisig of the affected chain schedules the fix, an upgrade at the normal delay (5 days, never below 4); a fix to exit logic is an AaveAdapter or LPVaultSwap upgrade that every PositionProxy uses at once, because proxies are logic-free and immutable and never change code (Q-349); a fault in an immutable LPVault or Collateralization follows P-19; once it executes and the post-deployment checks pass, the pause guardian unpauses. Because exits never read the pause state, a breaker, the freeze or a gate, every borrower can repay and withdraw inside the delay of any upgrade they distrust. (Source: Q-223, Q-227, Q-228, Q-242, Q-243, Q-252, Q-262)

### Circuit breakers, address freeze, standby routes and route-failure monitoring

- `EMG-089` Circuit breakers stop new activity only, per market (`EMG-068`) or per (CDP Spoke, debt reserve) (`EMG-069`), following the v1 table of `Q-399`:

  | Trigger | Setting | Action |
  |---|---|---|
  | Oracle or stablecoin band breach | The existing oracle and peg checks (`EMG-003` to `EMG-007`, `EMG-020`, USD band $0.90–$1.02) | Block new borrowing and collateral withdrawals that leave debt |
  | Price drop | 3% in 2 hours | Alert only (no on-chain trip) |
  | Avana's combined share of a pool | Above 10% (`poolShareMaxBps`) across all borrowers and Spokes on the chain | Block new borrowing and pledges that increase pool exposure |
  | Collateral seized | Above 20% of a market in 1 hour | Alert only |
  | Swap-route failures | 3 in 1 hour | Alert; a guardian freezes new borrowing only once the approved routes are confirmed unable to settle valid liquidations at the required size (P-04) |
  | Outstanding GHO deficit | Above 10,000 GHO for the affected CDP Spoke (per-reserve materiality for other assets, set at listing) | Freeze new borrowing on that debt reserve at once; alert on every new deficit |
  | Reopening | 30 minutes of healthy observations, for temporary price or depth breaches (provisional launch setting) | Clear the temporary gate; a deficit or security freeze needs authorized reopening (timelock or guardian, [2.15](../02-contracts/15-governance-and-timelock.md)) |

  Condition breakers are evaluated by the RiskModule from live on-chain state in `checkPledge`, `preBorrowCheck` and `preWithdrawCheck`: ORACLE (the price-state and debt-price gates, with no blocking of its own beyond those rows), POOL_SHARE (`SPEC-BRK-002`), POOL_DEPTH, ISSUER (a pool token or debt asset reporting `paused()` or a non-zero USDT fee rate through the probe recorded at listing, a reverting probe counting as tripped, `SPEC-BRK-003`), and on the debt side DEFICIT and ISSUER (`SPEC-BRK-004`; a Coverage Spoke below its minimum is a monitoring alert, `Q-399`). The price-drop, seizure-surge and route-failure conditions are not on-chain: a reverted liquidation cannot persist a count, and an on-chain counter could be fed by a caller to trip it, so monitoring computes them from events and simulations and pages on-call. DEFICIT latches when it first holds, so it stays tripped after a partial coverage until reset. `APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN` trips a market or debt breaker manually at once (`tripMarketBreaker`, `tripDebtBreaker`, reason MANUAL, latched until reset) and `APPLICATION_ADMIN` at the short delay; `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN` resets a latched or manual breaker at once and `APPLICATION_ADMIN` at the normal delay (`REQ-ACCESS-158`). A reset clears only latched reasons; a condition that still holds keeps blocking. Repay, debt-free withdrawal and partial removal, fee collection, zero-credit release, pending settlement, liquidation, rescue top-ups and repay with collateral never read a breaker, and LPVaultSwap never reads them; a liquidation still needs a valid price, Aave eligibility and executable settlement, and an Aave reserve pause can block repay or liquidation independently. Example: the GHO deficit of the Uniswap v3 Stable CDP Spoke on Ethereum reaches 10,000.5 GHO after a liquidation; the next `preBorrowCheck` for GHO on that Spoke finds `10,000.5 > 10,000` and reverts `DebtBreakerTripped(S, r_GHO, DEFICIT)`; the Avana Umbrella (Coverage Spoke) covers the deficit within 72 hours, and the pause guardian resets the breaker after confirming `getSpokeDeficitRay = 0`. Checked by: TEST-228, PROP-066. (Source: Q-241, Q-252, Q-399)
- `EMG-090` Address freeze procedure: `freezeAddress(account)` is immediate for `APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN` and on the short-delay list for `APPLICATION_ADMIN`; each caller may freeze at most `MAX_ADDRESS_FREEZES_PER_DAY` (20) addresses in the 24 one-hour buckets ending with the current one (a window of 23 to 24 hours of wall-clock time), so a compromised guardian cannot freeze users in bulk. It reverts `ProtectedAddress` for the AvanaTimelock, the Aave governance executor, every Avana contract and vault token, every LPOracle and DebtOracle, every Hub, every approved Spoke and Avana Umbrella (Coverage Spoke), every approved debt asset, the flash source of repay with collateral and the Aave V3 Pool it calls, the public flash liquidation helper, every recorded swap route and every registered proxy. It checks no keeper floor (`Q-400`): a guardian can freeze any committed operator, including the last active one, and while 0 active committed operators remain new borrowing halts on every Hub of the chain while `liquidate` stays open to every address that is not frozen. A freeze stops only the address's direct `liquidate` calls: through the public flash liquidation helper the frozen address still liquidates, because LPVaultSwap checks the helper, not the helper's caller (`REQ-SWAP-105`, `Q-404`), and the public helper cannot be frozen, so no freeze stops liquidation through it. `unfreezeAddress(account)` is an `APPLICATION_ADMIN` operation at the normal delay that only `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` can cancel, so the guardian that froze an address cannot keep it frozen by cancelling every unfreeze (`REQ-ACCESS-157`, `REQ-ROLE-107`). Checked by: TEST-229, PROP-071. (Source: Q-242, Q-263, Q-404)
- `EMG-091` Standby price routes: each listed token records, beside its primary and alternate routes, a standby route that passed at listing every check of the slot it can replace (decimals, `minAnswer`/`maxAnswer` guards, heartbeat recorded from the feed's own configuration, with freshness `feedHeartbeat + heartbeatMargin`, no shared intermediate feed with the other active route). `useStandbyRoute(token, slot)` replaces the primary or alternate slot with the standby at once for `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN`, or at the short delay for `APPLICATION_ADMIN` in an operation only `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` can cancel; `cancelStandbySwitch(token)` lets the Aave governance executor undo a guardian's switch at once; `restorePriceRoute(token)` restores the listed routes at the normal delay (`REQ-ACCESS-159`, `REQ-MREG-027`, `REQ-MREG-028`). After a switch the LPOracle and the DebtOracle read the active routes through `activePriceRoutes`; the switch increments the token's `priceRouteVersion`, so the next `poke` restarts the disagreement timer, keeps the failure timer and evaluates the market afresh; a switch never forces Live and never bypasses freshness, validity or the agreement check, so a guardian can only choose among routes the listing reviewed. Checked by: TEST-231. (Source: Q-248, Q-274)
- `EMG-106` Standby second source (AMMN-INT-01). Each token with a second source also records a pre-approved standby second source: another oracle network or a deep pool's TWAP, independent of the token's primary, alternate and standby price routes and of its listed second source, which passed the `setSecondSource` checks ([2.9](../02-contracts/09-parameter-store.md) `REQ-PSTORE-028`) when it was set at listing or by a normal-delay `setStandbySecondSource(token, src)` operation. Either guardian (`APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN`, held by the Emergency Guardian and the Aave Protocol Guardian) switches the token to its standby at once with `useStandbySecondSource(token)`. Switching back to the listed second source needs the timelock: `restoreSecondSource(token)` at the normal delay. The switch changes only which source confirms `borrowAllowed`: the price state, the served price and every liquidation input stay as they were, and the standby must itself agree within its own `toleranceBps` before borrowing resumes, so a guardian can only choose a source the listing reviewed. Why: one second source is a single gate on every borrow and withdraw-with-debt that touches its token, and USDC is in every launch Stable market, so a failed USDC second source would otherwise stop borrowing on the whole chain for the 5-day normal delay of `setSecondSource`. Example: USDC's listed second source, a deep-pool TWAP, stops reading at 09:00 and every borrow touching USDC reverts `SecondSourceDisagrees(USDC)` while the market stays Live. At 09:10 the Emergency Guardian calls `ParameterStore.useStandbySecondSource(USDC)` ([2.9](../02-contracts/09-parameter-store.md) `REQ-PSTORE-029`); the standby is a second oracle network reading $1.0001 with `toleranceBps = 50` against the served $0.9999: `|0.9999 − 1.0001| × 10⁴ = 2 ≤ 50 × 0.9999 = 49.995`, so borrows pass again from the next block. Once the TWAP pool is healthy, the governance multisig schedules `restoreSecondSource(USDC)`, which executes 5 days later. (Source: Q-323, Q-385)
- `EMG-092` Route-failure monitoring replaces the automatic in-kind trigger and the shortfall report (`Q-399`, `Q-401`). There is no `reportRecoveryShortfall`, no `autoEnableInKind`, no `inKindAutoDelay` and no `inKindAutoTrigger`. Avana's monitoring counts, per market, each `RouteFailed` event and each `RecoveryBelowFloor` or `NoRouteAvailable` revert of a committed operator's call or a simulation (a moved pool spot alone is never a failure, Q-350), and alerts at 3 in 1 hour. A committed operator answers a floor failure with a shortfall top-up in the debt asset from its own funds (`REQ-SWAP-101`) and liquidates whatever Aave allows per call, never halving the size (`Q-395`). If the failures continue, on-call confirms on a fork whether the approved routes can settle a valid liquidation at the required size; only then does a guardian call `tripMarketBreaker(m)` and P-04 runs. Example: three USDT→USDC failures on market 1 between 14:00 and 14:20 page on-call; the fork shows the backup fills at 0.6% slippage, so no breaker is tripped and the keepers move to `BackupOnly`. Checked by: TEST-233, TEST-234. (Source: Q-241, Q-251, Q-350, Q-395, Q-399, Q-401)

## Playbooks

Every playbook runs on the chain where the incident is, with that chain's Safes, Timelock and keepers, and is scoped to the affected Hub, CDP Spoke or market. On Base, every Aave-side step goes through the Aave DAO's Base executor, which receives payloads over Aave's cross-chain governance, so it takes the bridge delivery time on top of the Aave delay and can lag the same step on Ethereum ([2.15](../02-contracts/15-governance-and-timelock.md) `REQ-ACCESS-173`); Base playbooks therefore send the Aave-side request at detection, in parallel with Avana's own steps. On Base the borrower exit window of `SPEC-GOV-014` also counts the OP Stack forced-inclusion delay `q_Base = 43,200` s (12 h) for a borrower who must force a transaction in from L1 while the sequencer is down: with a 3-day reaction time and 10 transactions at 2 s, `259,200 + 43,200 + 20 = 302,420` s fits inside the 4-day minimum normal delay of 345,600 s (`SPEC-GOV-015`). Every playbook names its detection (alerts of [services](../../services/README.md#monitoring-and-alerting)), what trips by itself, who acts at once and with which call, the ordered steps, what stays open and the recovery. Unless a playbook says otherwise, **Stays open** is always: repay, debt-free withdrawal and partial removal, `repayAllAndWithdraw`, fee collection, zero-credit release, pending settlement, payout-escrow (`PayoutEscrow`) claims, rescue top-ups (except under `Paused(MintPathSuspect)`) and liquidation of every reserve whose own prices are fresh.

- `EMG-074` Rules for every emergency. **Exits and liquidation never stop**: no playbook step pauses, freezes or trips anything that reaches the paths under Stays open. **Immediate, no delay**: the Emergency Guardian and the Aave Protocol Guardian call `freeze`, `pause(reason)`, `freezeAddress`, `tripMarketBreaker`, `tripDebtBreaker`, `useStandbyRoute` and `cancel`; the pause guardian also calls `unfreeze`, `unpause`, `resetMarketBreaker` and `resetDebtBreaker`; the Aave governance executor calls `cancelStandbySwitch` and cancels role-change and restoring operations; committed operators use their kill switch; breakers trip by themselves. **Always through the timelock**: `unfreezeAddress`, `restorePriceRoute`, every new price source and route, every parameter loosening and every upgrade; the short delay applies only to `SHORT_ELIGIBLE` operations (`EMG-049`), so one loosening call makes a whole batch wait the normal delay. **Paper trail**: every incident has an incident channel and a log of every transaction hash, decision and actor with timestamps; a public status note goes out within 1 hour of confirmation, and a post-mortem within 7 days (`EMG-088`). Stopping new exposure first, keeping exits and liquidation open, and changing configuration only through reviewed operations keeps every response within the powers each role holds. (Source: Q-243, Q-262)
- `EMG-075` Triage, the same for every incident: (1) an alert fires; (2) the on-call engineer confirms it on-chain within 15 minutes from the Lens, the transaction trace and the state readers; (3) the engineer classifies the incident as one of P-02 to P-19 below; (4) the engineer pages both Safes and opens the incident log; (5) **P-01 Unclassified incident**: if the incident does not yet fit a playbook and new activity is at risk, a guardian pauses the affected Hub with reason `General` first (with `MintPathSuspect` if minting may be involved), then the team reclassifies and follows the matching playbook; Stays open as above, and the pause is lifted only under that playbook's recovery. (Source: Q-243, Q-244)

### `EMG-076` P-02 Oracle failure, staleness, disagreement or manipulation

- **Detection**: `M-01` (price state Cached; Expired pages), `M-02` (disagreement start; page 30 minutes before `disagreementPauseMax` ends), `M-03` (leg age), `M-04` (debt feed age).
- **Automatic**: the ORACLE condition stops borrowing, withdraw-with-debt and new pledges in the market; stress valuation keeps the fresh reserves of every affected account liquidatable (`EMG-073`); a debt feed failure stops borrowing that debt.
- **Immediate actors**: the Emergency Guardian (or the Aave Protocol Guardian) calls `useStandbyRoute(token, slot)` for the failing slot.
- **Steps**: (1) identify the failing leg and route from `LPOracle.getPriceState` or `DebtOracle.getPriceState`; (2) if the token's standby route is fresh and agrees with the healthy route, switch the failing slot to it; (3) if the standby also fails, `tripMarketBreaker(m)` and leave the market's own seizures waiting while keepers keep liquidating fresh reserves; (4) for a failing debt feed, do the same on the debt asset's DebtOracle and `tripDebtBreaker` if the standby fails; (4a) if only a token's second source fails or disagrees, so borrowing stops while the price stays Live (`EMG-103`), call `useStandbySecondSource(token)` once the standby agrees with the served price (`EMG-106`); (5) the governance multisig prepares a permanent source as a normal-delay `setPriceSource` operation. A 24 h stablecoin feed that has not updated for 20 hours is normal, not an incident: freshness is `feedHeartbeat + heartbeatMargin` (88,200 s), and borrow needs the second source to agree.
- **Stays open**: as above; the market's own seizure waits for a fresh, agreeing price.
- **Recovery**: keepers `poke` until the market is Live; grace applies per account (`EMG-034`); `restorePriceRoute` or `setPriceSource` at the normal delay once the listed route is healthy; the pause guardian resets a manual breaker once the market has been Live for one `cachedWindow`. ### `EMG-097` P-14 Spoke-setting drift

- **Detection**: Avana's keeper simulates `RiskModule.pokeConfig(spoke)` ([2.7](../02-contracts/07-risk-module.md)) on every CDP Spoke every block and sends it only when the simulation finds a drift (`EMG-053`), and anyone may call it; a failed check emits `ConfigDriftDetected(spoke, reasons)`, on which `M-20` pages, so Avana's exposure to a changed setting lasts at most the block of the change and the next block (`Q-382`).
