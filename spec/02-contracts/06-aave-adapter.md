# 2.6 Aave Adapter

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12
>
> **Requirement prefixes:** `REQ-ADAPTER-NNN`, `REQ-PROXY-NNN` (borrower entry points) · **Math prefix:** `SPEC-PROXY-NNN`

## Purpose

The AaveAdapter is the single entry point for every borrower action on every CDP Spoke of its chain: pledge, re-credit, borrow, repay, withdraw, partial liquidity removal, repay-all-and-withdraw, repay with collateral, fee collection and zero-credit release. One Adapter runs per chain behind one fixed address, scoped by Hub inside: every call resolves the market's home Hub and CDP Spoke from the MarketRegistry for new activity, or from the market's immutable vault token for exits (`Q-308`). It is the only entry point through which a position is pledged, released or re-credited, so every LPVault and Collateralization entry point that moves value runs inside an Adapter (or LPVaultSwap) call tree and the Lock never needs a new member ([2.16](16-lock.md), AMMN-FLOW-01). Every call goes wallet → AaveAdapter → the wallet's PositionProxy for that market → CDP Spoke (`Q-223`). Positions are named by `(integrationId, positionKey)` and every token amount is an n-token array in the market's pool order; no signature carries a `tokenId` or `amount0`/`amount1`. The Adapter executes each action: it resolves the proxy from `msg.sender` and the market (or from the named borrower for repay), creates the proxy on the first pledge to that market through the ProxyManager, asks Collateralization to take custody, record, reduce and re-credit backing, mints and burns vault tokens, runs the RiskModule's checks and reverts with their errors, and runs every account step (exact approvals, supply, enable-and-assert, borrow forwarding, repay with refund, withdrawal, balance-delta checks, collateral cleanup) as calls the logic-free proxy makes through `execute` ([2.5](05-position-proxy-and-proxy-manager.md), `Q-306`). It also burns the seized vault tokens that LPVaultSwap holds during a liquidation (`burnSeized`, `Q-224`) and runs the repay and release steps LPVaultSwap asks for inside a repay with collateral (`deleverageRepay`, `deleverageRelease`, `Q-255`).

The Adapter keeps the call bounds itself: the deadline, the per-proxy borrow nonce, the receiver rule, the proxy resolution, the debt-reserve enablement for new debt, the pause state of the market's Hub and its reason, and the address freeze, read from the MarketRegistry for new activity only. Every risk check (price states and ages of the account's market and every debt reserve of the account, the sequencer gate, Aave reserve pauses and freezes, drift, Hub shares == assets, liquidation halt, circuit breakers, and the post-action health factor with rescue units excluded against Aave's 1.00, the caller's `minHealthFactor` and any per-market `hardHealthFloor`) belongs to the RiskModule ([2.7](07-risk-module.md)), which the Adapter calls on every borrow, every withdraw-with-debt and every partial removal with debt (`Q-229`, `Q-241`, `Q-253`). Exits read nothing from the MarketRegistry: debt-free is Aave's own borrow count, and the vault token comes from the account's binding `accountOf(proxy)`, written once at proxy creation after the token was checked against the VaultTokenFactory and its own immutables; its Hub, Spoke and reserve come from the token's immutables and Aave (`Q-260`).

It is explicitly **not** responsible for: position custody, liquidity removal and fee mechanics of each AMM protocol (the market's LPVault, [2.2](02-lp-vault.md), reached through Collateralization); backing records, credit math, exposure counters and the pending-settlement balance (Collateralization, [2.3](03-collateralization.md)); pledge eligibility, rescue eligibility and caps (RiskModule `checkPledge`, called by Collateralization); circuit breakers (RiskModule, with breach times and latched freezes in the MarketRegistry); liquidation (LPVaultSwap, [2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)), apart from burning the seized units on LPVaultSwap's request; valuation, flash funding of the debt asset and conversion in a repay with collateral (LPVaultSwap); any interest fee (`Q-231`: none exists); setting or lifting a Hub's pause state, its reason or an address freeze (MarketRegistry, [2.8](08-market-registry.md)).

**Trust model.** The Adapter's code and the timelock that upgrades it are trusted for custody of every proxy position (`Q-223`). Blast radius of a faulty or hostile implementation, stated once and reused by [1.3](../01-architecture/03-trust-boundaries.md), [2.15](15-governance-and-timelock.md), [4.1](../04-security/01-global-invariants.md) and [4.2](../04-security/02-attack-scenarios.md):

- AaveAdapter implementation faulty or hostile (the PositionProxies and the ProxyManager are immutable and carry no logic, so they add nothing): make any proxy call anything through `execute`, so borrow every debt reserve against every proxy up to each `drawCap` and withdraw supplied vault tokens; mint vault tokens to any registered proxy (bounded by the Hub `addCap`); burn any registered proxy's or LPVaultSwap's vault tokens; register any address as a proxy; spend allowances granted to the Adapter; pledge any position whose owner approved the market's LPVault. It cannot send a position to anyone but its depositor of record, change any Spoke setting, raise any Aave cap, or spend an Avana Umbrella (First-Loss Reserve), the protocol safety module of each (Hub, debt asset) (`Q-402`). Bound: 5-day timelock of the chain, cancellers, Hub/Spoke caps; exits (repay, debt-free withdraw and removal, `repayAllAndWithdraw`) read no pause state, breaker, freeze or MarketRegistry state, so borrowers can leave inside the delay.

## Outputs

- Proxy calls: `execute(target, data)` only, carrying the Spoke and token calls of [2.5](05-position-proxy-and-proxy-manager.md).
- Vault-token calls: `mint(proxy, credited)`, `mint(proxy, added)`, `burn(proxy, units)`, `burn(LP_VAULT_SWAP, units)`, `addValidProxy(proxy)`. Vault tokens move only as the transfer allowlist of [2.4](04-vault-tokens.md) allows: mint to a proxy; proxy → home Hub on supply; home Hub → proxy on withdraw (the Spoke's `withdraw` sends to its caller, the proxy); home Hub → LPVaultSwap on seizure; burn from a proxy or from LPVaultSwap.
- Collateralization calls: `pledge`, `release`, `releasePartial`, `recredit`, `releaseForDeleverage`, `collectFees`, `releaseZeroCredit`, `markIndebted`, `markDebtFree`; ProxyManager `deployProxy`; LPVaultSwap `deleverage`.
- Debt-asset `transferFrom(payer, proxy, x)` on repay and `transferFrom(LP_VAULT_SWAP, proxy, amount)` in `deleverageRepay`; nothing stays in the Adapter at the end of any call.
- Events below.

## Storage

All mutable storage sits in one ERC-7201 namespace (`avana.storage.AaveAdapter`).

| Variable | Type | Description | Written by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `proxyOf` | `mapping(address wallet => mapping(uint256 marketId => address proxy))` | Account binding, one proxy per (wallet, market); the market fixes the Spoke | `pledge` (first pledge of the wallet to the market), once |
| `walletOf`, `accountOf` | `mapping(address proxy => address)`, `mapping(address proxy => Account{spoke, marketId, vaultToken, reserveId})` | Reverse binding; `accountOf` also holds the market's vault token and collateral reserve, so exits never read the MarketRegistry | Same call, once |
| `borrowNonce` | `mapping(address proxy => uint256)` | Next expected borrow nonce, starts at 0 | `borrow` |
| `PROXY_MANAGER`, `COLLATERALIZATION`, `RISK_MODULE`, `MARKET_REGISTRY`, `PARAMETER_STORE`, `LP_VAULT_SWAP`, `LOCK`, `VAULT_TOKEN_FACTORY`, `TIMELOCK`, and the four logic contracts | `address` (implementation immutables) | Fixed per-chain dependencies and the implementation's own logic contracts (`REQ-ADAPTER-004`); change only with an upgrade. No Hub, Spoke, AMM-protocol or debt-asset immutable (`Q-313`) | Implementation constructor |
| Repay-with-collateral session | transient storage (EIP-1153): `proxy`, `marketId`, `positionKey`, `units`, `debtReserveId`, `keccak256(abi.encode(minAmounts))`, `repaid`, `released` | Open only between the Adapter's call to `LP_VAULT_SWAP.deleverage` and its return; empty at the end of every transaction | `repayWithCollateral`, `deleverageRepay`, `deleverageRelease` |
| OpenZeppelin `AccessControl` roles | `mapping` | `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` and `APPLICATION_ADMIN`, both held only by the timelock | `initialize`; role changes by timelock operation |

There is no parameter, fee index, fee recipient, pause flag, freeze list, breaker state or per-market restriction in the Adapter.

`repayWithCollateral` takes `RepayWithCollateralParams{uint256 marketId; PositionRef position; uint128 units; uint256 debtReserveId; uint256[] minAmounts; uint256 minDebtRepaid; uint256 minAssetOut; uint256 deadline; uint256 minHealthFactor}`: `minAmounts` holds one raw minimum per pool token in the market's token order for the unwind of `units`, or is empty for no minimum (read as one zero per token); `minDebtRepaid` and `minAssetOut` are in the debt asset's base units, `minAssetOut` bounding the debt asset paid to the wallet after the flash loan and its premium (LPVaultSwap flash-borrows the debt asset and sells the released tokens straight into it, so there is no buy leg); `minHealthFactor` in WAD. Every swap leg also carries its oracle `minOut`, so the caller's minimums only tighten it.

## Restrictions

### Form and powers

- `REQ-ADAPTER-001` — The chain's one Adapter MUST sit behind one fixed ERC-1967 proxy address that never changes; each vault token (immutable `adapter()`), Collateralization, the ProxyManager and every PositionProxy (constructor immutable `ADAPTER`), LPVaultSwap, the Lock and the Lens MUST reference that address. A new Hub, CDP Spoke, market or AMM integration on the chain is served by the same Adapter with no redeployment. (Source: Q-223, Q-226, Q-308)
- `REQ-ADAPTER-002` — The user entry points (`pledge`, `recredit`, `borrow`, `repay`, `withdraw`, `removeLiquidity`, `repayAllAndWithdraw`, `repayWithCollateral`), `collectFees`, `releaseZeroCredit`, `burnSeized` and the deleverage callbacks (`deleverageRepay`, `deleverageRelease`) MUST keep their selectors, argument order and meaning across upgrades: the app's bundled ABI, keepers and LPVaultSwap call them, and LPVaultSwap's own code fixes `burnSeized` and the deleverage callbacks. A change of signature ships as a new implementation together with an app release and, for `burnSeized` and the callbacks, an LPVaultSwap upgrade in the same timelock batch. (Source: Q-223, Q-224, Q-254, Q-255)
- `REQ-ADAPTER-003` — The Adapter MUST be a UUPS proxy whose `_authorizeUpgrade` requires `APPLICATION_ADMIN`, held only by the timelock; upgrades MUST run as timelock operations at the normal delay (5 days), cancellable by every `CANCELLER_ROLE` holder, to an implementation that is already deployed and source-verified. Reviewers check the implementation's bytecode hash and simulate the payload on a fork during the delay; the deployment check asserts that no other account holds `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` or `APPLICATION_ADMIN`. There is no ProxyAdmin. (Source: Q-223, Q-226, Q-228)
- `REQ-ADAPTER-004` — The implementation MUST use ERC-7201 namespaced storage, call `_disableInitializers()` in its constructor, run any `reinitializer(n)` atomically in the same `upgradeToAndCall`, link no external libraries, and stay within the file-size limits of `Q-240`; because the whole surface does not fit EIP-170 in one contract, the implementation is a router, `AaveAdapter`, holding the roles, the views, the upgrade gate and the dispatch, plus four immutable logic contracts reached by `delegatecall` with the unchanged calldata: `AaveAdapterUserLogic` (`pledge`, `borrow`, `repay`), `AaveAdapterExitLogic` (`withdraw`, `repayAllAndWithdraw`, `collectFees`, `releaseZeroCredit`, `refreshDebtMark`), `AaveAdapterCollateralLogic` (`removeLiquidity`, `recredit`, `repayWithCollateral`) and `AaveAdapterSettlementLogic` (`burnSeized`, `deleverageRepay`, `deleverageRelease`). The logic contracts are part of the implementation: their addresses are implementation immutables; each is built with the same nine dependency addresses, which the router's constructor checks through `logicConfig()` (kind and hash of the immutables, `InvalidLogic(logic)` otherwise); each refuses a call that is not a `delegatecall` from the Adapter with `NotDelegated()`; and a new logic contract ships only as a new implementation through `upgradeToAndCall` at the normal delay. ### Repay

- `REQ-PROXY-027` `repay(borrower, marketId, debtReserveId, maxRepaySpend, repayAll, deadline)` MUST accept any caller as the payer: it acquires the Lock, reverts with `DeadlineExpired(deadline)` unless `block.timestamp ≤ deadline` and resolves `proxy = proxyOf[borrower][marketId]` (`NoProxy(borrower, marketId)` if zero); the Spoke is `accountOf[proxy].spoke`, stored at creation. It MUST NOT check the debt reserve against the MarketRegistry: the Spoke repays only reserves it lends, a listed reserve the account does not owe has `D = 0` and returns 0, and the Spoke's own error propagates for a reserve it does not list. The payer is always `msg.sender`: the asset is pulled from `msg.sender` and any unconsumed part is refunded to `msg.sender`, never to or from `borrower` unless `borrower` is the caller. It MUST NOT read the MarketRegistry, the pause state, the address freeze (of the payer or the borrower), any breaker, any price, any parameter or the RiskModule. Why: repaying only lowers the account's risk; letting any payer repay lets the borrower's other wallets, a rescue service or the protocol save a position near liquidation, including one whose key is lost; and reading no upgradeable configuration means no faulty or hostile MarketRegistry can block a repay. A payer can never move collateral or borrow, because those entry points act only on `proxyOf[msg.sender][marketId]`. Repay has no path that bypasses the Adapter: Aave's `Spoke.repay` accepts only the position owner or its position manager, and a proxy approves none; so repay depends on Adapter code, not on proxy code, and a faulty Adapter is fixed by an upgrade at the normal delay (`REQ-ADAPTER-060`). Checked by: TEST-243. (Source: Q-212, Q-223, Q-227, Q-242, Q-260)
- `REQ-PROXY-028` `repay` MUST read `D = spoke.getUserTotalDebt(debtReserveId, proxy)` (drawn plus premium accrued to `block.timestamp`, in the asset's base units) and size the pull by `SPEC-PROXY-003`. With `repayAll == false` it reverts with `ZeroAmount()` if `maxRepaySpend == 0`. With either flag it then returns 0 with no token movement and no event if `D == 0`, so a repay that lands after a liquidation or another payer cleared the debt does not fail. With `repayAll == true` and `D > 0` it reverts with `RepayExceedsMax(D, maxRepaySpend)` before any transfer if `D > maxRepaySpend`; otherwise it pulls `maxRepaySpend` and refunds the unused part (`REQ-ADAPTER-085`). A partial repay pulls exactly `min(maxRepaySpend, D)`. (Source: Q-153, Q-180, Q-209, Q-234, Q-269)
- `REQ-PROXY-029` `repay` MUST move the sized pull `x` (`min(maxRepaySpend, D)` for a partial repay, `maxRepaySpend` for a repay-all) with `SafeERC20.safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, proxy, x)` (the payer approves the Adapter, not the proxy or the Spoke), measure `received` as the proxy's balance increase across that transfer, revert with `UnitsMismatch(x, received)` if `received == 0` or `received > x`, and run the repay step of [2.5](05-position-proxy-and-proxy-manager.md) `REQ-PROXY-088` through `proxy.execute` with `amount = received` and payer `msg.sender`, which approves the Spoke for exactly `received`, repays (Aave takes at most the live debt), resets the approval to 0 and refunds `received − spent` to the payer. The pinned Aave V4 Hub then checks that the Spoke's own transfer of the repaid amount reaches it in full and reverts `InsufficientTransferred(shortfall)` otherwise, so a debt asset whose issuer switches on a transfer fee cannot be repaid through Aave at all while the fee is on: that block is Aave's, never the Adapter's, and it lasts until the issuer turns the fee off (2.11 `REQ-SWAP-123`). Approved debt assets have no transfer fee at listing; a fee switched on later trips the issuer breaker for new debt ([2.7](07-risk-module.md) `SPEC-BRK-003`), and monitoring raises it at once because it also stops repay and liquidation of that asset on Aave. (Source: Q-124, Q-144, Q-212, Q-223, Q-271)
- `REQ-PROXY-030` `spent` is the proxy's balance decrease across `Spoke.repay` measured by the Adapter ( `spent ≤ received ≤ x`), and the refund to the payer is `received − spent`; neither the Adapter nor the proxy holds any of the asset at the end of the call. In `repayAllAndWithdraw` the payer is the calling wallet, so any refund goes to it. (Source: Q-124, Q-212, Q-217, Q-269, Q-271)
- `REQ-PROXY-031` With `repayAll == true`, after the proxy call the Adapter MUST revert with `DebtRemaining(debtReserveId, d)` unless `d = spoke.getUserTotalDebt(debtReserveId, proxy) == 0`. - `REQ-PROXY-032` Any partial amount MUST be accepted; there is no repay floor. Repay is open in None, Frozen and Paused (both reasons) of every Hub, for a frozen payer or borrower, while any breaker is tripped and whatever the sequencer gate; only Aave itself (the debt reserve paused, the Hub halting the Spoke) can block it. `repay` emits `Repaid(msg.sender, borrower, marketId, debtReserveId, spent, debtAfter, repayAll)` whenever funds moved, so indexers can tell a third-party repay from the borrower's own. When a repay leaves the repaid reserve at zero debt (the only way it can bring `borrowCount` to 0), the Adapter calls `COLLATERALIZATION.markDebtFree(proxy)`, which reads `borrowCount` itself and clears the account's indebted mark only at 0; `repayAllAndWithdraw` calls it once after its repay steps find `borrowCount == 0`. - `REQ-PROXY-083` Concurrent payers. Because `repay` sizes its pull from the debt read in the same call, a repay by one payer that executes first MUST NOT make a later repay take more than the debt remaining at its own execution: a later partial repay pulls `min(maxRepaySpend, D')`, a later repay-all pulls its cap and Aave takes exactly the remaining `D' ≤ D`, the rest returning to its payer in the same call, and at `D' = 0` the later call returns 0 and moves nothing. A `repayAllAndWithdraw` whose debts another payer already cleared repays nothing and withdraws debt-free. Example: the borrower submits a repay-all of its USDC reserve with `maxRepaySpend = 5,030e6` against `D = 5,024.657535` USDC; a rescue service's partial repay of 2,000 USDC lands first and leaves `D' = 3,024.657535` USDC; the borrower's repay-all then pulls 5,030,000,000 base units, Aave takes 3,024,657,535 and 2,005,342,465 return to the borrower, clearing the reserve. - `REQ-PROXY-084` No token transfer of a zero amount MAY be attempted: a repay sized at 0 makes no pull, approval, Spoke call or refund, and the proxy skips a zero refund. - `REQ-ADAPTER-085` — With `repayAll == true` and `0 < D ≤ maxRepaySpend`, `repay` MUST pull exactly `maxRepaySpend` from the payer to the proxy, have the proxy repay the received amount (Aave takes the live debt and no more) and refund `received − spent` to the payer in the same call, so a payer who approved exactly `maxRepaySpend` ends with zero allowance and the reserve at zero debt (`SPEC-PROXY-007`). Example: record #7 in market A (`d = 6`) credits `5×10^10` units with original charge `9,999,500,000,000` ($99,995.00); a debt-free removal of `10^10` units releases `1,999,900,000,000` ($19,999.00) of exposure, burns `10^10` units and pays `[USDC amount, USDT amount]` from the exact range to `W`; with GHO debt outstanding, a removal of `49,996,000,000` units leaves `4,000,000` units worth `⌊4×10^6 × 199,990,000 / 10^6⌋ = 799,960,000` ($7.9996) and reverts `RecordBelowMinPledge(799,960,000, 1,000,000,000)`. ### Repay with collateral

- `REQ-ADAPTER-080` — `repayWithCollateral(p)` repays one debt reserve with liquidity released from one record, through LPVaultSwap, with no liquidation bonus, no Avana fee and no keeper reimbursement. It preserves: the backing gap; debt never increases and is repaid before any collateral leaves the account; the flash loan of the debt asset and its premium are repaid in the same call or the call reverts; while debt remains, Aave's health factor after the call is at least `max(hfBefore, p.minHealthFactor)` (`SPEC-PROXY-009`); the released pool tokens leave custody only to LPVaultSwap and the debt asset left after repaying the flash loan goes only to the calling wallet; the Adapter holds nothing at the end. It MUST, in this order: acquire the Lock; revert with `DeadlineExpired(p.deadline)`, with `ZeroAmount()` if `p.units == 0`, with `NoProxy(msg.sender, p.marketId)`; revert with `ApplicationPaused()` if the market's Hub is Paused with reason `MintPathSuspect`, and with `SequencerDown()` while the chain's sequencer gate is Down; revert with `IntegrationMismatch` unless `p.position.integrationId` is the market's; revert with `UnknownDebtReserve(spoke, p.debtReserveId)` unless enabled (`REQ-ADAPTER-067`); revert with `NothingToRepay(p.debtReserveId)` if the account's debt on it is 0; read the backing gap and `sharesBefore`; open the session `(proxy, p.marketId, p.position.positionKey, p.units, p.debtReserveId, keccak256(abi.encode(mins)))` in transient storage, with `mins = p.minAmounts`, or one zero per market token when `p.minAmounts` is empty, the same normalised array LPVaultSwap passes to `deleverageRelease` ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md) `REQ-SWAP-106`), so an empty `minAmounts` binds the session it opens; call `(debtRepaid, debtAssetToWallet) = LP_VAULT_SWAP.deleverage(DeleverageRequest{proxy, msg.sender, p.marketId, p.position, p.units, p.debtReserveId, p.minAmounts, p.minDebtRepaid})`, during which LPVaultSwap applies liquidation-grade price gates to the market and the debt reserve, flash-borrows the debt asset through the IFlashSource, and calls back `deleverageRepay`, which records `hfBefore` before it repays, then `deleverageRelease` ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)); `hfBefore` is read there, after LPVaultSwap has poked the market's LPOracle and the reserve's DebtOracle, because a poke can move the price Aave reads within the block (the LPOracle verifies token decimals only in `poke()`, [2.10](10-lp-oracle.md) `REQ-ORACLE-107`), and a health factor read before the pokes could make the direction check refuse a call that improves the account at the price Aave applies, or pass one that lowers it; revert with `NoDeleverageSession()` unless both callbacks ran; revert with `UnitsMismatch(p.units, sharesBefore − sharesAfter)` unless supplied shares fell by exactly `p.units`; if `borrowCount > 0`, revert with `HealthFactorNotImproved(hfBefore, hfAfter)` unless `hfAfter ≥ max(hfBefore, p.minHealthFactor)`, else `hfAfter = type(uint256).max`; check the backing gap; revert with `AssetOutBelowMinimum(debtAssetToWallet, p.minAssetOut)` unless `debtAssetToWallet ≥ p.minAssetOut`; if `borrowCount == 0`, call `COLLATERALIZATION.markDebtFree(proxy)` ([2.3](03-collateralization.md) `REQ-COLL-040`), so a full deleverage leaves the market's debt count right for `closeMarket`; clear the session; emit `RepaidWithCollateral(msg.sender, p.marketId, vaultToken, p.position.positionKey, p.units, p.debtReserveId, debtRepaid, debtAssetToWallet, hfBefore, hfAfter)`; release the Lock. It reads no circuit breaker and is open under Frozen, under `Paused(General)` and for a frozen wallet, because it only reduces debt and the direction check bounds what any route returns (`Q-356`). Why `minAssetOut`: each leg's oracle minimum leaves up to the market's `slippageAllowanceBps` of the leg to whoever moves the leg's pool, and the repayment is covered either way, so without the borrower's own bound a sandwich takes that slice out of `debtAssetToWallet` with no revert; example at 100 bps: units worth 50,000 USDC repaying 20,000 USDC leave about 30,000 USDC for the wallet, of which up to 500 could be taken, and `minAssetOut = 29,850` USDC caps it at 150; it stays refused under `Paused(MintPathSuspect)`, which suspects the code path it runs. A record whose every credited unit is released returns its position to the depositor. Example: `SPEC-PROXY-009`. Checked by: TEST-239, PROP-072. (Source: Q-242, Q-250, Q-255, Q-257, Q-356)

## Math

Units: Aave "Value" is USD × 10^26 (1e26 = $1), as the Spoke computes it. `price8` is an 8-decimal USD price from the reserve's Aave oracle source. `e_r` is the decimals of debt asset `r`, `d_m` the decimals of market `m`'s vault token. WAD = 10^18.

- `SPEC-PROXY-001` **Post-action health gate.** After the proxy's Spoke call in `borrow`, and after Collateralization's `release` or `releasePartial` in a withdrawal or removal when debt remains (so the rescue units read are those left on the account), with `hf = spoke.getUserAccountData(proxy).healthFactor` read in the same call: pass ⇔ `hfB ≥ max(1.00e18, hardHealthFloor(m), minHealthFactor)`, evaluated by `RiskModule.postHealthCheck`, where `hfEx` is `hf` with the account's rescue units valued at zero and `hfB = hfEx × min(s, A) / s` (rounded down) is that value held to the market's accepted price `A`, with `s` the served price ([2.7](07-risk-module.md) `SPEC-CF-005`; `hfB = hfEx = hf` for an account with no rescue units whose market's served price is at most `A`, as in the example below). `postHealthCheck` returns `hfB`, and the `hf` the Adapter emits in `Borrowed`, `CollateralWithdrawn` and `CollateralReduced` is `hfB`. The bound is exact because an account's only collateral is its market's vault token, so the health factor is linear in that price: a borrow is held to the accepted price, and a rally since the last accepted price adds no borrowing power until `poke()` accepts it, while Aave's liquidation keeps using the served price. Units: WAD, inclusive comparison. Rounding: Aave rounds `hf` down against the user (`SPEC-PROXY-006`), so for any WAD threshold `k`, `hf ≥ k` holds only if the exact ratio is at least `k`. Overflow: `max` of three `uint256` cannot overflow; `hf = type(uint256).max` only with zero debt, which never reaches this gate. **Why:** Aave already refuses a borrow below health factor 1.00, but its check counts rescue units; requiring `hfEx ≥ 1.00` keeps rescue units from supporting new debt. Avana sets no opening floor above 1.00 (`Q-398`): the borrower chooses the headroom, the Lens recommends 1.10 next to the protocol maximum at 1.00, and the caller's `minHealthFactor` protects the borrower against a state change between signing and execution. `hardHealthFloor(m)` (ParameterStore, per market, 0 = off by default) exists for a market whose stress tests justify a hard floor. **Example:** in the multi-reserve account of the worked example (`B = $24,600`, GHO debt 8,000), a further USDC borrow of 15,000 USDC gives `D = 8,000 + 15,000 × 0.9999 = 22,998.5` and `hf = floor(24,600 / 22,998.5 × 10^18) = 1,069,634,976,194,099,615`; with no hard floor and the caller's `minHealthFactor = 1.10e18` (the Lens recommended level) the required value is `max(1.00e18, 0, 1.10e18) = 1.10e18`, so the call reverts `HealthFactorTooLow(1,069,634,976,194,099,615, 1,100,000,000,000,000,000)` and the USDC transfer to the receiver is undone. Checked by: MATH-AR-03, MATH-AR-06, MATH-UO-76, SYM-05, HYP-13, HYP-20, HYP-54, QNT-20. (Source: Q-018, Q-161, Q-229, Q-253, Q-398)
- `SPEC-PROXY-002` **Account price check.** Evaluated by `RiskModule.preBorrowCheck` and `preWithdrawCheck` after poking the account's market's LPOracle. For the account's market `m`: `(state, inputs) = O_m.getPriceState()`; pass ⇔ `state == Live` and, for every input `i` with original source timestamp `t_i` and feed `f_i`, `t_i ≤ block.timestamp` and `block.timestamp − t_i ≤ borrowPriceMaxAge(f_i) = heartbeat(f_i) + heartbeatMargin(f_i)`, from each feed's own published heartbeat. For each debt reserve `r` in the scope (every reserve the account borrows, plus the reserve being borrowed): `src = IAaveOracle(spoke.ORACLE()).getReserveSource(r)` must equal `MarketRegistry.getDebtReserve(spoke, r).priceSource`; for a fixed-price source (GHO on its Hub) there is no age; otherwise `src` is the asset's DebtOracle, poked first, and with `v = src.getPriceState()` pass ⇔ `v.state == Live`, `t_r = v.servedUpdatedAt ≤ block.timestamp` and `block.timestamp − t_r ≤ src.borrowPriceMaxAge()` ([2.10](10-lp-oracle.md) `SPEC-ORC-015`, `REQ-ORACLE-093`). Units: seconds; integer arithmetic, no rounding. Overflow: the subtraction runs only after `t ≤ block.timestamp` holds. A `borrowPriceMaxAge` of 0 admits only a same-block price (fails closed); a failed DebtOracle reports 0. **Why:** new debt must never be sized on a price that has stopped updating, on the collateral side or the debt side: Aave values the debt with the reserve's source, so a stale debt price misstates the health factor just as a stale collateral price does (`Q-238`). The check uses source timestamps, so a cache that keeps serving an old round cannot hide its age; it requires the DebtOracle Live, so a debt counted at its higher route during a disagreement or at its stress price once Expired never sizes new debt; and it compares the debt source with the recorded one, so a swapped source cannot inflate borrowing power. **Example:** at `block.timestamp = 1,762,700,000` an account holds GHO and USDC debt; its market is Live with inputs aged 1,800 s and 2,400 s; the USDC DebtOracle serves its primary USDC/USD round (heartbeat 86,400 s, margin 600 s, so `borrowPriceMaxAge() = 87,000` s) with `servedUpdatedAt = 1,762,612,000` (age 88,000 s). A GHO borrow on this account reverts `DebtPriceTooOld(r_USDC, USDC DebtOracle, 88,000)` although GHO itself has a fixed price: the stale USDC price blocks every borrow on the account. Checked by: QNT-18, HYP-14. (Source: Q-128, Q-182, Q-229, Q-234, Q-238, Q-248, Q-274)
- `SPEC-PROXY-003` **Repay sizing.** `D = spoke.getUserTotalDebt(r, proxy)` in the asset's base units (`e_r` decimals), equal to Aave's `rayMulUp(drawnShares, drawnIndex) + fromRayUp(premiumDebtRay)` accrued to `block.timestamp`. If `D = 0`: return 0 with no token movement, for both flags (after the `ZeroAmount` check of a partial repay). Partial: `x = min(maxRepaySpend, D)`, requires `maxRepaySpend > 0`. Repay-all: require `D ≤ maxRepaySpend` and `x = maxRepaySpend` (`SPEC-PROXY-007`). Pulled = `x`; `received` = the proxy's balance increase across the pull (`≤ x`); `spent` = the proxy's balance decrease across `Spoke.repay` (`≤ min(received, D)`); refund = `received − spent`. After a repay-all, `getUserTotalDebt = 0`. After a partial repay `D − x ≤ D' ≤ D − x + ceil(drawnIndex / RAY)` base units. Rounding: Aave rounds the amount it takes up and the debt removed down, both against the payer and never below the debt removed; with 6-decimal assets one base unit is $0.000001, so the bound is at most a few base units. Overflow: amounts are below `2^120` (Hub share bound); `min` and `x − spent` cannot overflow. **Why:** the payer signs a spending cap, so the pull never exceeds `maxRepaySpend`; repay-all reverts above the cap instead of silently repaying part, because its purpose is to leave zero debt so a withdrawal can follow; the live debt is read in the call, so interest accrued since signing is included; zero debt returns 0 because a liquidation or another payer may clear the debt first. **Example:** USDC debt (6 decimals) borrowed 30 days ago at 5,000 USDC with a 6.00% rate reads `D = ⌈5,000 × (1 + 0.06 × 30 / 365) × 10^6⌉ = 5,024,657,535`. A partial repay with `maxRepaySpend = 2,000e6` pulls 2,000,000,000; a repay-all with `maxRepaySpend = 5,030e6` pulls 5,030,000,000, Aave takes 5,024,657,535, 5,342,465 return to the payer and the debt is 0; with `maxRepaySpend = 5,020e6` it reverts `RepayExceedsMax(5,024,657,535, 5,020,000,000)` before any transfer. GHO debt of 8,000 at 5.00% over the same 30 days reads `D = 8,032,876,712,328,767,123,288` wei and sizes the same way. Checked by: MATH-AR-16, MATH-AR-17, MATH-AR-20, HYP-16, HYP-17, HYP-40, HYP-43, HYP-47, QNT-14, QNT-17. (Source: Q-153, Q-180, Q-212, Q-234, Q-269, Q-271)
- `SPEC-PROXY-004` **Borrow forwarding.** With `asset = spoke.getReserve(r).underlying`: `drawn = asset.balanceOf(proxy)_after − asset.balanceOf(proxy)_before` across `spoke.borrow`; require `drawn = amount`; forwarded to the receiver = `drawn`; the proxy's balance at the end equals its balance at the start. Debt recorded by Aave for the borrow is `≥ amount` (shares rounded up). Units: the asset's base units; no rounding in the proxy or the Adapter. Overflow: the Hub `draw` bounds `amount` by the Spoke's `drawCap`, far below `2^256`. **Why:** measuring the delta makes tokens donated to the proxy irrelevant (never forwarded, never blocking), and the equality detects a Hub or Spoke that delivers a different amount than the debt it records. **Example:** the proxy holds 0.37 USDC of donated dust (370,000 base units). A USDC borrow of `amount = 5,000,000,000` (5,000 USDC) raises the balance from 370,000 to 5,000,370,000, so `drawn = 5,000,000,000 = amount`; the proxy sends 5,000 USDC to the receiver and ends at 370,000, its start value. Checked by: MATH-AR-07, HYP-15, HYP-47, HYP-52. (Source: Q-064, Q-124, Q-233)
- `SPEC-PROXY-006` **Account health factor with several debt reserves.** For the account's one market `m` with supplied units `s_m`, served price `p_m` (8 decimals) and collateral factor `CF_m` (bps, the account's dynamic key), and debt reserves `r` with live debt `q_r` (base units) and price `P_r` (8 decimals, the reserve's Aave source): `V_m = s_m × p_m × 10^(18 − d_m)`, `DV_r = q_r × P_r × 10^(18 − e_r)` (Aave Value, exact), and `hf = floor(V_m × CF_m / 10^4 × WAD / Σ_r DV_r)`; `hf = type(uint256).max` when `Σ DV_r = 0`. Units: WAD. Rounding: Aave computes the numerator and denominator in its own fixed point and rounds `hf` down against the user; every Avana gate reads the Spoke's value rather than recomputing it. Overflow: `V_m`, `DV_r` < `2^200` under the Hub's `2^120` amount bound and 8-decimal prices below `2^64`; Aave's math uses 256-bit intermediates. **Why:** each account holds one market and every debt reserve it borrows on that market's Spoke under one health factor, so the market's collateral backs debt in any enabled asset, while a second market of the same wallet is a separate account whose health a price move in this market never touches (`Q-307`); within the account, a debt-asset price rise lowers `hf` without any collateral move, and any reserve's stale price blocks the account (`Q-234`). **Example:** the two-reserve account of the worked example has `V_B = $82,000` at `CF_B = 3,000`, so the CF-weighted collateral is `$24,600`; GHO debt `8,000e18 × 10^8 × 1 = 8×10^29` and USDC debt `5,000e6 × 99,990,000 × 10^12 = 4.9995×10^29`, total $12,999.50; `hf = floor(24,600 / 12,999.5 × 10^18) = 1,892,380,476,172,160,467`. Checked by: MATH-AR-01, MATH-AR-03, MATH-AR-05. (Source: Q-233, Q-234, Q-238, Q-307)
- `SPEC-PROXY-007` **Repay-all pull and refund.** With `D_e` the live debt of the reserve at execution and `M = maxRepaySpend` (both in the asset's base units): require `D_e ≤ M`, else `RepayExceedsMax(D_e, M)` before any transfer; pulled `= M`; `received` = the proxy's balance increase (`= M` for a token without a transfer fee); `spent = min(received, D_e)`, taken by Aave; refund `= received − spent`; required debt after `= D_e − spent = 0`. The app sets `M`, and the approval, to `quoteRepayAll = ⌈D_s × (1 + ρ × (t_d − t_s) / 31,536,000) × 1.001⌉`, with `D_s` the debt read at signing time `t_s`, `ρ` the account's current borrow rate in the reserve (drawn rate plus premium, per year) and `t_d` the deadline ([2.17](17-lens.md) `REQ-LENS-036`). Units: base units and seconds. Rounding: the quote rounds up; Aave rounds the debt it takes up against the payer, which the 0.1% margin absorbs. Overflow: amounts are below `2^120` and the products fit in `uint256`. **Why:** debt accrues every second, so a cap equal to the debt read at signing always fell short at inclusion; quoting to the deadline with a 0.1% margin makes the first attempt succeed, and refunding what Aave did not take leaves the payer's allowance at zero. **Example:** USDC debt `D_s = 5,024,657,535` at 6.00% with the deadline 600 s away: `quoteRepayAll = ⌈5,024,657,535 × (1 + 0.06 × 600 / 31,536,000) × 1.001⌉ = 5,029,687,935`; included 24 s later with `D_e = 5,024,657,765`, the Adapter pulls 5,029,687,935, Aave takes 5,024,657,765, 5,030,170 return to the payer, the debt is 0 and the allowance is 0. Checked by: TEST-248, TEST-247. (Source: Q-268, Q-269, Q-271)
- `SPEC-PROXY-008` **Backing gap.** For the touched market, `G = totalCredited + pending.units − totalSupply` (vault-token base units), from `COLLATERALIZATION.getTotalCredited(vaultToken)`, `getPending(vaultToken).units` and `vaultToken.totalSupply()`. Pass ⇔ `totalSupply_after ≤ totalCredited_after + pending.units_after` and `G_after = G_before`, with `G_before` read before the call's first Collateralization state change, mint or burn and `G_after` after its last Collateralization call. Units: vault-token base units. Rounding: none; all quantities are integers that change by exact unit counts. Overflow: each term is below `2^128`; the comparison uses the first form, so `G` is computed only when it is non-negative. **Why:** every conforming pledge, withdrawal, removal, re-credit or repay with collateral changes supply and credited units by the same amount, so the gap is unchanged by exactly those calls; an absolute equality would turn one unsettled seizure burn into a revert of every later pledge and withdrawal in the market, debt-free exits included, while the gap form keeps the market working and still proves every unit in circulation is backed. **Example:** in market A a faulty seizure burned 1,575,000,000 units without debiting them, so `G = 1,575,000,000`. A pledge crediting 50,000,000,000 units raises both `totalCredited` and `totalSupply` by 50,000,000,000, `G` stays 1,575,000,000 and the pledge passes; a mint of 50,000,000,001 would leave `G = 1,574,999,999` and revert `BackingMismatch`. With no faulty burn, `G = 0` before and after every call. Checked by: TEST-241, PROP-065, QNT-03. (Source: Q-224, Q-257)
- `SPEC-PROXY-009` **Repay with collateral: health factor direction.** With `A` the account's CF-weighted collateral value and `D` its debt value (Aave Value), `HF = A / D`. Releasing units of market `m` worth `V` at the oracle price removes `a = CF_m × V` from `A`; repaying debt worth `y` removes `y` from `D`. While debt remains the call requires `HF' = (A − a) / (D − y) ≥ max(HF, minHealthFactor)`. Lemma: `HF' ≥ HF ⇔ y × A ≥ a × D ⇔ y ≥ a / HF`. LPVaultSwap sizes `y ≤ (V × (1 − s) − U) / (1 + f)` (slippage allowance `s`, one-unit-per-leg allowance `U`, flash fee `f`, [2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)); when that bound is met, `HF' ≥ HF` holds for every account with `HF ≥ CF_m × (1 + f) / (1 − s)` plus the `U` term, so the check refuses only an account far below liquidation or a conversion that returned less than the oracle bound. Units: Aave Value (USD × 10^26), `HF` in WAD. Rounding: the Adapter compares Aave's floored health factors read before and after (inclusive), both after LPVaultSwap's pokes (`REQ-ADAPTER-080`). Overflow: none beyond Aave's own. **Why:** the call must only reduce risk; repaying before releasing keeps the health factor from dipping during the call, and checking the direction afterwards bounds the result whatever a route returns. **Example:** `A = $12,000`, GHO debt 10,000 (`HF = 1.20`); releasing $3,000 of a market at `CF = 0.312` gives `a = $936`; with `s = 100` bps, `U = 0.01` GHO and a 9 bps flash fee (0 at launch), `y = (3,000 × 0.99 − 0.01) / 1.0009 = 2,967.32` GHO; `HF' = 11,064 / 7,032.68 = 1.5732 ≥ 1.20`, against the lemma's bound `y ≥ 936 / 1.20 = 780`. The critical health factor is `0.312 × 1.0009 / 0.99 = 0.3154`. Checked by: TEST-239, PROP-072. (Source: Q-255)

## Worked examples

Launch parameters on Ethereum: no Avana opening floor (`hardHealthFloor = 0`, off, `Q-398`), Lens recommended health factor 1.10, `borrowPriceMaxAge(f) = heartbeat(f) + heartbeatMargin(f)` with `heartbeatMargin = 1,800` s for each feed (84,600 s for the 82,800 s USDC/USD feed, 88,200 s for an 86,400 s feed, 5,400 s for a 3,600 s feed), `minPledgeUsd = $10` per market, no minimum debt. CDP Spoke `S` (Uniswap v3 Stable, home Hub the Ethereum Stable LP Hub) has two enabled debt reserves, both funded by that Hub: `r_GHO` (GHO, 18 decimals, Aave's fixed $1.00 source) and `r_USDC` (USDC, 6 decimals, priced by its DebtOracle on the USDC/USD Chainlink route through the AaveOracle, reading $0.9999).

**Non-GHO borrow.** Wallet `W` has an account `P_A` for market A (USDC/USDT, `d = 6`) with one record: `L = 25,000,000,000` units at $2.00 per whole vault token, collateral $50,000, `CF_A = 3,120`, so `B = $15,600`. No debt; `borrowNonce = 4`.
