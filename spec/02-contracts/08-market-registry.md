# 2.8 Market Registry

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-11
>
> **Requirement prefixes:** `REQ-CONFIG-NNN` (registry rules kept from the earlier configuration contract), `REQ-MREG-NNN`

## Purpose

The MarketRegistry is the upgradeable record of what Avana lists on one chain and of each Hub's pause switch. One MarketRegistry runs per chain and records every Hub, CDP Spoke, AMM integration and market on that chain (`Q-312`). It is responsible for:

- the AMM integrations of the chain: `integrationId` → the AMM protocol's immutable LPVault and its pricing implementation, pinned at registration (`REQ-CONFIG-075`); the LPVault pins its own position manager and factory (or pool manager) and validates every pool and shape in `bindMarket`;
- the Hubs of the chain and, for each CDP Spoke, its home Hub, AMM integration, risk tier, Spoke implementation with its approved code hash, and AaveOracle (`REQ-CONFIG-083`, `REQ-CONFIG-074`);
- market definitions: the one-to-one map market ↔ (AMM integration, pool, exact position shape) ↔ vault token ↔ LPOracle ↔ Hub asset `(homeHub, hubAssetId)` ↔ CDP Spoke reserve `(spoke, reserveId)`, and each market's status (`Registered`, `Active`, `Offboarding`, `Closed`);
- the enabled debt reserves of each CDP Spoke: asset, decimals, funding Hub, funding asset ID, Aave price source, Avana Umbrella (Coverage Spoke) of `(funding Hub, asset)`, the protocol safety module of that pair (`Q-402`);
- the credit-line registry: per (funding Hub, asset) the recorded line and the sum of every drawing Spoke's `drawCap`, the Coverage Spoke of each (Hub, asset), the cross-Hub line caps per (funding Hub, asset, borrowing Spoke) with their sum, and the chain switch `crossHubLinesEnabled` (false at launch) (`REQ-CONFIG-081`);
- the three deployment operations `deployCdpSpoke` (records the standard Spoke the Aave payload deployed), `listCollateralMarket` (binds the vault token and LPOracle deployed earlier in the listing batch) and `enableDebtReserve` (`REQ-CONFIG-077`…`REQ-CONFIG-079`);
- listed tokens (pool tokens and debt assets): decimals, the stable flag, primary, alternate and pre-approved standby Chainlink routes (only the monitoring feed for a fixed-price debt asset that is no pool token, `REQ-CONFIG-021`), which slot the standby replaces, the issuer probe and the peg module;
- swap routes per market, pool token and debt asset (pool token → debt asset), each with a primary and a backup slot;
- the payout-escrow address only: there is no in-kind settlement window or automatic trigger (`Q-401`);
- the pause state `None`, `Frozen` or `Paused` of each Hub with its `PauseReason`, its transitions and who may make them (`REQ-CONFIG-082`);
- the breaker state per market and per (CDP Spoke, debt reserve): the security-freeze latch (`DEFICIT`, `ISSUER`, `CONFIG_DRIFT`, `MANUAL`) and the time of the last temporary-gate breach (`lastBreachAt`), both written only by the RiskModule's `pokeBreakers` (through `recordBreach`) and by the guardians' manual trips and resets (`Q-399`);
- the set of frozen addresses, the per-caller freeze limit and the record of the AMM protocol contracts of each integration (the position managers and factories, or the singleton, its LPVault pins), keyed to the chain and the integration's build, which the freeze protects and which no swap route may use; an integration's routes are accepted only once its record is finalized (`REQ-MREG-035`);
- the market-count limit `MAX_MARKET_COUNT`, which bounds the ParameterStore's chain-wide re-checks (`REQ-MREG-034`);
- the on-chain listing, activation, relisting, offboarding, wind-down and closing checks.

It is **not** responsible for: any risk parameter value, bound, feed setting, tolerance, cap, `minPledgeUsd`, slippage allowance, route depth threshold, breaker threshold or `gateRecoverySeconds` (ParameterStore, [2.9](09-parameter-store.md)); enforcing gates or evaluating condition breakers (RiskModule, [2.7](07-risk-module.md), called by the AaveAdapter, [2.6](06-aave-adapter.md), and Collateralization, [2.3](03-collateralization.md)); price computation and price states (LPOracle and DebtOracle, [2.10](10-lp-oracle.md)); route code (Settlement Routes, [2.12](12-settlement-routes.md)); the keeper list, which is the registry of committed operators (LPVaultSwap, [2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)); delays and the timelock (AvanaTimelock, [2.15](15-governance-and-timelock.md)); and any Aave-side setting (Hub, Spoke, AaveOracle, Coverage Spokes and their caps, flags and collateral factors belong to the Aave DAO).

Immutable contracts (vault tokens, LPVaults, Collateralization, Lock, ProxyManager, PositionProxies) never read their Spoke, Hub, reserve IDs, debt asset or pool from the MarketRegistry; they read them from immutables set at creation or receive them from the AaveAdapter and LPVaultSwap. The MarketRegistry is read by the AaveAdapter, RiskModule, ParameterStore, LPVaultSwap and the Lens. Global invariants are defined in [4.1 Global Invariants](../04-security/01-global-invariants.md); in words, the MarketRegistry never maps one pool, vault token, LPOracle, Hub asset or Spoke reserve to two markets that are not cancelled, never lists a debt reserve the CDP Spoke does not lend, and neither its pause state, its breakers nor its address freeze ever blocks repay, debt-free withdrawal or removal, fee collection, zero-credit release, pending settlement or liquidation (`INV-081`, `INV-085`).

## Outputs

- View functions returning market, token, price-route, Spoke, debt-reserve, route, pause, breaker and freeze data to the AaveAdapter, RiskModule, ProxyManager, ParameterStore, LPOracle, DebtOracle, LPVaultSwap and the Lens.
- Market lifecycle changes (Registered → Active → Offboarding, or Registered → Cancelled → Registered on relisting), debt-reserve approvals and removals, price-route and standby changes, route changes, pause-state and pause-reason changes, breach records, breaker latches and resets, and address freezes, each with an event.
- No token or NFT movement ever originates in the MarketRegistry.

## Storage

All mutable storage sits in one ERC-7201 namespace (`avana.storage.MarketRegistry`). The implementation serves every view and checks every write's caller itself; each write body runs by delegatecall in one of six immutable logic contracts (hubs, listing, activation, routes, safety, wind-down) built with the same immutables, which the constructor checks through each logic contract's `logicConfig()`, because the whole surface does not fit the 24,576-byte code limit in one contract; a write body called directly on a logic contract reverts. Implementation immutables (changed only by an upgrade at the normal delay), all per chain: `TIMELOCK`, `AAVE_GOVERNANCE_EXECUTOR`, `VAULT_TOKEN_FACTORY`, `AAVE_ADAPTER`, `COLLATERALIZATION`, `LOCK`, `PROXY_MANAGER`, `PARAMETER_STORE`, `RISK_MODULE`, `LP_VAULT_SWAP`, `PAYOUT_ESCROW` (the payout escrow, `Q-401`), `FLASH_LIQUIDATOR` (the public flash liquidation helper of [2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md), protected from the address freeze), `STANDARD_SPOKE_IMPLEMENTATION` (an audited standard Aave V4 Spoke implementation whose code hash is approved from deployment; every other approved hash is a record, `REQ-CONFIG-074`), and `MAX_MARKET_COUNT`, the most markets the registry ever lists (`REQ-MREG-034`). There is no Hub, GHO, AMM-protocol, position-manager or factory immutable: Hubs, AMM integrations and debt assets are records, so a new Hub, AMM protocol or debt asset needs no upgrade (`Q-313`). Constants: `MAX_ADDRESS_FREEZES_PER_DAY = 20`; `STANDBY_RESWITCH_COOLDOWN = 5 days` (`REQ-MREG-028`); the wind-down schedule `OFFBOARD_NOTICE_SECONDS = 5 days`, `WIND_DOWN_RAMP_SECONDS = 30 days`, `WIND_DOWN_STEP_SECONDS = 1 day`, `WIND_DOWN_FINAL_LT_BPS = 5,000` (`REQ-CONFIG-080`). There is no breaker window: seizures and route failures are counted by monitoring, not on-chain (`Q-399`). The view `spokeSettingsDrift` runs its per-market Aave reads in the activation logic's `marketDrift`, which reads no registry storage and is called directly, so the registry stays within the code limit.

## Restrictions

### Form

- `REQ-CONFIG-001` — The MarketRegistry MUST sit behind one fixed ERC-1967 proxy address using the UUPS pattern, whose `_authorizeUpgrade` requires `APPLICATION_ADMIN`, held only by the Avana timelock; Avana's upgradeable contracts and the Lens reference that address. It is upgradeable at the normal delay together with the AaveAdapter, ProxyManager, ParameterStore, RiskModule, each LPOracle and LPVaultSwap. (Source: Q-226, Q-228, Q-240)
- `REQ-CONFIG-002` — Upgrades MUST go only through `upgradeToAndCall` on the proxy, executed by the timelock at the normal delay and cancellable by any `CANCELLER_ROLE` holder while queued, to an implementation that is already deployed and source-verified (reviewers check its bytecode hash and simulate the operation on a fork during the delay). The implementation MUST use ERC-7201 namespaced storage diffed in CI against the last deployed layout, call `_disableInitializers()` in its constructor, run any `reinitializer(n)` atomically in the same `upgradeToAndCall`, and link no external libraries; `initialize(timelock)` runs once, atomically with the proxy deployment; deployment manifests record each value's activation block. (Source: Q-043, Q-119, Q-226, Q-228)
- `REQ-CONFIG-003` — The MarketRegistry MUST NOT hold or approve any token or NFT and MUST NOT call any state-changing function on Aave, Uniswap, Chainlink or another Avana contract, with one exception: `listCollateralMarket` calls the market's integration's `bindMarket` once per market, which records the pool and shape in that LPVault (`REQ-VTOKEN-022`). It has no rescue or sweep function. A standby switch therefore changes only the registry's record and `priceRouteVersion`; each oracle sees the new routes and version on its next read. (Source: Q-067, Q-104, Q-124, Q-248)
- `REQ-CONFIG-004` — Immutable Avana contracts (vault tokens, LPVaults, Collateralization, the Lock, the ProxyManager, PositionProxies) MUST NOT read Spoke, Hub, reserve IDs, debt asset or pool from the MarketRegistry; they read their own immutables or take them from the AaveAdapter and LPVaultSwap, which read the registry for new activity only. (Source: Q-224, Q-260, Q-313)
- `REQ-CONFIG-005` — Canonical per-chain Avana addresses (`TIMELOCK`, `AAVE_GOVERNANCE_EXECUTOR`, `VAULT_TOKEN_FACTORY`, `AAVE_ADAPTER`, `COLLATERALIZATION`, `LOCK`, `PROXY_MANAGER`, `PARAMETER_STORE`, `RISK_MODULE`, `LP_VAULT_SWAP`, `PAYOUT_ESCROW`, `FLASH_LIQUIDATOR`, `STANDARD_SPOKE_IMPLEMENTATION`) MUST be implementation immutables, checked against the chain's deployment manifest; there is no SettlementBudget (`Q-397`). `FLASH_LIQUIDATOR` is the public flash liquidation helper ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md) `REQ-HELPER-010`): its constructor reads `isRecordedHub(POOL)` from this registry, so the deployment fixes its address in the manifest first, builds the registry implementation with it, and deploys the helper at that address once the registry proxy is initialized; until then the protected address holds no code and nothing calls it. The chain's IFlashSource is not an immutable: it is the stored `flashSource()`, replaced only by `setFlashSource(newSource)` at the normal delay, which reverts `InvalidRoute` unless `newSource.LP_VAULT_SWAP() == LP_VAULT_SWAP` and `isRecordedHub(newSource.LENDER())` is false, where `LENDER()` is the pool or Hub the loan draws from, never an intermediate contract (for a flash Spoke on an Aave V4 Hub, that Hub, not the Spoke); LPVaultSwap repeats the test at every `deleverage` flash loan (the only LPVaultSwap path that borrows, `Q-404`), so a Hub registered after the source was set is caught at the call, and `recordHub(hub, tier)` reverts `InvalidRoute` when `hub == IFlashSource(flashSource()).LENDER()` (`Q-386`, `Q-388`). Hubs, AMM integrations (LPVault, position manager, factory), debt assets and CDP Spokes MUST be records written by timelock operations, never immutables: the core then serves any number of Hubs, AMM protocols and debt assets with no upgrade (AMMN-GAS-16). (Source: Q-226, Q-313, Q-404)

### Market definitions

- `REQ-CONFIG-006` — A market is one (chain, AMM integration, pool, exact position shape) = one vault token = one Hub asset `(homeHub, hubAssetId)` = one reserve `(spoke, reserveId)` on exactly one CDP Spoke, whose home Hub and integration the market inherits; `marketId` is a sequential `uint256` from 1, below `2^32`, and the external market key in events and indexing is `(chainId, spoke, reserveId)`. For concentrated liquidity the shape is the exact `[tickLower, tickUpper]` range; two markets on the same pool with different ranges are different markets. (Source: Q-017, Q-076, Q-119, Q-312)
- `REQ-CONFIG-007` — Each of `marketOfKey`, `marketOfVaultToken`, `marketOfLpOracle`, `marketOfHubAsset` and `marketOfReserve` MUST map a key to at most one market that is neither `Cancelled` nor `Closed`; a write that would map a key already held by such a market MUST revert with `DuplicateMarketKey`. `marketOfKey` is keyed by the VaultTokenFactory's market key `marketKeyOf(integrationId, pool, poolId, shape)` `= keccak256(abi.encode(chainId, integrationId, pool, poolId, kind, tickLower, tickUpper))`, so the chain has one live market per (AMM integration, pool, exact shape) across all its Spokes; the factory keys tokens by `(spoke, marketKey)` ([2.4](04-vault-tokens.md) `REQ-VTOKEN-021`). `marketOfHubAsset` is keyed by `(hub, hubAssetId)`, because Aave V4 numbers each Hub's assets from 0. A market wound down to `Closed` frees its keys, so the same pool and shape can be relisted on another Spoke, or under a new `integrationId`, with a new token and a new `marketId`; its old token stays bound to the closed market. Cancelling a market deletes its market-key, vault-token and LPOracle entries and keeps its `marketId` reserved for its (Spoke, market key) pair, and only a relisting of that same pair reuses it (`REQ-MREG-030`). `marketOfLpOracle` lets the ParameterStore resolve the market of the LPOracle that asks for its configuration. Example: the USDC/USDT `[−50, 50]` market 3 on the Uniswap v3 Stable Spoke is wound down to `Closed`; a listing of the same pool and range on a second Stable Spoke reuses the free market key, deploys a new token at `predictAddress(spoke2, marketKey)` and gets a new `marketId`; while market 3 was Active, that listing reverted `DuplicateMarketKey`, and a second listing on the first Spoke reverts `TokenExistsForMarket` at the factory. (Source: Q-068, Q-076, Q-119, Q-265, Q-355)
- `REQ-CONFIG-008` — `hub`, `spoke`, `integrationId`, `integration`, `pool` or `poolId`, `shape`, `tokens`, `tokenDecimals`, `vaultToken`, `decimals` and `lpOracle` MUST be immutable after `listCollateralMarket`, and `hubAssetId` and `reserveId` immutable after activation; pool-specific settings (fee tier, tick spacing, factory generation, rate providers) belong to the integration's own per-market configuration, validated at listing. (Source: Q-075, Q-119, Q-313)
- `REQ-CONFIG-009` — A market's `lpOracle` address MUST be immutable for the market; a new price source or a change to price computation is an LPOracle upgrade or a `setPriceSource` at the normal delay ([2.10](10-lp-oracle.md)). Failover to the pre-approved alternate route is automatic, and replacing a route with the pre-approved standby route is a guardian's immediate `useStandbyRoute` (`REQ-MREG-028`); neither introduces a source that was not reviewed at the normal delay. (Source: Q-091, Q-130, Q-226, Q-228, Q-248)
- `REQ-CONFIG-010` — Each vault token is permanently bound to its market's home Hub and CDP Spoke; a market moves to another Hub or Spoke only by offboarding and relisting, and users move by repay, withdraw and re-pledge. (Source: Q-032, Q-068, Q-119, Q-312)
- `REQ-CONFIG-011` — A market MUST be registered only for an AMM integration registered on this chain whose readiness guards hold ([2.2](02-lp-vault.md)): at launch, Uniswap v3 on Ethereum and Aerodrome Slipstream on Base, each on the chain's Stable LP Hub; Uniswap v2, Curve StableSwap-NG, Balancer and Uniswap v4 integrations register only after their pricing model and listing guards (`getReserves` and pending fee supply for v2, minimum-price valuation and reentrancy-lock check for Curve and Balancer, a hook allowlist with no remove-liquidity hooks or deltas for v4, a per-block quote-change cap for all) pass. The pool tokens' class must fit the Hub's tier (stable/stable for Stable LP). (Source: Q-017, Q-312, Q-313)
- `REQ-CONFIG-012` — A market in `Registered` or `Cancelled` status MUST be treated as not listed by every Avana contract and the Lens: no pledge, borrow or price-based action uses it. (Source: Q-068, Q-092, Q-114, Q-265)
- `REQ-CONFIG-013` — A market MUST NOT be deleted or returned to `Active` once it leaves `Registered`; offboarding is `setMarketOffboarding` (short delay), which moves an `Active` market to `Offboarding` for good, paired with an Aave freeze of the vault-token reserve, notice, and any stepped collateral-factor reduction that keeps CF > 0; there is no force-close, and the market's data stays readable for repay, withdrawal and liquidation until its backing supply is 0. (Source: Q-048, Q-119, Q-227)
- `REQ-CONFIG-014` — The MarketRegistry MUST NOT store a risk parameter value, a settlement mode, an allowlist or a markets-per-wallet cap; every parameter, including `minPledgeUsd`, lives in the ParameterStore, and the per-account record bound (16) is a Collateralization constant. Why: the funding Hub's suppliers bear the loss, so that Hub owns the cap, and only a sum across every drawing Spoke bounds it (AMMN-FLOW-06, AMMN-SM-01, AMMN-RT-11). Checked by: MATH-CAP-10, TEST-273. (Source: Q-312, Q-353, Q-391)

### Price routes and the standby switch

The standby switch preserves: no route is ever in force that did not pass the listing checks at the normal delay; the registry changes only its own record (oracles keep their own freshness, validity and agreement checks, and a switch never makes a state Live); exits and liquidation of other reserves never depend on it.

- `REQ-MREG-027` — `TokenDef` MUST record, beside the primary and alternate routes, a `standbyRoute` (`address[]`, one or two feeds), a `replacedSlot` (`RouteSlot`, `None` at listing and after `setPriceSource`) and an `issuerProbe` (`IssuerProbe`): `PausedFlag` for a token whose issuer can pause transfers through `paused()`, `UsdtFeeRate` for a token with a transfer-fee switch read through `basisPointsRate()`, `UsdtFeeRateAndPaused` for a token with both (USDT on Ethereum), `None` otherwise. The combined kind runs both reads and trips when either trips; every reader treats it exactly as it treats each single kind it contains: the RiskModule's `ISSUER` freeze runs both reads, each failing closed on a revert, an out-of-gas or a malformed answer ([2.7](07-risk-module.md) `SPEC-BRK-003`), and the fee-switch rules of `setRoute` (`REQ-ROUTE-008`) and `enableDebtReserve` (`REQ-MREG-006`) apply to it as to `UsdtFeeRate`. Every kind is allowed on every chain; the listing package sets each token's kind per chain from the fork check of the deployed token: the combined kind when it has both functions, the single kind when it has one, `None` when it has neither (bridged USDT on Base, a standard-bridge token, has neither and is listed with `None`). `listToken` and `setPriceSource` run the route checks of `REQ-CONFIG-021` on all three routes; `listToken` reverts `ListingCheckFailed("replacedSlot")` unless `def.replacedSlot == None`. The RiskModule runs the recorded probe as the `ISSUER` security freeze ([2.7](07-risk-module.md)), which reopens only by an authorized reset once the probe is clear (`Q-399`). Example: USDC is listed with `issuerProbe = PausedFlag`, USDT on Ethereum with `UsdtFeeRateAndPaused`, GHO and DAI with `None`; if Tether pauses USDT, every market holding it and every USDT debt reserve trips `ISSUER`, exactly as a `PausedFlag` token would, and a USDT fee switched on trips them as `UsdtFeeRate` would. (Source: Q-241, Q-248, Q-274)
- `REQ-MREG-028` — Standby switch:
  - `useStandbyRoute(token, slot)` MUST require `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN` (immediate) or `APPLICATION_ADMIN` (short delay; the timelock lets only `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` cancel it), else `Unauthorized`; it reverts `InvalidRouteSlot` unless `slot` is `Primary` or `Alternate`, `TokenNotListed` for an unlisted token, `NoStandbyRoute(token)` when the standby is empty, `StandbyAlreadyActive(token)` unless `replacedSlot == None`, and `ListingCheckFailed("feed")` or `ListingCheckFailed("heartbeat")` if a standby leg no longer has a feed definition or its definition has no heartbeat (each leg is fresh within its own published heartbeat, `REQ-CONFIG-021`; there is no global heartbeat ceiling). A pause guardian's call (a caller without `APPLICATION_ADMIN`) reverts `StandbySwitchLocked(token, until)` while `block.timestamp < until = standbyCancelledAt[token] + STANDBY_RESWITCH_COOLDOWN` (5 days, the normal delay), so a guardian cannot re-apply a switch the Aave DAO undid before a timelocked review could; the timelock's own call is not locked, every switch that runs clears `standbyCancelledAt[token]`, and so does `setPriceSource`. `standbySwitchLockedUntil(token)` returns `until` (0 when no cancel was recorded). It sets `replacedSlot = slot`, increments `priceRouteVersion[token]` and emits `StandbyRouteActivated(token, slot, msg.sender)`.
  - `cancelStandbySwitch(token)` MUST require `msg.sender == AAVE_GOVERNANCE_EXECUTOR` (the holder of the timelock's `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE`), else `Unauthorized`, and `replacedSlot != None`, else `StandbyNotActive(token)`; it sets `replacedSlot = None`, records `standbyCancelledAt[token] = block.timestamp`, increments `priceRouteVersion[token]` and emits `StandbySwitchCancelled`. It is immediate, so the Aave DAO can undo a switch it disagrees with at once, and the 5-day lock keeps a guardian from switching the token straight back.
  - `restorePriceRoute(token)` MUST require `APPLICATION_ADMIN` (normal delay) and `replacedSlot != None` (`StandbyNotActive`); it has the same effect and emits `PriceRouteRestored`.
  - `activePriceRoutes(token)` MUST return `(primary, alternate, replacedSlot)` with the standby substituted into the replaced slot, and `priceRouteVersion(token)` the counter. The LPOracle and the DebtOracle read their routes only through these two views ([2.10](10-lp-oracle.md)); when the version differs from the one an oracle stored at its last `poke`, the oracle restarts its disagreement timer and keeps its failure timer, so a switch can end a disagreement or an outage but never delays Cached or Expired and never forces Live.

  Why: when a feed fails or two routes disagree, the market's own seizures wait for a usable price; a normal-delay fix makes that wait at least five days, longer than the 48-hour window the cap-sizing guidance assumes, while a standby reviewed at listing ends it within minutes without letting a guardian choose a source. Example: USDC's primary USDC/USD feed stops at 10:00; at 10:20 the Emergency Guardian calls `useStandbyRoute(USDC, Primary)`; `priceRouteVersion(USDC)` goes from 3 to 4, the next `poke` of every USDC market and of the USDC DebtOracle reads the standby, and if the standby and the alternate agree the markets return to Live with the grace window. At 11:00 the Aave governance executor could call `cancelStandbySwitch(USDC)` to undo it, after which a guardian's `useStandbyRoute(USDC, ·)` reverts `StandbySwitchLocked` until 11:00 five days later while a timelocked `useStandbyRoute` or `setPriceSource` stays possible; otherwise `restorePriceRoute(USDC)` executes after the normal delay once the primary recovers. Checked by: TEST-231. (Source: Q-248, Q-262, Q-263)

### Listing order

The listing order is: (1) the Aave V4 risk service providers and Avana prepare the listing package (thresholds, manipulation-cost study, minimum pool depth, route depth thresholds, `minPledgeUsd`, the `SPEC-CF-001` computation, each feed's published heartbeat, the standby route of each token and its checks, each token's issuer probe, and the cap values the Aave DAO sets under the cap-sizing guidance of `SPEC-CAP-007`, which Avana does not enforce on-chain); (2) the market's LPOracle is deployed behind its proxy and seeded with a valid Live price; (3) the listing operation, one timelock batch (`scheduleBatch`) at the normal delay, runs in order any new feed definitions (with heartbeats), token collateral factors and tolerances in the ParameterStore and `listToken` here (three routes, or a fixed-price debt asset's monitoring feed, and the issuer probe), `deployVaultToken(integrationId, pool, poolId, shape, hub, spoke, marketCount + 1, lpOracle, decimals, name, symbol)` on the vault-token factory (for a relisting of a cancelled pair the token already exists and is not deployed again), `listCollateralMarket`, the ParameterStore's `setInitialMarketParams`, `setInitialMarketCaps`, `setInitialTokenCap` and route depth thresholds, and `setRoute` for each non-GHO pool token and slot, so a failed registration also reverts the token deployment; (4) the Aave DAO payload adds the Hub asset, the CDP Spoke reserve, the Hub caps and the AaveOracle source; (5) after the Aave DAO payload has executed and the off-chain live-config check passes, the governance multisig schedules `activateMarket` at the short delay, and it runs the on-chain match checks against the Aave payload. A listing therefore carries one normal delay at registration and one short delay at activation (`REQ-CONFIG-073`).

- `REQ-MREG-033` — **Settlement debt reserve of a market.** Each market MUST record `settlementReserve(marketId)`, the debt reserve `r_s` of its CDP Spoke into whose asset pending settlement converts: set by `listCollateralMarket` to the first debt reserve enabled on the Spoke (GHO funded by the chain's Stable LP Hub at launch, `REQ-MREG-009`) and changed only by `setSettlementReserve(marketId, reserveId)` at the normal delay, which reverts `UnknownDebtReserve(spoke, reserveId)` unless `reserveId` is enabled on the market's Spoke. Why: a market can have several debt reserves, so the destination of recovered value must be one recorded reserve, and that reserve's Coverage Spoke covers deficits in the same asset on the Hub that lent it (AMMN-FLOW-23). Example: a Base "Aerodrome Slipstream Stable" market with GHO and USDC enabled records `r_s = r_GHO`; pending USDC and USDT from an unsettled seizure convert to GHO and fund the (Base Stable LP Hub, GHO) Coverage Spoke. (Source: Q-338, Q-358)
- `REQ-MREG-034` — **Market-count limit.** `listCollateralMarket` MUST revert `MarketCountLimit(MAX_MARKET_COUNT)` when it would give a new market the identifier `marketCount + 1 > MAX_MARKET_COUNT`, before the market is written or bound; a relisting (`REQ-MREG-030`) reuses an identifier `≤ marketCount` and is accepted at the limit; no other path assigns or raises `marketCount`. `MAX_MARKET_COUNT` is an implementation immutable, non-zero (else every constructor reverts `ListingCheckFailed("immutables")`), set by the deployment and stated again by every upgrade. Why: the ParameterStore's chain-wide re-checks ([2.9](09-parameter-store.md) `REQ-PSTORE-009`) walk market identifiers `1 … marketCount` and run `checkConfig` of every Registered, Active or Offboarding market's LPOracle in one transaction; Cancelled and Closed markets keep their identifiers inside that walk, so the limit counts lifetime listings, closed ones included, and it is what keeps the walk within one transaction. An immutable rather than a timelock setter: the safe value is a property of the deployed code's re-check cost, which only an upgrade changes, so each upgrade states the value with the code it was measured for, and no governance call can raise it past the measurement. Sizing is a launch requirement: `MAX_MARKET_COUNT` is measured on a fork of the target chain with the most expensive ParameterStore setter (`setFeedAgreementMarginBps`: every market's `checkConfig` and each pool token's tolerance bound), every market at the worst supported configuration (the most pool tokens and route legs, the costliest LPOracle path) and cold reads, and set to the largest count whose re-check uses at most 50 % of the chain's per-transaction gas limit (on Ethereum `2^24 = 16,777,216` gas, EIP-7825, so at most 8,388,608 gas), leaving the other half for later cost increases and for the setter's own work; it is measured again whenever an upgrade of the MarketRegistry, the ParameterStore or the LPOracle raises the re-check cost, and when the new bound is below the deployed limit the registry upgrade with the lower limit goes in the same governance batch. When the lifetime limit becomes a practical constraint, the registry moves to a kept list of configured markets plus the limit, so retired markets leave the walk. Units: market counts; integers. Example: in the test fixture (mock LPOracles whose `checkConfig` is a storage read), `setFeedAgreementMarginBps` costs 302,539 gas with 1 market and 9,515,531 gas with 64, about 146,237 gas per market; the real LPOracle's `checkConfig` and cold reads cost more per market, so the fork-measured limit is lower than these figures suggest. With `MAX_MARKET_COUNT = 3`, markets 1 to 3 list, market 1 is closed and market 2 cancelled, and a fourth pair still reverts `MarketCountLimit(3)`; cancelling market 3 and relisting its pair reuses identifier 3 and passes. (Source: Q-312, Q-228)

### Offboarding

- `REQ-MREG-015` — `setMarketOffboarding(marketId)` MUST revert with `MarketNotActive` unless the market is `Active`, set `Offboarding`, record `offboardedAt` and emit `MarketOffboarding`. The RiskModule MUST reject every pledge into the market and every borrow against its accounts while it is `Offboarding`; repay, withdrawals and liquidation continue. (Source: Q-118, Q-227)
- `REQ-CONFIG-080` — **Wind-down ends.** After `OFFBOARD_NOTICE_SECONDS` (5 days, the normal delay) from `offboardedAt`, anyone MAY call `stepWindDown(marketId)`, which records and announces the next point of the linear ramp of the market reserve's liquidation threshold, `LT(e) = LT0 − ⌊(LT0 − LTf) × min(e, R) / R⌋` for `LT0 > LTf` (else `LT(e) = LT0`), with `LT0` the collateral factor of the reserve's latest dynamic key when `setMarketOffboarding` ran, `LTf = WIND_DOWN_FINAL_LT_BPS = 5,000`, `R = WIND_DOWN_RAMP_SECONDS = 30 days` and `e` the seconds since the notice ended; units bps and seconds, rounding down the drop, so the threshold never rises and equals `LTf` from the end of the ramp. The registry calls no Aave function (`REQ-CONFIG-003`): the Aave DAO's listing payload approves the steps, and the Aave-side executor applies each announced value as the reserve's dynamic configuration; the RiskModule accepts each step's collateral factor. Once the ramp ends, any remaining indebted account below health factor 1 is liquidated through the normal flow. `stepWindDown` reverts `MarketNotOffboarding(marketId)` unless the market is `Offboarding`, `WindDownStepTooEarly(nextAt)` before `offboardedAt + OFFBOARD_NOTICE_SECONDS` or within `WIND_DOWN_STEP_SECONDS` (1 day) of the previous step, and emits `WindDownStepped(marketId, liquidationThresholdBps)`. When the notice has ended and `Collateralization.getMarketDebtFree(vaultToken)` is true (no account of the market has debt), anyone MAY call `closeMarket(marketId)` (`MarketNotOffboarding(marketId)` unless `Offboarding`, `WindDownStepTooEarly(offboardedAt + OFFBOARD_NOTICE_SECONDS)` before the notice ends, so a market announced for offboarding is never closed in the block that announced it, `MarketHasDebt(marketId)` while an account has debt): status `Closed`, event `MarketClosed(marketId)`, its keys are free for a relisting (`REQ-CONFIG-007`), its LPOracle, swap routes and keeper duty are released, and every debt-free position stays withdrawable by its depositor forever because a debt-free withdrawal reads no price. `windDownState(marketId)` returns `WindDownState{offboardedAt, lastStepAt, startLtBps, currentLtBps}` and never reverts (all zero for a market never offboarded). A bug in an immutable LPVault or in Collateralization is handled by pausing the Hubs, letting users repay and withdraw, deploying a fixed contract and relisting the markets on a new CDP Spoke (market identity includes the Spoke). Why: without a terminal step one dormant borrower keeps a market's feed, routes and keepers alive forever (AMMN-FLOW-22). Example: offboarded at day 0 with `LT0 = 8,000`; the step at day 5 announces 8,000, the step at day 20 announces `8,000 − ⌊3,000 × 15/30⌋ = 6,500`, and from day 35 every step announces 5,000; an account at health factor 1.30 under 8,000 bps falls to `1.30 × 5,000 / 8,000 = 0.8125` and is liquidated; the market closes once its last debt is repaid. Checked by: MATH-AR-70. (Source: Q-355)

### Application pause state

- `REQ-MREG-001` — The MarketRegistry MUST hold one `PauseState` per registered Hub, initially `None`, changed only by these transitions: `freeze(hub)` None → Frozen; `pause(hub, reason)` None → Paused and Frozen → Paused, and Paused → Paused with a different reason (`REQ-MREG-024`); `unfreeze(hub)` Frozen → None; `unpause(hub)` Paused → None. `freezeAll()` and `pauseAll(reason)` apply the same transition to every registered Hub, skipping Hubs already in the target state, and revert `AlreadyFrozen` (`freezeAll`) or `AlreadyPaused` (`pauseAll`) if none changes. Any other call reverts: `freeze` when Frozen (`AlreadyFrozen`) or Paused (`AlreadyPaused`); `pause` with `reason == None` or, while Paused, with the current reason (`InvalidPauseReason`); `unfreeze` when None (`NotFrozen`) or Paused (`CannotUnfreezeWhilePaused`); `unpause` when not Paused (`NotPaused`); an unregistered Hub (`InvalidHub(hub)`). Each change emits `PauseStateChanged(hub, previous, current, msg.sender)`. The AaveAdapter reads the state of the market's home Hub; a cross-Hub borrow also reads the funding Hub's state. Example: the Ethereum Stable LP Hub is Frozen at 10:00 and Paused(General) at 10:20; at 14:00 `unfreeze(stable)` reverts `CannotUnfreezeWhilePaused`, and `unpause(stable)` returns it to None; a Correlated LP Hub on the same chain stays None throughout. (Source: Q-227, Q-252, Q-308)
- `REQ-MREG-002` — Effects: Frozen MUST block new pledges by accounts with zero debt on every debt reserve, and every borrow; it allows every top-up and re-credit of an indebted account, repay, fee collection, debt-free withdrawal and partial removal, withdraw-with-debt with every borrow-side check, zero-credit release, liquidation and pending settlement. Paused(General) MUST block every pledge, top-up and re-credit except rescue top-ups (`REQ-RISK-066`), every borrow, withdraw-with-debt, removal-with-debt and repay with collateral. Paused(MintPathSuspect) blocks the same and also every rescue top-up, so no vault token is minted. Both Paused reasons allow repay, fee collection, debt-free withdrawal and partial removal, `repayAllAndWithdraw`, zero-credit release, both liquidation entry points and pending settlement. The AaveAdapter reads the state and reason for pledge, re-credit, borrow, withdraw-with-debt and repay with collateral, and the Lens reports them. LPVaultSwap never reads them, and the exits and liquidation listed never depend on them, so no pause stops an account from leaving or from being liquidated; liquidation is gated only by the seized market's and repaid reserve's own price states and Aave-side reserve pauses (`Q-247`). Example: under Paused(General) a borrower at HF 1.04 tops up $500 within the restore amount and the top-up passes as a rescue; under Paused(MintPathSuspect) the same top-up reverts `ApplicationPaused`, and the account can still repay. (Source: Q-051, Q-103, Q-227, Q-247, Q-252, Q-254, Q-255)
- `REQ-MREG-003` — The pause state MUST have no on-chain expiry. Avana's only restrictions on new activity are the pause state with its reason, the market and debt breakers of `Q-399` (temporary gates and security freezes, evaluated by the RiskModule, with their latches and breach times stored here), the address freeze, market offboarding and the RiskModule's gates; the ProxyManager, ParameterStore and LPVaultSwap hold no switch, and there is no per-function, per-keeper or per-route Avana pause. None of them reaches repay, debt-free withdrawal or removal, fee collection, zero-credit release, pending settlement or liquidation (`INV-081`). A Paused application keeps liquidating, so the loss that can build while it is Paused is bounded by liquidation running, not by a window. The liquidation stops that remain (Aave-side reserve pauses, which the listing AIP asks the Aave DAO to lift within 48 hours, and a market's or debt reserve's own failed price, which a guardian can end with the standby switch of `REQ-MREG-028`) fall inside the 48-hour incident window of the cap-sizing guidance (`SPEC-CAP-007`), whose values the Aave DAO and its risk service providers set. (Source: Q-192, Q-227, Q-241, Q-242, Q-247, Q-248, Q-249, Q-399)
- `REQ-MREG-004` — `freeze()` MUST require `APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN` or `APPLICATION_ADMIN`; `pause(reason)`, `unfreeze()` and `unpause()` MUST require `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN` or `APPLICATION_ADMIN`; any other caller reverts `Unauthorized(caller)`. A holder of only the freeze role therefore cannot undo a freeze. Guardian calls take effect in the same block; `APPLICATION_ADMIN` calls are timelock operations, at the short delay for `freeze` and `pause` (both in `SHORT_ELIGIBLE`, so the governance Safe can stop new activity in 12 hours when both guardians are unavailable) and at the normal delay for `unfreeze` and `unpause`. `freezeAll()` takes the roles of `freeze` and `pauseAll(reason)` those of `pause`; from `APPLICATION_ADMIN` both wait the normal delay and neither is in `SHORT_ELIGIBLE`, because the guardians pause and freeze every Hub at once, so the admin path waits public review. (Source: Q-227, Q-228, Q-252, Q-262)
- `REQ-MREG-005` — `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` and `APPLICATION_ADMIN` MUST be held only by the timelock: `_grantRole` MUST revert `AdminRoleOnlyTimelock(role, account)` when `role` is either of them and `account != TIMELOCK`, so no role operation can hand upgrade or setter power to an address that acts without a delay; moving to a new timelock is an upgrade to an implementation whose `TIMELOCK` names it. The admin of `APPLICATION_ADMIN` is `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` and the admin of both guardian roles is `APPLICATION_ADMIN`. The Emergency Guardian (3-of-5), the Aave Protocol Guardian and the joint configuration multisig (2-of-3: Avana Labs, Aave Labs, LlamaRisk) each hold `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN` and `APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN`; the registry assumes no fixed number of holders, and each holder has its own freeze allowance (`REQ-MREG-019`). Granting or revoking a role is a timelock operation at the normal delay that only `DAO_CANCELLER_ROLE` can cancel, so a guardian cannot block its own removal. Example: an executed operation `grantRole(APPLICATION_ADMIN, 0xSafe)` reverts `AdminRoleOnlyTimelock`, while `grantRole(APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN, 0xSafe)` succeeds. Checked by: TEST-242, PROP-070. (Source: Q-228, Q-259)

- `REQ-MREG-024` — `pause(reason)` MUST record a `PauseReason` with the Paused state: `General` (new activity stopped; rescue top-ups (`REQ-RISK-066`) stay open, since adding collateral is the cheapest way out of liquidation) or `MintPathSuspect` (the minting code itself is suspect; no pledge, top-up or re-credit runs, rescue included). Transitions: None or Frozen → Paused(reason); Paused(r) → Paused(r′) for `r′ ≠ r` by the same roles, without a change of state; `unpause()` → None with the reason cleared to `None`. `pause(None)` and `pause(r)` while Paused(r) revert `InvalidPauseReason`. Every change of reason emits `PauseReasonChanged(previous, current, msg.sender)`. `pauseReason()` returns `None` exactly when the state is not Paused. Either guardian raises General to MintPathSuspect at once. Example: the Emergency Guardian pauses with `General` at 09:00 after an oracle incident; at 09:40 a mint-path bug is suspected and the Aave Protocol Guardian calls `pause(MintPathSuspect)`; rescue top-ups stop from that block, repay and liquidation continue, and `unpause()` at 18:00 restores None. Checked by: TEST-223, TEST-235. (Source: Q-252)

### Address freeze

The address freeze preserves: exits and liquidation never read it (`INV-081`); it never covers Avana's own contracts (the public flash liquidation helper included), the Aave contracts it depends on, a route or a registered proxy (`INV-085`), and checks no keeper floor: freezing the last active committed operator halts new borrowing on the chain while `liquidate` stays open to every address (`REQ-MREG-021`); unfreezing waits a normal-delay operation only the Aave DAO can cancel.

- `REQ-MREG-019` — `freezeAddress(account)` MUST require `APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN` (immediate) or `APPLICATION_ADMIN` (short delay), else `Unauthorized`; revert `AddressAlreadyFrozen(account)` for a frozen address, `ProtectedAddress(account)` for the protected set (`REQ-MREG-020`), with no keeper-floor check (`REQ-MREG-021`); and revert `FreezeLimitReached(msg.sender)` unless the caller's freezes in the 24 one-hour buckets ending with the current hour are below `MAX_ADDRESS_FREEZES_PER_DAY` (20): with `H = ⌊block.timestamp / 3,600⌋`, `Σ_{h = H−23}^{H} freezeBuckets[msg.sender][h] < 20`, a window of 23 to 24 hours of wall-clock time, so 20 freezes in the last second of hour `H₀` and 20 more at the start of hour `H₀ + 24`, 23 hours and 1 second apart, both pass; after which it increments `freezeBuckets[msg.sender][H]`, sets `frozen[account] = true` and emits `AddressFrozen(account, msg.sender)`. `freezesInWindow(caller)` returns that sum. Units: counts and hour buckets; integer arithmetic, no rounding. Why: a limit per caller rather than a global one stops a compromised guardian from freezing many wallets while leaving the other guardian its full allowance. Example: the Emergency Guardian freezes 20 attacker addresses between 09:05 and 11:50 on day 1 (hours `H₀ … H₀+2`); a 21st freeze at 12:10 reverts `FreezeLimitReached`; at 09:30 on day 2 (`H₀ + 24`) the window covers `H₀+1 … H₀+24`, so the freezes made in hour `H₀` (say 7) no longer count and 7 more are allowed. Checked by: TEST-229, PROP-071. (Source: Q-242, Q-262)
- `REQ-MREG-020` — `freezeAddress` MUST revert `ProtectedAddress(account)` for: `TIMELOCK` and `AAVE_GOVERNANCE_EXECUTOR`; the AaveAdapter, Collateralization, LPVault, LPVaultSwap, ProxyManager, this MarketRegistry, ParameterStore, RiskModule, PayoutEscrow (the payout escrow), Lock and VaultTokenFactory; every vault token and LPOracle of a market that is not cancelled (`marketOfVaultToken`, `marketOfLpOracle`); every debt reserve's price source (its DebtOracle); every registered Hub, every approved Spoke and every known Avana Umbrella (Coverage Spoke); every listed debt asset, the IFlashSource and its `LENDER()` (the Aave V3 Pool behind the source, read live at the freeze, so a new source moves the protection with it; a source whose `LENDER()` cannot be read protects only itself); the public flash liquidation helper (`FLASH_LIQUIDATOR`, [2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md) `REQ-HELPER-010`); every registered integration's LPVault and pricing implementation and every AMM protocol contract recorded for it with `recordIntegrationContracts(integrationId, contracts)` at the normal delay (its position managers and factories, or its singleton), which reverts `InvalidIntegration(integrationId)` for an unregistered integration, an empty list or one of more than 16 addresses, an address that is 0, has no code or is already recorded for it, or a record key that no longer matches the integration (`REQ-MREG-035`), and emits `IntegrationContractsRecorded(integrationId, recordKey, contracts)`; a recorded contract is protected from the call that records it; the LPVault pins these contracts as immutables but exposes them through no common view, so the integration's listing batch records them; every swap route ever recorded (`knownRoute`); and every registered proxy (`AAVE_ADAPTER.walletOf(account) != address(0)`). Why: a freeze limits wallets that start new activity; freezing a contract Avana calls would stop Avana itself, and freezing a proxy would let a guardian block one borrower's account through its contract rather than its wallet. LPVaultSwap checks the freeze on its direct caller only (`REQ-SWAP-105`), so a freeze of the public helper would stop every liquidation funded through it with one call. An operator's helper (`REQ-HELPER-011`) is deployed by that operator and registered as its committed operator, so it is not in the set and a guardian freezes it like any committed operator. Example: `freezeAddress(FLASH_LIQUIDATOR)` reverts `ProtectedAddress(FLASH_LIQUIDATOR)` for the freeze guardian and for the timelock alike, and counts no freeze toward the caller's 20; so does `freezeAddress` of the Aave V3 Pool behind the flash source, and of the Uniswap v3 NonfungiblePositionManager once the integration's batch recorded it. (Source: Q-242, Q-404)
- `REQ-MREG-035` — **Record of an integration's AMM contracts.** `recordIntegrationContracts(integrationId, contracts)` and `finalizeIntegrationContracts(integrationId)` (both `APPLICATION_ADMIN`, normal delay) keep, per integration, the AMM protocol contracts its LPVault pins and a record key `K = keccak256(abi.encode(block.chainid, lpVault, lpVault.codehash, AMM_PROTOCOL(), capabilities().poolKeying))`: the chain, the integration's LPVault address, its version (the LPVault is immutable, so its code hash identifies the build) and its pool identity (keyed by pool address or by pool identifier). The first `recordIntegrationContracts` writes `K`; every later call and `finalizeIntegrationContracts` MUST revert `InvalidIntegration(integrationId)` when the key computed then differs, as does any call for an unregistered integration. `finalizeIntegrationContracts` MUST revert `InvalidIntegration` for an empty record or one already finalized, sets `finalized` and emits `IntegrationContractsFinalized(integrationId, K, count)`. `setRoute` and `restoreRoute` MUST refuse (`InvalidRoute`) every route of a market whose integration's record is not finalized or whose key no longer equals the integration's current key. Each recorded contract joins the protected set at once (`REQ-MREG-020`), finalized or not. An addition to a finalized record applies at once: in the same call the registry reads `dependencies()` of the active route in every slot ever set (`routeSlots`) of each of the integration's markets that is not Cancelled or Closed, at the market's current route epoch, and disables (`active = false`, `RouteDisabled(marketId, token, debtAsset, kind, caller)`) each route that names an added contract or whose dependency list cannot be read; such a route also fails `restoreRoute`. Why: the record is what the route rule (`REQ-CONFIG-040`) and the freeze protection check against, so a route accepted before the record is complete could run through a contract added later; finalizing marks the point from which the record is relied on, and the re-check keeps a newly protected address from leaving an approved route usable through it. A replacement integration has a new identifier and LPVault, so it starts with an empty record and is recorded, checked and finalized afresh. The loop of the re-check is bounded by `MAX_MARKET_COUNT` markets (`REQ-MREG-034`) and the slots governance set. Residual trust: the registry cannot read which contracts an LPVault pins (there is no common view; `ammContracts()` on the integration interface is reconsidered at its next revision), so an incomplete record weakens the route and freeze protections; the deploy stage's release checks compare the record with the integration's position manager, factory or singleton and every other address it pins, on the exact governance payload. Example: Uniswap v3 (integration 1) records its NonfungiblePositionManager and factory and is finalized in its listing batch; its markets' routes then pass; a later addition of a migrator contract disables the backup route of market 4 that swaps through it, in the same call, and governance sets another backup with `setRoute` at the normal delay. (Source: Q-313, Q-371, Q-242)
- `REQ-MREG-021` — `freezeAddress` MUST NOT check any keeper floor and MUST NOT read LPVaultSwap's registry: a guardian can always freeze a compromised committed operator, including the last active one (`Q-400`). Active committed operators are the registered operators that are not frozen (`LPVaultSwap.activeKeeperCount()`, [2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md) `REQ-SWAP-107`); `keeperFloor` is 1 (Avana's own keeper) and bounds only LPVaultSwap's `removeKeeper` ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md) `REQ-SWAP-035`); the MarketRegistry has no keeper-floor error and no keeper-floor check. When a freeze leaves 0 active committed operators on the chain, new borrowing halts on every Hub of the chain until an operator is registered or unfrozen, and `liquidate` stays open to every address that is not frozen, directly or through the public flash liquidation helper, which a freeze of the operator's own address does not stop (`Q-404`). Example: with 2 registered operators of which 1 is frozen, freezing the second succeeds; every `preBorrowCheck` on the chain then reverts `LiquidationHalted`, while any caller, the frozen operators included through the public helper, keeps liquidating. (Source: Q-242, Q-400, Q-404)
- `REQ-MREG-022` — `unfreezeAddress(account)` MUST require `APPLICATION_ADMIN` at the normal delay, revert `AddressNotFrozen(account)` unless frozen, clear the flag and emit `AddressUnfrozen(account)`; the selector is in the timelock's DAO-only cancel set, so the guardian that froze an address cannot keep it frozen by cancelling every unfreeze. `isFrozenAddress(account)` is a view that never reverts. A freeze has no on-chain expiry. (Source: Q-242, Q-263)
- `REQ-MREG-023` — The freeze MUST be read only on new-activity paths, by the calling wallet: the AaveAdapter (a frozen caller's pledge or re-credit is admitted only as a rescue top-up; a frozen caller or receiver cannot borrow; a frozen caller cannot withdraw or remove liquidity with debt or repay with collateral), the ProxyManager (`deployProxy` for a frozen wallet) and LPVaultSwap (`liquidate` from a frozen `msg.sender`, the only liquidation entry point; `addKeeper` of a frozen address), each reverting `AddressIsFrozen(account)`. LPVaultSwap checks `msg.sender` only, so a frozen address that liquidates through the public flash liquidation helper reaches LPVaultSwap as the helper and is not stopped ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md) `REQ-SWAP-105`). It MUST NOT be read by repay (payer or borrower), debt-free withdrawal or removal, `repayAllAndWithdraw`, `LPVault.collectFees`, `Collateralization.releaseZeroCredit`, `settlePending`, `burnSeized`, `PayoutEscrow.claim` (payout escrow), any contract-to-contract call, or the liquidation of a frozen wallet's positions. Why: a freeze stops an exploiting address from opening new exposure within minutes, and liquidation removes exposure whoever calls, without pausing every user, while a guardian, even a compromised one, can neither trap a borrower's collateral nor shield an account from liquidation. Checked by: TEST-229, PROP-066. (Source: Q-242, Q-404)

### Circuit breakers

Breaker state preserves (`Q-399`): only the RiskModule's permissionless `pokeBreakers` (through `recordBreach`) and the guardians' manual trips write it, never a liquidation; it blocks only new activity, through the RiskModule; repay, debt-free exits, fee collection, zero-credit release, pending settlement, eligible liquidation, rescue top-ups and repay with collateral never read it (`INV-081`). Liquidations still need a valid price, Aave eligibility and executable settlement, and an Aave reserve pause can independently block repay or liquidation. The conditions themselves (`ORACLE`, `POOL_SHARE`, `POOL_DEPTH`, `ISSUER`, `CONFIG_DRIFT`, `DEFICIT`) are evaluated live by the RiskModule ([2.7](07-risk-module.md) `SPEC-BRK-002` to `SPEC-BRK-004`), so a condition blocks even when nobody has poked; this contract keeps only what must outlast the condition. Price drops, seizure surges, swap-route failures and a Coverage Spoke below its minimum are monitoring alerts with no on-chain trip.

- `REQ-MREG-025` — The MarketRegistry MUST store, per key (market key `bytes32(marketId)`, debt key `keccak256(abi.encode(spoke, debtReserveId))`), `latched[key]` (the latched security-freeze reasons) and `lastBreachAt[key]` (the latest temporary-gate breach `pokeBreakers` recorded); the Spoke's `CONFIG_DRIFT` is evaluated by the RiskModule and reported through `spokeSettingsDrift(spoke)` (the first drifted market and its `DriftReason`). `marketBreaker(marketId)` and `debtBreaker(spoke, debtReserveId)` return `(latchedReasons, lastBreachAt)` and never revert. `tripMarketBreaker(marketId)` and `tripDebtBreaker(spoke, debtReserveId)` MUST require `APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN` (immediate) or `APPLICATION_ADMIN` (short delay) and a known market (`UnknownMarket`) or approved debt reserve (`UnknownDebtReserve`); they add `MANUAL` and emit `BreakerLatched`. They are the route freeze: route failures alert only, and a guardian or the timelock freezes new borrowing only when the approved routes are confirmed unable to settle valid liquidations at the required size; no on-chain failure counter exists, because a reverted call cannot persist a count and a counter can be gamed. `resetMarketBreaker` and `resetDebtBreaker` are the authorized reopening of [2.15](15-governance-and-timelock.md): they MUST require `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN` (immediate) or `APPLICATION_ADMIN` (normal delay), revert `NothingToReset` unless a reason is latched, and revert `ConditionStillHolds(key, reasons)` while the RiskModule reports any latched non-manual condition still true, read from `RISK_MODULE.liveBreakerReasons(marketId)` or `liveDebtBreakerReasons(spoke, debtReserveId)` (live security conditions only, without latched bits, so `reasons = live ∧ latched ∧ SECURITY`); otherwise they clear `latched[key]` and emit `BreakerReset(scope, key, msg.sender)`. A latch has no expiry and no cooldown. Temporary gates are never latched: they reopen by themselves once `gateRecoverySeconds` has passed since `lastBreachAt` with the condition false. Why: a deficit, an issuer action, a settings drift or a guardian's judgement are security events that need a person to confirm recovery, while a price or depth breach clears with healthy observations. Example: the Aave Protocol Guardian trips debt reserve (Uniswap v3 Stable CDP Spoke, 5) at 14:00 after monitoring confirms both USDC routes cannot fill liquidation size; borrowing USDC stops; the Emergency Guardian resets it at 14:30 once the backup route is restored. Checked by: TEST-228. (Source: Q-241, Q-399)
- `REQ-MREG-026` — `recordBreach(key, reasons)` MUST revert `NotRiskModule` unless `msg.sender == RISK_MODULE`; it ORs the security-freeze reasons of `reasons` (`DEFICIT`, `ISSUER`, `CONFIG_DRIFT`) into `latched[key]` and emits `BreakerLatched` when a new reason is added, and sets `lastBreachAt[key] = block.timestamp` and emits `GateBreached` when `reasons` holds `ORACLE`, `POOL_SHARE` or `POOL_DEPTH`. The RiskModule calls it only from `pokeBreakers(marketId)`, which anyone may call and which evaluates the live conditions first, so a caller cannot latch a condition that does not hold. No liquidation, withdrawal, repay or fee collection calls it; LPVaultSwap has no breaker call, so a liquidation can never fail on breaker bookkeeping and a borrower's own exit never counts toward a breaker. Example: a 10,500 GHO deficit on the GHO reserve of a CDP Spoke makes `pokeBreakers` record `DEFICIT` on that debt key; coverage brings the deficit to 0 at 16:00, borrowing of GHO on that Spoke stays frozen until the pause guardian's `resetDebtBreaker` at 16:20 succeeds. (Source: Q-241, Q-251, Q-399)
- `SPEC-BRK-005` — **Seizure surge (monitoring alert, no on-chain trip).** Monitoring alerts when the units seized in one market within any one hour exceed 20 % of that market's credited units at the window start: `unitsSeized_1h × 10⁴ > 2,000 × creditedAtStart`, vault-token base units, exact integers. No contract counts seizures or latches on them (`Q-399`): a latch written by LPVaultSwap would add bookkeeping to every liquidation, and the response to a surge (a guardian trip, a cap cut) needs a person's judgement. Why the alert: many seizures in one market within an hour are the signature of a price exploit or a pool failure. Example: 10,000,000,000 units credited; seizures of 1,200,000,000 and 800,000,000 units in one hour total exactly 20 %, no alert; one more unit raises the alert, and the guardians decide on `tripMarketBreaker`. Checked by: TEST-228. (Source: Q-241, Q-399)
- `SPEC-BRK-006` — **Swap-route failures (monitoring alert, no on-chain trip).** Monitoring alerts at 3 failed primary swap-route attempts for one market or one debt asset within one hour, counted from keeper reports and simulations off-chain. There is no on-chain failure counter (`Q-399`): a liquidation that fails reverts, so it cannot persist a count, and a counter fed by keeper reports can be gamed to block borrowing. New borrowing is frozen only by a guardian or the timelock (`tripMarketBreaker`, `tripDebtBreaker`, `REQ-MREG-025`) when the approved routes are confirmed unable to settle valid liquidations at the required size. Example: primary-route failures at 10:05, 10:31 and 10:52 raise the alert; the guardians check the backup route, find it fills the stressed size, and leave borrowing open. Checked by: TEST-228. (Source: Q-241, Q-251, Q-399)
- `REQ-MREG-031` — `activeMarketCount(spoke)` MUST return the number of markets on `spoke` whose status is `Active` or `Offboarding` (the markets that can still be liquidated), 0 for an unknown Spoke, and never revert; the Lens and monitoring read it, and no budget is divided by it because there is no SettlementBudget (`Q-397`). Example: three Active markets and one Offboarding market on the Stable LP CDP Spoke give 4. (Source: Q-267, Q-397)

### Conversion routes

- `REQ-CONFIG-059` — Each market MUST hold, per pool token and enabled debt asset, a fixed primary swap route and a pre-approved backup swap route that trade outside the market's own pool: for stablecoin pairs, a deep Uniswap or Curve pool or a peg module with a non-zero cap and real depth (the live USDC GSM holds about $0.03 and is never a primary route); for correlated and volatile tokens, a deep pool of the same chain sized at a fully one-sided unwind; CoW Swap is not used. (Source: Q-062, Q-084, Q-312)
- `REQ-CONFIG-060` — Adding or changing a route MUST go through `setRoute` at the normal delay, MUST re-run `REQ-CONFIG-039` and `REQ-CONFIG-040` (`InvalidRoute`), and MUST add the new address to the slot's set of previously approved routes. Disabling a route uses the short delay. (Source: Q-062, Q-119, Q-228)
- `REQ-CONFIG-061` — `disableRoute` (short delay) MUST set the record's status to `Disabled` until `restoreRoute` or `setRoute` changes it; the MarketRegistry stores no expiry, and no other route suspension exists. (Source: Q-062, Q-227, Q-228)
- `REQ-CONFIG-062` — `restoreRoute` (short delay) MUST revert with `NotPreviouslyApproved` unless `route` is in that slot's set of previously approved routes, and with `InvalidRoute` when the route checks fail; it sets the record to that route with status `Active`. It can only bring back a route that already passed a normal-delay review, which is why it may use the short delay. (Source: Q-151, Q-228)
- `REQ-CONFIG-063` — `getRoute(marketId, token, asset, kind)` MUST return the route and `usable = (status == Active)`; LPVaultSwap uses the primary when usable, else the backup ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)); there is no in-kind fallback (`Q-401`), and the RiskModule treats a market whose token has neither slot usable as liquidation-halted ([2.7](07-risk-module.md) `REQ-ADAPTER-010`). Route depth thresholds are read from the ParameterStore. (Source: Q-084, Q-133, Q-227, Q-401)

### Buy and sell-back routes

- `REQ-MREG-011` — There MUST be no buy route: the liquidation caller funds the repayment in the debt asset itself, from its own balance or through a flash liquidation helper, and `deleverage` flash-borrows the debt asset itself through the chain's IFlashSource (Aave V3 Pool `flashLoan`, single asset, mode 0); LPVaultSwap sells the released pool tokens straight into that asset over the swap routes of `REQ-CONFIG-039`, so no leg buys the debt asset with GHO. `setBuyRoute`, `disableBuyRoute` and `restoreBuyRoute` do not exist, and the `buyRoutes` mapping is always empty. (Source: Q-235, Q-312, Q-404)
- `REQ-MREG-012` — Swap-route containment MUST use `disableRoute` and `restoreRoute` per (market, token, asset, kind) at the short delay; no other route type exists to disable. (Source: Q-235, Q-312)
- `REQ-MREG-013` — There MUST be no sell-back route: leftover value of a liquidation is paid in the debt asset that was repaid (keeper reward, Avana's 20 % surplus fee and the borrower's surplus), and GHO moves only when the debt is GHO, so no conversion back to GHO is needed. `setSellBackRoute` does not exist. (Source: Q-312, Q-358)
- `REQ-MREG-014` — `getRoute(marketId, token, asset, kind)` MUST return the route and `usable = (status == Active)`, and `(address(0), false)` for an unset slot; it never reverts. (Source: Q-235, Q-312)

### In-kind window

- `REQ-MREG-016` — The MarketRegistry MUST hold no in-kind settlement window: `enableInKind`, `disableInKind`, `isInKindEnabled` and `inKindUntil` are removed (`Q-401`). A liquidation settles through the market's primary or backup swap route into the debt asset, or reverts; a recovery below the floor is closed by the caller's shortfall top-up, and a transfer of Avana's fee, the borrower surplus, a route residue or a dust leg that a token refuses goes to the PayoutEscrow, whose only job is that payout escrow; the caller's payout is paid directly to `msg.sender` or the liquidation reverts. Why: in-kind settlement added a governance path, an automatic trigger and a second payout split for a case the shortfall top-up and the backup route already cover. Example: with both USDT routes failing, a liquidation of a USDC/USDT market reverts until a route is restored or the caller tops up the shortfall; it never pays out USDT in kind. (Source: Q-163, Q-228, Q-251, Q-263, Q-401, Q-404)
- `REQ-MREG-029` — There MUST be no automatic in-kind trigger: `setInKindAutoTrigger`, `autoEnableInKind`, the listing flag and `InKindAutoTriggerNotSet` are removed with in-kind settlement, and LPVaultSwap accepts no recovery-shortfall reports (`Q-401`). A persistent route problem is an alert (`SPEC-BRK-006`) answered by a guardian route freeze of new borrowing and a route repair. (Source: Q-251, Q-263, Q-401)

### Views

- `REQ-CONFIG-064` — Every view MUST be `view`, never revert for a listed market, and return the values in force at the current block. A view for an unknown market, token, Spoke, debt reserve or route returns zeros and `false`, which makes every usability and approval check fail closed. `getSpokes()` returns every Spoke ever approved, in approval order. (Source: Q-056, Q-066, Q-119)

|---|---|---|
| `REQ-CONFIG-065` | Any registration check fails | `listCollateralMarket` reverts with the failing check's error; the listing batch fails and must be proposed again with the full normal delay. (Source: Q-114, Q-228) |
