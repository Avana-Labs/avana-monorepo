# 2.14 First-Loss Reserve

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12
>
> **Requirement prefix:** `REQ-FLR-NNN` · **Formula prefix:** `SPEC-FLR-NNN`

## Purpose

The Avana First-Loss Reserve covers the deficits Aave records for approved Avana CDP Spokes (also called Borrow Spokes), each in the asset it is recorded in, on the Hub that funded that debt reserve (`Q-236`, `Q-284`). It is held per covered `(chain, Hub, debt asset)`: each is one **Avana Umbrella (Coverage Spoke)**, Avana's protocol safety module, named after Aave's Umbrella because that design inspired it (`Q-402`): a supply-only Spoke registered on that asset of that Hub with zero draw capacity, funded by Avana Labs in that asset and controlled by the Aave DAO. It covers every CDP Spoke that draws that asset from that Hub, whatever the CDP Spoke's home Hub, and no other Spoke. A Coverage Spoke spends its shares only through `Hub.eliminateDeficit` on its own asset, only for CDP Spokes on a DAO-set allowlist, never more than the recorded deficit and never more than its funded balance. A deficit in one asset is never covered with another asset's reserve. Withdrawals need DAO approval and wait `reserveWithdrawalDelay`. Its size is set in the funding ARFC with the Aave service providers: the coverage rule `fundedBalance × 10⁴ ≥ coverageRatioBps × Σ drawCap` (`DEF-019`) is checked when a debt reserve is enabled and on every recorded `drawCap` raise, and `coverageRatioBps` and `firstLossReserveMinimum` are values of that ARFC, with 20 % only an illustrative guide (`Q-391`). A Coverage Spoke below its minimum raises a monitoring alert; it trips no on-chain breaker (`Q-399`). New borrowing of an asset stops automatically when that asset's recorded deficit on a CDP Spoke exceeds `breakerDeficitMax` (the deficit freeze); repay, exits and liquidation are never affected.

Behind each Avana Umbrella stands the **DAO deficit offset** of the same `(chain, Hub, debt asset)` (`Q-410`): a `DeficitOffsetSpoke`, which is the Coverage Spoke contract with one immutable more, `FIRST_LOSS_LAYER` (the Avana Umbrella in front of it), funded by the Aave DAO treasury to `daoDeficitOffset[hub, asset]` and controlled by the Aave DAO through the same roles. It eliminates a deficit only while the Avana Umbrella's funded balance is 0 or the Aave DAO has deactivated the Avana Umbrella on the Hub (a deactivated Spoke cannot call `eliminateDeficit`). The loss order is therefore fixed on-chain whenever the Avana Umbrella can pay: the Avana Umbrella first, then the DAO deficit offset; Aave Umbrella underwriters may be added later by the Aave DAO. Each layer is its own Hub position, because Aave V4's `Hub.eliminateDeficit` burns only the calling Spoke's own added shares. Aave V4 has no action that writes a deficit down across all suppliers, so a residual beyond both layers stays recorded and is disclosed rather than shared ([Losses beyond the reserve](#losses-beyond-the-reserve)). Unless a rule says otherwise, every rule of this page applies to both instances; the DAO deficit offset adds `REQ-FLR-046` to `REQ-FLR-050` and `SPEC-FLR-006`.

Avana launches on Ethereum and Base, with an Avana Stable LP Hub on each chain (`Q-279`, `Q-282`), whose debt assets are the stable debt assets the Aave DAO approves (for example GHO, USDC, USDT), so at launch there is one Coverage Spoke per approved asset on each chain's Stable LP Hub; with GHO approved on both chains, for example, there is a GHO Coverage Spoke on Ethereum and one on Base (`Q-408`). A Coverage Spoke for any (Hub, debt asset) pair (a stable asset on a Stable LP Hub, or any asset on a Correlated LP or Volatile LP Hub) is deployed and funded to its minimum before `enableDebtReserve` opens the first debt reserve on that pair, so before any `drawCap` on it rises above 0. Each chain's instances cover only deficits on that chain's Hubs; no instance on one chain ever covers a deficit on another. The interface is the same for every instance; the Hub and asset are fixed in each instance's immutables.

Not covered here: in-transaction settlement shortfalls during a liquidation, which revert or are paid by the caller through the shortfall top-up of [2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md) (there is no SettlementBudget, `Q-397`); keeper reimbursement (an off-chain budget); losses beyond both loss layers (see [Losses beyond the reserve](#losses-beyond-the-reserve)). The Hub-side registration of a Coverage Spoke is in [2.1](01-avana-hub-and-spokes.md); the operational flow is [FLOW-009 Deficit Coverage](../03-operations/09-deficit-coverage.md). The invariants these contracts preserve (coverage never exceeds the reserve or the recorded deficit, never crosses assets; deficits are recorded only when an account has no collateral left) are defined in [4.1 Global Invariants](../04-security/01-global-invariants.md).

## Inputs

- The instance's asset from Avana Labs (prefunding and top-ups, including committed fee share once received in that asset) and from any other funder, through `fund`; also from LPVaultSwap, which adds the value it recovers when it settles a pending-settlement balance, in the debt asset of the market's debt reserve, to the Coverage Spoke of that reserve's (funding Hub, asset) (`Q-211`, `Q-284`).
- Coverage requests from the holder of `COVERAGE_ROLE`: the CDP Spoke to cover and a maximum amount.
- DAO instructions through the Aave DAO's AccessManager: allowlist changes, the minimum balance, withdrawal requests, cancellations and executions.
- Hub state read in the same call: the covered Spoke's `deficitRay` on the instance's asset, the Coverage Spoke's added shares and their asset value.

## Outputs

- Hub added shares of the instance's asset credited to the Coverage Spoke on `fund`; burned on `coverDeficit` and `executeWithdrawal`.
- A covered CDP Spoke's `deficitRay` on that asset reduced, which restores that Spoke's draw capacity on the asset.
- The asset sent from the Hub directly to the DAO-named recipient on `executeWithdrawal`.
- Events for funding, coverage, minimum-balance breaches, allowlist and minimum changes, and the withdrawal lifecycle.

## Permissions

Both roles live in the Aave DAO's AccessManager, not in the AvanaTimelock or any Avana contract ([2.15](15-governance-and-timelock.md)); `COVERAGE_ROLE` and `RESERVE_ADMIN_ROLE` are defined here, and [1.4 Permissions and Roles](../01-architecture/04-permissions-and-roles.md) lists them among the Aave-side roles. Only the Aave DAO grants or revokes them, for every instance.

- `RESERVE_ADMIN_ROLE`: held only by the Aave DAO governance executor. Calls `setAllowedBorrowSpoke`, `setFirstLossReserveMinimum`, `requestWithdrawal`, `cancelWithdrawal`, `executeWithdrawal`.
- `COVERAGE_ROLE`: held by Avana's Emergency Guardian multisig and by the Aave Protocol Guardian multisig of the chain, granted in the Aave DAO's listing payload, so either can cover a deficit within the 72-hour window (`REQ-FLR-021`); the DAO stays the role admin and can revoke either. Calls `coverDeficit` only.
- `fund` and the views are public.
- On each Hub, each Coverage Spoke and each DAO deficit offset holds `HUB_DEFICIT_ELIMINATOR_ROLE`, and they are its only holders ([2.1](01-avana-hub-and-spokes.md) `REQ-HUB-021`). The DAO deficit offset's `coverDeficit` maps to the same `COVERAGE_ROLE` and its admin functions to the same `RESERVE_ADMIN_ROLE`, so the guardians who cover from the Avana Umbrella cover from the offset in the same batch.
- Every Coverage Spoke is in the MarketRegistry's address-freeze protected set (`Q-242`): `freezeAddress` reverts for it, so no guardian can stop LPVaultSwap's `fund` call or the coverage path through an Avana switch.
- Avana Labs, every chain's AvanaTimelock and every Avana contract hold no role on any Coverage Spoke. LPVaultSwap cannot call one except `fund`; LPVaultSwap calls `fund` only from `settlePending`, and only on the instance that the settled market's debt reserve names in `DebtReserveDef.coverageSpoke` ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)).
- The allowlist is set by the Aave DAO in the same joint listing as the Avana `enableDebtReserve` operation (`Q-286`): the Aave payload calls `setAllowedBorrowSpoke(spoke, true)` on the Coverage Spoke of the reserve's (funding Hub, asset), and `enableDebtReserve` reverts `CoverageSpokeMismatch(hub, asset, coverageSpoke)` unless the definition names that instance, the one the MarketRegistry records for the pair, and `CoverageNotFunded(hub, asset)` while it holds less than its `firstLossReserveMinimum` ([2.8](08-market-registry.md) `REQ-CONFIG-081`). Avana never writes the allowlist; the Coverage Spoke reads it itself and refuses to cover a deficit of a Spoke that is not on it (`SpokeNotAllowlisted`).

## Formulas

- `SPEC-FLR-001` — **Coverage amount.** `amount = min(maxAmount, ceil(deficitRay / RAY), fundedBalance())`, read in the same call, where `deficitRay` is the covered Spoke's deficit on `ASSET_ID`; the call reverts if `amount == 0`. The Hub then sets `dRay = (amount < ceil(deficitRay / RAY)) ? amount × RAY : deficitRay`, eliminates `dE = ceil(dRay / RAY)` asset units and burns `shares = ceil(dE × (S + 1e6) / (A + 1e6))` of this instance's added shares, where A and S are the asset's total added assets and added shares after accrual. Units: asset base units (18 decimals for GHO and WETH, 6 for USDC and USDT), deficit in RAY (1e27) times base units. Rounding: up, against the reserve, so coverage pays less than one base unit above the exact deficit. Guarantees: `dRay ≤ deficitRay` (coverage never exceeds the recorded deficit), `dE ≤ fundedBalance()` (coverage never exceeds the funded balance; capping at the floored balance means `shares ≤` held shares, otherwise the Hub's `uint120` subtraction reverts the whole call), and only `ASSET_ID` is touched (coverage never crosses assets); other suppliers' share price does not fall. The Hub eliminates exactly `dE = amount`: when `amount < ceil(deficitRay / RAY)` it eliminates `amount × RAY / RAY = amount`, and otherwise the minimum makes `amount = ceil(deficitRay / RAY)` and the Hub eliminates the whole deficit, `ceil(deficitRay / RAY)` units; so the Coverage Spoke adds `amount` to `totalCovered` before the Hub call. Bounds: `deficitRay < 2^200`, shares `< 2^120`. (Source: Q-101, Q-142, Q-190, Q-236)
  - Why: the coverage caller is a delegated role, so the three-way minimum bounds what it can spend: never more than it asked for, never more than Aave actually recorded as lost (the reserve does not pay for losses that did not happen), and never more than the reserve holds. Covering a short balance partially instead of reverting means a large deficit still gets reduced, and rounding the burned shares up means the remaining Hub suppliers never absorb the rounding. Fixing the asset per instance means a deficit owed to one asset's suppliers is paid in that asset, burning exactly the lost shares.
  - Example (illustrative balances): the Ethereum GHO instance; a CDP Spoke records `deficitRay = 475,250,000,000,000,000,000 × RAY + 4 × 10^26` (475.25 GHO and 0.4 wei). With `maxAmount = 1,000 GHO`, `fundedBalance() = 400,000 GHO` (illustrative), `A = 2,100,000 GHO` and `S = 2,000,000` shares, `amount = ceil(deficitRay / RAY) = 475,250,000,000,000,000,001` wei, so `dRay = deficitRay`, the whole deficit is eliminated and the Coverage Spoke burns `452,619,047,619,047,619,060` shares (about 452.62). With `maxAmount = 300 GHO` instead, `dRay = 300 × 10^18 × RAY`, 285,714,285,714,285,714,293 shares are burned and 175.25 GHO plus 0.4 wei stays recorded. On a WETH instance with `A = 12,000` WETH and `S = 11,800` shares, a 3.2 WETH deficit burns `3,146,666,666,666,666,672` shares; the GHO instance is not touched, and neither is any instance on another chain.
  - Checked by: MATH-ST-46, MATH-ST-47, MATH-ST-48, MATH-ST-49, MATH-ST-114, QNT-30, HYP-33.
- `SPEC-FLR-002` — **Funded balance.** `fundedBalance() = floor(addedShares_this × (A + 1e6) / (S + 1e6))` as returned by `HUB.getSpokeAddedAssets(ASSET_ID, address(this))`, with A including accrued interest and any recorded deficit on that asset. Units: asset base units. Rounding: down. The reporting totals are not a balance: `fundedBalance() + totalCovered + totalWithdrawn ≤ totalFunded + yield earned`, where every `fund` rounds shares down and every coverage and withdrawal rounds shares up, against the reserve; with no yield, `fundedBalance` equals `totalFunded − totalCovered − totalWithdrawn` up to that per-call rounding. Yield lets the reserve cover more than was ever funded, so `totalFunded − totalCovered − totalWithdrawn` is a signed quantity and any reader computes it as one. Example: 100,000 GHO funded, about 2,300 GHO of yield in a year, then 102,267 GHO covered: `totalCovered > totalFunded`. (Source: Q-101, Q-142)
  - Why: the reserve's claim is its Hub shares, not idle tokens, so its balance is what those shares redeem for at the current share price. Rounding down means the reserve never reports, or spends, more than its shares can pay.
  - Example (illustrative balances): holding `100,000 × 10^18` shares with `A = 2,100,000 GHO` and `S = 2,000,000` shares, `fundedBalance() = 104,999,999,999,999,999,997,500` wei, 2,500 wei below the exact 105,000 GHO because of the virtual offset and the floor.
  - Checked by: MATH-ST-47, QNT-30, QNT-31.
- `SPEC-FLR-003` — **Withdrawal.** A request `id` is executable only when `block.timestamp ≥ executableAt = requestTime + RESERVE_WITHDRAWAL_DELAY`; it withdraws `withdrawn = min(request.amount, fundedBalance())` and the Hub burns `ceil(withdrawn × (S + 1e6) / (A + 1e6))` shares, reverting with `InsufficientLiquidity` if the Hub's liquidity of the asset is below `withdrawn` and with the Hub's `InvalidAmount` if `withdrawn` is 0 (nothing funded); either revert leaves the request Pending. Units: asset base units, seconds. Rounding: shares up (against the reserve). Bounds: `executableAt` fits `uint64`. (Source: Q-101, Q-167)
  - Why: the 14-day delay lets borrowers, monitoring and Avana Labs see a DAO withdrawal before the loss buffer shrinks, and gives time to top up. Capping at the funded balance at execution means a request made before a coverage call still executes for what remains instead of reverting.
  - Example: a request for 50,000 GHO made at `1,780,000,000` is executable from `1,781,209,600` (`reserveWithdrawalDelay = 1,209,600`). If coverage left `fundedBalance() = 40,000 GHO`, execution withdraws 40,000 GHO and burns `38,095,238,095,238,095,239,003` wei of shares at `A = 2,100,000`, `S = 2,000,000`.
  - Checked by: QNT-30 (balance accounting); the delay is covered by Foundry tests in [5.1](../05-verification/01-test-requirements.md); no spec-stage model covers the timer.
- `SPEC-FLR-004` — **Reserve sizing against caps, per asset.** For each debt asset `a` and each underlying token `t`, with every account counted once across every market and every CDP Spoke that draws `a` from the Hub (whatever its home Hub), on that chain: `Σ_{markets m exposed to t} debtCap_{m,a} × lossFactor_{m,a} ≤ firstLossReserveMinimum[a] + daoDeficitOffset[a] ≤ fundedBalance_a`, where `fundedBalance_a` is the funded balance of both loss layers together, the Avana Umbrella's and the DAO deficit offset's (`Q-410`), all in units of `a`, where `lossFactor_{m,a} = max(0, (1 + g_a) − pauseLossStartHealthFactor × (1 − dLP_m) / (CF_m × LB_m × (1 + u_a)))`, `g_a` is the borrow interest of `a` over the 48-hour incident window of `Q-192`, `dLP_m` the market's worst historic drop in value per vault-token unit over that window plus margin, computed with the market's AMM integration's composition at the stressed oracle prices (so a concentrated range past its edge counts as fully one-sided), `u_a` the worst historic rise of `a`'s price against USD over that window plus margin (0 for GHO, which Aave prices at a fixed $1), `CF_m` from `SPEC-HUB-001` and `LB_m` the maximum liquidation bonus; equivalently each cap ≤ reserve ÷ loss factor. Each market's debt cap is also at most the asset's credit line, the flash liquidity of `a` in the chain's `IFlashSource` and what LPVaultSwap can unwind and swap into `a` over the market's swap routes under stress. The incident window covers the liquidation stops that remain: an Aave Guardian or DAO pause of a vault-token or debt reserve, which the listing AIP asks the Aave DAO to lift within 48 hours, and LPOracle price-state pauses; Avana's own pause state never stops liquidation (`Q-227`). Units: asset base units, ratios. Rounding: the risk service providers round caps down. This incident term is one of two requirements that both bind on `firstLossReserveMinimum[a]`; the other is the tail term of `SPEC-FLR-005`. Both are Aave DAO and service-provider guidance with no on-chain enforcement (`Q-249`). Dividing by `LB_m` already counts the bonus paid from the seized collateral. (Source: Q-079, Q-186, Q-192, Q-197, Q-202, Q-227, Q-234, Q-236, Q-249)
  - Why: while liquidation is stopped for up to the incident window, an account whose health factor has already fallen to `pauseLossStartHealthFactor` (keeper lag) keeps losing collateral value, accruing interest and, for a non-GHO debt, owing more as the debt asset's price rises; what it owes beyond its seizable collateral becomes a deficit in the debt asset. Measuring both sides in units of `a` (collateral value divided by `1 + u_a`) gives the loss in the asset the deficit is recorded in, and summing over every market that holds the shocked token, with each account counted once, sizes each asset's reserve against one token failing everywhere at once.
  - Example: USDT falls 90 % within the 48-hour window. A Uniswap v3 USDT/USDC market whose exact range is the full tick range values like a constant-product position, so its value per unit falls by `1 − √0.10 ≈ 68.4 %`; a narrow stable range such as `[−10, 10]` turns fully into USDT past its lower edge and loses close to 90 % × its USDT share at the edge, which the risk service providers use as its `dLP`. For the full-range market, with a 2 % margin `dLP = 0.704`; with `CF = 0.312`, `LB = 1.05` and `pauseLossStartHealthFactor = 0.95`, `0.95 × 0.296 / 0.3276 = 0.858364`. GHO debt: `g = 6 % × 2/365 = 0.000329`, `u = 0`, `lossFactor = 1.000329 − 0.858364 = 0.141965`; two USDT markets with GHO debt caps of 400,000 and 300,000 GHO need `700,000 × 0.141965 ≈ 99,376 GHO`, within an illustrative 400,000 GHO minimum. WETH debt on the same markets with ETH up 20 % in the window: `g = 3 % × 2/365 = 0.000164`, `lossFactor = 1.000164 − 0.858364 / 1.2 = 0.284861`; a WETH debt cap of 40 WETH needs a WETH reserve of at least `40 × 0.284861 ≈ 11.39 WETH`. For a mild 8 % LP drop with no price rise the loss factor is 0.
  - Checked by: MATH-AR-29, MATH-AR-30, MATH-AR-31, SIM-02B, SIM-08A.
- `SPEC-FLR-005` — **Tail coverage per asset.** For each debt asset `a`: `tail_a = max over non-GHO pool tokens t of min(⌈(tokenUsdCap[t] + Σ_{m∋t} topUpSliceUsd[m]) × CF_t / 10⁴⌉ × 10^{d_a} / P_a, Σ_spoke drawCap_{spoke,a} × 10^{d_a})`, with the sum over every CDP Spoke that draws `a` from the instance's Hub and `tokenUsdCap[t]` the Hub-keyed token cap of the chain's ledger (`Q-279`), the tail term of `SPEC-CAP-007`, and the reserve requirement is `max(incident need of SPEC-FLR-004, tail_a) ≤ firstLossReserveMinimum[a] ≤ fundedBalance_a`. The configuration's distance from it is `tailCoverageBps_a = ⌊fundedBalance_a × 10⁴ / tail_a⌋` (`SPEC-CAP-008`), reported by `Lens.getTailCoverage(a)` from caps and by monitoring from current debt. `CF_t` is the largest collateral factor (bps) of a market holding `t`, the caps are USD with 8 decimals, `P_a` is `a`'s Aave price (8 decimals; 10⁸ for GHO), `d_a` its decimals, and the Hub's `drawCap` is in whole units of `a`, so `tail_a` is in base units of `a`. Rounding: the tail up and the ratio down, both against coverage. Overflow: when any `drawCap` on `a` is Aave's `MAX_ALLOWED_SPOKE_CAP`, the draw-capacity term is unbounded and `tail_a` is the capped-exposure term alone. Why: the incident term alone allowed any debt cap for stable pools, because their worst historic two-day drops are far below the drop at which it binds; the real tail is one pool token failing completely, which takes every position in every market holding it close to zero within hours, and an account that is not yet liquidatable owes at most its collateral value × `CF_t` against that token: the opening health factor bounds debt only at borrowing, and interest and the other leg's price can raise it to health factor 1 before liquidation. Capping each token's exposure, slices included, at what the asset's reserve can absorb covers that tail, and the `min` with the asset's total draw capacity stops the term from asking for more than can be lent. The values stay Aave DAO and service-provider values (`Q-197`); Avana enforces none of them on-chain, and the ratio shows how far the configuration is from the requirement. Example (illustrative values; the minimum is set in the funding ARFC, `Q-391`): with `CF_t = 3,120` bps, so the factor is 0.312, a 400,000 GHO reserve covers a token cap of $1,282,051 less the token's slices. A USDe token cap of $1,600,000 with $80,000 of slices across its markets and 2,000,000 GHO of draw capacity gives `tail_GHO = min(1,680,000 × 0.312, 2,000,000) = 524,160` GHO; with `fundedBalance = 400,000` GHO, `tailCoverageBps = ⌊400,000 × 10⁴ / 524,160⌋ = 7,631`, so the DAO lowers the USDe cap to at most $1,202,051 or raises the asset's minimum to 524,160 GHO and funds it. Checked by: MATH-ST-78, MATH-ST-91, TEST-216, TEST-247. (Source: Q-249, Q-236, Q-197)

## Restrictions

### Form and control

- `REQ-FLR-001` — The First-Loss Reserve of an asset MUST be that asset held as the added shares of that asset's Coverage Spoke on the Hub that funds the debt reserve. The on-chain loss layers of a (Hub, asset) are that Coverage Spoke (the Avana Umbrella, first) and the DAO deficit offset of the same pair (second, `REQ-FLR-046`); committed revenue and Aave's own Umbrella are off-chain or future, and Avana counts no coverage from them. (Source: Q-070, Q-101, Q-119, Q-142, Q-236, Q-410)
- `REQ-FLR-002` — Each Coverage Spoke MUST be funded by Avana Labs and controlled only by the Aave DAO through the roles in [Permissions](#permissions); Avana Labs, the deployer and every Avana contract MUST hold no role on it. Trust assumption: the Aave DAO's AccessManager is the instance's whole authority, and the DAO can use every power that gives it at once, with no delay inside the Coverage Spoke: map `coverDeficit` to `PUBLIC_ROLE` (coverage by anyone, still bounded by the allowlist and `SPEC-FLR-001`), grant `RESERVE_ADMIN_ROLE` to any address, cancel any withdrawal, and replace the authority with `updateAuthority`. The only limits inside the contract are the immutable 14-day withdrawal delay, the allowlist check, the coverage bound, the own-asset rule and the deficit guard of `REQ-FLR-026`. Avana Labs, the funder, has no claim, veto or cancel right over its funding; it relies on the DAO as it relies on the DAO for the Hub itself. (Source: Q-101, Q-142)
- `REQ-FLR-003` — A Coverage Spoke MUST NOT be upgradeable and MUST have no owner other than its AccessManager authority; a replacement is a new deployment funded through the withdrawal path of the old one. (Source: Q-101, Q-142)
- `REQ-FLR-004` — A Coverage Spoke MUST call only `Hub.add`, `Hub.remove` (from `executeWithdrawal`) and `Hub.eliminateDeficit` (from `coverDeficit`) as state-changing Hub calls, each only with its own `ASSET_ID`; it MUST NOT call `draw`, `restore`, `reportDeficit`, `transferShares`, `payFeeShares`, `refreshPremium`, `sweep` or `reclaim`, and it has no user positions. (Source: Q-070, Q-101, Q-142, Q-236)
- `REQ-FLR-005` — The Coverage Spoke MUST hold no tokens between transactions, MUST check token amounts by the Hub's returned values and before/after deltas, MUST NOT count tokens sent to it directly, and has no rescue or sweep function. (Source: Q-101, Q-124)
- `REQ-FLR-006` — Every state-changing function MUST be non-reentrant and follow checks-effects-interactions. (Source: Q-148)
- `REQ-FLR-007` — On its Hub, each Coverage Spoke MUST be registered only on its own asset with `drawCap = 0`, `addCap = MAX_ALLOWED_SPOKE_CAP` (no add cap: the instance receives only funding, Avana's and LPVaultSwap's settlement proceeds, and a finite cap could be filled by its own yield alone and then stop every `fund`, the pending-settlement top-up included) and `active = true`, and the Coverage Spokes and the DAO deficit offsets (each registered the same way) MUST be the only holders of `HUB_DEFICIT_ELIMINATOR_ROLE` ([2.1](01-avana-hub-and-spokes.md) `REQ-HUB-021`); the deployment check asserts these values, and the chain's MarketRegistry record of each debt reserve names the instance registered on that reserve's `(fundingHub, hubAssetId)`. (Source: Q-070, Q-101, Q-142, Q-236)
- `REQ-FLR-008` — The constructor `constructor(hub, asset, assetId, authority, reserveWithdrawalDelay)` MUST revert `ZeroAddress` if `hub`, `asset` or `authority` is the zero address, `InvalidHubAsset(assetId)` unless `HUB.getAssetUnderlyingAndDecimals(ASSET_ID)` returns `ASSET` with its token decimals (`ASSET.decimals()`), and `InvalidWithdrawalDelay(delay)` unless `RESERVE_WITHDRAWAL_DELAY` equals `reserveWithdrawalDelay` (1,209,600 s); the delay is a constructor input so the deployment manifest records it, and the check keeps every instance on the one value of 1.5. (Source: Q-101, Q-167, Q-236)
- `REQ-FLR-009` — Aave Labs' confirmation of the coverage caller (the Coverage Spoke as `eliminateDeficit` caller, the `COVERAGE_ROLE` holder) and their permissions is a launch condition: no CDP Spoke `drawCap` on any chain is raised above 0 until it is recorded in that chain's deployment manifest. (Source: Q-101, Q-142)

### Funding and minimum balance

- `REQ-FLR-010` — `fund(amount)` MUST revert `ZeroAmount` if `amount == 0`, MUST move exactly `amount` of `ASSET` from `msg.sender` to `HUB` with `safeTransferFrom`, MUST revert `TransferAmountMismatch(amount, received)` unless the Hub's balance of `ASSET` rose by exactly `amount` (the before/after delta of `REQ-FLR-005`, which refuses a fee-on-transfer or rebasing asset) and call `HUB.add(ASSET_ID, amount)` in the same call, MUST add `amount` to `totalFunded`, and MUST emit `ReserveFunded`; the Hub reverts if the Coverage Spoke is inactive or halted or `addCap` would be exceeded. Funds added give the funder no claim. (Source: Q-101, Q-142)
- `REQ-FLR-011` — Avana Labs MUST fund each Coverage Spoke with at least `firstLossReserveMinimum[asset]` of its asset before the `drawCap` of any Avana CDP Spoke on that (Hub, asset) is raised above 0, and MUST top it back up to the minimum after any coverage; committed fee share counts toward the minimum only once received in that asset through `fund`. (Source: Q-101, Q-236)
- `REQ-FLR-012` — A Coverage Spoke's asset MUST be Hub liquidity like any other added liquidity of that asset, so Avana borrowers can draw it within CDP Spoke `drawCap`s; the reserve's claim is its shares, not idle tokens. (Source: Q-142)
- `REQ-FLR-013` — `firstLossReserveMinimum` MUST be stored in each Coverage Spoke, set to the value of the funding ARFC agreed with the Aave service providers, and changed only by the Aave DAO; it MUST NOT block funding, coverage or withdrawal, nor any repay, exit, top-up or liquidation. Its only on-chain effects beyond the alert are the readiness checks of `enableDebtReserve` and of each recorded `drawCap` raise (`REQ-FLR-039`, `DEF-019`); it trips no breaker (`Q-399`). (Source: Q-101, Q-102, Q-241, Q-391, Q-399)
- `REQ-FLR-014` — After `coverDeficit` or `executeWithdrawal`, if `fundedBalance() < firstLossReserveMinimum`, the call MUST emit `ReserveBelowMinimum`; monitoring MUST alert on that event and on `isBelowMinimum()` of every instance. `isBelowMinimum()` MUST be a view that never reverts on a listed, active instance. A reserve below its minimum is a monitoring alert only: no breaker reads it, and no on-chain borrowing stop follows from it (`Q-399`). The alert and the decision stay: the Emergency Guardian (3-of-5) or the Aave Protocol Guardian decides whether to latch a manual debt breaker on the affected debt reserves (`tripDebtBreaker`, immediate) or freeze the instance's Hub (`MarketRegistry.freeze(hub)`), and the Aave DAO whether to freeze the affected debt reserve; Avana Labs tops the reserve back up under `REQ-FLR-011`. New borrowing stops automatically only through the deficit freeze of `REQ-FLR-021`. Why: the breaker table of `Q-399` keeps one automatic stop for losses, the recorded deficit, which is the realized loss; the coverage ratio is enforced where caps change (`DEF-019`), and a balance that fell because it covered a deficit is already behind a deficit freeze once that deficit passed `breakerDeficitMax`. Example (illustrative balances): a 5,200 GHO deficit is covered and leaves the GHO instance at 394,800 GHO against an illustrative 400,000 GHO minimum; `ReserveBelowMinimum(394,800·10¹⁸, 400,000·10¹⁸)` is emitted and monitoring pages the guardians; GHO borrows continue unless a guardian trips the debt breaker, and Avana Labs funds 5,200 GHO plus a few wei (the Hub rounds the minted shares and their value down) to clear the alert. Checked by: TEST-228. (Source: Q-102, Q-118, Q-227, Q-241, Q-399)
- `REQ-FLR-015` — The reserve's shares MUST accrue whatever supply yield the Hub's configuration of the asset pays; yield stays in the reserve, counts toward the funded balance and the minimum, and has no separate claim. (Source: Q-142)
- `REQ-FLR-038` — Every Coverage Spoke's `fund` MUST accept the pending-settlement top-up from LPVaultSwap exactly as any other funding: LPVaultSwap approves exactly `R` of the debt asset and calls `fund(R)` on the instance named by the market's settlement debt reserve `settlementReserveId` (`DebtReserveDef(settlementReserveId).coverageSpoke`, the instance of that reserve's (funding Hub, asset)); `settlementReserveId` is recorded at `listCollateralMarket` as the first debt reserve enabled on the market's CDP Spoke (for example the GHO reserve) and changes only at the normal delay, so a market with several debt reserves always names one instance, and `settlePending` converts the pending tokens into that reserve's asset ([2.8](08-market-registry.md), [2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md); `Q-338`, `Q-358`); the asset moves straight to `HUB`, the added shares belong to the Coverage Spoke, `ReserveFunded` names LPVaultSwap as funder, and the amount counts toward `totalFunded`, `fundedBalance()` and `firstLossReserveMinimum`. It gives LPVaultSwap no role, claim or withdrawal right, and the Coverage Spoke has no LPVaultSwap-specific code path. When the Coverage Spoke is halted or inactive, `fund` reverts with the Hub's `SpokeHalted` or `SpokeNotActive` and the whole `settlePending` reverts with it; the pending balance keeps backing its vault tokens until a later call succeeds ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)). A `fund` of less than one share's value (`R` with `floor(R × (S + 1e6) / (A + 1e6)) = 0`, possible once yield lifts the share price above 1) reverts with the Hub's `InvalidShares`, so `settlePending` does not call `fund` for such an `R`: it carries it to the next settlement ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)). With `addCap = MAX_ALLOWED_SPOKE_CAP` (`REQ-FLR-007`) the Hub's `AddCapExceeded` never stops it. Why: a pending-settlement balance exists only after an LPVaultSwap implementation seized collateral without settling it, and the reserve is the layer that absorbs the losses such an incident causes, so the value recovered from it belongs in the reserve rather than with Avana or the caller (`Q-211`); the recovered value is converted into the settlement reserve's asset and goes to the reserve of the Hub that lent it, the one that absorbs that reserve's deficits (`Q-284`). A market whose settlement reserve is USDC from the Stable LP Hub sends USDC to that Hub's USDC instance, never to the GHO instance. Example: for a market whose debt reserve is GHO on the Ethereum Stable LP Hub, with `A = 2,100,000` GHO and `S = 2,000,000` shares on the GHO asset and the reserve holding `100,000 × 10^18` shares (`fundedBalance() = 104,999,999,999,999,999,997,500` wei), `fund(83.8 × 10^18)` from LPVaultSwap mints `79,809,523,809,523,809,525` shares and raises `fundedBalance()` to `105,083,799,999,999,999,997,499` wei, 1 wei short of the 83.8 GHO added because the Hub rounds shares down. (Source: Q-211, Q-101, Q-142, Q-236, Q-284)

### Coverage

- `REQ-FLR-016` — `coverDeficit` MUST be callable only by `COVERAGE_ROLE` and MUST revert unless `allowedBorrowSpoke[borrowSpoke]` is true, because `Hub.eliminateDeficit` accepts any covered Spoke. (Source: Q-101, Q-142, Q-190)
- `REQ-FLR-017` — `setAllowedBorrowSpoke(spoke, true)` MUST revert `ZeroAddress` if `spoke == address(0)` and `BorrowSpokeNotListed(spoke)` if `HUB.isSpokeListed(ASSET_ID, spoke)` is false; it emits `BorrowSpokeAllowlistUpdated` on every successful call. `isSpokeListed` is the only on-chain check: any Spoke the Hub lists on the asset can be allowlisted, the Coverage Spoke itself included, so the set is the DAO's decision; the Aave DAO MUST allowlist exactly the Avana CDP Spokes that have a debt reserve on this asset of this Hub, whatever their home Hub, in the joint listing of each `enableDebtReserve` (`Q-286`); removal takes effect immediately. (Source: Q-101, Q-142, Q-190, Q-236, Q-284, Q-286)
- `REQ-FLR-018` — `coverDeficit` MUST compute `amount` per `SPEC-FLR-001`, revert if the covered Spoke's `deficitRay` on `ASSET_ID` is 0 or `amount` is 0, add `amount` to `totalCovered` (the amount the Hub then returns as eliminated, `SPEC-FLR-001`), call `HUB.eliminateDeficit(ASSET_ID, amount, borrowSpoke)`, and emit `DeficitCovered` with the deficit before and after. (Source: Q-101, Q-142, Q-190, Q-236)
- `REQ-FLR-019` — Coverage MUST NOT exceed the covered Spoke's recorded deficit on the instance's asset or the instance's funded balance (`SPEC-FLR-001`). (Source: Q-101, Q-142, Q-236)
- `REQ-FLR-020` — The reserve MUST cover only deficits Aave has recorded on the Hub; it MUST NOT fund in-transaction settlement shortfalls, keeper losses or flash-loan repayment, which revert or are paid by the liquidation's caller through the shortfall top-up; there is no SettlementBudget (`Q-397`). (Source: Q-071, Q-117, Q-127, Q-397)
- `REQ-FLR-021` — The `COVERAGE_ROLE` holder MUST cover every recorded deficit on an allowlisted CDP Spoke within `deficitCoverageWindow` (72 hours) of its `ReportDeficit` event, from the Coverage Spoke of the deficit's asset, while that instance's funded balance allows; Avana accepts Aave V4's deficit behavior as it is and relies on this fixed operations window instead; monitoring MUST alert on any non-zero `deficitRay` on any asset; until covered, the deficit counts against that Spoke's `drawCap` on that asset, and once the Hub deficit of the asset on that Spoke exceeds `breakerDeficitMax` (10,000 GHO for the GHO reserve of the affected CDP Spoke; for other assets a per-reserve materiality whose value the listing ARFC sets) the RiskModule's deficit freeze blocks new borrowing of that debt reserve (`SPEC-BRK-004`) and `pokeBreakers` latches it; coverage alone does not reopen it, and only an authorized reset (`resetDebtBreaker` by the pause guardian, or the timelock at the normal delay, [2.15](15-governance-and-timelock.md)) does, once the deficit is at or below the limit. (Source: Q-101, Q-119, Q-236, Q-241, Q-399)
- `REQ-FLR-022` — Coverage MUST keep working while the Coverage Spoke or the covered CDP Spoke is halted on the Hub. A Hub halt of a Spoke on an asset stops `add`, `remove`, `draw`, `restore` and share transfers, so it stops supply, withdrawal, borrowing, repay and liquidation of that Spoke on that asset; `reportDeficit`, `eliminateDeficit`, `payFeeShares` and `refreshPremium` check only that the Spoke is active and continue (`Q-290`). A halted CDP Spoke's existing deficit can therefore still be covered by a Coverage Spoke, halted or not, and the DAO position updater can still refresh premiums. Coverage stops only if the DAO deactivates the Coverage Spoke, an Aave-side state that Avana accepts and that no Avana state can cause. Example: the DAO halts the Ethereum CDP Spoke "Uniswap v3 Stable" on GHO after an exploit, with 3,000 GHO of deficit recorded; liquidation and repay of its GHO debt stop, but `coverDeficit(spoke, 3,000 GHO)` on the Ethereum GHO Coverage Spoke succeeds and clears the deficit. (Source: Q-051, Q-140, Q-290)

### Withdrawals

- `REQ-FLR-023` — `requestWithdrawal(amount, to)` MUST be callable only by `RESERVE_ADMIN_ROLE`, MUST revert `ZeroAmount` if `amount == 0` and `InvalidRecipient(to)` if `to` is the zero address, the Coverage Spoke itself (which holds no tokens and has no sweep, `REQ-FLR-005`) or `HUB` (which `Hub.remove` refuses), MUST record `executableAt = block.timestamp + RESERVE_WITHDRAWAL_DELAY` with status Pending under `id = nextWithdrawalId++`, and MUST emit `WithdrawalRequested`. (Source: Q-101, Q-167)
- `REQ-FLR-024` — `cancelWithdrawal(id)` MUST be callable only by `RESERVE_ADMIN_ROLE`, MUST revert `WithdrawalNotPending(id)` unless the request is Pending (a Pending request can be cancelled before or after `executableAt`), and MUST set it to Cancelled and emit `WithdrawalCancelled`. (Source: Q-101)
- `REQ-FLR-025` — `executeWithdrawal(id)` MUST be callable only by `RESERVE_ADMIN_ROLE`, MUST revert `WithdrawalNotPending(id)` unless the request is Pending, `WithdrawalNotReady(id, executableAt)` unless `block.timestamp ≥ executableAt`, and `DeficitOutstanding(spoke)` while any allowlisted Spoke has a recorded deficit on the asset (`REQ-FLR-026`), MUST set it to Executed before calling `HUB.remove(ASSET_ID, withdrawn, to)` with `withdrawn` per `SPEC-FLR-003`, MUST add `withdrawn` to `totalWithdrawn`, and MUST emit `WithdrawalExecuted`; the asset goes from the Hub directly to `to`. (Source: Q-101, Q-167)
- `REQ-FLR-026` — Unused reserve capital of an asset MUST return to Avana Labs only through the withdrawal path, after that (Hub, asset) credit line closes and every allowlisted CDP Spoke has zero debt and zero `deficitRay` in that asset; before that, the Aave DAO approves a withdrawal only when the balance left still meets that instance's `firstLossReserveMinimum`. Coverage comes before withdrawal on-chain: `executeWithdrawal` walks the Hub's Spoke list of the asset (`getSpokeCount`, `getSpokeAddress`, views only, bounded by the Spokes the DAO lists) and reverts `DeficitOutstanding(spoke)` for the first allowlisted Spoke with a non-zero `getSpokeDeficitRay`; the request stays Pending and executes once that deficit is covered or the DAO removes the Spoke from the allowlist. Why: the withdrawal price counts a recorded deficit as an asset (`SPEC-FLR-002`), so without the guard a withdrawal requested before a deficit could take the whole reserve inside the 72-hour coverage window and leave `coverDeficit` with nothing to cover. A deficit on a Spoke that is not allowlisted does not block it, because this reserve cannot cover that deficit. Example: the DAO requests a 10,000 GHO withdrawal; on day 13 a 50,000 GHO deficit is recorded on the allowlisted CDP Spoke; on day 14 `executeWithdrawal` reverts `DeficitOutstanding(cdpSpoke)`; the coverage caller covers the 50,000 GHO, and the withdrawal then executes. (Source: Q-101, Q-236)

### Losses beyond the reserve

- `REQ-FLR-027` — A deficit larger than both loss layers of its (Hub, asset) MUST stay recorded as the CDP Spoke's `deficitRay` on that asset of the Hub, keep counting against its `drawCap` and stay inside the asset's added assets; no Avana contract covers it and no other asset's or Hub's instance may cover it. Aave V4 has no action that writes it down across suppliers: `Hub.eliminateDeficit` is `restricted` and burns only the calling Spoke's own added shares, so the supply share price does not fall and the residual is not shared pro rata. Withdrawals stay open and the Hub pays each in full while liquidity lasts, first-come; once every borrower has repaid, liquidity falls short of supplier claims by the residual, and the last suppliers, the DAO's bootstrap supply and public Lend Spoke lenders alike (`Q-407`), hold claims with no liquidity behind them until either layer is topped up and covers the residual through `coverDeficit`. Playbook P-18 ([3.6](../03-operations/06-emergency-operations.md)) then sets the drawing Spokes' `drawCap` to 0, freezes new supply into the Lend Spoke reserve, raises the `UncoveredDeficit` alarm and has the Lens publish the uncovered figure (`uncoveredDeficit(hub, asset)`, 3.9 `DEF-014`). The protection is the layers and the caps sized against them: `SPEC-FLR-004` and `SPEC-FLR-005` size caps to `firstLossReserveMinimum + daoDeficitOffset`, and the on-chain bound `Σ drawCap × 10^dec × coverageRatioBps ≤ fundedBalance × 10⁴` on the Avana Umbrella, checked when a debt reserve is enabled and on every recorded `drawCap` raise, keeps a residual to losses above `coverageRatioBps` of the summed caps plus the DAO offset; `coverageRatioBps` is set in the funding ARFC with the Aave service providers (`Q-391`). The layers cover recorded deficits only, and they are the Hubs' only `eliminateDeficit` callers, so no loss is counted twice. Example: lenders 2,000,000 GHO, the Avana Umbrella 100,000, the DAO deficit offset 150,000; borrowers owe 1,500,000 GHO and a 300,000 GHO account is liquidated to zero collateral; the layers cover 100,000 then 150,000 and 50,000 stays recorded; after every borrower repays, liquidity is 1,950,000 GHO against 2,000,000 of claims, so the first 1,950,000 withdrawn is paid in full and the last 50,000 of claims is not. Checked by: TEST-299, MATH-AR-51e, SYM-193a–f. (Source: Q-070, Q-071, Q-101, Q-119, Q-236, Q-407, Q-410)
- `REQ-FLR-028` — Before caps are approved, the Aave V4 risk service providers MUST size per-market, per-token and per-debt-asset caps so that `SPEC-FLR-004` (incident term) and `SPEC-FLR-005` (tail term) both hold for every debt asset against its funded reserve, including the outage scenario at full utilization with recovery shortfalls from reverted settlements and a debt-asset price rise over the incident window. (Source: Q-186, Q-192, Q-197, Q-234, Q-236, Q-249)
- `REQ-FLR-039` — Each Coverage Spoke MUST expose `ASSET()`, `HUB()` and `ASSET_ID()`; `enableDebtReserve` for any debt reserve, GHO included, MUST name, in the chain's MarketRegistry `DebtReserveDef.coverageSpoke`, an instance whose `HUB()` and `ASSET_ID()` equal the reserve's `fundingHub` and `hubAssetId` and whose `ASSET()` equals the reserve's asset, and the reserve's `drawCap` MUST stay 0 until that instance holds at least its `firstLossReserveMinimum`. Why: a deficit is a shortfall of a specific asset owed to that asset's suppliers on the Hub that lent it; binding each debt reserve to the instance on the same `(hub, asset)` is what makes coverage burn exactly the lost shares, and it keeps one asset's losses from consuming the reserve set aside for another. Example: the USDC debt reserve 2 of the Ethereum CDP Spoke "Uniswap v3 Stable" is funded through the Ethereum Lend Spoke into the Ethereum Stable LP Hub (`hubAssetId` 3); its `enableDebtReserve` names the USDC Coverage Spoke with `HUB()` = that Hub, `ASSET_ID()` = 3, `ASSET()` = USDC, which Avana Labs funds with its USDC minimum before the Aave payload raises the reserve's `drawCap`. (Source: Q-233, Q-236, Q-239, Q-284, Q-286)
- `REQ-FLR-040` — A Coverage Spoke MUST NOT eliminate a deficit recorded on any asset other than its own `ASSET_ID`, and no Avana contract converts one asset's reserve into another to cover a deficit. A CDP Spoke with deficits in two assets is covered by two calls, one on each asset's Coverage Spoke of the funding Hub. A deficit on one chain is never covered from an instance on another chain, and a deficit on one Hub's asset never from another Hub's instance of the same asset. (Source: Q-236)
- `REQ-FLR-041` — Each debt asset's reserve MUST be sized per asset: the Aave V4 risk service providers state, in every listing or cap-raising proposal that touches a token or a debt asset, `tail_a`, the incident need of `SPEC-FLR-004` and the resulting `tailCoverageBps_a` of `SPEC-FLR-005` for every debt asset `a` the change touches, and the Aave DAO sets `firstLossReserveMinimum[a]` to at least the larger of the two needs. The rule is guidance owned by the DAO and its service providers (`Q-197`): no Avana or Coverage Spoke function checks it, and `Lens.getTailCoverage(a)` (`REQ-LENS-034`) and the monitoring ratio make any gap visible. A token whose tail exceeds one asset's reserve is reduced by lowering `tokenUsdCap` or `topUpSliceUsd`, never covered by another asset's reserve (`REQ-FLR-040`). Example: adding an $800,000 USDe market slice-free to the $1,600,000 USDe cap above would raise `tail_GHO` to `2,480,000 × 0.312 = 773,760` GHO; the proposal states `tailCoverageBps_GHO = ⌊400,000 × 10⁴ / 773,760⌋ = 5,169` against an illustrative 400,000 GHO reserve and is not approved until the minimum is raised and funded or the cap is cut. Checked by: TEST-216, TEST-247. (Source: Q-249, Q-197, Q-236)
- `REQ-FLR-042` — Coverage scope MUST be per `(chain, Hub, debt asset)`: each chain's Coverage Spoke for `(h, a)` covers deficits on `a` of `h` recorded by every CDP Spoke that draws `a` from `h`, whatever that Spoke's home Hub, and nothing else. A CDP Spoke whose home Hub is the Volatile LP Hub but whose debt reserve draws USDC from the Stable LP Hub (a cross-Hub credit line, off at launch) is covered by the Stable LP Hub's USDC instance, because its losses fall on that Hub's USDC suppliers. Why: a deficit is recorded on the Hub that funded the debt, so only that Hub's first-loss capital in that asset may pay it; tying coverage to the home Hub would leave the funding Hub's suppliers unprotected. (Source: Q-236, Q-283, Q-284)
- `REQ-FLR-043` — When cross-Hub credit lines are enabled on a chain, the cross-Hub lines MUST satisfy `crossHubLineTotal(fundingHub, a) ≤ fundedBalance()` of the Coverage Spoke of `(fundingHub, a)`, checked by `enableDebtReserve` and by every change of a cross-Hub cap or `drawCap` on that pair, and by the drift check on every borrow that draws a cross-Hub line; a coverage call or withdrawal that leaves the balance below the cap stops new cross-Hub draws on that pair until the cap is lowered or the reserve topped up. Units: base units of `a`. Rounding: the balance is the floored `SPEC-FLR-002` value, so the check is never looser than the real balance. Why: a foreign Spoke's losses fall on the funding Hub's suppliers, and bounding its draws by that Hub's first-loss capital keeps every cross-Hub loss within what that Hub's reserve can absorb. Example: the Stable LP Hub's USDC instance holds 250,000 USDC; a `setCrossHubLine` that would bring `crossHubLineTotal(Stable, USDC)` to 300,000 USDC reverts `CrossHubCapExceeded`, and 250,000 is accepted; after a 20,000 USDC coverage the balance is 230,000, so new cross-Hub USDC draws revert until the cap is lowered to 230,000 or 20,000 USDC is funded. At launch `crossHubLinesEnabled` is false on both chains and no cross-Hub cap exists. (Source: Q-283, Q-284)
- `REQ-FLR-044` — Avana MUST fund and the Aave DAO MUST control the launch instances on both chains under the same rules: each Coverage Spoke on Ethereum and Base (for example the GHO Coverage Spoke of each chain) holds at least its own `firstLossReserveMinimum` before any `drawCap` on its (Hub, asset) is above 0, each is sized by `SPEC-FLR-004` and `SPEC-FLR-005` against that chain's caps only, and on each chain `RESERVE_ADMIN_ROLE` is held by the Aave DAO's executor on that chain (on Base, reached through Aave cross-chain governance) and `COVERAGE_ROLE` by both Avana's Emergency Guardian multisig and the Aave Protocol Guardian multisig of that chain, granted in the Aave DAO's listing payload, so either can cover within the 72-hour `deficitCoverageWindow` without a governance proposal; the DAO stays the role admin and can revoke either at once. Why: each chain's Hubs settle and record deficits independently, so each chain needs funded first-loss capital of its own. Example (illustrative amounts; each chain's minimum and caps are set in the funding ARFC, `Q-391`, `Q-392`): an illustrative 400,000 GHO on Ethereum does not count toward Base; with Base GHO caps of 120,000 GHO at a loss factor of 0.141965, the Base instance needs at least `⌈120,000 × 0.141965⌉ = 17,036` GHO from the incident term before its tail term is checked. (Source: Q-279, Q-284, Q-285, Q-408)

### DAO deficit offset

- `REQ-FLR-046` — For each covered `(chain, Hub, debt asset)` the Aave DAO's listing payload that registers the Avana Umbrella MUST also register one DAO deficit offset, whose size the DAO sets (0 until the funding ARFC sets it): a `DeficitOffsetSpoke` with the Coverage Spoke's constructor inputs plus the Avana Umbrella of that pair as `firstLossLayer`, registered on the Hub like a Coverage Spoke (`REQ-FLR-007`: own asset only, `drawCap = 0`, `addCap = MAX_ALLOWED_SPOKE_CAP`, active, holding `HUB_DEFICIT_ELIMINATOR_ROLE`), with `coverDeficit` mapped to `COVERAGE_ROLE`, the admin functions to `RESERVE_ADMIN_ROLE`, the CDP Spokes of the pair on its own allowlist, `firstLossReserveMinimum = daoDeficitOffset[hub, asset]`, and funded by the Aave DAO treasury through `fund`. Every Coverage Spoke rule applies to it (`REQ-FLR-002` to `REQ-FLR-045`) except that the funder is the DAO treasury, not Avana Labs (`REQ-FLR-011` binds only the Avana Umbrella), and no Avana contract reads it: the MarketRegistry's readiness check and the coverage bound (`REQ-FLR-039`, `DEF-019`) read the Avana Umbrella only, and LPVaultSwap's pending-settlement proceeds go to the Avana Umbrella only (`REQ-FLR-038`). (Source: Q-410)
- `REQ-FLR-047` — The `DeficitOffsetSpoke` constructor MUST revert as the Coverage Spoke constructor does (`REQ-FLR-008`), then `ZeroAddress` if `firstLossLayer` is the zero address and `FirstLossLayerMismatch(firstLossLayer)` unless `firstLossLayer.HUB()`, `ASSET()` and `ASSET_ID()` equal its own `hub`, `asset` and `assetId`; it fixes `FIRST_LOSS_LAYER` immutably and exposes it. (Source: Q-410)
- `REQ-FLR-048` — Loss order: `coverDeficit` on a DAO deficit offset MUST, after the allowlist and recorded-deficit checks and before computing the amount, revert `FirstLossLayerNotExhausted(FIRST_LOSS_LAYER, balance)` while `HUB.getSpokeConfig(ASSET_ID, FIRST_LOSS_LAYER).active` is true and `FIRST_LOSS_LAYER.fundedBalance()` is above 0; otherwise it covers per `SPEC-FLR-001` with its own shares (`SPEC-FLR-006`). The Avana Umbrella's `coverDeficit` runs the same hook, which accepts every call. The `COVERAGE_ROLE` holder covers from the Avana Umbrella first and from the offset in the same batch. Why: the Hub burns the caller's own shares, so only a check at the second layer keeps the DAO's capital behind Avana's whatever the caller does; a halted Avana Umbrella is still active and pays first. A deactivated Avana Umbrella cannot pay, so the offset may then pay although the Umbrella still holds funds: coverage never waits on a reactivation, and the trade-off, accepted on 2026-11-12, is that DAO money is spent before Avana's while the DAO keeps the Umbrella deactivated; only the DAO can deactivate it. Example: `coverDeficit(cdpSpoke, max)` on the GHO offset with the Avana Umbrella at 80,000 GHO reverts `FirstLossLayerNotExhausted(avanaUmbrella, 80,000 GHO)`; after the Avana Umbrella covers its 80,000, the same call covers up to the offset's balance; with the Avana Umbrella deactivated and still at 80,000 GHO, the call covers up to the offset's balance at once. Checked by: TEST-298, MATH-AR-51h. (Source: Q-410)
- `REQ-FLR-049` — Accounting separation: each layer's capital MUST be its own Hub share position (the Hub's `getSpokeAddedShares` of that instance), and funding, coverage, withdrawals, reporting totals and events are per instance, so whose funds a coverage spent is read from the instance that called (`EliminateDeficit.callerSpoke`, `DeficitCovered` emitter). The DAO withdraws its offset only through the offset's own withdrawal queue, after `RESERVE_WITHDRAWAL_DELAY` and behind the same deficit guard (`REQ-FLR-026`); the offset's funds never count toward the Avana Umbrella's minimum, its coverage bound or `REQ-FLR-011`. `daoDeficitOffset[hub, asset]` is set by the Aave DAO in the funding ARFC with the service providers (`SPEC-FLR-004`, `SPEC-FLR-005`), stored as the offset's `firstLossReserveMinimum`, funded from the DAO treasury and refilled by the DAO; below it the offset emits `ReserveBelowMinimum` like any instance. Checked by: TEST-298. (Source: Q-410)
