# 2.4 Vault Tokens

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-11
>
> **Requirement prefix:** `REQ-VTOKEN-NNN` · **Math prefix:** `SPEC-UNIT-NNN`

## Purpose

A vault token is the collateral-only ERC-20 that represents the credited units of one market inside Aave V4. A market is one (chain, AMM protocol, pool, exact position shape) listed on one CDP Spoke ([2.19](19-amm-integration-interface.md)), and there is exactly one vault token per (CDP Spoke, market): one vault token = one collateral reserve of that Spoke, on the Spoke's home Hub. One base unit equals one unit credited by the chain's Collateralization ([2.3](03-collateralization.md)) in a position the market's LPVault holds ([2.2](02-lp-vault.md)).

The vault token is responsible for: fixed per-market decimals; mint and burn exclusively by the AaveAdapter; the transfer rule (units move only into the Hub on supply, out of the Hub to a registered proxy on withdrawal, and out of the Hub to LPVaultSwap on seizure; mint to and burn from only registered proxies and LPVaultSwap); the registry of valid proxies, written only by the Adapter; and exposing its immutable bindings, including the market's identity (`integrationId()`, `integration()`, `marketKey()`, `marketId()`, `lpOracle()`), for Collateralization, the Lens and manifest checks (`Q-224`).

It is not responsible for: valuation (LPOracle, [2.10](10-lp-oracle.md)), backing records (Collateralization), position custody and the market's shape (the LPVault), proxy creation (ProxyManager, [2.5](05-position-proxy-and-proxy-manager.md)), or Aave reserve configuration (Hub and Spokes, [2.1](01-avana-hub-and-spokes.md)). The VaultTokenFactory deploys each token, keeps the per-market-key token registry and the per-integration LPVault registry; its rules are under [Deployment](#deployment).

## Requirements

### Form and bindings

- `REQ-VTOKEN-001` — Each vault token MUST be a non-upgradeable contract with no owner, no admin, no role, no setter, no pause, no blocklist and no `selfdestruct`; nothing about it can change after deployment except balances, allowances and the append-only proxy registry. Its minter, the AaveAdapter, is upgradeable behind the timelock's normal delay; the token itself is not, so no upgrade can change who may receive, mint or burn it beyond the Adapter it names. (Source: Q-068, Q-224, Q-226)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-002` — The vault token MUST implement ERC-20 with OpenZeppelin Contracts v5.1.0 `ERC20` semantics for balances, allowances and events, and MUST NOT add EIP-2612 `permit`, transfer hooks or callbacks (ERC-777, ERC-1363 or similar), flash minting, rebasing, fees on transfer or emissions. Without hooks no code runs while vault tokens move, so no Avana contract reads or acts on Spoke or Hub state in the middle of a transfer; without `permit` no signature can create an allowance, and proxies have no ERC-1271 to sign one. (Source: Q-064, Q-068, Q-119)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-003` — The vault token MUST store as immutables and expose `integrationId()`, `integration()`, `marketKey()`, `hub()`, `spoke()`, `marketId()`, `lpOracle()`, `adapter()`, `lpVaultSwap()`, `collateralization()` and `decimals()`. These are the immutable sources from which Collateralization reads the market's integration, custodian, shape key, Hub, Spoke, policy key and LPOracle and checks that the token is bound to it, so the immutable ledger never takes market identity from the upgradeable MarketRegistry. The token holds no AMM-protocol-specific field, so one token contract serves every AMM protocol. (Source: Q-068, Q-224, Q-226, Q-291)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-004` — `decimals()` MUST return `d` per `SPEC-UNIT-001`, fixed at deployment. (Source: Q-066, Q-191)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-005` — One base unit of the vault token MUST equal one unit credited in the market (`SPEC-UNIT-002`; liquidity for concentrated liquidity, LP base units for fungible LP shares); the multiplier is 1 and no conversion exists anywhere in mint, burn or transfer. (Source: Q-066, Q-291)

### Recipients and transfer rules

- `REQ-VTOKEN-006` — The token MUST allow exactly these balance changes of a market's vault token and no other (`isAllowedTransfer(from, to)` for transfers): (1) **mint**: `from = 0`, `to` a registered proxy of the market, caller the AaveAdapter (pledge, re-credit); (2) **supply**: `from` a registered proxy, `to == hub()`, executed by the market's CDP Spoke as spender through `transferFrom` (`Spoke.supply` does `safeTransferFrom(proxy, hub, amount)`); (3) **withdraw**: `from == hub()`, `to` a registered proxy (`Spoke.withdraw` called by the proxy runs `hub.remove(assetId, amount, msg.sender)`, so the Hub pays the proxy); (4) **seizure**: `from == hub()`, `to == lpVaultSwap()` (`liquidationCall` runs `hub.remove(assetId, amountToLiquidator, liquidator)`); (5) **burn**: `to = 0`, `from` a registered proxy (withdrawal, partial removal, deleverage release) or `lpVaultSwap()` (`burnSeized`), caller the AaveAdapter. There is no Hub → Adapter transfer: withdrawn units go Hub → proxy, and the Adapter then burns them from the proxy in the same call. Every other transfer reverts, zero amounts included: proxy to proxy, LPVaultSwap to the Hub, anything to or from the Adapter, and any transfer to the CDP Spoke, the Hub's reinvestment controller, a fee receiver, a keeper, a wallet, an LPVault or Collateralization. Why: the CDP Spoke is a standard Aave V4 Spoke whose `liquidationCall` (5 arguments, called with `receiveShares = false`) anyone may call; the Hub then transfers the seized units to the caller, so any liquidator other than LPVaultSwap reverts on the token transfer; a registered proxy, which the withdrawal path admits as a Hub → proxy recipient, never calls `liquidationCall`, because the Adapter's call builder has no such selector ([2.5](05-position-proxy-and-proxy-manager.md) `REQ-PROXY-008`); and only LPVaultSwap can seize and settle Avana collateral. Two cases move no token and so cannot be refused by the token: a `liquidationCall` whose seized amount rounds to zero shares (Aave skips the Hub transfer, so any caller may complete it; the caller only repays part of the borrower's debt at its own expense and takes no collateral, `REQ-VTOKEN-025`), and the share moves of the settings below. This holds only while the reserve has `receiveSharesEnabled = false`, `liquidationFee = 0` and no reinvestment controller, because those paths move Spoke shares without a token transfer (`REQ-VTOKEN-025`). (Source: Q-068, Q-116, Q-119, Q-224, Q-300)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-007` — The proxy registry MUST live in the token as `isValidProxy`, written only by `addValidProxy` from the Adapter (`REQ-VTOKEN-039`); no address can remove an entry, and the token reads no external registry. (Source: Q-224, Q-225)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-008` — Every `transfer` and `transferFrom`, including transfers to `address(0)` and transfers by a spender, MUST revert with `UnauthorizedTransfer(from, to)` unless `isAllowedTransfer(from, to)` and the amount is non-zero (`REQ-VTOKEN-048`), where `from` is the caller of `transfer` and the named holder of `transferFrom`. The path check runs before OpenZeppelin's receiver, allowance and balance checks, so an off-path `transferFrom` reverts with `UnauthorizedTransfer` whatever the spender's allowance, unlimited included, and spends none of it. An allowance can therefore never move a unit off the allowed paths, and a unit can rest only in the Hub, a registered proxy or LPVaultSwap. Example: alice's proxy `P` approves her wallet for `type(uint256).max`; the wallet's `transferFrom(P, wallet, 10)` reverts `UnauthorizedTransfer(P, wallet)` and the allowance stays unlimited, while the Spoke's `transferFrom(P, hub, 10)` on an approval of 10 succeeds and leaves it at 0. (Source: Q-116, Q-224, Q-300)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-009` — Transfers into the Hub MUST be accepted only from a registered proxy, the only caller of `Spoke.supply` in Avana's flows (the Adapter has the proxy supply through `proxy.execute`, setting the proxy's exact allowance to the CDP Spoke and resetting it to 0). The Hub's balance stays equal to its recorded liquidity because Avana's reviewed code never transfers vault tokens to the Hub outside `Spoke.supply`, and neither the Adapter nor LPVaultSwap can transfer to the Hub at all. The Lens reports any excess of the Hub balance over recorded liquidity as stranded (`REQ-VTOKEN-018`). (Source: Q-119, Q-224, Q-300, Q-349)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-010` — On `Spoke.supply`, the Spoke calls `transferFrom(proxy, hub, amount)` as spender of the proxy's exact approval before `Hub.add`; the token MUST accept it because a registered proxy to the Hub is an allowed path, and the Spoke itself never holds a unit. The proxy's `approve` emits `Approval(proxy, spoke, amount)`; OpenZeppelin v5.1.0's `_spendAllowance` emits no `Approval`, so fork tests expect `Approval(proxy, spoke, amount)`, then `Transfer(proxy, hub, amount)`, then the Adapter's reset `Approval(proxy, spoke, 0)` on every pledge. Because a zero-amount transfer reverts (`REQ-VTOKEN-048`), every `Transfer` from a proxy has a non-zero value. (Source: Q-119, Q-224)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-011` — `approve` MUST follow OpenZeppelin v5.1.0 semantics; an allowance never bypasses `REQ-VTOKEN-008`, because the path check applies to the `from` and `to` of every transfer. (Source: Q-224)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-040` — `isAllowedTransfer(from, to)` MUST return `(to == hub() && isValidProxy[from]) || (from == hub() && (isValidProxy[to] || to == lpVaultSwap()))`; `isAllowedRecipient(account)` MUST return `account == hub() || account == lpVaultSwap() || isValidProxy[account]` (the set any unit can reach); `isValidProxy(account)` MUST return the registry entry; all three are views that never revert. (Source: Q-224, Q-300)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-041` — A transfer from one registered proxy to another, from LPVaultSwap to the Hub, or to or from the Adapter MUST revert (`UnauthorizedTransfer(from, to)`): no Avana flow makes one, so the token refuses them instead of relying on the reviewed code of the proxy, the Adapter and LPVaultSwap. Why: with one PositionProxy per (wallet, CDP Spoke, market), a proxy-to-proxy transfer would move collateral between isolated accounts, and a transfer from LPVaultSwap to the Hub would let seized units re-enter Aave as someone's collateral without settlement. (Source: Q-224, Q-225, Q-300, Q-304)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-048` — A `transfer` or `transferFrom` of 0 units MUST revert with `UnauthorizedTransfer(from, to)` on every path, the allowed ones included, before any allowance is read or spent: a zero amount is no movement, so it is on no path. `mint` and `burn` of 0 by the Adapter keep their path checks, change no balance and emit a zero `Transfer`; Avana's flows never make them. Why: on an allowed path a zero `transferFrom(proxy, hub, 0)` needs no allowance, so without this rule anyone could emit `Transfer(proxy, hub, 0)` events that look like supplies to monitors and fork assertions; Aave V4 never transfers 0 units (`Hub.add` and `Hub.remove` revert `InvalidAmount` for 0, and `liquidationCall` skips the Hub transfer when the seized shares round to 0), so the rule blocks no Aave path. Example: `transferFrom(P, hub, 0)` by any address reverts `UnauthorizedTransfer(P, hub)`. (Source: Q-224, Q-300)

### Mint, burn and registry

- `REQ-VTOKEN-012` — `mint(to, amount)` MUST revert with `OnlyAdapter` unless `msg.sender == adapter()`, with `ZeroAddress` if `to == address(0)`, and with `UnauthorizedRecipient(to)` unless `isValidProxy[to]`. The Hub, the Adapter and LPVaultSwap are never mint destinations: LPVaultSwap receives units only from the Hub on seizure, and the Hub only through `Spoke.supply`. (Source: Q-224, Q-300)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-013` — The Adapter mints only inside a pledge, exactly the `credited` units Collateralization returns for the new record, and inside a re-credit, exactly the `added` units Collateralization credits to the record, in both cases to the depositor's registered proxy ([2.3](03-collateralization.md) `SPEC-BACK-002`, `REQ-COLL-015`, [2.6](06-aave-adapter.md)). The Hub `addCap` bounds how much reaches Aave. The registry bounds a faulty Adapter only as far as its registrations go: it may register any address that is not a binding (`REQ-VTOKEN-047`), and a registered address receives units both by mint and from the Hub through any Aave call that pays it, a `liquidationCall` it makes included; so the bound on what a faulty Adapter can mint or seize to is "any non-reserved address it registers, up to the Hub `addCap`", and the protection against a proxy liquidating is the Adapter's call builder having no `liquidationCall` selector ([2.5](05-position-proxy-and-proxy-manager.md) `REQ-PROXY-008`). A reviewed Adapter registers only the proxies the ProxyManager deployed, never the TreasurySpoke, its owner or a reinvestment controller. (Source: Q-066, Q-223, Q-224, Q-254)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-014` — `burn(from, amount)` MUST revert with `OnlyAdapter` unless `msg.sender == adapter()`, with `ZeroAddress` if `from == address(0)` and with `UnauthorizedTransfer(from, address(0))` unless `isValidProxy[from]` or `from == lpVaultSwap()`, and MUST burn from `from` without an allowance. Avana's flows burn only from the proxy that has just withdrawn the units (a withdrawal, a partial removal or a repay with collateral) and from LPVaultSwap (`burnSeized`, the units it received in a seizure, before `settleSeizure` and `settlePending`); the token refuses a burn from the Hub, and a reviewed Adapter never burns from a proxy outside its own withdrawal. Collateralization's supply checks prove each seizure burn exactly and that no pending settlement pays for units still in circulation ([2.3](03-collateralization.md) `SPEC-BACK-007`, `SPEC-BACK-012`). (Source: Q-224, Q-254, Q-255, Q-257)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-015` — Total supply MUST change only through `mint` and `burn`. (Source: Q-066, Q-224)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-039` — `addValidProxy(proxy)` MUST revert with `OnlyAdapter` unless `msg.sender == adapter()`, with `ZeroAddress` if `proxy == address(0)` and with `ReservedAddress(proxy)` if `proxy` is `hub()`, `spoke()`, `adapter()`, `lpVaultSwap()`, `collateralization()`, `integration()`, `lpOracle()` or the token itself (`REQ-VTOKEN-047`); if `isValidProxy[proxy]` is already true it MUST return without a state change or event, otherwise set it to true and emit `ProxyAdded(proxy)`. There is no removal. The Adapter registers a proxy in a market's token at that proxy's first pledge into the market, after checking `isValidProxy` itself; a reviewed Adapter registers only addresses the ProxyManager deployed with CREATE2 for `(wallet, spoke, marketId)` of this token's Spoke and market ([2.5](05-position-proxy-and-proxy-manager.md)). Why append-only: a proxy's registration backs Aave positions that must stay withdrawable and seizable, so removing it could strand units in the Hub. Why the bindings are refused: a registered Hub would make the Hub's balance burnable and `Hub → Hub` an allowed path, a registered LPVaultSwap would open `LPVaultSwap → Hub` (seized units re-entering Aave unsettled), and a registered Adapter would make the Adapter a mint destination and a seizure recipient of its own `liquidationCall`; refusing them in the immutable token keeps `REQ-VTOKEN-006`, `REQ-VTOKEN-041` and `REQ-VTOKEN-042` true for any Adapter implementation. Example: alice's first pledge into the USDC/USDT market deploys her proxy for (alice, Spoke, USDC/USDT) and registers it, emitting `ProxyAdded`; her second pledge into the same market finds it valid and the Adapter skips the call; her first pledge into the DAI/USDC market deploys a second proxy for (alice, Spoke, DAI/USDC) and registers that one in the DAI/USDC token, because Aave V4 computes one health factor per account over all its collaterals and a liquidator may seize any of them, so each market has its own account. (Source: Q-224, Q-225, Q-307)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-042` — Because `burn` needs no allowance, a faulty or hostile Adapter implementation can destroy the units of any registered proxy or of LPVaultSwap; it cannot burn the Hub's balance, which the token refuses (`REQ-VTOKEN-014`), and it cannot register the Hub as a proxy to get around that (`REQ-VTOKEN-047`), so Hub liquidity always stays backed by tokens the Hub holds. This is part of the Adapter's blast radius (`Q-223`), bounded by the timelock's normal delay and its cancellers. Repay and debt-free withdrawal stay open in every pause state, so borrowers can leave during that delay. (Source: Q-223, Q-224, Q-228)

### Supply and balances

- `REQ-VTOKEN-016` — At the end of every transaction, `totalSupply()` MUST be at most the sum of credited units of the market's open backing records in Collateralization plus the units of its pending-settlement balance for the market (unsettled seizures reconciled on exit, [2.3](03-collateralization.md)), for any LPVaultSwap implementation, and equal to it while every `burnSeized` is followed in the same call by the `settleSeizure` or `settlePending` that debits it; this is the backing invariant in [4.1 Global Invariants](../04-security/01-global-invariants.md), maintained by Collateralization and the Adapter (`SPEC-BACK-009`). The Adapter checks after every pledge, withdrawal, partial removal, re-credit and repay with collateral that the call left the backing gap unchanged, and Collateralization's supply checks enforce the bound on seizure and pending settlement. Why one-sided: a burn a faulty LPVaultSwap never settled leaves more backing than units, which must not stop the market's pledges and exits. (Source: Q-066, Q-201, Q-224, Q-257)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-017` — Between transactions vault tokens MUST rest only in the Hub, except seized units LPVaultSwap holds after a seizure that was never settled; those stay backed by Collateralization's pending-settlement balance until a later settlement has the Adapter burn them. Inside a call, a proxy holds units only between the mint and the supply of a pledge or re-credit and between the Spoke withdrawal and the Adapter's burn of a withdrawal, partial removal or repay with collateral, and an honest LPVaultSwap has every seized unit burned in the same liquidation. The LPVault, Collateralization and the Adapter never hold a unit: no path leads to them. (Source: Q-119, Q-124, Q-201, Q-224, Q-254, Q-255)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-018` — The Hub's vault-token balance MUST equal the Hub's recorded liquidity for the asset after every Avana flow; no Avana contract pushes vault tokens to the Hub outside `Spoke.supply` and the reviewed Adapter never burns from the Hub. Fork tests assert the equality after every flow, and the Lens reports any excess of the Hub balance over recorded liquidity as stranded and any shortfall as an alarm. (Source: Q-064, Q-066, Q-119, Q-224)

### Deployment

- `REQ-VTOKEN-019` — Vault tokens MUST be deployed only by the chain's VaultTokenFactory, whose `deployVaultToken` reverts with `NotTimelock` unless `msg.sender == TIMELOCK`. The chain's governance multisig (4-of-7) schedules it in the market's listing batch (`listCollateralMarket`) on that chain's AvanaTimelock at the normal delay (5 days at launch); any `CANCELLER_ROLE` holder can cancel it, and anyone can execute it after the delay and within the 14-day grace period ([2.15](15-governance-and-timelock.md)). (Source: Q-114, Q-228)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-020` — `deployVaultToken` MUST deploy with CREATE3 using `salt = keccak256(abi.encode(spoke, marketKey))`, where `marketKey = marketKeyOf(integrationId, pool, poolId, shape) = keccak256(abi.encode(block.chainid, integrationId, pool, poolId, shape.kind, shape.tickLower, shape.tickUpper))` ([2.19](19-amm-integration-interface.md) `SPEC-AMM-CORE-002`), so the token's address depends only on the factory, the CDP Spoke and the market and can be predicted (`predictAddress(spoke, marketKey)`) before the listing batch executes, and every manifest can check it in advance. The address commits only to (factory, Spoke, market key), not to the other bindings, so a manifest checks the address before the listing batch and reads the token's immutables (`marketId()`, `integrationId()`, `integration()`, `hub()`, `spoke()`, `lpOracle()`, `adapter()`, `lpVaultSwap()`, `collateralization()`, `decimals()`) after it executes; on chain, `listCollateralMarket` checks them in the same batch (`REQ-VTOKEN-022`). - `REQ-VTOKEN-022` — `deployVaultToken(integrationId, pool, poolId, shape, hub, spoke, marketId, lpOracle, decimals, name, symbol)` MUST revert with `UnknownIntegration(integrationId)` unless `lpVaultOf(integrationId) != address(0)`, with `ZeroAddress` unless `hub`, `spoke` and `lpOracle` are non-zero and the pool key is exactly the one the integration's recorded keying requires (`pool` set and `poolId` zero for `PoolKeying.Address`, the reverse for `PoolKeying.Id`), with `InvalidShape` unless the shape is canonical for the integration's recorded shape kind (`REQ-VTOKEN-049`), with `DecimalsOutOfRange` unless `6 ≤ decimals ≤ 18` (`SPEC-UNIT-001`), with `InvalidMarketId` unless `0 < marketId < 2^32` ([2.8](08-market-registry.md) `REQ-CONFIG-006`), with `TokenExistsForMarket` if the key has a token, and with `InvalidTokenMetadata` unless `name` has 1 to 128 bytes and `symbol` 1 to 32 bytes (`REQ-VTOKEN-050`), in that order after `NotTimelock`. `marketKey != 0` and `integrationId != 0`, two of the token's constructor conditions, hold here without a check: the key is a keccak output and only registered identifiers pass. A binding equal to the token's own address would make the token its own Hub, Spoke or oracle, and it is known before deployment because the address depends only on the factory, the Spoke and the key. The factory writes `tokenOf[spoke][marketKey]` and `isVaultToken` for the predicted address before the CREATE3 deployment (the constructor calls nothing, and a failed deployment reverts the writes and the event), then emits `VaultTokenDeployed` with every binding and the market definition. The implementation is `public` with `name` and `symbol` in memory, with the same ABI and selector as the interface's `external` and calldata form, because the calldata decoder of eleven parameters with two calldata strings does not fit the EVM stack. It passes `integrationId`, `lpVaultOf(integrationId)` (as `integration`), `marketKey`, `hub`, `spoke`, `marketId`, `lpOracle`, `ADAPTER`, `LP_VAULT_SWAP`, `COLLATERALIZATION`, `decimals`, `name` and `symbol` to the token as one `VaultTokenBindings` struct, the constructor's only argument: the CREATE3 creation code is `abi.encodePacked(type(VaultToken).creationCode, abi.encode(bindings))`. The token never learns the factory's address; `isVaultToken` and `tokenOf` in the factory are the only record of origin. The factory calls no integration: the AMM protocol's listing guards (pool identity against the pinned factory and position manager, exact-shape alignment, token count and token flags) run in `bindMarket`, which the MarketRegistry calls in the same `listCollateralMarket` batch ([2.19](19-amm-integration-interface.md) `REQ-AMM-CORE-003`), and the MarketRegistry requires the token's `marketKey()` to equal `marketKeyOf(def)`, `tokenOf(spoke, marketKey)` to be the token, and its `integrationId()`, `integration()`, `marketId()`, `hub()`, `spoke()`, `lpOracle()` and `decimals()` to match the market and the integration's `unitDecimals`, so a batch cannot pair one shape's token with another shape's market ([2.8](08-market-registry.md) `REQ-CONFIG-032`). The factory does not check that `hub` is the home Hub of `spoke`; the MarketRegistry does in the same batch: `spoke` must be a CDP Spoke registered by `deployCdpSpoke` whose `hubOf(spoke)` is the market's Hub, and the token's `hub()` and `spoke()` must match the market ([2.8](08-market-registry.md) `REQ-CONFIG-032`, `REQ-CONFIG-033`, `REQ-CONFIG-083`), so a token bound to another Hub reverts the whole listing and its pair stays empty. These on-chain checks complement the off-chain manifest check of every listing: market activation also verifies that the vault token, the market, the Spoke reserve, the Hub asset, the feeds and the swap routes belong to one market, that the whole-token price is within the band of `SPEC-UNIT-001`, and that every market token has a configured feed. (Source: Q-043, Q-119, Q-177, Q-191, Q-293, Q-298, Q-374)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-023` — The VaultTokenFactory MUST be non-upgradeable and hold no power over deployed tokens. (Source: Q-068, Q-226, Q-228, Q-291)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-024` — The factory MUST emit `VaultTokenDeployed` for every token. (Source: Q-076)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-038` — The token's `marketId()` and `lpOracle()` MUST equal the `marketId` the MarketRegistry assigns to the market and the market's registered `lpOracle`. - `REQ-VTOKEN-044` — The factory's `registerIntegration(uint32 integrationId, address lpVault)` MUST revert with `NotTimelock` unless `msg.sender == TIMELOCK`, and with `InvalidIntegration` if `integrationId == 0`, if `lpVault` is `address(0)` or one of the factory's `ADAPTER`, `LP_VAULT_SWAP` and `COLLATERALIZATION` (every token bound to it would fail `REQ-VTOKEN-046`, so the ID would be spent for life on an integration that can list nothing), if `lpVaultOf(integrationId)` is already set or `lpVault` is already registered under another ID, or unless `lpVault.INTEGRATION_ID() == integrationId`, `lpVault.COLLATERALIZATION() == COLLATERALIZATION`, `lpVault.LOCK() == COLLATERALIZATION.LOCK()`, `lpVault.TIMELOCK() == TIMELOCK`, `lpVault.MARKET_REGISTRY() == MARKET_REGISTRY`, `lpVault.AMM_PROTOCOL() != 0` and `lpVault.capabilities().shapeKind != None`, and unless Collateralization points back at this factory and its bindings (`VAULT_TOKEN_FACTORY() == address(this)`, `ADAPTER() == ADAPTER`, `LP_VAULT_SWAP() == LP_VAULT_SWAP`). Why: the immutable Collateralization trusts an LPVault only through this registry, so the binding must be write-once and checked against the chain's own core, the timelock that controls `returnStray` and the MarketRegistry that alone calls `bindMarket` included; a mis-wired core is found at the first registration, before any registry slot is spent; a new AMM protocol is one timelock operation, with no change to the ledger or the Lock (`AMMN-ARCH-01`). Example: Ethereum's listing of the first Uniswap v3 market is preceded by `registerIntegration(1, v3LpVault)` (`AMM_PROTOCOL() = keccak256("UNISWAP_V3")`); Base registers integration 2 with its Slipstream LPVault (`keccak256("AERODROME_SLIPSTREAM")`). (Source: Q-291, Q-374)

- `REQ-VTOKEN-046` — The token's constructor MUST revert with `ZeroAddress` if any of `integration`, `hub`, `spoke`, `lpOracle`, `adapter`, `lpVaultSwap` and `collateralization` is `address(0)`, and otherwise with `InvalidBinding` if any two of those seven addresses are equal, if one of them is the token's own address, or if `integrationId == 0`, `marketKey == bytes32(0)`, `marketId == 0` or `decimals` lies outside `[6, 18]` (`SPEC-UNIT-001`); the zero-address check runs first, so a call with a zero binding and another fault reverts `ZeroAddress`; none of the bound contracts needs code at deployment, because the token only compares and exposes them and never calls them (`REQ-VTOKEN-029`). Why: the token is immutable and every rule of `REQ-VTOKEN-006` compares addresses, so a zero or shared binding would silently merge two roles for life (a Hub equal to LPVaultSwap would make the Hub's balance burnable, an Adapter equal to the Hub would make the minter a sender on every withdrawal path); the factory checks the listing arguments first (`REQ-VTOKEN-022`), and the token refuses any deployment, by the factory or anyone else, that would break the rule. Example: a listing batch that passes the Hub's address as `lpOracle` reverts `InvalidBinding` (checked by the factory before the CREATE3 deployment, `REQ-VTOKEN-022`, and again by the token's constructor), so `deployVaultToken` and the whole batch revert and `tokenOf[spoke][marketKey]` stays empty. (Source: Q-299, Q-370, Q-371)

### Aave configuration the token depends on

- `REQ-VTOKEN-025` — The token's transfer rule works together with the CDP Spoke and Hub configuration in [2.1](01-avana-hub-and-spokes.md). The CDP Spoke is a standard, unmodified Aave V4 Spoke; the listing AIP sets, and Avana's drift check enforces, on every vault-token reserve: `receiveSharesEnabled = false` (else a liquidator takes Spoke shares with no token transfer and can borrow against them), `liquidationFee = 0` with no alternative (Aave pays a fee as Hub shares moved to the fee receiver with no token transfer: a third party's liquidation that pays only fee shares moves the borrower's shares without any unit leaving the Hub, which the token cannot refuse, and in LPVaultSwap's own liquidation the borrower's share drop exceeds the units LPVaultSwap receives, so the fee units stay in the Hub for good, because the Hub can pay only registered proxies and LPVaultSwap; the DAO's share of a liquidation comes from LPVaultSwap's settlement proceeds instead), and no reinvestment controller for the vault-token asset (else `Hub.sweep` targets an address the token refuses). The reserve is non-borrowable, every other Spoke has `drawCap = 0` on the asset, and the asset is listed only on its own CDP Spoke plus the Hub asset's fee receiver at zero caps (zero caps do not stop Aave's `payFeeShares`, which is why the fee itself must be 0). Drift in any of these settings trips a breaker that stops new activity, never liquidation ([2.7](07-risk-module.md)). With them, any `liquidationCall` not made by LPVaultSwap reverts on the Hub's transfer of seized units whenever it seizes at least one unit, and LPVaultSwap reverts any liquidation of its own that seizes 0 units ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)). A third party's dust liquidation whose seizure rounds to zero shares moves no token, so the token cannot refuse it: it repays part of the borrower's debt at the caller's expense and takes nothing, emits a `LiquidationCall` with a liquidator other than LPVaultSwap and refreshes the account's risk premium; monitors and Collateralization never treat a `LiquidationCall` as LPVaultSwap's by its event alone. (Source: Q-068, Q-116, Q-144, Q-222, Q-224, Q-237, Q-300)

## Math

The examples use Uniswap v3 markets on Ethereum: full-range USDC/USDT (6 decimals each), DAI/USDC (18 and 6) and GHO/DAI (18 each), and a USDC/USDT market on the range `[−50, 50]`, with `stressFloorUsd = 1.00000000` per whole vault token unless stated.

- `SPEC-UNIT-001` — Decimals. `d` is the smallest integer in `[6, 18]` with `10^d · v ≥ $1`, where `v` is the USD value of one credited unit at the listing's reference prices, computed by the integration's fair composition (`SPEC-AMM-CORE-001`), the same rule as [2.10](10-lp-oracle.md) `SPEC-ORC-005`; the listing fails if even `d = 18` gives less than $1. The factory requires `6 ≤ d ≤ 18` (`DecimalsOutOfRange`) and the market's activation check requires the LPOracle's whole-token price to lie in `[$0.50, $20.00]`. Why: Aave V4 values a balance as `n · price8 · 10^(18 − d)`, which needs `d ≤ 18`; the floor of 6 keeps one whole token from being a dust amount of units; and a whole token worth between $1 and $10 at listing keeps every market's 8-decimal price at 8 or more significant digits and puts caps, health factors and the stress floor on one scale across AMM protocols and shapes; the activation band allows for price moves between listing and activation. Units: decimal digits. Rounding: `d` rounds up to the next integer. Bounds: `uint8`, 6 to 18. Example: a full-range USDC/USDT market has `v = 2 × 10^−6` USD per unit, so `d = 6` and a whole token is worth $2.00; full-range DAI/USDC has `v = 2 × 10^−12` and `d = 12`; a USDC/USDT market on `[−10, 10]` holds `2 × (1 − 1.0001^−5) ≈ 0.00099975` raw units of tokens per unit at par, so `v ≈ 9.9975 × 10^−10`, `10^9 · v ≈ $0.9998 < $1` and `d = 10` (a whole token worth $10.00); on `[−50, 50]` `v ≈ 4.997 × 10^−9` and `d = 9` ($4.997); on `[−100, 100]` `v ≈ 9.975 × 10^−9` and `d = 9` ($9.975); full-range GHO/DAI gives `d = 18`; a Uniswap v2 USDC/USDT pair has `v ≈ 2 × 10^−6` per LP base unit and `d = 6`. Checked by: MATH-AMM-V3-PRICE-03, MATH-UO-01, SYM-30, HYP-44, AMM-A3a. (Source: Q-066, Q-119, Q-191, Q-298, Q-322)
- `SPEC-UNIT-002` — Unit. `1 base unit = 1 credited unit` of the market; for `n` credited units the Adapter mints exactly `n` base units at pledge or re-credit and burns exactly `n` at withdrawal, partial removal, repay with collateral, seizure or pending settlement; one whole token is `10^d` base units. Why: a 1:1 unit leaves no conversion that could round in anyone's favour between custody, Aave and seizure, and lets Collateralization prove each seizure with one equality between the share drop, the supply drop and the debit. Units: market units. Rounding: none. Bounds: `n < 2^120`. Example: a USDC/USDT position with liquidity 5,000,000,000 mints 5,000,000,000 base units = 5,000 whole tokens; a seizure of 1,575,000,000 units burns exactly 1,575,000,000 from LPVaultSwap and removes exactly that many units; a partial removal of 1,000,000,000 units burns 1,000,000,000 from the proxy and removes exactly that many. Checked by: MATH-UO-30, SYM-25, HYP-44. (Source: Q-066, Q-224, Q-254, Q-255)
- `SPEC-UNIT-003` — Whole-token value. With the oracle price vector `p` (USD per whole market token, 8 decimals), one whole vault token is worth `Σ_i a_i · p_i / 10^{dec_i}`, where `(a_1 … a_n) = composition(marketId, 10^d, p)` is the fair composition of `10^d` units at `p` ([2.19](19-amm-integration-interface.md) `SPEC-AMM-CORE-001`; the Uniswap v3 form is [2.20](20-uniswap-v3-integration.md) `SPEC-AMM-V3-001`). Why: the value follows from the token amounts the units hold at the oracle-implied price over the market's exact shape, so a pool trade cannot move it, and no AMM-specific closed form is needed in the core. Units: USD per whole token. Rounding: each amount is rounded down and reduced by 1 raw unit, and the LPOracle rounds its 8-decimal answer down ([2.10](10-lp-oracle.md)). Bounds: the activation band of `SPEC-UNIT-001`. A removal rounds each token down by less than one raw unit, while the quote already subtracts one raw unit per token, so the realizable value of a removal is not below the quote by rounding; the collateral factor and LPVaultSwap's rounding allowance absorb the remaining slippage of conversion. Example: the full-range USDC/USDT token is worth $2.00 with both legs at $1.00 and $1.60 with USDT at $0.64; the `[−50, 50]` token (`d = 9`) is worth $4.99 at peg and $3.19 with USDT at $0.64, because the clamped ratio puts the whole position in USDT. Checked by: MATH-AMM-V3-PRICE-01, MATH-AMM-V3-PRICE-03, MATH-AMM-V3-PRICE-04, MATH-UO-02, MATH-UO-07, SYM-22, SYM-30, SYM-31, HYP-44, AMM-A1a, AMM-A3f, AMM-A4a, HYP-AMM-01, HYP-AMM-04. (Source: Q-066, Q-119, Q-293)
- `SPEC-UNIT-004` — Aave value of a balance. For `n` base units and `price8` (USD per whole token, 8 decimals), USD value `= n · price8 / 10^d` (8 decimals); Aave V4 computes `n · price8 · 10^(18 − d)`, which is USD × 10^26 and needs `d ≤ 18`. Why: the activation band of `SPEC-UNIT-001` keeps prices near `1e8`–`2e9`, which leaves more than `1e20` of headroom in Aave's value math, so no balance a market can hold overflows it. Units: USD. Rounding: Aave's own. Bounds: no overflow for `n < 2^120` and `price8 ≤ 2e10`, or for a finite Hub cap and `price8 ≤ 1e20`. Example: 5,000,000,000 base units of the full-range USDC/USDT token at `price8 = 200,000,000` are worth `5,000,000,000 × 200,000,000 / 10^6 = 1,000,000,000,000` ($10,000.00000000); Aave's value is `5,000,000,000 × 200,000,000 × 10^12 = 10^30`. Checked by: MATH-UO-05, SYM-31, HYP-41. (Source: Q-066, Q-119, Q-298)
- `SPEC-UNIT-005` — Hub cap unit. One Hub `addCap` unit is one whole token, `10^d` base units; `addCap · 10^d < 2^120` for every finite `addCap < 2^40 − 1` and `d ≤ 18` (`2^40 · 10^18 ≈ 1.1 × 10^30`), so the Hub's `uint120` shares never bind before the cap. The cap is sized in whole tokens at the market's `stressFloorUsd[m]` ([2.1](01-avana-hub-and-spokes.md)). Why: Aave's `addCap` is the DAO-set supply limit checked on every supply, while the RiskModule's USD caps are checked only at pledge; sizing the Hub cap at the stress floor makes every pledge that fits Avana's budgets also fit Aave's cap. Units: whole tokens. Rounding: the sizing rounds up to whole tokens. Bounds: as stated. Example: a full-range USDC/USDT market with `marketUsdCap = $5,000,000`, `topUpSliceUsd = $500,000` and `stressFloorUsd = $1.00` needs `addCap ≥ ⌈5,500,000 / 1.00⌉ = 5,500,000` whole tokens, `5.5 × 10^12` base units; the `[−50, 50]` market (`d = 9`) with the same budgets and `stressFloorUsd = $2.50` needs `addCap ≥ 2,200,000` whole tokens, `2.2 × 10^15` base units, far below `2^120`. Checked by: MATH-UO-03, MATH-UO-28. (Source: Q-054, Q-055, Q-066, Q-070, Q-119, Q-175)
- `SPEC-UNIT-006` — Supply bound. `totalSupply ≤ min(units the AMM protocol can hold for the market, addCap · 10^d) < 2^120`, so neither `totalSupply` (`uint256`) nor the Hub's `uint120` shares can overflow. For concentrated liquidity every credited position of a market references the market's `tickLower`, so the market's credited liquidity is at most `maxLiquidityPerTick(s)`: about `1.92 × 10^32` for `s = 1`, `3.83 × 10^34` for `s = 200` and `3.8 × 10^35` for Slipstream's `s = 2000`, all below `2^120 ≈ 1.33 × 10^36`. For fungible LP shares the Hub `addCap` of `SPEC-UNIT-005` is the binding bound. Why: every unit is minted against units held in custody, and the Hub cap bounds supply on every supply. Units: base units. Rounding: none. Bounds: as stated. Example: a full-range USDC/USDT pool holding $100 million of liquidity carries about `5 × 10^13` units. Checked by: MATH-AMM-V3-PRICE-05, MATH-UO-04, MATH-UO-33, HYP-41, AMM-A5d. (Source: Q-066, Q-191, Q-291)

| AaveAdapter ([2.6](06-aave-adapter.md)) | Internal, upgradeable | None (it calls the token) | Only minter, burner and registry writer |

## Events

| Event | Emitted when | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| `Transfer` | Every transfer; mint (`from = address(0)`); burn (`to = address(0)`) | `from` (indexed), `to` (indexed), `value` |
| `Approval` | `approve` and allowance changes per OpenZeppelin v5.1.0 | `owner` (indexed), `spender` (indexed), `value` |
| `ProxyAdded` | `addValidProxy` registers a new proxy | `proxy` (indexed) |
| `VaultTokenDeployed` (factory) | `deployVaultToken` succeeds | `vaultToken` (indexed), `marketId` (indexed), `spoke` (indexed), `bindings` (the token's `VaultTokenBindings`: market key, integration ID and LPVault, Hub, Spoke, market ID, LPOracle, Adapter, LPVaultSwap, Collateralization, decimals, name, symbol), `pool`, `poolId`, `shape`; an indexer rebuilds the market key and every binding from the event alone |
| `IntegrationRegistered` (factory) | `registerIntegration` succeeds | `integrationId` (indexed), `lpVault` (indexed), `ammProtocol`, `poolKeying`, `shapeKind` |

## Restrictions

- `REQ-VTOKEN-026` — A vault token MUST NOT be held by, minted to, or transferred to any address outside the Hub, LPVaultSwap and the market's registered proxies, and seized units MUST leave the Hub only to LPVaultSwap, so Aave can never deliver seized vault collateral to anyone other than LPVaultSwap. (Source: Q-116, Q-144, Q-224, Q-300)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-027` — A vault token MUST NOT be minted or burned, and no proxy registered, by any address other than the AaveAdapter, including LPVaultSwap, Collateralization, the LPVault and governance. (Source: Q-224)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-028` — A vault token MUST NOT be bound to more than one market, Hub or CDP Spoke, and no second token MUST ever be registered for a (Spoke, market key) pair; a market key has at most one token on each CDP Spoke, and after wind-down on one Spoke it can be relisted on another with a new token (`Q-355`). Each token is permanently bound to its Spoke and that Spoke's home Hub; a later Hub or Spoke gets new tokens, and users move by repay, withdraw and re-pledge. (Source: Q-032, Q-068, Q-119, Q-299)
- `REQ-VTOKEN-029` — A vault token MUST NOT read from, call, or be configured by any other contract: it stores the Adapter's, LPVaultSwap's, Collateralization's, the LPVault's and the LPOracle's addresses only to check callers and paths and to expose them, and never calls them. (Source: Q-224, Q-226)

## Failure Conditions

| ID | Condition | Behavior (revert / pause / fallback) |
|---|---|---|
| `REQ-VTOKEN-030` | A transfer off the allowed paths of `REQ-VTOKEN-006`, or a zero-amount `transfer` or `transferFrom` on any path | Reverts with `UnauthorizedTransfer(from, to)`, transfers to `address(0)` and spender transfers included, before any allowance is read or spent (Source: Q-224, Q-300) |
| `REQ-VTOKEN-031` | Transfer to the Hub from a registered proxy outside `Spoke.supply` | Accepted (the path is allowed); the units sit in the Hub unrecorded, which the Lens reports as stranded (Source: Q-119, Q-224, Q-300) |
| `REQ-VTOKEN-032` | `mint`, `burn` or `addValidProxy` called by anyone other than the Adapter | Reverts with `OnlyAdapter` (Source: Q-224) |
| `REQ-VTOKEN-033` | `mint` to an address other than a registered proxy, or `burn` from an address other than a registered proxy or LPVaultSwap | Reverts with `UnauthorizedRecipient(to)` or `UnauthorizedTransfer(from, address(0))` (Source: Q-224) |
| `REQ-VTOKEN-034` | Insufficient balance or allowance on an allowed path, including a `burn` above the holder's balance | Reverts with the OpenZeppelin v5.1.0 `ERC20InsufficientBalance` or `ERC20InsufficientAllowance` error (Source: Q-068) |
| `REQ-VTOKEN-035` | `deployVaultToken` for a (Spoke, market key) pair that already has a token | Reverts with `TokenExistsForMarket` (Source: Q-068, Q-119, Q-299) |

| `REQ-VTOKEN-043` | `mint` to, `burn` from or `addValidProxy` of `address(0)` | Reverts with `ZeroAddress` (Source: Q-224) |
| `REQ-VTOKEN-047` | `addValidProxy` of `hub()`, `spoke()`, `adapter()`, `lpVaultSwap()`, `collateralization()`, `integration()`, `lpOracle()` or the token itself | Reverts with `ReservedAddress(proxy)`, whoever the Adapter implementation is (Source: Q-223, Q-224) |
| `REQ-VTOKEN-045` | `registerIntegration` by an address other than the timelock, for an ID or LPVault already registered, or for an LPVault bound to another core | Reverts with `NotTimelock`, or with `InvalidIntegration` for a zero or registered ID, a zero or registered LPVault, an LPVault equal to a factory binding, an LPVault whose `INTEGRATION_ID()`, `COLLATERALIZATION()`, `LOCK()`, `TIMELOCK()` or `MARKET_REGISTRY()` does not match, whose `AMM_PROTOCOL()` is 0 or whose `capabilities()` shape kind is `None`, a getter that reverts or answers malformed data, or a Collateralization that does not name this factory, its Adapter and its LPVaultSwap (Source: Q-291) |
