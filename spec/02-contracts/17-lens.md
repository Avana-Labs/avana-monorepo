# 2.17 Lens

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-10
>
> **Requirement prefix:** `REQ-LENS-NNN`

## Purpose

An immutable, read-only periphery contract, one per chain, that gathers protocol state into single-call views for the app, the keeper, monitoring and the public backing proof. It sits outside the audited core: no core contract calls it, stores its address or depends on anything it returns, and nothing it returns authorizes an action (`Q-319`). It reports: the account of one wallet in one market (its PositionProxy on that market's CDP Spoke, with the market's collateral and every debt reserve), the backing check (including any pending settlement of a reconciled seizure, as n-token amounts), collateral and debt price states with the served price next to the last accepted one, the pause state and reason of each Hub, circuit-breaker status, address freezes, cap headroom keyed by Hub and market, the tail coverage of each (Hub, debt asset) First-Loss Reserve held in its Avana Umbrella (Coverage Spoke), Avana's protocol safety module (`Q-402`), and previews built to succeed when they land in a later block: a liquidation preview that returns ready-to-send `LiquidationParams` (`Q-232`, `Q-268`), the recommended borrow at health factor 1.10 next to the protocol maximum at 1.00 (`Q-398`), the repay-all spend cap, a re-credit and a repay with collateral. Positions are named by `(integrationId, positionKey)`, the AMM integration's key for a position (for an NFT position, `bytes32(tokenId)` on Uniswap v3 and `bytes32((uint256(generation) << 248) | tokenId)` on Aerodrome Slipstream, the generation naming the market's position manager and factory pair, [2.19](19-amm-integration-interface.md) `SPEC-AMM-CORE-002`), never by an NFT ID alone; every token amount is an array in the market's pool-token order. Every value it returns is read from the deployed Avana and Aave contracts in the same call. Every account-level function takes the market (and so its CDP Spoke and Hub) as an argument, so one Lens serves every Hub, CDP Spoke, AMM protocol and market on its chain.

The Lens is **not** part of the protocol's security. Its liquidation preview sizes `debtToCover` with the pinned Aave V4 liquidation formulas so that a caller without keeper tooling can submit a correct call, but LPVaultSwap recomputes and enforces every check on execution, Aave sizes the seizure inside `liquidationCall`, and settlement uses balance deltas, never a preview. Each preview carries the margin that keeps the transaction made from it valid between the block it was read at and the block that includes it (interest accrual, a one-wei repay near Aave's dust threshold), so a margin can only make the preview more conservative than execution, never less (`Q-268`). A Lens bug can mislead a user or keeper into a transaction that reverts or pays less than shown; it cannot move value, so the Lens is reviewed and tested as periphery, not audited with the core.

## Restrictions

### Form and role

- `REQ-LENS-001` The Lens MUST be immutable: no proxy, no state-changing function, no storage other than constructor immutables and compile-time constants, no owner, no admin and no role; every external function is `view`. Being stateless, it can be replaced at any time without migrating anything. (Source: Q-119, Q-226)
- `REQ-LENS-002` A new Lens is a new deployment: one is deployed when a dependency's interface changes or a new Avana contract version needs new reads, and the verified deployment manifest bundled in `packages/protocol` records the address consumers use. Replacing the Lens requires no governance action and changes no protocol state; the previous Lens keeps answering until its reads fail. ABIs are generated from the release-commit build into `packages/protocol`, and the ABI hash in the manifest is checked at startup by the app, the indexer and the keeper. (Source: Q-119, Q-226)
- `REQ-LENS-003` No Avana core contract (AaveAdapter, ProxyManager, PositionProxies, Collateralization, every LPVault and AMM integration, vault tokens, Lock, RiskModule, MarketRegistry, ParameterStore, LPOracle, DebtOracle, LPVaultSwap, swap routes, the IFlashSource implementation, PayoutEscrow, AvanaTimelock) MAY store the Lens address or call it, and no Aave payload or keeper contract MAY use a Lens result on-chain as a condition. The Lens is periphery outside the audited core: its code is reviewed and tested with the conformance suite, and a Lens defect is a display or preview defect, never a custody or accounting one. The liquidation sizing in `previewLiquidation` (`REQ-LENS-017`) is the pinned Aave V4 formula compiled into the Lens for previews only; on execution Aave sizes the seizure inside `liquidationCall`, and LPVaultSwap measures what was spent and seized by balance delta. (Source: Q-060, Q-116, Q-119, Q-232, Q-319)
- `REQ-LENS-004` Lens results are advisory. The keeper and the app MUST re-read the authoritative values by RPC at a fixed block before acting, and every Avana contract re-checks its own conditions on execution: LPVaultSwap recomputes every liquidation gate, the flash-premium bound, the per-leg minimum outputs and the recovery floor, so a stale or wrong preview can only make a call revert or pay its caller less, never move value it should not. Indexed data (debt shares, index snapshots) is historical only; every displayed health factor, keeper decision and preparation re-reads the chain, and the keeper enumerates proxies from on-chain `ProxyDeployed` (ProxyManager) and `UserProxyCreated` (AaveAdapter) logs through two independent RPC sources. (Source: Q-119, Q-232)

### Consistency and robustness

- `REQ-LENS-005` Every return struct MUST include `blockNumber` and `timestamp` (`block.number`, `block.timestamp`). Values within one Lens call are from one block. A consumer combining several Lens calls or other RPC reads MUST issue them against the same block tag and reject results whose `blockNumber` differs. (Source: Q-119)
- `REQ-LENS-006` Every Lens function MUST revert with `Locked()` when `LOCK.isLocked()` is true, so no read observes accounting during an in-flight Avana call; Collateralization's own record views refuse reads for the same reason. (Source: Q-119, Q-148)
- `REQ-LENS-007` Lens functions MUST NOT revert because of a price state: LPOracle feeds never revert and always serve the last accepted positive price, so the Lens reports the state next to the price in Expired as in Live. The Lens MUST NOT wrap calls in `try/catch` that hide a dependency failure; an unexpected revert in a dependency propagates. (Source: Q-056, Q-119, Q-128)

### Positions

- `REQ-LENS-008` `predictProxy(wallet, marketId)` MUST read `spoke` from `MARKET_REGISTRY.getMarket(marketId)` and return `proxy = ADAPTER.proxyOf(wallet, marketId)` and `deployed = true` when that stored address is non-zero, and otherwise `proxy = PROXY_MANAGER.predictProxy(wallet, spoke, marketId)`, the CREATE2 address salted by `keccak256(abi.encode(wallet, spoke, marketId))`, and `deployed = false`. A wallet has one proxy per (CDP Spoke, market), created by the AaveAdapter on the wallet's first pledge to that market, so the returned address is the wallet's account in that market whether or not it exists yet. Proxies are immutable and the ProxyManager is immutable, so the stored and the predicted address are always equal; the Lens still prefers the stored binding because it is the account Aave and every Avana contract act on. Example: Alice pledged to market 1 (USDC/USDT on the Uniswap v3 Stable CDP Spoke) and not to market 2 on the same Spoke; `predictProxy(Alice, 1)` returns `(P1, true)` and `predictProxy(Alice, 2)` returns `(P2, false)` with `P2 ≠ P1`. (Source: Q-225, Q-223, Q-268, Q-306, Q-307)
- `REQ-LENS-009` `getAccount(wallet, marketId)` MUST return: wallet, `marketId`, its `spoke` and `hub`, proxy, `deployed`, `ADAPTER.borrowNonce(proxy)`; the Spoke's `UserAccountData` for the proxy (health factor WAD, total collateral value, total debt value, average collateral factor, risk premium, active collateral count, borrow count), which Aave computes over the account's one collateral reserve and every debt reserve with the account's own stored dynamic configuration keys, never the reserve's latest key; the RiskModule's `getAccountGateStatus(proxy)`; one `DebtPosition` per enabled debt reserve of the Spoke (`MarketRegistry.getDebtReserveIds(spoke)`, zero-debt reserves included) with reserve ID, asset, funding Hub, drawn and premium parts from `getUserDebt`, `total = getUserTotalDebt(reserveId, proxy)` in the asset's base units, `paused` (the Aave reserve is paused), `priceFresh` (the reserve's price is Live and within `borrowPriceMaxAge`, the stricter limit, so `true` means borrowing and liquidation can both use it; always `true` for a fixed $1.00 source such as GHO's) and `breakerReasons` (`RISK_MODULE.getDebtBreakerStatus(spoke, reserveId)`); and one `MarketPosition` for the market: vault token, reserve ID, supplied shares and assets, collateral enabled flag, the proxy's credited units (`Collateralization.getAccountCredited(proxy)`), the open record count (`getOpenRecordCount(proxy)`, at most `MAX_RECORDS_PER_ACCOUNT_MARKET` = 16), `shortfall = max(0, credited − suppliedShares)` (units seized on Aave and not yet reconciled by Collateralization), the market's price state from `REQ-LENS-015`, `breakerReasons` (`RISK_MODULE.getBreakerStatus(marketId)`) and `rescueUnits` (`Collateralization.getAccountRescueUnits(proxy)`, the units admitted as rescue top-ups that never support new debt, `Q-253`). The account also carries `walletFrozen = MARKET_REGISTRY.isFrozenAddress(wallet)`, `hubPauseState` and `hubPauseReason` of the market's Hub, and `breakerReasons`, the bitwise OR of the market's breaker reasons and those of every debt reserve with debt, which are the breakers its next borrow or withdraw-with-debt would meet (`Q-241`, `Q-242`). For an undeployed proxy it returns the predicted address with zero positions and every enabled debt reserve at zero debt. Example: Alice's account in market 1 (USDC/USDT, Uniswap v3 Stable CDP Spoke on Ethereum) holds 71,913.2 vault tokens (143,826.40 USD at 2.00 USD) and owes 40,456.119927 USDC on reserve 2 and nothing on the GHO reserve 0; `getAccount(Alice, 1)` returns `healthFactor = 0.9997e18` (`143,826.40 × 0.2812 / 40,456.119927`), two `DebtPosition` entries (reserve 0 total 0, reserve 2 total 40,456,119,927) and a `MarketPosition` with `credited = suppliedShares = 71,913,200,000`, `shortfall = 0`, `rescueUnits = 0` and `breakerReasons = 0`. Her separate account in market 2 has its own health factor and is not counted here. (Source: Q-076, Q-119, Q-128, Q-201, Q-225, Q-233, Q-234, Q-238, Q-241, Q-242, Q-253, Q-307)
- `REQ-LENS-010` `getAccount` loops once over the Spoke's enabled debt reserves and reads the account's one collateral reserve; per-account output is bounded by the Spoke's per-account reserve limit (`spokeUserReservesLimit`), which counts collateral and debt reserves. It MUST NOT loop over records. (Source: Q-193, Q-194, Q-234)
- `REQ-LENS-011` `getRecords(proxy, offset, limit)` MUST return at most `limit` records starting at position `offset` of Collateralization's seizure-list order for the proxy's account-market (largest-first at insertion, ties by earliest pledge; a partly seized record keeps its place), read with `Collateralization.getAccountRecords(proxy, offset, limit)`, each with `integrationId`, `positionKey`, depositor, credited units, live position liquidity from the market's LPVault, and `creditedAfterReconcile`: the credited units the record will have after Collateralization reconciles the account-market's `shortfall` (`REQ-LENS-009`), computed by debiting the shortfall from the head of the list in list order, each record losing `min(credited, remaining)`. It equals `Collateralization.previewRelease(position.integrationId, position.positionKey)` for the same block, and a borrower passes it as `units` to `AaveAdapter.withdraw`. While the Hub asset's added shares differ from its added assets Collateralization skips reconciliation ([2.3](03-collateralization.md)), and `creditedAfterReconcile` then equals `credited`. Computing it walks the list from the head only through the records the shortfall covers, the same records the reconciliation will touch. Records with zero credited units (fully seized and still open) are not in the seizure list and are not returned; `Collateralization.getRecord` reads them and `checkRecordBacking` includes them. An account-market holds at most 16 open records, so one page of 16 covers it. (Source: Q-134, Q-170, Q-194, Q-201, Q-240)

### Backing check

Why: Aave sizes the seizure from drawn shares and premium in ray, not from the asset amount the caller sends, and rounds the pulled debt up; a seizure computed from `debtToCover` alone differed from execution by the rounding of the shares and, once `debtToCover` is the whole debt (`REQ-LENS-028`), by the whole unused part. `expectedPayout` is the payout before swap-route fees, which only the routes know at execution; `recoveryFloor` is the least `R` any successful call has without a shortfall top-up; together they bound what a caller can expect. `expectedCallerLoss` replaces the removed remaining-capacity view `remainingToday` (`Q-397`, [2.13](13-settlement-budget.md) `SPEC-SET-005`): no Avana funds close a loss, so the caller sizes its own funding and top-up bound from it. **Example:** for Alice's USDC call (liquidation fee 0, drawn index 1 RAY, shares = assets, USDC debt at $0.9999 rounded here to $1.00), `c = ⌊4,990 × 1.00 × 1.02 / 2.00⌋ = 2,544.9` vault tokens (2,544,900,000 units), `expectedUnwound ≈ [2,544.9 USDC, 2,545.4 USDT]` at USDT $0.9998, `V = 5,089.80` USDC, `slippageBps = 100`, `recoveryFloor = ⌈5,089.80 × 0.99⌉ − 0.000002 − 1 = 5,037.901998` USDC; `G = S = 5,089.80 − 4,990 = 99.8`, `A = ⌊99.8 × 2,000 / 10⁴⌋ = 19.96`, `expectedPayout = 4,990 + 99.8 − 19.96 = 5,069.84` USDC and `expectedNetPayout = 79.84` USDC; `Rv ≥ D`, so `expectedCallerLoss = 0`. A keeper expecting a few USDC of swap-route cost sets `minPayout` near `4,990 + 72 = 5,062` USDC; through the public helper with `F = 4,990` USDC at 5 bps (`φ = 2.495` USDC) its net payout is 77.345 USDC before route costs and it needs no top-up. For the dust liquidation of [2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md) `REQ-SWAP-073`, `D = 3` USDC against `Rv = 2.95` USDC gives `expectedCallerLoss = 0.05` USDC, and a helper loan of 3 USDC at 5 bps needs a top-up bound of at least `0.0015 + 0.05 − 0 = 0.0515` USDC. Checked by: TEST-247. (Source: Q-085, Q-097, Q-190, Q-198, Q-232, Q-235, Q-256, Q-267, Q-268, Q-345, Q-397, Q-404)

### Cap headroom

- `REQ-LENS-018` `getCapHeadroom(marketId, depositor)` MUST report Avana USD headroom (8 decimals) as `max(0, cap − used)` for each of: the market regular budget (`marketUsdCap` minus the regular counter), the market top-up slice (`topUpSliceUsd` minus the slice counter), each pool token of the market under the market's Hub (`tokenUsdCap(hub, token)` minus the Hub-token counter, one entry per token in pool order), and the depositor under the Hub (`depositorUsdCap(hub)` minus the Hub-depositor counter), with counters read from Collateralization and cap values from the ParameterStore; the market's `minPledgeUsd` (8 decimals), the smallest pledge value `RiskModule.checkPledge` accepts, valued as `mulDiv(L, price8, 10^d, Floor)`; Avana's share of pool liquidity in the market's shape against the market's `poolShareMaxBps`; and the charge per whole vault token used for cap purposes, `max(oracle price, stressFloorUsd)`. Example: with `minPledgeUsd = 10e8` and the vault token at 2.00 USD, a pledge must carry at least 5 whole vault tokens of liquidity; a USDT/DAI market listed later on the same Hub as a USDC/USDT market reports the same USDT headroom, because both draw on `tokenUsdCap(hub, USDT)`. (Source: Q-055, Q-150, Q-157, Q-158, Q-174, Q-175, Q-197, Q-230, Q-240, Q-310)
- `REQ-LENS-019` `getCapHeadroom` MUST also report Aave headroom with the Hub's own formulas: vault-token `addHeadroom = max(0, addCap × 10^decimals − addedAssetsUp)` for the market's CDP Spoke on the market's Hub on the vault-token asset, where `addedAssetsUp = getSpokeAddedAssets` when the asset's added shares equal added assets and `getSpokeAddedAssets + 1` otherwise; and one `DrawHeadroom` per enabled debt reserve of that Spoke, on the funding Hub and Hub asset its `DebtReserveDef` names, `headroom = max(0, drawCap × 10^decimals − getSpokeTotalOwed − ⌈getSpokeDeficitRay / 1e27⌉)` in the debt asset's base units; a cap equal to `type(uint40).max` is reported as unlimited (`type(uint256).max`). Rounding never overstates headroom. The Lend Spoke's `addCap` (or the source Hub's `drawCap`) of each asset equals its credit line and the CDP Spokes' draw caps on an asset sum to no more than that line; both are raised only by Aave DAO governance, and Avana governance has no authority over them. (Source: Q-095, Q-119, Q-150, Q-175, Q-233)

### Coverage, breakers and freezes

- `REQ-LENS-034` `getTailCoverage(hub, debtAsset)` MUST return a `TailCoverageView` with the cap-based tail coverage of `SPEC-CAP-008` for that (Hub, debt asset) pair, computed in the same block: `coverageSpoke`, the `DebtReserveDef.coverageSpoke` of the enabled debt reserves whose asset is `debtAsset` and whose funding Hub is `hub` (one Coverage Spoke per (Hub, debt asset)), and `UnknownDebtAsset(debtAsset)` if no enabled debt reserve of any CDP Spoke (`MarketRegistry.getSpokes()`, `getDebtReserveIds`) draws it from `hub`; `funded = coverageSpoke.fundedBalance()` and `minimum = coverageSpoke.firstLossReserveMinimum()` in the asset's base units; `fundedUsd = ⌊funded × Pa / 10^da⌋` with `Pa` the asset's served Aave price (8 decimals, 10⁸ for GHO) and `da` its decimals; `drawCapUsd = Σ drawCap × Pa`, the sum over every CDP Spoke with an enabled reserve of the asset funded by `hub` of its `drawCap` on that Hub (whole units); when any `drawCap` is Aave's `MAX_ALLOWED_SPOKE_CAP`, `drawCapUsd = type(uint256).max` and the sum is not computed, so the view never overflows; for every pool token t other than `debtAsset` of a listed market whose debt is drawn from `hub` (`marketCount`, `getMarket`, `getToken`), `tail_t = min(⌈(tokenUsdCap(h, t) + Σ_{m∋t} topUpSliceUsd[m]) × CF_t / 10⁴⌉, drawCapUsd)`, summed over each home Hub `h` of those markets in USD with 8 decimals, where `CF_t` is the largest collateral factor (bps, the vault-token reserve's latest dynamic configuration on its Spoke) of a market holding t; `worstToken` the token with the largest `tail_t` (the first in listing order on a tie); `tailUsd = tail_worstToken`; and `ratioBps = ⌊fundedUsd × 10⁴ / tailUsd⌋`, or `type(uint256).max` when `tailUsd = 0`. Units: USD 8 decimals for `fundedUsd`, `tailUsd`; bps for the ratio and `CF_t`. Rounding: the tail rounds up and the funded value down, so the ratio never overstates coverage. The view loops over the MarketRegistry's Spokes, their enabled debt reserves and its markets, all bounded by governance listings. Why: the real tail of a token-pair market is one pool token failing, which takes every position holding it to near zero within hours; the most debt that tail can leave unbacked is the token's capped exposure, slices included, times the collateral factor (the most debt those positions carry before they become liquidatable, at health factor 1), and never more than the asset's draw caps. `SPEC-CAP-007` requires `tail_t ≤ firstLossReserveMinimum` as DAO guidance (`Q-249`), and this view gives monitoring and the DAO the live distance to it (monitoring alerts below 15,000 and 10,000 bps, `Q-244`). **Example:** for the Ethereum Stable LP Hub and GHO, `funded = 100,000` GHO (`fundedUsd = $100,000`), draw caps sum to 600,000 GHO; USDT has `tokenUsdCap = $300,000` and two markets with slices of $20,000 and $10,000, USDC `tokenUsdCap = $250,000` and one slice of $25,000, `CF_t = 3,120` bps for both: `tail_USDT = min($330,000 × 0.312, $600,000) = $102,960`, `tail_USDC = $275,000 × 0.312 = $85,800`, so `worstToken = USDT`, `tailUsd = 10,296,000,000,000` and `ratioBps = ⌊100,000 × 10⁴ / 102,960⌋ = 9,712`. If one Spoke's `drawCap` on GHO is `MAX_ALLOWED_SPOKE_CAP`, `drawCapUsd = type(uint256).max` and the tails are the capped exposures. Checked by: TEST-247. (Source: Q-249, Q-244)
- `REQ-LENS-037` `getBreakerStatus(marketId)` and `getDebtBreakerStatus(spoke, debtReserveId)` MUST return `RISK_MODULE.getBreakerStatus(marketId)` and `RISK_MODULE.getDebtBreakerStatus(spoke, debtReserveId)` unchanged: `reasons`, the bitwise OR of the conditions true in this block, the temporary gates still inside `gateRecoverySeconds` of their last recorded breach, and the latched security freezes (`BreakerReasons` bits: ORACLE 1, POOL_SHARE 4, ISSUER 8, DEFICIT 32, CONFIG_DRIFT, POOL_DEPTH, MANUAL 512; the bits of the removed PRICE_DROP, COVERAGE, BUDGET, SEIZURE_SURGE and ROUTE_FAILURES reasons are never set, `Q-399`), and `reopensAt`: for temporary gates `lastBreachAt + gateRecoverySeconds`, `type(uint64).max` while a security freeze is latched (it reopens only by an authorized reset), 0 when nothing blocks. `isFrozenAddress(account)` MUST return `MARKET_REGISTRY.isFrozenAddress(account)`, the chain-wide freeze that applies under every Hub. `getAccount` carries the same values per market, per debt reserve and for the wallet (`REQ-LENS-009`). Why: a tripped breaker or a frozen address blocks only new activity (borrow, withdraw-with-debt, non-rescue top-ups; for a frozen address also liquidation calls it makes as a keeper), never repay, debt-free exits, rescue top-ups or liquidation (`Q-241`, `Q-242`); the app shows which action is closed and why, and monitoring alerts on every reason (`Q-244`). Price drops, seizure surges, swap-route failures and a Coverage Spoke below its minimum are monitoring alerts with no breaker bit. Example: Avana's share of a pool reaches 10.4 % at 09:00 and `pokeBreakers` records it; at 09:20 the share is 9.6 % and `getBreakerStatus(1)` returns `(4, 09:50)` while the 30-minute recovery runs; a 10,500 GHO deficit on the GHO reserve returns `(32, type(uint64).max)` from `getDebtBreakerStatus` until the pause guardian's reset; the borrower's repay and debt-free withdrawal stay open throughout. Checked by: TEST-228, TEST-229. (Source: Q-241, Q-242, Q-244, Q-399)

### Borrow, repay and collateral previews
