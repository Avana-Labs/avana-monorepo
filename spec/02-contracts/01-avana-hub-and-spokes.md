# 2.1 Avana Hub and Spokes

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-10
>
> **Requirement prefix:** `REQ-HUB-NNN` · **Formula prefix:** `SPEC-HUB-NNN`

## Purpose

Avana runs on Aave V4 contracts that the Aave DAO owns, on each chain it is deployed to: one or more Avana Hubs (Stable LP, Correlated LP, Volatile LP; at launch a Stable LP Hub on Ethereum and on Base); the CDP Spokes, one standard Aave V4 Spoke per (chain, Hub, AMM protocol, risk tier) with its AaveOracle, each listing several markets as collateral reserves ("Uniswap v3 Stable" on Ethereum and "Aerodrome Slipstream Stable" on Base at launch); one supply-only Lend Spoke per chain whose reserves point to (Hub, asset) pairs and can list any asset, carrying each credit line (GHO first); and the Hub-side registration of one Avana Umbrella (Coverage Spoke) per (Hub, debt asset), each holding that pair's First-Loss Reserve. Avana Umbrella is Avana's protocol safety module, named after Aave's Umbrella because that design inspired it (`Q-402`). Every Hub, Spoke, AaveOracle and configurator is unmodified Aave V4; there is no liquidation-manager variant. Liquidation is exclusive to LPVaultSwap because the vault token accepts seized collateral only to LPVaultSwap, and three settings that the listing AIP sets and Avana's drift check enforces on every Avana collateral reserve keep that rule the only gate: `receiveSharesEnabled = false`, `liquidationFee = 0` (or paid to Avana's fee receiver) and no reinvestment controller ([CDP Spoke implementation](#cdp-spoke-implementation)). A credit line is a draw cap per (Hub, asset, Spoke); each CDP Spoke has a home Hub and each debt reserve records its funding Hub; cross-Hub credit lines are fully specified and off at launch ([Debt reserves](#debt-reserves)). Avana writes no code for any of these contracts. Avana requires the configuration below: every Hub and Spoke flag and value Avana depends on, the exact semantics of each Aave function Avana contracts call, the effect of Aave-side pauses, freezes and halts, the drift-check list the RiskModule re-checks, and the split of responsibilities between the Aave DAO and Avana Labs.

Not covered here: the Coverage Spoke contract itself ([2.14 First-Loss Reserve](14-first-loss-reserve.md)); the PositionProxy that calls the Spoke ([2.5](05-position-proxy-and-proxy-manager.md)) on the AaveAdapter's instruction ([2.6](06-aave-adapter.md)); the RiskModule that runs the drift checks ([2.7](07-risk-module.md)); the MarketRegistry's records of Spokes and debt reserves ([2.8](08-market-registry.md)); the USD caps that feed the Hub `addCap` ([2.9](09-parameter-store.md)); the LPOracle feed of each market and the DebtOracle of each non-GHO debt asset, which AaveOracle reads ([2.10](10-lp-oracle.md)); LPVaultSwap, the only liquidation caller ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)). Invariants this configuration must preserve (1:1 vault-token backing, Hub shares equal assets for vault tokens, repay liveness, no seizure outside LPVaultSwap) are defined in [4.1 Global Invariants](../04-security/01-global-invariants.md).

## Inputs

- From the Aave DAO: deployment and listing AIP payloads executed through `HubConfigurator`, `SpokeConfigurator` and the Hub and Spoke `restricted` functions; each (Hub, asset) credit line, supplied by the DAO treasury through the chain's Lend Spoke, with the CDP Spokes' `drawCap`s on it; refresh payloads executed by the DAO's `SPOKE_USER_POSITION_UPDATER_ROLE`.
- From Avana Labs: the proposed value of every Hub and Spoke setting below (per market, per debt reserve and per Spoke), the market listing file with the collateral-factor computation, the list of every PositionProxy (from Ponder) for refresh payloads, and funding of each Coverage Spoke in its own asset; LPVaultSwap also adds to the Coverage Spoke of a market's debt reserve (its funding Hub and asset), through its public `fund`, the debt asset it recovers when it settles a pending-settlement balance (`Q-211`, `Q-236`, `Q-284`).
- From the Aave V4 risk service providers, in each market's listing payload: `poolRiskFactor` (within its registry bound), the USD caps `marketUsdCap`, `tokenUsdCap`, `depositorUsdCap` and `minPledgeUsd`; in each debt reserve's listing payload (`enableDebtReserve`): its funding Hub and credit line, its `drawCap` within the asset's Aave V3 Pool flash liquidity (which the flash liquidation helper borrows) and route depth, and the asset's `debtStressRiseBps`.
- From the risk reviewer: each token's Core Hub collateral factor, `ghoLegCollateralFactorBps`, `collateralRisk`, `maxLiquidationBonus`, `healthFactorForMaxBonus`, `liquidationBonusFactor`; `topUpSliceUsd` and `stressFloorUsd` come from the parameter registry.
- From Avana contracts at run time: PositionProxy calls to `supply`, `setUsingAsCollateral`, `borrow`, `repay`, `withdraw`, each made on the AaveAdapter's instruction; LPVaultSwap calls to `liquidationCall`; view calls from the AaveAdapter, RiskModule, Collateralization, MarketRegistry, LPVaultSwap and the Lens.
- From AaveOracle: prices from each market's LPOracle feed (`latestAnswer()`, 8 decimals), Aave's fixed GHO price source for the GHO reserve and the DebtOracle of every other debt reserve (`Q-248`); under a failing price both oracles serve the conservative prices of [Aave valuation under failing prices](#aave-valuation-under-failing-prices) (`Q-247`).

## Outputs

- Hub state: liquidity of each debt asset, drawn and premium debt per Spoke and asset, deficits per Spoke and asset, vault-token added shares per Spoke.
- Spoke state: each proxy's supplied vault-token shares, collateral flags, drawn shares and premium per debt reserve, stored dynamic-config keys, risk premium, health factor.
- Token movements initiated by Spoke and Hub calls: vault tokens proxy → Hub on `supply`; vault tokens Hub → proxy on `withdraw`; debt asset funding Hub → proxy on `borrow`; debt asset proxy → Hub on `repay`; vault tokens Hub → LPVaultSwap and debt asset LPVaultSwap → Hub on `liquidationCall`.
- Aave events (see [Events](#events)) that Ponder indexes and Avana monitoring reads.

## Permissions

Aave-side authority (these are Aave DAO AccessManager roles, listed in the Aave-side roles table of [1.4 Permissions and Roles](../01-architecture/04-permissions-and-roles.md); Avana's own roles and timelock are defined in [2.15](15-governance-and-timelock.md)):

- **Aave DAO** (governance executor): owns every Hub, CDP Spoke, Lend Spoke and AaveOracle on each chain; holds every `HubConfigurator` and `SpokeConfigurator` role; is the only holder of `SPOKE_USER_POSITION_UPDATER_ROLE` on each CDP Spoke; sets the three required settings in every listing AIP; grants `HUB_DEFICIT_ELIMINATOR_ROLE` on each Hub only to the Coverage Spokes registered on it.
- **Aave Protocol Guardian**: on Aave, only the one-way `SpokeConfigurator.freezeReserve` and `pauseReserve` selectors on each CDP Spoke; unfreezing and unpausing stay with the DAO. On Avana it holds `APPLICATION_PAUSE_GUARDIAN`, `APPLICATION_FREEZE_GUARDIAN` and `CANCELLER_ROLE` ([2.15](15-governance-and-timelock.md)).
- **GHO Reserve / DAO treasury**: supplies and withdraws the Lend Spoke position of each (Hub, asset).
- **Avana Labs and every Avana contract**: no role on the Hub, the Spokes, AaveOracle or the configurators. Avana's timelock has no authority over any value here. PositionProxies act on the Spoke only as `onBehalfOf == msg.sender`; LPVaultSwap holds no Aave role either: it is the only address that can complete `liquidationCall` because it is the only seizure recipient the vault token accepts.

## Formulas

- `SPEC-HUB-001` — **LP collateral factor.** `CF_bps = floor(min(CF0_bps, CF1_bps) × prf_bps / 10_000)`, where `CF0_bps`, `CF1_bps` (and `CFi_bps` for each further token of an n-token pool) are each pool token's Aave V4 Core Hub collateral factor in BPS (`tokenCollateralFactorBps`), except that a GHO leg uses the explicit `ghoLegCollateralFactorBps` from the listing file (never the Core Hub GHO value), and `prf_bps` is `poolRiskFactor` in BPS, set per pool by the Aave V4 risk service providers in the listing payload. Units: BPS, stored as `uint16`. Rounding: down, so `CF_bps ≤ min(CF0, CF1) × prf / 10_000` and at most 1 bps below it. Bounds: `0 < CF0_bps, CF1_bps < 10_000`, `0 < prf_bps ≤ 10_000` within `poolRiskFactor`'s registry bound; the product is below `1e8`, no overflow. Constraints: `0 < CF_bps < 10_000` and `SPEC-HUB-005`. The pool risk factor is applied only here; LPOracle reports the unhaircut value. The risk reviewer takes `CF0` and `CF1` from each token's Core Hub collateral factor at listing, records the source in the listing file and recalibrates manually; there is no automatic link to the Core Hub. (Source: Q-020, Q-028, Q-072, Q-119, Q-186, Q-188, Q-197)
  - Why: an LP position is only as good as its weakest leg, so the smaller token CF bounds it; `poolRiskFactor` then discounts for what an LP position adds on top of its tokens (price-path loss inside the pool, pool manipulation, and the cost of unwinding liquidity during a liquidation). Rounding down means Aave never lends more than the computed factor allows. A GHO leg uses an explicit factor because the Core Hub does not list GHO as collateral at a usable value.
  - Example: a USDC/USDT pool with `CF0_bps = 7_800` (USDC), `CF1_bps = 7_500` (USDT) and `poolRiskFactor = 3_750` bps gives `7_500 × 3_750 / 10_000 = 2_812.5`, stored as `CF_bps = 2_812`. At the registry ceiling `poolRiskFactor = 4_000` with both legs at 7,800 bps the result is exactly 3,120 bps (31.2%).
  - Checked by: MATH-AR-01, SYM-01, SYM-02a–d, SYM-03, SYM-07, SIM-08A.
- `SPEC-HUB-002` — **Hub `addCap` per vault-token asset.** `addCap = ceil((marketUsdCap + topUpSliceUsd) / stressFloorUsd)`, with `marketUsdCap` and `topUpSliceUsd` in USD with 8 decimals and `stressFloorUsd` in USD with 8 decimals per whole vault token, all read from the ParameterStore; the result is in whole vault tokens (`uint40`) and is set on the CDP Spoke's `SpokeConfig` for that asset. Rounding: up. Correctness: every pledge and top-up is booked for cap purposes at a price of at least `stressFloorUsd`, so the units Avana admits never exceed `(marketUsdCap + topUpSliceUsd) / stressFloorUsd`; with Hub shares equal to assets (`REQ-HUB-075`) the Hub check `addCap × 10^decimals ≥ toAddedAssetsUp(addedShares) + amount` compares exact units, so a pledge or top-up within its Avana budget always fits under `addCap` (Aave pause, freeze and halt still apply). A tightening of `marketUsdCap` or `topUpSliceUsd` leaves existing bookings in place (`REQ-RISK-042`), so `addCap` is never set below `ceil((max(marketUsdCap, regularUsed) + max(topUpSliceUsd, sliceUsed)) / stressFloorUsd)` ([2.7](07-risk-module.md) `SPEC-CAP-006`); at listing the counters are 0 and this is the formula above. Bounds: `addCap < MAX_ALLOWED_SPOKE_CAP` (finite); `addCap × 10^decimals < 2^120` for decimals 6–18; cap counters ≤ `2^128`. The RiskModule checks the USD caps only at pledge, and the Hub checks `addCap` on every supply, so the two layers bound the same collateral in USD and in units. (Source: Q-054, Q-055, Q-066, Q-070, Q-119, Q-150, Q-157, Q-175, Q-197, Q-240)
  - Why: Avana's caps are in USD while the Hub counts vault-token units. Converting the USD budget at the lowest price Avana ever books (`stressFloorUsd`) gives the largest unit count Avana can admit, so the Hub never rejects a pledge Avana accepted; and if the upgradeable RiskModule or ParameterStore were replaced by a hostile implementation, the DAO-owned `addCap` still bounds the units that can reach the Hub. Sizing at the current price instead would let a later price drop push admitted units above the Hub cap (the MATH-UO-28a counterexample).
  - Example: `marketUsdCap = $5,000,000` (`5_000_000 × 10^8`), `topUpSliceUsd` at 10% of it, `$500,000`, and `stressFloorUsd = $1.00` (`10^8`) give `addCap = ceil(5_500_000 × 10^8 / 10^8) = 5,500,000` whole vault tokens. A market listed with `stressFloorUsd = $1.30` would get `ceil(5_500_000 / 1.30) = ceil(4,230,769.23) = 4,230,770`.
  - Checked by: MATH-UO-28 (28b proves the sizing; 28a is the counterexample for sizing at the current price), MATH-UO-03, MATH-UO-29, MATH-AR-26.
- `SPEC-HUB-003` — **Credit-line caps per (Hub, asset, Spoke).** For each debt asset `a` on each funding Hub `h` of a chain: `LendSpoke.addCap(h, a) = creditLine(h, a)`; `Σ_s drawCap(h, a, s) ≤ creditLine(h, a)` over every CDP Spoke `s` of the chain that draws (h, a), whatever its home Hub; `drawCap(h, a, s) ≤ crossHubLineCap(h, a, s)` for each Spoke `s` with `home(s) ≠ h`, so `Σ_{s : home(s) ≠ h} drawCap(h, a, s) ≤ crossHubLineTotal(h, a) = Σ_s crossHubLineCap(h, a, s) ≤ coverageBalance(h, a)` (0 at launch, since cross-Hub lines are off); for every debt reserve `drawCap(h, a, s) ≤ min(flashLiquidity(a), routeDepth(a))` measured at listing; `CDPSpoke_s.addCap(h, a) = 0`; `LendSpoke.drawCap(h, a) = 0`; `CoverageSpoke_{h,a}.drawCap = 0`. The Hub admits a draw only if `drawCap_s × 10^dec(a) ≥ drawn_s + premium_s + ceil(deficitRay_s / RAY) + amount`, so accrued interest, premium and uneliminated deficits consume draw capacity. The cap sums are checked by the MarketRegistry's register per (funding Hub, asset) in `enableDebtReserve` and in every cap change, and by the drift check; they are configuration invariants on caps, while drawn amounts are bounded per draw by the Hub's gate (interest can leave a Spoke above its cap, and only repayment, liquidation and coverage lower it). Units: whole asset units in `SpokeConfig` (`uint40`), asset base units (`dec(a)` decimals: 18 for GHO and WETH, 6 for USDC and USDT) and RAY (1e27) inside the Hub. Rounding: Hub debt values round up against the Spoke. Bounds: `(2^40 − 1) × 10^dec(a)` fits every Hub cap check without overflow.
  - Why: each credit line is the lender's exposure to Avana in that asset on that Hub. Summing over every Spoke that draws the line, not only the Spokes homed on the funding Hub, stops caps on one Hub's asset summing above its line when another Hub's Spoke draws it; capping foreign draws by the funding Hub's coverage keeps a foreign Spoke's losses within that Hub's first-loss capital; and bounding each cap by flash liquidity keeps every borrowed amount liquidatable in one transaction by a caller that holds no debt asset and borrows it through the flash liquidation helper. Capping drawn debt plus premium plus uncovered deficit keeps everything Avana borrowers owe inside the line, and a recorded deficit keeps occupying it until the Coverage Spoke eliminates it.
  - Example (illustrative; each credit line and draw cap is set in the funding ARFC, `Q-392`, `Q-394`): on Ethereum, `creditLine(Stable, GHO) = 2,000,000`: the Lend Spoke `addCap` is 2,000,000 and the single CDP Spoke "Uniswap v3 Stable" has `drawCap` 2,000,000. If that Spoke has 1,900,000 GHO drawn, 3,000 GHO premium and a 475 GHO uncovered deficit, a 100,000 GHO borrow needs `2,003,475 ≤ 2,000,000` and reverts with `DrawCapExceeded`; the largest borrow that passes is 96,525 GHO. Cross-Hub case (when enabled): Stable USDC line 20; Stable Spokes S1 = 8, S2 = 7, Volatile Spoke S3 = 5 with `crossHubLineCap(Stable, USDC, S3) = 5`. Raising S1 to 13 reverts (`13 + 7 + 5 = 25 > 20`), and setting S3's line to 0 closes it at once: S3's existing debt can still be repaid or liquidated, and every new S3 draw reverts `CrossHubLineClosed`.
  - Checked by: QNT-20, QNT-31, QNT-TOPO-05.
- `SPEC-HUB-004` — **Risk premium threshold.** For each CDP Spoke on each debt asset: `riskPremiumThreshold_bps ≥ max_r collateralRisk_r + 1` over every collateral reserve of that Spoke, with `riskPremiumThreshold_bps < MAX_RISK_PREMIUM_THRESHOLD` (the threshold check stays active). The Hub requires after every premium change (`restore`, `refreshPremium`, `reportDeficit`) `premiumShares_s ≤ percentMulUp(drawnShares_s, riskPremiumThreshold)`, where `premiumShares_s = Σ_i percentMulUp(drawnShares_i, riskPremium_i)` and each user's `riskPremium_i = ceil(Σ w·collateralRisk / Σ w) ≤ max_r collateralRisk_r`. With the 1 bps margin the check passes whenever every indebted user holds at least 9,999 drawn shares of that asset, or in aggregate when `Σ_i drawnShares_i × (T − riskPremium_i) ≥ 9,999 × (number of indebted users)`. Units: BPS (`uint24`); `collateralRisk ≤ 1000_00`. Rounding: premium shares up (`percentMulUp`), user premium up (`divUp`). The threshold is never lowered while debt in that asset exists, and a fork test proves repay and liquidation pass at the configured values. (Source: Q-111, Q-141, Q-178, Q-233)
  - Why: if the Hub's premium check fails, `repay`, `borrow`, `withdraw` and `liquidationCall` all revert with `InvalidPremiumChange`, which would freeze every Avana account holding that debt asset on the Spoke. A threshold equal to the top collateral risk is not enough, because each user's premium shares round up separately while the threshold applies once to the Spoke total; the extra 1 bps absorbs up to one share of rounding per user.
  - Example: markets with `collateralRisk` 1,000 and 1,500 bps need `riskPremiumThreshold ≥ 1,501`. A user with 5,000 GHO of drawn shares (`5,000 × 10^18`) at premium 1,500 bps holds `percentMulUp = 750 × 10^18` premium shares, and the threshold allows `750.5 × 10^18`, so the check passes. Two users each holding 1 drawn share at premium 1,500 would hold 1 premium share each (2 in total) against a threshold allowance of `ceil(2 × 1,501 / 10,000) = 1`, which fails. An account can still hold a single drawn share after a repay or liquidation (there is no minimum debt, `Q-381`); the aggregate form still holds because the other accounts' slack absorbs it: one 1,000 GHO account at the 1 bps margin covers more than 10^16 one-share accounts.
  - Checked by: MATH-AR-20, MATH-AR-21, MATH-AR-22, MATH-AR-23, MATH-AR-24, MATH-AR-25, SYM-52, SYM-54.
- `SPEC-HUB-005` — **Liquidation configuration bounds.** (a) Every vault-reserve key satisfies `percentMulUp(maxLiquidationBonus_bps, CF_bps) < 10_000` (Aave rejects the key otherwise; the pre-proposal fork check asserts it). (b) `minLB_bps = 10_000 + floor((maxLiquidationBonus_bps − 10_000) × liquidationBonusFactor_bps / 10_000)` and `minLB_bps − 10_000 ≥ minLiquidationBonus`; the bonus at health factor `h` (WAD) is `maxLiquidationBonus` if `h ≤ healthFactorForMaxBonus`, else `minLB + floor((maxLB − minLB) × (1e18 − h) / (1e18 − healthFactorForMaxBonus))`, so `minLB ≤ bonus ≤ maxLB` and the bonus a keeper earns as an account first crosses HF 1 is `minLB`. (c) `targetHealthFactor = aaveTargetHealthFactor` (WAD) and `targetHealthFactor − maxLB × CF > 0`, which (a) guarantees because `targetHealthFactor ≥ 1e18`. Units: BPS and WAD. Rounding: down. All markets on one Spoke share its liquidation curve; the risk reviewer sets the curve and each reserve's CF and maximum bonus, and the keeper model uses V4's dynamic bonus. (Source: Q-072, Q-098, Q-119, Q-179, Q-185, Q-404)
  - Why: (a) means an account at HF 1 holds collateral worth more than its debt times the maximum bonus, so seizing the bonus-inclusive amount never needs more collateral than exists when an account first becomes liquidatable. (b) sets the bonus at the moment liquidation starts high enough to pay the caller's gas, the swap cost of the unwound tokens into the debt asset and Avana's fee share, with room for the premium a caller pays when it borrows the debt asset through the flash liquidation helper; if it did not, no keeper would act until the account was deeper under water. (c) makes Aave size each liquidation toward a defined post-liquidation health factor rather than Aave's 1.00 default.
  - Example: with `CF_bps = 3_120`, `maxLiquidationBonus = 10_500` (5%), `liquidationBonusFactor = 4_000` and `healthFactorForMaxBonus = 0.90`: (a) `ceil(10_500 × 3_120 / 10_000) = 3_276 < 10_000`; (b) `minLB = 10_000 + floor(500 × 4_000 / 10_000) = 10_200`, a 2% bonus that meets `minLiquidationBonus = 200`; at `h = 0.97` the bonus is `10_200 + floor(300 × 0.03 / 0.10) = 10_290`; (c) `targetHealthFactor = 1.10`. These values are illustrative: `aaveTargetHealthFactor` 1.10 and `minLiquidationBonus` ≥ 2 % are Avana's recommended guidance for the listing AIP, and the Aave DAO decides them (`Q-393`).
  - Checked by: MATH-AR-02, MATH-AR-09, MATH-AR-10, SYM-06a, SYM-06b, SYM-15a–d, SYM-15e, SYM-44a–b, SIM-00A.
- `SPEC-HUB-006` — **Listing margin.** For every price route `r` (primary, alternate and standby) of either pool token, `10_000 − CF_bps > dev(r) + drift(r)`, where `dev(r)` is the sum of `feedDeviationThresholdBps` and `drift(r)` the sum of `heartbeatDriftBps` over the feeds composing `r`; `feedDeviationThresholdBps` is each Chainlink feed's published deviation threshold and `heartbeatDriftBps` the largest price move within one heartbeat that the risk reviewer publishes for that feed. Units: integer BPS. Rounding: none (comparison of published integers). Why: a feed can be off by up to its deviation threshold plus the drift within one heartbeat before it updates, and that error must stay inside the collateral buffer `1 − CF`; the standby is included because a guardian can put it in force at once, without a new review. The same inequality is the RiskModule's `SPEC-CF-004` and is checked by the ParameterStore when it writes the market's parameters and by the MarketRegistry at activation and when `setPriceSource` changes a route of a listed token. Example: `CF_bps = 3,000`; the USDC primary USDC/USD (deviation 25, drift 25) gives 50, the alternate USDC/ETH × ETH/USD (deviations 100 + 50, drifts 100 + 100) gives 350, and a standby second USDC/USD aggregator (25, 25) gives 50; `7,000 > 350` holds for all three routes. Checked by: TEST-139. (Source: Q-113, Q-240, Q-248)
  - Why: a Chainlink feed can sit up to its deviation threshold away from the market without updating, and the market can move further before the next heartbeat. Collateral factor headroom `1 − CF` must exceed that worst unreported error on every route the oracle may serve, including the alternate it fails over to and the standby a guardian can switch to, or an account could read as healthy while its true collateral no longer covers its debt.
  - Example: for the USDC/USDT market with `CF_bps = 2_812` the headroom is 7,188 bps. The USDC primary route (USDC/USD, 25 bps deviation, 50 bps drift) needs 75 bps; a USDT alternate route through USDT/ETH (100 bps deviation, 100 bps drift) and ETH/USD (50 bps deviation, 100 bps drift) needs `150 + 200 = 350` bps. Both are below 7,188, so the market can be listed.
  - Checked by: the listing fork check of the MarketRegistry and ParameterStore; no spec-stage model covers this inequality.
- `SPEC-HUB-007` — **GHO borrow rate.** The GHO asset's strategy is flat: `drawnRate = baseRate` with both slopes 0, so the rate does not depend on utilization, and `baseRate ≥` the Aave V4 Core Hub GHO base rate at the time each AIP executes. A borrower's debt grows as `drawnShares × drawnIndex` plus premium accruing at `drawnRate × riskPremium_user / 10_000` on drawn debt (premium does not compound on itself); the effective rate at refresh is `baseRate × (1 + riskPremium_user / 10_000)`. Units: annual rate as the `AssetInterestRateStrategy` encodes it; index in RAY. Rounding: Aave's. The floor on `baseRate` is not enforced on-chain: the DAO owns the strategy and keeps it at or above the Core Hub GHO rate plus Aave's risk premium through its parameter process, and Avana monitoring alerts when it falls below. Every other debt asset's rate strategy is set by the Aave DAO in the AIP that lists it (on its funding Hub), with the same premium mechanics; Avana adds no interest fee on any asset (`Q-231`). (Source: Q-096, Q-119, Q-231, Q-233)
  - Why: the credit line is a fixed GHO allocation, not a market where utilization should set the price, so a flat rate gives borrowers a predictable cost. Keeping it at or above the Core Hub rate stops borrowers from using Avana as cheaper GHO than Aave's main market, and the per-user premium charges riskier collateral more.
  - Example: with the Core Hub GHO base rate at 5.00%, the DAO sets `baseRate = 5.50%`. A borrower of 100,000 GHO whose collateral carries `riskPremium = 1_500` bps pays about 5,500 GHO of drawn interest and 825 GHO of premium over one year (effective `5.50% × 1.15 = 6.325%`), before compounding of the drawn index.
  - Checked by: SYM-50, SYM-51a, SYM-52, SYM-53a–c, SIM-07A.
- `SPEC-HUB-008` — **Rounding of the Aave calls Avana uses.** `supply`: shares `= floor(amount × (S + 1e6) / (A + 1e6))`, exactly `amount` while A = S. `withdraw`: amount `= min(requested, previewRemoveByShares(suppliedShares))` (down), shares removed `= ceil(amount × (S + 1e6) / (A + 1e6))`. `borrow`: drawn shares `= ceil(amount × RAY / drawnIndex)`, so recorded debt ≥ amount. `repay`: premium first, amount capped at total debt, restored drawn shares `= floor(drawnRestored × RAY / drawnIndex)`, debt asset pulled `= drawnRestored + ceil(premiumRestoredRay / RAY)`. `getUserTotalDebt = ceil(drawnShares × drawnIndex / RAY) + ceil(premiumRay / RAY)`. Health factor `= floor(Σ(CF_bps × value_i) × 1e14 × RAY / Σ debtValueRay)` in WAD, with collateral value from `previewRemoveByShares` (down), each debt reserve valued at its own Aave price and decimals, and `type(uint256).max` when debt is 0. `liquidationCall`: collateral seized rounds down, debt asset pulled rounds up, and the seized value ≤ debt repaid × bonus. Every rounding is against the account and every amount is in the debt asset's own base units (18 decimals for GHO, 6 for USDC). Bounds: shares and index `< 2^120`, prices `< 2^64`, vault decimals 6–18. The repay path reads the debt during the call and moves exactly `min(maxRepaySpend, debt)` from the payer to the proxy through the AaveAdapter, and the proxy approves the Spoke for that amount only. (Source: Q-068, Q-072, Q-075, Q-119, Q-124, Q-144, Q-160, Q-180, Q-223, Q-233)
  - Why: rounding every conversion against the account means no sequence of small calls can extract value from the Hub, and the Hub's books stay solvent. For Avana it also fixes how repay-all must work: the debt of each reserve has to be read inside the same call with `getUserTotalDebt`, because a figure computed off-chain or in an earlier block is short by accrued interest and rounding.
  - Example: borrowing 10,000 GHO at `drawnIndex = 1.05 RAY` records `ceil(10_000 × 10^18 / 1.05) = 9,523,809,523,809,523,809,524` drawn shares, and `getUserTotalDebt` in the same block returns `ceil(9,523,809,523,809,523,809,524 × 1.05) = 10,000,000,000,000,000,000,001` wei: 1 wei above the amount received, which a repay-all pays.
  - Checked by: MATH-AR-03, MATH-AR-05, MATH-AR-07, MATH-AR-12, MATH-AR-16, MATH-AR-26, MATH-ST-37, MATH-ST-41.

### Vault-token reserves

- `REQ-HUB-028` — Each vault-token reserve MUST be created by `Spoke.addReserve` with `borrowable = false`, `receiveSharesEnabled = false`, `paused = false` and `frozen = false` set in that same call. `receiveSharesEnabled = false` is load-bearing on the standard Spoke: it is the setting that makes `liquidationCall(…, receiveShares = true)` revert in Aave's own validation, closing the one liquidation path that moves no vault token and so would bypass the vault token's transfer rule. `borrowable = false` and the Hub-side `drawCap = 0` (`REQ-HUB-013`) together keep vault tokens from ever being lent out. - `REQ-HUB-029` — Every dynamic-config key of every vault-token reserve MUST have `liquidationFee = 0` (or a fee paid to Avana's `feeRecipient`, which the listing AIP states), `collateralFactor` from `SPEC-HUB-001` and greater than 0, and `maxLiquidationBonus` set by the risk reviewer within `SPEC-HUB-005`. A non-zero fee paid elsewhere would move vault-token shares with `payFeeShares` and no token transfer, so Collateralization's debit would no longer match the share drop; any other value is drift. (Source: Q-020, Q-068, Q-119, Q-281)
- `REQ-HUB-030` — Each vault-token reserve's `collateralRisk` MUST be set by the risk reviewer in BPS and MUST be at most `riskPremiumThreshold − 1` of its CDP Spoke on every debt asset (`SPEC-HUB-004`). (Source: Q-119, Q-141, Q-178, Q-233)
- `REQ-HUB-031` — Each vault token MUST be listed as a reserve on exactly one Spoke across its Hub, its CDP Spoke `vaultToken.spoke()`, and the vault-token Hub asset MUST have exactly that one Spoke with non-zero caps (plus the fee receiver at zero caps); a second Spoke on the asset could draw vault tokens out of the Hub and leave proxy withdrawals and seizures unable to settle, so a second listing added by the DAO is caught by the drift checks `REQ-HUB-070` and `REQ-HUB-071`, and the Adapter and LPVaultSwap take the Spoke only from `vaultToken.spoke()`. (Source: Q-017, Q-032, Q-172, Q-281)

### Liquidation configuration

- `REQ-HUB-036` — Each CDP Spoke MUST have one `LiquidationConfig` shared by all its markets, with `targetHealthFactor = aaveTargetHealthFactor` set explicitly (never the 1.00 default); a market that needs a different target or bonus curve MUST get its own Spoke. Avana recommends a target of 1.10 and a `minLiquidationBonus` of at least 2 % in the listing AIP; the Aave DAO decides both, and the ParameterStore records the values the DAO set. (Source: Q-119, Q-185, Q-393)
- `REQ-HUB-037` — The risk reviewer MUST set `healthFactorForMaxBonus`, `liquidationBonusFactor` and each reserve's `maxLiquidationBonus` so that `SPEC-HUB-005` holds. The bonus just below HF 1 MUST cover the swap legs from the unwound tokens into each debt asset and leave room for a flash premium up to `maxFlashFeeBps` (paid by a caller using the flash liquidation helper, or by a borrower's `deleverage`), and that is checked on-chain: the liveness inequality `minLB × (10⁴ − slippageAllowanceMinBps(m)) ≥ 10⁴ × (10⁴ + maxFlashFeeBps)` of [2.9](09-parameter-store.md) `REQ-PSTORE-021`, stronger than the `minLB × (10⁴ − s) ≥ 10⁸` a liquidation alone needs because LPVaultSwap takes no flash loan ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md) `SPEC-LIQ-016`), gates `enableDebtReserve` and `activateMarket` for every market of the Spoke and every ParameterStore change of its terms, and an Aave change that lowers `minLB` after activation is drift (`REQ-HUB-108`) that stops new borrowing while liquidation continues. The listing fork check also asserts that the bonus covers keeper gas and Avana's share. Example: on a Spoke with `maxLiquidationBonus = 10,500` and `liquidationBonusFactor = 4,000` (`minLB = 10,200`) and `maxFlashFeeBps = 0`, a market with `slippageAllowanceMinBps` up to 196 passes, since `10,200 × (10⁴ − 196) = 10,200 × 9,804 = 100,000,800 ≥ 10⁸`; at 197 bps `10,200 × 9,803 = 99,990,600 < 10⁸` and `activateMarket` reverts `LivenessBound`. Checked by: TEST-245. (Source: Q-072, Q-098, Q-119, Q-179, Q-235, Q-261, Q-270, Q-282, Q-404)
- `REQ-HUB-038` — Every vault-token liquidation MUST go through the standard Aave V4 5-argument `liquidationCall(collateralReserveId, debtReserveId, user, debtToCover, receiveShares)` called by LPVaultSwap with `receiveShares = false`, one collateral reserve against one debt reserve per call. The CDP Spoke has no liquidation-manager code; exclusivity comes from the vault token's transfer rule: Aave's liquidation has the Hub send the seized vault tokens to `msg.sender`, and the vault token accepts a seizure transfer only to LPVaultSwap, so any other caller's `liquidationCall` reverts on that transfer. The receive-shares path, which moves no token, is closed by `receiveSharesEnabled = false` (`REQ-HUB-028`); fee shares, which also move no token, are closed by `liquidationFee = 0` (`REQ-HUB-029`); a reinvestment controller, which could move the Hub's vault tokens, is never set. These three settings are set in the listing AIP and enforced by the drift check, whose breaker stops new activity and never liquidation. Avana's one liquidation entry point, `LPVaultSwap.liquidate`, open to any address that is not frozen, directly or through a flash liquidation helper, runs inside LPVaultSwap, so it reaches `liquidationCall` and receives the seized tokens (`Q-404`). Deployment MUST include the fork-test proof that no successful seizure of vault collateral and no deficit report happens except through LPVaultSwap, over every path including calls routed through proxies and every allowed holder. Example: a keeper that calls `Spoke.liquidationCall(vaultReserveId, ghoReserveId, proxy, 5_000e18, false)` directly from its own contract on an account at HF 0.97 reverts when the Hub's vault-token `transfer` to the keeper fails the recipient rule; with `receiveShares = true` it reverts in Aave's validation because `receiveSharesEnabled` is false; the same keeper calling `LPVaultSwap.liquidate` with `debtToCover = 5_000e18` liquidates the account. Residual risk: if the Aave DAO set `receiveSharesEnabled = true`, a third party could take vault-token shares in the same block before the drift breaker trips; reconcile on exit then moves the matching units into pending settlement so backing stays whole. (Source: Q-025, Q-068, Q-116, Q-144, Q-213, Q-235, Q-281, Q-404)

- `REQ-HUB-046` — Avana's gates MUST treat a Hub halt, an Aave reserve pause or freeze, and drift as external states with the effects in [Pause, freeze and halt](#pause-freeze-and-halt), and MUST NOT add an Avana block on repay, fee collection or debt-free withdrawal. (Source: Q-051, Q-118, Q-140)
- `REQ-HUB-047` — Avana contracts MUST check amounts received or spent within a call (before/after deltas) and MUST NOT push vault tokens to the Hub; vault tokens reach the Hub only through Spoke-pulled `supply`, and fork tests assert the Hub's vault-token balance equals its recorded liquidity after every flow. (Source: Q-119, Q-124)
- `REQ-HUB-048` — No Avana contract MAY read or act on Spoke or Hub state while receiving vault tokens, and the vault token has no transfer hooks ([2.4](04-vault-tokens.md)). (Source: Q-119)
- `REQ-HUB-049` — The Lens, LiquidationPreview and keeper MUST use each user's stored `dynamicConfigKey` (or the Spoke's `getUserAccountData`), never a reserve's latest key; the opening-gate check MUST read health after the Spoke call, which already reflects the key refresh that `borrow` and `withdraw` perform. (Source: Q-119)
- `REQ-HUB-050` — `supply(reserveId, amount, proxy)`: `msg.sender` is the proxy; the Spoke reverts with `ReservePaused` or `ReserveFrozen` if the reserve is paused or frozen; it pulls exactly `amount` vault tokens from the proxy to the Hub with `safeTransferFrom`; `Hub.add` reverts if the Spoke is inactive (`SpokeNotActive`), halted (`SpokeHalted`), over `addCap` (`AddCapExceeded`) or would mint 0 shares; supplied shares follow `SPEC-HUB-008` and equal `amount` while A = S; emits `Supply`; returns `(suppliedShares, amount)`. - `REQ-HUB-052` — `borrow(debtReserveId, amount, proxy)`: checks only that debt reserve (not paused, not frozen, `borrowable`), not the collateral reserves; `Hub.draw` on the reserve's Hub sends `amount` of the debt asset to `msg.sender` (the proxy), reverting if the Spoke is inactive or halted, over `drawCap` (`DrawCapExceeded`, per `SPEC-HUB-003`) or above liquidity (`InsufficientLiquidity`); drawn shares round up; the Spoke then refreshes every collateral key, reverts with `HealthFactorBelowThreshold` if HF < 1, and updates the risk premium through the Hub's `riskPremiumThreshold` check (`InvalidPremiumChange`); emits `Borrow`. (Source: Q-064, Q-081, Q-119, Q-233)

With Aave's 1,000 GHO minimum debt, an account near the minimum is always fully liquidated by the dust rule, so partial settlement helps only larger accounts. (Source: Q-018, Q-119)

### CDP Spoke implementation

Each CDP Spoke is the standard Aave V4 Spoke from the pinned release, deployed by the Aave DAO and unmodified; Avana adds no code to it. Its liquidation entry point is the standard 5-argument `liquidationCall(collateralReserveId, debtReserveId, user, debtToCover, receiveShares)`, which LPVaultSwap calls with `receiveShares = false`. Avana uses no liquidation-manager Spoke variant: the variant Aave Labs offers replaces this call with a collateral-capped one and allows one collateral reserve per Spoke, while a CDP Spoke lists several markets. Exclusivity comes from the vault token's transfer rule, under which the Hub can send seized vault tokens only to LPVaultSwap (2.4 `REQ-VTOKEN-006`, `REQ-VTOKEN-025`), and three settings set in the listing AIP and enforced by the drift check close every path around it: `receiveSharesEnabled = false`, `liquidationFee = 0` (or paid to Avana's fee receiver) and no reinvestment controller.

- `REQ-HUB-092` — Each CDP Spoke MUST be the standard Aave V4 Spoke, whose only liquidation entry point is the 5-argument `liquidationCall(uint256 collateralReserveId, uint256 debtReserveId, address user, uint256 debtToCover, bool receiveShares)` that `IAaveV4Spoke` declares and LPVaultSwap calls with `receiveShares = false`. ### Aave valuation under failing prices

- `REQ-HUB-106` — Aave values every reserve at the price its source serves, so under a failing price the Spoke's health factor and liquidation sizing MUST be conservative, never in the borrower's favour. The LPOracle and the DebtOracle serve the prices in the table ([2.10](10-lp-oracle.md) `SPEC-ORC-017`, `SPEC-ORC-018`); GHO keeps its fixed $1. Aave itself does not check price freshness: its `liquidationCall` would size a seizure of an Expired market at price 1 as every supplied share for almost no debt, and a repayment of stale debt at its stress price as more collateral than the debt is worth. The vault token's transfer rule and the three required settings make LPVaultSwap the only liquidator (`REQ-HUB-092`), and LPVaultSwap seizes only a market whose own price is fresh and agreeing and repays only a debt reserve whose own price is Live ([2.11](11-lpvaultswap.md)); every other reserve of the account counts at its served price. A failing price therefore only lowers the health factor, can only make an account liquidatable on its fresh reserves, and never sizes a seizure or a repayment. Repay calls no oracle; a debt-free withdrawal has health factor `type(uint256).max`; borrow and withdraw-with-debt keep Avana's account-wide gate (every price Live within `borrowPriceMaxAge`), so no new debt is priced on a served stress price. The cost falls on a borrower who holds a failing market or stale debt beside other positions and is bounded by the bonus on what is seized from the fresh reserves. Example: an account with $10,000 in market A and $5,000 in market B (both at 3,120 bps) and 4,000 GHO of debt has HF `(3,120 + 1,560) / 4,000 = 1.17`; when B is Expired it is served at 1 and the HF falls to `3,120 / 4,000 = 0.78`; LPVaultSwap liquidates against A, and Aave's sizing toward 1.10 at the 5% maximum bonus repays `(1.10 × 4,000 − 3,120) / (1.10 − 1.05 × 0.312) ≈ 1,657` GHO and seizes about $1,740 of A, a bonus of about $83, while B is untouched until its price recovers. Checked by: TEST-230, TEST-226. (Source: Q-247, Q-248)

| State of the source | LPOracle `latestAnswer()` (vault-token reserve) | DebtOracle `latestAnswer()` (non-GHO debt reserve) | Effect in Aave |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live (or Drifting for a market), routes agree | Accepted price | Accepted price | Normal valuation |
| Routes disagree (pause, or disagreement past `disagreementPauseMax`) | LP price built from the lower of each token's two route answers, each capped | Higher of the two route answers | Collateral lower, debt higher |
| Cached (at most `cachedWindow`) | Last accepted price | Last accepted price | Unchanged valuation; this reserve is neither seized nor repaid |
| Expired | 1 (10⁻⁸ USD per whole vault token, the smallest price Aave accepts) | `⌈accepted × (10⁴ + debtStressRiseBps) / 10⁴⌉` | Collateral counts as worthless; debt at its stress price |
