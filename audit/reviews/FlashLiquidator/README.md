## Checked and holding

- Forged callbacks: `executeOperation` requires an open, unused context (transient, so empty in any other transaction), `msg.sender == POOL`, `initiator == address(this)`, one asset, amount and premium equal to the context's, and the `params` hash. A third party's `Pool.flashLoan` naming the helper as receiver carries `initiator = third party` and reverts; one started from inside the helper's own `liquidate` (a hostile token or route) meets the `USED` state and reverts; `flashLoanSimple`'s callback selector does not exist on the helper.
- Reentrancy and reuse: `flashLiquidate` reverts `ContextActive` while the context is open (through the payout and market-token transfers); the used flag is set before any external call; a skipped callback reverts `InvalidFlashCallback`.
- Premium: the pre-loan ceiling quote and the charged premium are both held to `maxFlashFeeBps` and the charge to `maxPremium`; a Pool that changed its premium cannot charge more than the caller accepted.
- Payout accounting: the payout is the rise of the helper's `T` balance over the whole call; a stray or donated balance is never paid out and never pays for a call (a transfer-fee asset with a pre-held balance reverts `PayoutTooLow(0, minPayout)`); market tokens are forwarded by rise only. The allowance-consumed reading of `keeperOut` affects only the top-up size: an over- or under-read is corrected by the whole-call balance check.
- Approvals: LPVaultSwap's allowance is reset after `liquidate`, the Pool's after the loan; nothing remains after a successful call, and a failed one reverts as a whole.
- Payer and recipient are fixed by the variant (`msg.sender`, or `FUNDING_SAFE`), never by the request; a victim's approval to the public helper is usable only by the victim's own call. `claimEscrowed` is `SIGNER`-only and claims only the helper's own credit, only to `FUNDING_SAFE`.
- Frozen callers: a frozen address liquidates through the public helper and is paid through it, as `REQ-SWAP-105` decides; LPVaultSwap's freeze check sees the helper. An operator helper is not in the protected set, so freezing its signer does not stop it; the helper address itself must be frozen.

## Not settled

- GHO debt through the helper on Ethereum: the helper borrows only from the Aave V3 Core Pool, and GHO there is minted by its facilitator rather than supplied, so `maxFlashLoan(GHO)` may be 0 (no aToken balance, virtual accounting off). If so, GHO-debt liquidations cannot use the helper on Ethereum, and the `REQ-FLASH-008` sizing rule reads 0 for GHO. This depends on the deployed Pool's GHO configuration, which this review could not check offline.
- The PoCs model LPVaultSwap from its spec; FL-01 and FL-02 should be re-run against LPVaultSwap once it is written.
