# Avana internal audit mapping

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-10

For each control-area property proved on the AMM-neutral specification (run `FV-CTL-R5`; rows marked *changed* were restated for that design), this mapping names the prover rule (CVL) that proves it on the real code, the contract it runs against, a sketch, and the `PROP-NNN` of [5.2 Formal Properties](../../../spec/05-verification/02-formal-properties.md) it corresponds to or adds. The CVL sources go in `contracts/certora/` (location only); rules marked **new** have no `PROP-NNN` yet and take the next free numbers when 5.2 is next edited. Every rule carries `rule_sanity: basic` and a `satisfy` witness for its success path.
