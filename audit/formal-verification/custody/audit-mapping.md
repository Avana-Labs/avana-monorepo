# Avana internal audit mapping

> **Status:** In review · **Owner:** Avana Labs · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-10

For each custody property this mapping gives:
- the prover rule (CVL) that proves it on the real code, with its name, contract and a sketch;
- the spec-stage check that proves the specified behaviour against the spec (run `FV-CUS-R3`; rows marked *changed* or *new* were brought to the AMM-neutral design in run `FV-CUS-R5`);
- the `PROP-NNN` it corresponds to in 5.2, or the new number it adds.

New numbers continue after `PROP-084`, the last number in 5.2 after the AMM-neutral rewrite, which gave `PROP-075`…`PROP-084` to the Lock, the proxy triple, Hub-keyed caps, standard-Spoke settings, the cross-Hub cap, the flash source, the draw gate, the record bound, exact range and clamp, and the per-market budget window. The proposals this mapping made before the rewrite as `PROP-075`…`PROP-084` are renumbered `PROP-086`…`PROP-095` (*changed*); `PROP-085` is the record key by integration. The rules go in `contracts/certora/`; nothing there is edited by this mapping.
